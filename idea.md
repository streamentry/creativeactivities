# Creative Activities product direction

The original Golden Moments nostalgia-game concept evolved into **gentle shared photo activities**, and is now the first slice of **Creative Activities**: free, accessible, startable activities people do together away from the screen. The north star and 10-year direction are in [MISSION](MISSION.md) and [VISION](VISION.md); the look and voice are in [DESIGN](DESIGN.md).

Do not use this file as a second roadmap. Current decisions are in [product specs](docs/specs/product.md), [ASO strategy](docs/strategy/us-aso.md), [workboard](WORKBOARD.md), and [canonical metadata](metadata/app-store.en-US.json).

The old concept remains in Git history. Brain-training benefits, unverified testimonials, subscriptions, automatic family sync and diagnosis features are not current commitments.
