# Keyword Planner export, 2026-11-13: what it does and does not show

Source: `keywords.csv` (UTF-16, tab-separated; 1,211 rows; Nov 2025 to Oct 2026).
Per [SEO.md](../../SEO.md) section 1, every figure keeps its source, locale and caveats.

## Measurement caveats

- **Currency column is `VND` on every row**, so the account or targeting is Vietnam. The volumes are not a US or worldwide demand map.
- Volumes are Google's rounded buckets (50, 500, 5,000, 50,000, 500,000), not counts. Per-month columns are empty.
- Competition and bid columns are Google Ads data, not organic difficulty.

## Findings

- None of the Tier A or B clusters in SEO.md section 3.2 appear as entries: `dementia activities`, `activities for seniors`, `senior art activities`, `reminiscence`, `bedridden`, `seated`, `printable`, `sensory`, `grandparent`. Blank means unknown, not zero.
- Closest relevant rows, all Vietnam targeting: `therapeutic art activities` 5,000; `therapeutic art activities for adults` 500; `art therapy activities for adults` 500; `for seniors` 5,000; `dementia patients` 5,000; `art therapy crafts` 50.
- The large rows (`dementia`, `senior care`, `elderly care`, `activities`) are head terms with mixed intent and are not targets.
- `therapeutic` and `art therapy` phrases are the only activity-shaped demand in the file. SEO.md section 2 warns against marketing crafts as therapy, so use them only as accurate, scoped secondary language.
