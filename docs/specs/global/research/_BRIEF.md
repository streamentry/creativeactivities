# Research brief (shared by all research agents)

Goal: inform a specification for making creativeactivities.org (website + iOS/Android app) the world's most-used, most-trusted place to find and do creative activities together — across crafts, art for wellbeing, parenting/kids, older adults and dementia, and intergenerational families. Think at the scale of the biggest sites in the world (Pinterest, YouTube, wikiHow, Duolingo, Khan Academy), while honouring the project's own constraints.

Read first (in /Volumes/SANDISK-Data/creativeactivities-2026): MISSION.md, VISION.md, DESIGN.md (short), then skim docs/strategy/category-leader.md and docs/plan/competitors.md so you do not repeat dementia-app research that already exists.

Non-negotiables to respect in your findings:
- "Art therapy" is a credentialed clinical profession. Report how leaders talk about it, but flag any treatment/healing claims; our product must describe activities as creative, recreational or supportive, never as therapy, unless delivered or reviewed by qualified professionals.
- No dark patterns (streak shame, manipulative notifications), minimal data, free core, no ads that degrade trust.

Method and evidence rules:
- Use WebSearch (load it with ToolSearch "select:WebSearch" if needed) and, where useful, the web-fetch agent or WebFetch. Prefer primary sources (company pages, app store listings, official reports, peer-reviewed reviews, government/WHO).
- Every number gets a source URL and an "as of" date. If you can only estimate, say "estimate" and how. Never invent figures, quotes or features. If a page blocks you, say so.
- Separate: (a) what the leader does, (b) why it works (mechanism), (c) what we should copy, adapt or reject and why (against MISSION/DESIGN).

Output: write ONE markdown file at the path your task gives you (1,800–3,000 words). Structure:
1. Scope and date
2. Landscape table (name, audience, scale with source, core features, business model, what makes it win)
3. Feature patterns worth adopting (numbered, each with source examples)
4. Content patterns (types, formats, volume, cadence, SEO/structure)
5. Games / interactive patterns (if relevant)
6. Gaps no one serves well (our openings)
7. Anti-patterns and risks to avoid
8. Sources (numbered list of URLs with access date 2026-11-14)

Do not edit any other file. Do not commit. Reply to me in under 150 words: file path, the 5 most important takeaways.
