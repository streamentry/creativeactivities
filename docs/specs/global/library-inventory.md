## What the file does not know

A column that says `none` or `not recorded` means the front matter holds no such fact; nothing is inferred.

- **Reviewer, safety check date:** no card, hub or guide records a credentialed reviewer, so every row reads `none`.
- **Tested date:** cards do not record when a team member tested them (the `testedWith` field proposed in 06 §2 is not in the schema).
- **AI-assisted:** the `aiAssisted` flag (CNT-032) is not in the schema, so every row reads `not recorded`. AI assistance in drafting has been used in this repository; the flag, and what a page may claim about testing, need a maintainer decision.
- **Sources present:** `n/a` for cards, which cite no outside sources.
