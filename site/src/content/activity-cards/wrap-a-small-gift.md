safety:
  - "Keep the scissors with the helper. Cut the paper, tape and ribbon before you start."
  - "Tape can be hard to peel for some hands and can tug on skin. Stick strips to the edge of the table ready to pick up."
  - "Choose a gift that cannot break and has nothing loose that could come off. A book, a scarf or a pack of cards works well."
  - "Use ribbon in short lengths and take it away at the end. Do not leave loose lengths within reach."

Wrapping a present is a familiar job with a clear purpose, and many people have done it for years. The gift goes to someone real, so the work has a point beyond the table. There is no right way to wrap, and a loose parcel with a bright ribbon is just as welcome as a neat one.
