A leaf rubbing needs no paint and very little tidying up. The crayon picks up the raised veins under the paper, so the picture appears as you rub, and each leaf gives a different pattern. It works with leaves from a garden, a park or a houseplant, and you can do it at a table or from a chair with a tray.
