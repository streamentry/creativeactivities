description: "Press pre-torn colored tissue paper onto clear sticky film and hang it in a window so light shows through. No glue or cutting for the person; large-grip version."
summary: Press torn pieces of colored tissue paper onto clear sticky film, then hang it in a window to let light shine through.
enjoy: "People who like bright colors, sunny windows or watching light change a picture. A good choice for someone who enjoys placing things freely, with no plan and no wrong place."

settings: [tabletop, seated, bedside]
groups: [one-to-one, small-group, intergenerational]
materials:
  - "Clear self-adhesive film (contact film), cut by the helper to about letter size"
  - "Colored tissue paper, torn beforehand into pieces about the size of a palm"
  - "Two or three shallow bowls to sort the colors"
  - "Masking tape, for the helper to hold the film and to hang it"
  - "Scissors, for the helper to use beforehand only"
substitutions:
  - "No contact film? Use clear laminating sheets that are sticky on one side."
  - "No tissue paper? Use thin colored cellophane or the thin paper from gift wrap."
senses: [sight, touch]
steps:
  - "Before you start, the helper cuts the film to size, peels off the backing and tapes it sticky side up to the table."
  - "Put the bowls of tissue pieces within easy reach."
  - "Choose a color together. Any color is a good one."
  - "Pick up a piece and press it onto the sticky film anywhere you like."
  - "Add more pieces, and let them overlap so the colors mix."
  - "Stop when the picture feels full or you are ready to be done."
  - "The helper lays a second piece of film over the top or folds the first one in half, and smooths it down."
  - "Hang the picture in a window and look at the light coming through."
plainSteps:
  - Before you start, the helper cuts the film to size.
  - The helper peels off the backing.
  - The helper tapes it sticky side up to the table.
  - Put the bowls of tissue pieces within easy reach.
  - Choose a color together.
  - Any color is a good one.
  - Pick up a piece.
  - Press it onto the sticky film anywhere you like.
  - Add more pieces so the colors overlap and mix.
  - Stop when it feels full or you are ready to be done.
  - The helper lays a second piece of film over the top.
  - Or the helper folds the first one in half.
  - The helper smooths it down.
  - Hang it in a window.
  - Look at the light coming through.

safety:
  - "The helper cuts the film with scissors beforehand and puts them away. The person does not cut anything."
  - "Keep small torn tissue pieces and the film away from the mouth, and stay nearby while they are out. Clear them away afterward."
  - "Check for sensitivity to the sticky film or tape, and keep the sticky side off bare skin and hair."
  - "The helper hangs the picture, using a stable chair or step stool if needed. The person does not climb or lean out."

The sticky film holds every piece where it lands, so there is no glue to spread and nothing to cut. Hanging the finished picture in a window gives the colors a reason to be looked at again through the day.
