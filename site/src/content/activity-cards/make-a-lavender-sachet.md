name: Make a lavender sachet
seoTitle: "Make a Lavender Sachet: A Simple No-Sew Craft"
description: "Fill and tie a small fabric sachet with dried lavender together. No sewing, five minutes to set up, low cost, with bedside, unscented and easier versions."
summary: Spoon dried lavender onto a square of fabric and tie it into a small scented bundle.
type: sensory
prepMinutes: 5
activityMinutes: [10, 25]
cost: low
mess: low
energy: low
supervision: nearby
settings: [tabletop, seated, bedside]
groups: [one-to-one, small-group, intergenerational]
materials:
  - A square of cotton fabric, about 8 inches across
  - Two or three spoonfuls of dried lavender
  - A ribbon or piece of yarn, about 12 inches
  - A small bowl and a teaspoon
  - A tray or plate to catch spills
substitutions:
  - "No fabric? Use a clean cotton handkerchief, an old pillowcase square or a small organza bag."
  - "No lavender? Dried rice with a few dried rose petals, or a cinnamon stick, gives a different scent."
  - "No ribbon? A rubber band covered with a strip of fabric works."
senses: [smell, touch, sight]
steps:
  - "Set the tray on the table and pour the lavender into the bowl."
  - "Offer the bowl to smell, and ask if they would like to make a sachet with it."
  - "Lay the fabric square flat on the tray, patterned side down."
  - "Spoon lavender into the middle of the fabric together."
  - "Gather the four corners up into a small bundle."
  - "Hold the bundle while one person wraps the ribbon around the neck."
  - "Tie the ribbon in a knot or a bow. The helper can do this."
  - "Decide together where the sachet should go, such as a drawer or a coat pocket."
variations:
  - kind: easier
    text: "Fill the bundle beforehand and leave only the ribbon to tie, or offer a choice between two fabrics and two ribbons."
  - kind: bedside
    text: "Use a lap tray with raised edges and a pre-filled spoon. Make one small sachet and tie it loosely together."
  - kind: less-sensory
    text: "Skip the lavender and fill the sachet with plain dried rice or beans, so it is something to hold with no scent."
  - kind: low-vision
    text: "Use a bright fabric on a dark tray, and a thick, textured ribbon that is easy to find and grip."
  - kind: with-children
    text: "A child spoons and gathers the fabric while the older adult chooses the fabric and ties or holds the ribbon."
safety:
  - "Strong scents can be unpleasant or trigger headaches or breathing problems for some people. Smell a little first and stop if anyone dislikes it."
  - "Some people react to lavender on the skin. Use a spoon and wash hands afterward if there is any sensitivity."
  - "Dried lavender and rice are small enough to swallow. Supervise closely if anything might be put in the mouth."
  - "Do not put the sachet near a heater, radiator or open flame."
stopNote: "It is fine to stop at any point. A bowl of lavender to smell is enough on its own. Cover the bowl and offer to finish the sachet another day."

A sachet is small and quick, and the steps work well with one person spooning and another tying. The finished bundle is something to hold, give as a gift, or tuck into a drawer.
