steps:
  - "Set the paper in front of the person and choose a pencil or marker together."
  - "Hold the cup steady on the paper while they trace round its rim to make a circle."
  - "Draw three or four curved or straight lines across the circle to split it into sections."
  - "Pick one section and fill it with a slow repeat, such as short lines all leaning one way."
  - "Move to the next section and try a different repeat, such as small circles, dots or zigzags."
  - "Go one section at a time, resting the pencil between sections if you like."
  - "Stop when the circle is full or whenever they want to, then turn the page around and look at it together."

Here the circle sets the edge and each section is a small, separate job. Because the structure is already there, there is nothing to plan, and some people find the slow repeating lines calming.
