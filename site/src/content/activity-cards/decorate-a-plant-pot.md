summary: A plain pot decorated with paint, paper or twine, ready for a plant or herb to live in.
enjoy: "People who have grown herbs or houseplants, or who enjoy painting and practical projects. A nice fit for someone who likes to see a small job finished and put to use."
type: craft
prepMinutes: 10
activityMinutes: [20, 45]
cost: low
mess: medium
energy: low
supervision: nearby
settings: [tabletop, seated]
groups: [one-to-one, small-group, intergenerational]
materials:
  - A small terracotta or plastic plant pot
  - Non-toxic, water-based paint and a thick-handled brush
  - Garden twine or yarn (optional)
  - Paper napkins or magazine pictures with a glue stick (optional)
  - Newspaper to cover the table
  - A small plant, herb or bulb, and a little potting soil
substitutions:
  - "No pot? Use a clean food can with no sharp edges, a yogurt cup or a tin with a hole punched by the helper."
  - "No paint? Wrap the pot in twine, ribbon or strips of fabric held with tape."
senses: [sight, touch, smell]
steps:
  - "Cover the table and put the pot and materials within reach."
  - "Offer two ways to decorate, such as paint or wrapped twine, and let them choose."
  - "Paint the outside of the pot, or wrap twine around it while you hold the end in place."
  - "Leave paint to dry for about an hour; twine is ready straight away."
  - "Smell the plant or herb together and talk about where it might sit."
  - "Spoon soil into the pot and set the plant in it, taking turns if that suits you both."
  - "Water it lightly and put it on a windowsill or table where you can both see it."
variations:
  - kind: easier
    text: "Paint a base coat in advance and offer one color for stripes or dots, or a ready-cut strip of paper to glue on."
  - kind: less-sensory
    text: "Skip the soil and paint, and wrap the pot in soft yarn while it sits on a clean tray."
  - kind: bedside
    text: "Decorate a small plastic pot on a lap tray with twine or a glue stick, and pot the plant later at a table."
  - kind: with-children
    text: "A child paints the pot while the older adult chooses the plant and shows how to firm the soil."
  - kind: for-a-group
    text: "Each person decorates a pot and the group plants a row of herbs to share on a common windowsill."
safety:
  - "Use non-toxic, water-based paint, and wash it off skin with soap and water."
  - "Wear gloves or wash hands after handling potting soil, and keep it away from mouths."
  - "Choose plants that are safe to touch and not harmful if nibbled, such as basil, mint or parsley."
  - "Check food cans for sharp rims before use, and tape over any rough edge."
stopNote: "It is fine to stop once the pot is painted. Let it dry and plant it another day, or use it as it is for pens, buttons or dried flowers."
