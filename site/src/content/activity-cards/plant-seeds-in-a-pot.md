materials:
  - A small plant pot with a drainage hole and a saucer
  - Bagged potting mix
  - A few large seeds, such as beans, peas, nasturtiums or sunflowers
  - A spoon or small trowel
  - A small watering can or a cup of water
  - Gardening gloves, if wanted

substitutions:
  - "No pot? Use a yogurt cup or a cut-down milk carton with a hole poked in the base."
  - "No seed packet? Dried beans or peas from the kitchen often sprout, though not always."

senses: [touch, sight, smell]
steps:
  - "Spread newspaper or a tray on the table and set the pot on it."
  - "Spoon potting mix into the pot together until it is nearly full."
  - "Press the soil down gently with flat hands."
  - "Show the seeds and ask which ones they would like to plant."
  - "Push each seed into the soil about as deep as a fingertip."
  - "Cover the seeds with a little soil and pat it down."
  - "Water slowly together until the soil looks dark and damp."
  - "Choose a bright windowsill for the pot and agree who will check on it."

Planting seeds is a familiar job for many people, and it gives you a reason to look at the pot together over the following days. Large seeds are easy to see and hold, and it does not matter how neatly they go in.
