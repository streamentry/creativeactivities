steps:
  - "Sit where you can both reach the table or tray, with any drum or blocks in front of you."

  - "Tap the beat gently, with one finger or a flat palm, at a speed that feels comfortable."
  - "Invite them to tap along, copying what you do."
  - "Swap places, so they lead and you copy the tap they choose."
  - "Pause the song whenever they want a rest, and start again when they are ready."
  - "Finish with one last tap together, then rest both hands on the table."

Keeping time is a shared pattern rather than a skill to get right. One person leads, the other follows, and either can stop whenever the rhythm feels like enough. It works at a table, in a bedside chair, or with a child keeping the beat alongside.
