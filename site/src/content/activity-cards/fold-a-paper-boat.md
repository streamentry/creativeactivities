senses: [sight, touch, hearing]
steps:
  - "Fold the sheet in half from top to bottom and run a thumbnail along the crease."
  - "Fold the two top corners down to the middle to make a triangle."
  - "Fold the bottom strip up on each side, over the triangle."
  - "Open the shape from the bottom and press it flat into a diamond."
  - "Fold the bottom point of the diamond up to the top, on each side, to make a small triangle."
  - "Open it again from the bottom and press it flat into a smaller diamond."
  - "Gently pull the two top points apart, and the boat will open out."
  - "Float it together in a basin of water, or set it on a shelf to keep."

  - "Use only a few inches of water in a basin or tray, and stay with anyone near water."
