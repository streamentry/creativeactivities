steps:
  - "Lay the card flat in front of the person, with the long side across if they prefer."
  - "Tear a few long strips of pale blue or white paper for the sky and place them in a tray."
  - "Tear strips in greens for hills, and strips in browns or yellows for the ground."
  - "Rub glue on the top third of the card and press the sky strips on, letting them overlap."
  - "Glue the middle of the card and press on the green strips, tearing a wavy edge along the top for hills."
  - "Glue the bottom and add the ground strips, overlapping the bottom of the hills."
  - "Add a torn round of yellow paper for a sun, or a few small green scraps for trees, if wanted."
  - "Press the picture flat with a clean hand and leave it to dry, then choose where to put it."

Long strips layered in bands are quick to place and hard to get wrong, since torn edges look like hills and clouds on their own. Each band of color is a small step you can stop after.
