Hands that are stiff in the morning, sore after a long day or slow to grip can still enjoy making something. The change is usually in the tool, not the activity: a thicker handle, a bigger piece, or a press with the whole palm instead of a pinch with the fingertips.

## How do you make a tool easier to hold?

Start with what is already in the house. A rolled face cloth around a pen or brush makes it thicker and softer. A foam hair roller slides over a pencil. A thick wooden spoon handle pushed into a slit in the back of a sponge makes a stamp that can be held in a whole fist.

- **Make it thicker,** not heavier. A light, fat handle is easier than a thin, heavy one.
- **Tape the paper down** so the other hand does not need to hold it still.
- **Cut everything first.** The [sponge prints](/activity/print-with-sponge-shapes/) and the [window picture](/activity/make-a-tissue-paper-window-picture/) both start with the helper cutting, and the scissors go away before the person sits down.

## Which crafts need the least grip?

The [tissue paper window picture](/activity/make-a-tissue-paper-window-picture/) only asks for pressing with a flat hand onto sticky film. [Oil pastels on dark paper](/activity/draw-with-oil-pastels-on-dark-paper/) glide with very little pressure, and a chunky pastel is easy to hold. [Shaping a pinch pot](/activity/shape-a-pinch-pot/) uses soft clay, which can be pressed with the palm as well as pinched.

## How do you share the work without taking it over?

Let the person choose the color, the shape and where it goes, and do the holding, cutting and tying yourself. [Wrapping a small gift](/activity/wrap-a-small-gift/) works well this way: one person folds, the other tapes. [Winding a ball of yarn](/activity/wind-a-ball-of-yarn/) can be shared too, with one person holding the skein and the other winding.

The [guide to crafts for limited dexterity](/crafts/crafts-for-limited-dexterity/) has more ideas for tools and setup, and the [art supplies guide](/activities/art-supplies-for-older-adults/) explains which materials are easiest to hold.
