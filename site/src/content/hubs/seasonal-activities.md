Seasons give a gentle shape to the year. Seeds in spring, flowers in summer, leaf rubbings in autumn or a window picture in winter can each be a small reason to make something together.

## How do you choose a celebration without assuming one?

Ask, and offer a few choices: is there a day coming up you would like to make something for? A festival from the person's own tradition, a family birthday, a new year or nothing at all are all good answers. Let the person decide what a decoration looks like and what it means. Do not turn something sacred to them into a craft without asking.

## Which activities suit which time of year?

- **Spring:** [plant seeds in a pot](/activity/plant-seeds-in-a-pot/) or [decorate a plant pot](/activity/decorate-a-plant-pot/).
- **Summer:** [arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) or [press leaves and flowers](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/).
- **Autumn:** [make leaf rubbings](/activity/make-leaf-rubbings/) with leaves from a garden or park.
- **Winter:** [watch birds from a window](/activity/watch-birds-from-a-window/) or hang a [tissue paper window picture](/activity/make-a-tissue-paper-window-picture/).
- **Any celebration:** a [festival decoration](/activity/make-a-festival-decoration/), a [paper chain garland](/activity/make-a-paper-chain-garland/) or a [greeting card](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/).

Which season is it where you are? The seasons run opposite ways in the two halves of the world, so [choose your own on the seasons page](/seasons/) and see activities that suit it.

## What if a holiday is a hard time?

Some dates bring back people who have died or times that were difficult. If an occasion seems to upset the person, leave it out. Making something for the season, with no date attached, is just as good. The [seasonal crafts guide](/crafts/seasonal-crafts/) has more ideas.
