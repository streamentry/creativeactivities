Sɛ wobɛhwehwɛ art therapist a ɛfata no, ɛhwehwɛ adwuma kakra. Din no nyɛ pɛ baabi nyinaa, na obiara a ɔma art nhyiam no nyɛ therapist a wɔasua therapy mu. Saa krataa yi kyerɛkyerɛ sɛnea wɔbɛhwɛ nkyerɛkyerɛmu wɔ United States ne United Kingdom, sɛnea wɔbɛhwehwɛ kyerɛwtohɔ a ɛyɛ nokorɛ, ne nsɛm a wobɛbisa ansa na wobɛba nhyiam a edi kan. Ɛka nso nea wobetumi ayɛ fie bere a woreyɛ nhwehwɛmu no.

## Hena na ɛsɛ sɛ wobisa kan?

Bisa dɔkota anaa obi a ɔwɔ mmara mu ayaresa adwuma so a ɔwɔ tumi. Wɔbɛka kyerɛ wo sɛ art therapy fata anaa nnyɛ ma onipa a wo re hwɛ no, na wɔbɛma wo kɔ baabi a ɛfata. Sɛ onipa no wɔ dwiriw anaa dementia a, dɔkota a ɔhu ne abakɔsɛm no nso betumi aka therapist a ɔwɔ nimdeɛ wɔ saa ho no ho.

Sɛ wontumi nnfa obi a ɛho hia nkɔ ntɛm na onipa no wɔ amanehunu anaa ɔwɔ akwan a ɛbɛma ɔhaw, fa ho ka dɔkota, ɔhaw mmoa mu nkurɔfo, anaa ntɛm mmoa ho nkɔsoɔ.

## Ɛdeɛ na mɛhwɛ nkyerɛkyerɛmu wɔ United States?

Hwehwɛ nkyerɛkyerɛmu a Art Therapy Credentials Board de ama no. [American Art Therapy Association](https://arttherapy.org/credentials-and-licensure/) kyerɛkyerɛ no:

- **ATR** (Registered Art Therapist) hia sɛ wosua art therapy wɔ sukuu a ɛkɔ akyiri ho, na wɔyɛ adwuma a wɔhwɛ so wɔ akyi.
- **ATR-BC** (Board Certified) nya bere a wotwe nhwehwɛmu a ɛyɛ aman mu, ka sukuu ne adwuma a wɔhwɛ so nso ho.

Ebinom nso ma art therapist nkyerɛkyerɛmu wɔ mmara so. Wɔ ɔman no mu no, ɔman no kyerɛwtohɔ betumi aka sɛ therapist no tumi yɛ adwuma wɔ wo baabi. Bisa therapist no nkyerɛkyerɛmu din ne nɔma, na wo ankasa hwɛ so.

## Ɛdeɛ na mɛhwɛ kyerɛwtohɔ wɔ United Kingdom?

Wɔ UK mu no, “art therapist” ne “art psychotherapist” yɛ din a mmara ahwɛ ma. Ɛsɛ sɛ adwumayɛfoɔ no atwerɛ wɔ Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC) mu. [GOV.UK krataa a ɛka art therapist registration ho](https://www.gov.uk/art-therapist-registration) kyerɛkyerɛ saa mmara no.

Sɛ wobɛhwɛ therapist bi:

1. Bisa wɔn din koro ne wɔn kyerɛwtohɔ nɔma.
2. Hwehwɛ [HCPC kyerɛwtohɔ](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/check-the-register/), a obiara tumi hwehwɛ no wɔ intanɛt so a ɛnni ka.
3. Hwɛ sɛ din no ne nea therapist no aka kyerɛ wo no ntɛmpa.

Sɛ wohu therapist no wɔ kyerɛwtohɔ no mu a, nnyɛ nhyiam. Bisa wɔn sɛ wɔnkyerɛkyerɛ.

## Nsɛm bɛn na mɛbisa ansa na mɛba nhyiam a edi kan?

Nsɛm kakra a ɛboa wo sɛ wobɛtumi asiesie nneɛma a ɛwɔ hɔ:

- **Nimdeɛ.** Woayɛ adwuma wɔ mpanimfoɔ ho anaa? Wɔ nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia ho anaa? Wɔ dodow bɛn?
- **Baabi.** Nhyiam no kɔ baabi bɛn: ayaresabea, ahemfie a wɔhwɛ nnipa, wo fie, anaa intanɛt so?
- **Sika.** Nhyiam biako ye sika bɛn? Wogye akatua anaa ahodoɔ foforɔ? Wɔ sika a ɛsesa sɛnea obi tumi tua no anaa?
- **Nhyehyɛe.** Nhyiam a ɛfata yɛ sɛn? Ɛkyɛ sɛn, na nnipa dodow bɛn na wɔsɛ?
- **Abusua.** Abusuafoɔ betumi aka ho anaa? Wɔbɛkasa fa nkɔsoɔ ho sɛnea?
- **Gyae.** Ɛbɛyɛ dɛn sɛ obi no pɛ sɛ ɔntoa so, anaa nhyiam no ma ɔhaw?

Therapist a ɛyɛ papa bɛbua saa nsɛm yi pefee. Sɛ wo ho yɛ wo ntɛntɛn anaa wɔde ntɛmpa ma wo a, ɛyɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ mfaso.

## Ɛdeɛ na yɛbɛtumi ayɛ fie bere a yɛreyɛ nhwehwɛmu no?

Sɛ wotena ase de ahyɛ mu a, ɛnkyerɛ sɛ biribiara remfa nkɔ so. Adwuma a wɔyɛ fie betumi ayɛ wo nnawɔtwe mu anigye, na ɛnsɛ sɛ wofrɛ no therapy. - [Yɛ ayɛyie kaad](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) ma obi a mo baanu nim.
- [Hyehyɛ nhwiren](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) wɔ akuw mu wɔ ɔpon no so, mo baanu nyinaa.
