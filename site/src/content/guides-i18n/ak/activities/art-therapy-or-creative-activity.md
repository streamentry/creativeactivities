Nsɛnkyerɛnne ayaresa ne nsɛnkyerɛnne adwuma betumi ahwɛ te sɛ nea ɛyɛ pɛ wɔ hɔ. Wɔde pampa, peinti ne ebɔdɛ di dwuma nyinaa. Wɔhyɛ wɔn nkyɛn wɔ botaeɛ mu, wɔ nea ɔdi wɔn anim mu, ne wɔn adesua mu. Sɛ wunim nea ɛhyɛ wɔn nkyɛn a, ɛbɛboa wo ma wopaw boafoɔ a ɛfata, na ɛbɛkora wo ho ano sɛ wobɛfrɛ anigyeɛ a wɔde peinti yɛ wɔ anadwo bi sɛ ɛyɛ nea ɛnyɛ. Saa krataa yi kyerɛ nea ɛhyɛ wɔn nkyɛn ne nea wobetumi ayɛ wɔ fie nnɛ.

## Nsɛnkyerɛnne ayaresa ye dɛn?

Nsɛnkyerɛnne ayaresa yɛ adwuma wɔ ayaresa adwene mu. Nsɛnkyerɛnne ayaresafo yɛ adwumfoɔ a wɔasua wɔn yɛ, na wɔde nsɛnkyerɛnne yɛ adwuma wɔ adwumayɛ nkitahodi mu, na wɔyɛ adwuma kɔ botaeɛ a wɔde ne onipa no bɛyɛ ho. Wɔn adwuma gyina ayaresa anaa ahwɛyiye nhyehyɛe a ɛkɔ akyiri so.

Wɔ America, nsɛnkyerɛnne ayaresafo betumi anya ntease te sɛ ATR anaa ATR-BC, a ɛhwehwɛ sukuu a wɔkɔ akyiri ne adwuma a wɔayɛ wɔ akwankyerɛ ase. [American Art Therapy Association](https://arttherapy.org/credentials-and-licensure/) kyerɛkyerɛ saa ntease yi. Wɔ UK, "nsɛnkyerɛnne ayaresafo" ne "nsɛnkyerɛnne adwene ayaresafo" yɛ din a wɔabɔ ho ban, na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔkyerɛw wɔn din. [British Association of Art Therapists](https://www.baat.org/art-therapy/what-is-art-therapy/) kyerɛkyerɛ adwuma no.

## Nsɛnkyerɛnne adwuma ye dɛn?

Nsɛnkyerɛnne adwuma yɛ biribi a wobɛyɛ ma anigyeɛ ne nkitahodi. Ɛnni ayaresa nhyehyɛe, ɛnni nhwehwɛmu, ne ayaresa botaeɛ biara. Nsɛnkyerɛnne a yɛbɛyɛ bɛka, ebɔdɛ a yɛde bɛyɛ mosaic, anaa kaad a yɛde bɛma da a obi awo, nyinaa yɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne adwuma.

Saa nhwɛsoɔ a ɛwɔ ha yɛ saa adwuma yi a ɛnyɛ den. Wɔyɛ nkitahodi bere, na ɛnyɛ ayaresa. Ɛnyɛ ayaresa, ɛnsa yare biara, ɛnsiw yare ano, na ɛnyɛ ma nkaebɔ yɛ yiye.

## Ɛberɛ bɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wohwehwɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne ayaresafo?

Kɔ ɔdokota kan. Ɔdokota betumi aka kyerɛ wo sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wofa obi a ɔwɔ adwuma a wɔde ho ka ho akɔ hu. Sɛ obi a wo ho hia no wɔ anidasoɔ, awerɛhyɛmu, ɔhaw a ɛfa adwene ho anaa ɔhaw foforo a ɛfa adwene ho a, ayaresafo betumi aboa wo wɔ nneɛma a nsɛnkyerɛnne kɔnkɔn nni hɔ.

Sɛ wohwehwɛ ayaresafo na wobɛhwɛ sɛ ɔyɛ adwuma yie a, kenkan [sɛdeɛ wobɛhwehwɛ na wobɛhwɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne ayaresafo](/activities/finding-an-art-therapist/). Sɛ wopɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw wɔ adwuma yi ne yɛn adwuma ntam a, hwɛ [nsɛnkyerɛnne ayaresa ne nsɛnkyerɛnne adwuma](/art-therapy-and-creative-activities/). Sɛ obi ka sɛ ɔrepira ne ho anaa wo ho hia wo sɛ ɔbɛyɛ biribi a ɛnyɛ yie, nnyina ase. Frɛ ɔdokota, nsɛmmisa mmoa anaa ntɛm akwankyerɛ sɛdeɛ, ntɛm.

## Ɛdeɛn na wobetumi ayɛ wɔ fie nnɛ?

Ɛnhia sɛ wo wɔ ayaresafo ma wo ne obi nni nsɛnkyerɛnne anigyeɛ. Paw biribi a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, siesie no kan, na gyae bere biara a ɛremfa anigyeɛ nkɔ so. Yei yɛ beaeɛ pa a wobɛfi ase:

- [Yɛ peinti wɔ watercolour mu](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/), na ma nsu nkɔ so wɔ nkrataa a ɛyɛ mmɔho so.
- [Yɛ torn paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/), a ɛnhia sɛ wode sikyɛ, na ɛnni ɔkwan pɛpɛɛpɛ a wɔfa so de nkyekyɛmu ahyehyɛ.
- [Yɛ memory collage](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/) fi mfonini ne magazine mfonini so.
- [Hyɛ nhaban ne nhwiren](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/) wɔ buuk kɛse bi mu.

Sɛ wopɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne foforo a, hwɛ yɛn [adwuma ne nsɛnkyerɛnne akwankyerɛ](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/). Nsɛnkyerɛnne betumi ayɛ wo dapɛn mu anigyeɛ. Ɛnhia sɛ wofrɛ no ayaresa na ɛfata sɛ wodi.
