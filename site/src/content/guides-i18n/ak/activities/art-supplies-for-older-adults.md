Akit adepa a ɛyɛ kakra yɛ mmerɛw sen studio kɛse, na nnipa dodow nhia sen nneɛma du. Saa asɛmfua yi kyerɛ nneɛma a wɔtɔ, nneɛma a wɔde bɛsesa, nsakrae ma nkyɛnkyɛn ayɛ yaw ne ani a ɛnhu yie, adaka so adwuma, nea ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ, ne sɛnea wɔbɛkora akit no sɛ ɛbɛyɛ adwuma ntɛm.

## Nneɛma bɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ ɛwɔ adepa akit mu?

Tebul a ɛwɔ hɔ no kyerɛ nneɛma a ɛho hia. Biara yɛ den, sika a ɛboro so na ɛboa adwuma pii. Nneɛma a wɔde besesa no yɛ nneɛma a wobetumi anya wɔ sika kakra a wɔtɔ anaa nneɛma kɛse.

| Nneɛma | Adeɛ a ɛboa | Nea wɔde bɛsesa |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mpensaw a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, 1 inch anaa ɛboro saa | Ɛma nkyɛnkyɛn kɛse, ɛyɛ yie sɛ wɔkyere | Mpensaw foam |
| Nhoma pɛnsel, 12 colors | Nsuo nhoma a ɛwɔ ntwa, nkyɛnkyɛn nyɛ | Nhoma kɛse a ɛwɔ sika kakra anaa ɔkyɛ ketewa |
| Krataa a ɛyɛ den, 140 lb anaa 300 gsm | Ɛntumi nnyɛ nsuo mu ɛnsɛn ɛnyɛ ɛdwa | Krataa a ɛyɛ den anaa nkrataa a wɔde ayɛ adwuma |
| Gɔm kyerɛ | Ɛma ɛnyɛ nsuo nkɔ, ɛnyɛ nnuru | Gɔm kyerɛ biara a ɛnyɛ nnuru |
| Pɛnsel a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, 2B anaa 4B | Ma ɛmmɛ a ɛyɛ den ɛnyɛ den | Pɛnsel graphite a ɛboro so |
| Oil pastels | Ɛwɔ ahoni a ɛyɛ den, ɛnhia nsuo anaa mpensaw | Crayons a ɛyɛ kɛse |
| Air-dry clay | Ɛma wɔbɛkyekye ne ɛma ɛyɛ ade, ɛnhia ɔhyew | Modeling dough a wɔakyerɛkyerɛ sɛ ɛnnyɛ nnuru |
| Masking tape | Ɛma krataa no ngyina hɔ | Painter's tape |
| Tiyaa a ɛwɔ ntwa | Ɛma nneɛma nyinaa ahyia, ɛma nsuo nnwene | Baking sheet a ɛwɔ ntwa |
| Ntoma anaa atade a ɛyɛ kɔtɔn | Ɛbɔ ntadeɛ ho ban | Atade a wɔde ka ho sɛ wobɛdan ankasa |

Hwɛ akɔnnwa, clay ne nsuo nhoma ho nsɛm a ɛde ɔyaredwuma ba, na hwɛ mmerɛ a wɔka sɛ ɛnnyɛ nnuru. Nkyerɛwde a ɛwɔ [Tear paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/) ne [Paint with watercolors](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/) de nneɛma a ɛyɛ den yi nko.

## Nsakrae bɛn na ɛboa nkyɛnkyɛn ayɛ yaw, ani a ɛnhu yie anaa adaka so adwuma?

**Nkyɛnkyɛn ayɛ yaw.** Fa foam tape kata mpensaw ne nneɛma a wɔde kyere so, na ɛbɛyɛ kɛse na ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛkyere. Fa mpensaw ne nkrataa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw. Fa clip anaa tape di krataa so, na ɔbarima anaa ɔbaa no remfa ne nsa nnhyɛ ase. Ma ne nsa ngyina ɛpono so.

**Ani a ɛnhu yie.** Fa ahoni a ɛwɔ ntwa, sɛ tɛntɛnsɛ nkrataa a ɛyɛ tuntum wɔ krataa a ɛyɛ fitaa so, ne nneɛma a ɛyɛ kɛse a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, sɛ ɔdua baako. Fa sanba ma ɛmfa mmɔdensɛm nnhyɛ ase. Nkyerɛwde a ɛwɔ [Arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) no wɔ ani a ɛnhu yie ho nhwɛso.

**Adwuma wɔ adaka so.** Fa tiyaa a wɔde ka ho anaa clipboard a wɔde ntadeɛ ma so de ka ho. Fa nkrataa a ɛnnyɛ nnuru ase. Clamp light a wɔde bɛka adaka anaa shelf ho boa. Kora akit no ma ɛyɛ nneɛma kakra, sɛ oil pastels, gɔm kyerɛ ne kaad a wɔbɛyɛ adwuma so.

## Nneɛma bɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wosiesie?

- Glitter, nsɔnsɔnsɔn a ɛyɛ ketewa ne nsɔnsɔnsɔn a wɔde ka ho, a ɛbɛtumi akɔ anom.
- Nsuo nhoma a ɛwɔ solvent, gɔm a wɔde gu so ne varnish a ɛnnyɛ ɔhyew pa.
- Sakre ne ɛpɔ a ɛde ɔhyew ba. Yɛ nneɛma a wɔbɛtwa biara ankasa, ansa na ɛreba.
- Nsuo a ɛwɔ mu a ɛyɛ ɛdwa a ɛbɛtumi ahwe.
- Akit a ɛwɔ mmoa anaa nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmofra adwuma. Mpanyimfo pɛ sɛ wɔmmfa nkyɛ.
- Akit kɛse a wobɛhyɛ wo sɛ wɔde bɛdi nyinaa.

## Sɛnea wɔbɛkora akit no sɛ ɛbɛyɛ adwuma ntɛm?

Fa nkotodwe a ɛyɛ tẽẽ, a wɔakata so, nyinaa mu. Ma mpensaw nkɔ abɔsoɔ mu, nkyɛnkyɛn no ɛnyɛ soro. Fa pɛnsel ne oil pastels wɔ shoebox mu, na tow krataa no nyɛ tẽẽ wɔ sakre ase. Kora nkotodwe no wɔ beaeɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw nkɔ na ɛnsɛ mmoa ne mmofra ketewa. Hwɛ clay ne nsuo nhoma osu biara, na yi nneɛma a wɔbɔ ayɛ den. Kyerɛwde ketewa bi a ɛwɔ sakre mu, sɛnea obiara behu nea ɛnni hɔ. Sɛ ɛbɛyɛ ɔberɛ a wobɛdi, fa tiyaa no, na wo ne wo ho asiesie sɛ wobɛfi ase.

[Sensory activities guide](/creative-calm/sensory-activities-for-adults/) no wɔ afotuo pii ma nneɛma a wɔbɛtɔ na wɔbɛhwɛ, na [Make a texture board](/activity/make-a-texture-board/) nkyerɛwde no de nneɛma a ɛte saa bi di dwuma. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wohu adwuma no mu yie, hwɛ [arts and crafts guide](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/).
