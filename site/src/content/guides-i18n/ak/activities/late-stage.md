Wɔ dementia a ɛdɔɔ so mu no, obi bɛtumi aka kakra, ɔbɛhia obi boa no wɔ ne nneyɛe nyinaa mu, na ɔbɛtena mpa so anaa agyinatenaa so bere tenten. Ɛbɛtumi ayɛ sɛ ɛnte sɛ biribi nni hɔ a yɛbɛyɛ bio. Ɛwɔ hɔ. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) kyerɛ sɛ obi ankasa adwene bi bɛtena hɔ, na wɔ mmere yi mu no, wiase no nnyinasosɔ denam adwene so. Nnwom, tɔ, dwom a ɔnim, ne wo ntam tenabɔ bɛtumi aba ahoɔfɛ ne nkitahodie.

## Nneyɛe bɛn na ɛboa wɔ dementia a ɛdɔɔ so mu?

Nneyɛe a ɛfa adwene ho ne ntam tenabɔ boa paa wɔ mmere yi mu. Alzheimer's Association kyerɛ sɛ yɛnnwen nnwom a ɔpɛ, kenkan nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛfa nneɛma a ɛsɔ ho ho, sɛ yɛde nnɔnhwerɛ a ɔpɛ paa bɛsra nipa so, ne sɛ wobɛtena ɔpon ano anaa abɔnten so bere a ɛyɛ mmerɛ. [National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/care-last-stages-alzheimers-disease) ka nkrasɛm, mfonini, tɔ a ɛyɛ mmerɛ anaa nsa nhyɛ, nnwom, nsuo anaa ɔhyew nsɛn ho, na ɔka sɛ wo ankasa ne wo ntam tenabɔ nso bɛtumi ama ɔdwodwo.

[Alzheimer's Foundation of America](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) kyerɛ sɛ obi a ɔwɔ dementia a ɛdɔɔ so no, ɛbɛtumi ayɛ sɛ ɔpɛ paa sɛ ɔbɛkyekyere biribi a ɔbɛbɔ ne ho ban, na ɔtaa hwehwɛ nneyɛe a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, nkɔnkɔnsɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, nnwom, mmoa a wɔbɛbɛhyia, nipa ntam tenabɔ ne nhwɛsoɔ a ɛyɛ ɔkwan foforɔ.

## Nneyɛe ma obi a ɔwɔ mpa so anaa agyinatenaa so

1. **Gyina ne nnwom.** Fa nnwom a ɔtee wɔ ne mmerante mu, ne gyidie anaa ne abusua nnwom. Ma nne no nyɛ kɛse, na tena ne nkyɛn. Ɛbɛtumi ayɛ sɛ obi bɛtumi ato ne nsa anaa ɔbɛbɔ mmɔ. Hwɛ [nnwom nneyɛe](/creative-calm/music-for-calm-and-connection/).
2. **Kyekyere ne nsa.** Wɔ ho nko ara a wɔkyekyere ne nsa, ne ɔtɛntɛn ne ntɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, ɛyɛ nneyɛe. Ka nea woreyɛ kane.
3. **Hyɛ ne nsa nhyɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.** Hyew lotion kakra wɔ wo nsa mu, na tɔ ne nsa fi ne nsa pɛn kɔsi ne nsa ntuo ase. Fa lotion a nʼaho nnye anaa nea ɔpɛ paa sɛ ne ho yɛ.
4. **Kenkan nkrasɛm.** Ɛbɛtumi ayɛ dwom a ɔpɛ paa, nkyerɛkyerɛ bi a ɛwɔ nwoma a wɔdɔ, Bible nkyerɛkyerɛ anaa nsɛm a ɛfa agodi ho. Wo nne ne nkɔmhyɛ a wode kenkan no ho hia sen nkrasɛm no ara.
5. **Ka asɛm.** Ka abusuafo bi a wɔkɔe anaa bere a wɔwareɛ no. Wunhia sɛ ɔde mmuae bɛba.
6. **Kyerɛ mfonini.** Fa mfonini kɛse baako, sɛ nʼani dwodwo ne ho, na ka ho asɛm wɔ ɔdɔ mu. Mma wo mmisa nkyerɛ sɛ hena na ɔwɔ mu.
7. **Ma ɔnwene biribi a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.** Ɛbɛtumi ayɛ ahyɛ a wɔde-yɛ-kɔ, nkyɛnkyɛn ɛnsɛn anaa aboa a ɛyɛ mmerɛ. NIA kyerɛ sɛ ma ɔnwene ahyɛ bi bere a wɔrehyɛ no, ama ɔnnhwehwɛ wo anaa agyinatenaa no.
8. **Gyina ɔhyew nsɛn.** Osutɔ, nsuo anaa ɔhyew nsɛn bɛtumi ayɛ ɔfie a ɛyɛ nnyimnyim ho.
9. **Fa wiase no ba mu.** Bue ɔpon no, de aduaba-anwodie bi ka ɔpon ano, anaa fa nnua nhwiren kakra ba mu ma wohwɛ na wɔtie.
10. **Fa kɔ abɔnten so.** Sɛ ɔbɛtumi atena sini mmɔdenbɔ so a, nkɔ abɔnten so nkyɛn ɛnsɛ anaa bere tiaa bi bɛtumi ayɛ ɔhaw ɛnyɛ.
11. **Hohoro ne tirim anaa fa nsuo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ma ne nsa ne ne ahyɛ.** Nnimnnim kɛse a wɔyɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ne nsuo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ma nʼaboa ne nʼanim ka ahoɔfɛ.
12. **Nantew nnwom ho.** Ka ne nsa pɛn wɔ nnwom bi ho, anaa yɛ sɛ nsa twerɛ wɔ nnwom bi ano. Sɛ wopɛ nkɔsoɔ anaa ahyɛ ahyɛ kɛse a, bisa ayaresafo anaa ɔyaresafo a ɔyɛ nkɔsoɔ adwuma sɛ ɔbɛkyerɛ wo akwan a ɛyɛ ahotɔ kan.
13. **Yɛ adwene a wɔnhwɛ kwan.** Fa lɛntɛ a ɛnnyɛ asɛe sra pepa kɛse so de sponge anaa wo nsa, ɛnam wo nsa so. Ɛnni nea ɛsɔ pefee.
14. **Ma mmoa bɛbɔ wo ho.** Sɛ ɔpɛ mmoa a, mmoa a ɛyɛ mmerɛ bi wɔ mpa so anaa ne nkyɛn, sɛ ɛbɛtumi ayɛ.
15. **Hom mmɔden kɔ so.** Tena ne nkyɛn, hom mmɔden ne mmɔden ano, na ɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛnyɛ den. Hwɛ [ahome a ɛma adwene dwo](/games/calm-breathing/). Wo ne ɔno betumi ahom, na wo ankasa wɔ ho tumi asi anaa atwa.
16. **Te hɔ kɔ.** Tena nʼakyi, hom, ka nea wobɛyɛ wɔ ho, anaa kenkan kakra. Sɛ wo wɔ hɔ no, ɛno ara yɛ.

## Akwan a wɔfa so kyɛ nneyɛe yi wɔ mmere yi mu

- **Bɛba ne anim.** Frɛ ne din ne wo din, kasa ntɛmntɛm na mmɔ dwom ansa na wobɛtɔ ne nsa.
- **Ma biribi biako pɛn.** Twe TV no ansa na wobɛgyina nnwom. Yɛ sɛnsɛ biako pɛn.
- **Ma ɛnyɛ tenten.** Mmɔden kakra bɛtumi ayɛ. Mmere kakra wɔ da no mu yɛ krɔnkrɔn sen bere tenten biako.
- **Hwɛ ne ntease ansa na woreyɛ.** Hwɛ sɛ wɔatumi ahwɛ ne ho yiye. NIA kyerɛ sɛ lap board a wɔde ma nsa ne nsa so bɛboa wɔ sɛ obi bɛtena ase.
- **Hwɛ ne nsakrae.** Nsa a ɛnnyɛ den, anim a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, ani a ɔbɛhwɛ wo, anaa ahome bɛtumi ahyɛ wo nkuran sɛ ɔpɛ. Ɛyɛ ahotɔ sɛ ɔnkwati anaa ɔsɔ nnwom anaa nneyɛe bi. NIA kyerɛ sɛ wɔbɛtumi ahunu ahohia sɛ ɔteɛ nnwom, ɔyi anim, ɔkyere ne nipa biara, anaa ne ho nyinaa sɛ ɔwɔ ahohia kɛse. Gyae na ka kyerɛ ahoɔfɛ adwumayɛfo.

## Ahobammɔ wɔ mmere yi mu

Nsɔre mu ka ebia ɛbɛyɛ basaa wɔ dementia a ɛdɔɔ so mu, na NIA ka sɛ eyi ma basabasayɛ hɔ asiane kɔ soro. Mma nneɛma a ɛyɛ nketewa nnkɔ ahodeɛ bi a ɛbɛtumi aba mu, na ma nnuane anaa nsuo nnyɛ ne mu anaa ɛbɛtumi aba mu sɛdeɛ ahwɛfo adwumayɛfo no kyerɛ ara. Hwɛ nsa ne nipa yiye bere a wɔreyɛ tɔ nneyɛe, efisɛ nipa bɛtumi atu anaa ayɛ nsɛe ntɛmntɛm. Ma nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛfa nneyɛe a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ho, hwɛ [nneyɛe a ɛfa adwene ho](/creative-calm/sensory-activities-for-adults/).

## Hwɛ wo ho nso

Ɛyɛ den sɛ wobɛboa obi a ɔwɔ dementia a ɛdɔɔ so, sɛ ɛwɔ fie anaa sɛ wɔrekɔ ayaresabea kɔhwɛ no, nʼadwene ne nʼonipa ho nso. Hospice ne palliative care adwumayɛfo bɛtumi aboa wo wɔ ahoɔfɛ, mmoa ne nhyehyɛe ho, na NIA kyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ mmoa a wobɛtumi anya. Mmɔden a ɛyɛ tenten ne ahotɔ, nanso ɛyɛ mmerɛ, ɛsɛ; hwɛ [sɛdeɛ wobɛkɔ hwɛ obi a ɔwɔ dementia](/together/visiting-someone-in-care/). Sɛ wokyinkyin mpɔtam nneyɛe a, [nneyɛe ma ayaresabea mpɔtam](/crafts/craft-group-sessions/) wɔ akwan a wɔbɛsesa nhyehyɛe so. Na kenkan [caregiver self-care](/creative-calm/creative-self-care-for-caregivers/): ɔdwo ne wo ho ahoɔfɛ ka.
