Nnipa pii ka sɛ “Merentumi ntrɛ” na wɔgyae ansa na wɔahyɛ ase. Saa kratafa yi yɛ ma mmusuafo ne ahoboafo a wɔboa nnipa a saa adwene no wɔ mu, a nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia nso wɔ mu. Ɛboa mo ahyɛ ase nsɛnkyerɛ ntrɛ ntam wɔ bere kakra mu, a ɔkwan biara nni ho nokorɛ. Ɛnyɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ ntrɛ, na obiara nhia sɛ ɔyɛ ɛno yiye.

## Dɛn ne mark-making, na ɛdɛn nti na yɛfiri ho bɛhyɛ ase?

Mark-making kyerɛ sɛ wɔde nkotɔ, nkyɛn, nsɛnkyerɛ ne nkotodwe kɔ krataa so a wɔnnwene ade bi a ɛyɛ den. Yɛfiri ho bɛhyɛ ase efisɛ ntrɛ biara nni ho bɔne. [Kratafa a ɛfa aduro ne nnwom](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) a Alzheimer's Association de ama no ka sɛ nnwom ntrɛ ka ahodeɛ ne botaeɛ ho, ne ɔkwan a obi betumi de ne ho aka ho. Mark-making ma saa nneyɛe no a ɛnni sɛ wobɛhwɛ ho. Sɔ:

- nkotɔ tenten wɔ krataa so de kraayɔn anaasɛ ntrɛ kɛse
- nkyɛn nkuruwa, de ntrɛ ntami no ntɔ krataa no so
- nkotodwe ne nkyɛn a wɔyɛ, biribi biara ankyɛ, na mo nyinaa fa ɔkwan so de krataa koro no so

Sɛ ntrɛ a ɛnni ɔkwan ankasa ma wo ahonyadeɛ a, [Tear paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/) ma wo ade a ɛbɛyɛ sɛ wɔawie, nanso ɛnhia sɛ wotwe ntrɛ.

## Ɛdeɛn na wobetumi antrɛ nnwom reto?

Tɔ nnwom a ɛwɔ nkɔnmu a ɛye anaasɛ ahoɔden a ɛda so. Bisa sɛ, “Nnwom yi kɔ soro anaasɛ ɛkɔ fam?” Ma obi no ntrɛ bere a ɔtie, na wo nso ntrɛ ne ho. Nsɛm a wɔka wɔ fa ntrɛ ho wɔ [Draw to music](/activity/draw-to-music/) kaad no mu. Nnwom a wɔatete no nso betumi ahyɛ mo animuonyam. Ma nnwom no nyɛ nnyɛ, na ma efie no nni nnesɔ, sɛdeɛ nnwom no remmfa nkasa nni mu.

## Ɛdeɛn na wobetumi ahwehwɛ nsuo-nsoaa?

Nsuo-nsoaa ne ɔkwan a ɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wubenya ade a ɛba mu wɔ krataa so, a ɛnhia ntrɛ nimdeɛ. Fa kraataa a ɛyɛ kɛse so, de nsuo bɛtow krataa no, afei ma obi no de agorɔ a wɔde aduro abɔ ma no nnɔ krataa no so. Aduro no bɛtrɛw na ɛbɛbɔ mmɔden. [Paint with watercolors](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/) kaad no wɔ nsɛnkyerɛ ho nhyehyɛeɛ. Fa aduro a ɛnnyɛ oboɔ de, na fa nsuo nkorɔ a wɔde nsuo bɛgu so ma ɛnkɔ nnipa a wɔbɛnom so.

## Ɛdeɛn nsɛnkyerɛ a wobetumi asan asan yɛ?

Nsɛnkyerɛ a wɔsan yɛ mu no ma obi no tumi tie, efisɛ nea ɛbɛba ɛkan no ankyɛ. Twe nsɛnkyerɛ bi kɔ krataa so, na ma obi no ka nea ɛbɛba ɛkan no ho. Anaasɛ de nkotɔ koro bi ma ɛnyɛ ntɛmkɛse wɔ krataa no so. Gyae bere a obi no ahyɛ ho ase. Wɔ [Paint a garden stone](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/) so no, wobetumi ade abo ɛboɔ no saa ara.

## Ɛdeɛn na wobetumi aboa obi a n'asa nni ahoɔden?

Fa mpaboa a ɛyɛ kɛse de: kraayɔn a ɛyɛ kɛse, ntɔkwa a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, anaasɛ mpaboa a ɛyɛ kɛse a mpini no tiaa. Sɛ mpini no yɛ tenten a, fa foam tape anaasɛ rubber band hyɛ so ma ɛyɛ sɛ wobɛkyere ano. Fa krataa no hyɛ abɔde so de masking tape ma ɛnnhwehwɛ. Krataa kɛse ma abasa no tumi kɔ so. Sɛ mpini a wɔde kyere a ɛma yaw a, gyae.

## Ɛdeɛn na boafo bɛhyɛ nsɛnkyerɛ ase bom?

Tena obi no ho, nnye sɛ ɛne no ka ho. Hyɛ wo nsa wɔ wɔn nsa so, anaasɛ hyɛ no ahyɛ wɔn nsa so kakra, na mo nyinaa nsɛnkyerɛ no ahyɛ ase. Afei gyae wo nsa na ma wɔnkɔ so. Nnipa pii tua ka sɛ ɛwɔ hɔ bere a nsɛnkyerɛ no ahyɛ ase. Alzheimer's Association ka sɛ obi a ɔrepenta bɔ ahoa no betumi ahia mmoa de ahyɛ ase. Wɔ nsɛnkyerɛ abien anaa abiɛsa akyi no, gyina akyi na ka nea wuhu ho asɛm.

## Dɛn na wobɛka, na dɛn na wobɛpow?

Ka nsɛm a ɛma obi no ka nsɔano a ɛnni sɛ wobɛhwɛ ho:

- “Saa abuburɔ yi ne ahoɔfɛ wɔ ɔfitiri ne ɔkɔkɔɔ no akyi.”
- “Ka me nkyerɛ me ho asɛm wɔ saa krataa yi ho.”
- “Ɛhe na ɛsɛ sɛ yɛde ɔkɔkɔɔ no bɛka?”

Mpow nsɛmmisa te sɛ “Dɛn ne eyi?”, ayeyi te sɛ “Woayɛ adwuma yiye” a ɛbɛma obi no ahunuiɛ sɛ ɔyɛ mmofra, ne nkaebɔ biara a ɛka sɛ wɔnsesa nkotɔ. Ɛmfa nkɔso a ɛbɛba ɛkan no nni, hwɛ [nea ɛdi kan wɔ nsɛnkyerɛ akyi](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/). NHS [kratafa a ɛfa nneyɛe](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ka sɛ wɔ bere a ɛsen so no, nneyɛe yi hia sɛ wɔsɛe, na ɛbɛtumi asi hwehwɛ ho. Ntrɛ a ɔboaboa a wɔde aduro ahyɛ ne ho, na ɔka krataa no ho, ebetumi ayɛ.

Sɛ obi no nni ahotɔ nnɛ a, sɔ [ntrɛ](/crafts/coloring-pages-for-adults/) da foforo, anaasɛ mo nyinaa twe mo ho ntɔ ka asɛm. Ɛyɛ yie sɛ wogyae ntɛm.
