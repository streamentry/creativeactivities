Nnwuma ahonyadeɛ ayaresa yɛ ayaresa adwuma nsia a wɔyɛ wɔ abusuabɔ mu. Biara de nhwɛsoɔ bi di dwuma: nsɛnkyerɛ, nnwom, asaw ne nneyɛe, drama ne nkyerɛwde. Biara wɔ ne nkyerɛaseɛ, na ayaresafo biara wɔ ne nkyerɛwde a ɛfa ne adwuma ho. Saa asɛmfua yi kyerɛkyerɛ nnwuma no mu, sɛnea ahonyadeɛ ayaresa de nnwuma nsia kɔ ho, sɛnea ɛte sɛ nnwuma a ɛyɛ soronko fi nnwom bom anaa nsɛnkyerɛ bere, ne nea dementia nhwehwɛmu kyerɛ.

## Nnwuma ahonyadeɛ ayaresa nsia ne dɛn?

[National Endowment for the Arts](https://www.arts.gov/initiatives/creative-forces/clinical-program/type-of-creative-arts-therapies) kyerɛkyerɛ nnwuma ahonyadeɛ ayaresa no. [National Coalition of Creative Arts Therapies Associations](https://nccata.org/) boa ayaresa adwumfo mu kuw ahyiadeɛ mu.

### Nsɛnkyerɛ ayaresa

Nsɛnkyerɛ ayaresa de nsɛnkyerɛ yɛ, sɛ nsɛnkyerɛ, adeyɛ a wɔde nkaribo ahyɛ so, anaa nsɛnkyerɛ a wɔyɛ ɛnam dade so, bere a wɔrekɔ so wɔ ayaresa abusuabɔ mu. Ayaresafo no yɛ adwuma wɔ ne ho ne ne nsɛnkyerɛ so, de kɔ nhyehyɛe no mu nsɛm a wɔapene so. Wɔma nsɛnkyerɛ ayaresafo biara ntoto so wɔ ne ankasa ɔman ahyiadeɛ mu. Sɛnea wɔbɛhwehwɛ nsɛnkyerɛ ayaresafo a wɔasua yie no, hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛhwehwɛ nsɛnkyerɛ ayaresafo](/activities/finding-an-art-therapist/).

### Nnwom ayaresa

Nnwom ayaresa de nnwom yɛ adwuma, sɛ atie nnwom, ka nnwom, to nnwomfo ne nnwom a wɔkyerɛw, de kɔ ayaresa nkɔso. [American Music Therapy Association](https://www.musictherapy.org/about/musictherapy/) kyerɛkyerɛ adwuma no. Wɔ Amerika, nnwom ayaresafo nya MT-BC nkyerɛwde.

### Asaw ne nneyɛe ayaresa

Asaw ne nneyɛe ayaresa de ne nipa nneyɛe ne asaw di dwuma, de hwehwɛ anigye ne nkɔso. Ayaresafo a wɔasua yie na ɔdi nhyehyɛe no so, na ɔhwɛ sɛnea obi no kɔ so, sɛnea ɔsi so, ne sɛnea ɔka ho ne afoforo.

### Drama ayaresa

Drama ayaresa de adwadie, abakɔsɛm ne adwuma a wɔyɛ sɛ asɛmpa di dwuma wɔ ayaresa nhyehyɛe mu. Drama ayaresafo a wɔasua yie na ɔdi so.

### Nkyerɛwde ayaresa

Nkyerɛwde ayaresa de nkyerɛwde ne nkyerɛw a wɔkenkan ne nkyerɛwde afoforo di dwuma wɔ ayaresa abusuabɔ mu. Nkyerɛwde ayaresafo a wɔasua yie na ɔdi so.

## Ahonyadeɛ ayaresa nyinaa yɛ dɛn?

Ahonyadeɛ ayaresa nyinaa de nhwɛsoɔ bɔne sɔ bere koro mu. Ayaresafo bi bɛkɔ nsɛnkyerɛ mu akɔ nkyerɛwde tiaa bi, afei ɔkɔ asaw ho nneyɛe mu. Ɛyɛ ayaresa akwan, na ɛnyɛ adwuma a wɔyɛ ɛnam adwene ho nkɔ so. Sɛ nnwuma ayaresa afoforo, ayaresafo a wɔasua yie na ɔdi so.

## Ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ soronko fi nnwom bom anaa nsɛnkyerɛ bere?

Nnwom bom, nsɛnkyerɛ kuw anaa asaw kilaase yɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ soronko. Ɛyɛ anigye, afoforo ne ho, ne adwuma a wo ne obi bɛyɛ. Wɔnni ayaresa ho ntoatoaso, nhwehwɛmu, anaa ayaresa nhyehyɛe.

Ɛfa adwuma ne ɛkyerɛ ho. Nnwuma ayaresafo wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛfa adwene ho ho asɛm, na ɔhyehyɛ kɔ nkɔso. Obi a ɔdi dwuma sɛ ɔbɛnnwom nnwom a wɔka bom ne abusuafo wɔ ayaresa fie no yɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ, ɛyɛ soronko, na ɛboa, nanso ɛnyɛ ayaresa. Biara nni nea ɛyɛ den sen afoforo; ɛno nkutoo na ɛyɛ soronko.

Nneyɛe te sɛ [nsɛnkyerɛ nnwom so](/activity/draw-to-music/) anaa [nnwom nkyerɛwde a ɛyɛ wo ankasa](/activity/make-a-personal-songbook/) yɛ adwene ahonyadeɛ a ɛyɛ kwan nyinaa, ɛnyɛ ayaresa.

## Nhwehwɛmu a wɔayɛ wɔ dementia ho kyerɛ dɛn?

Nhwehwɛmu no nyɛ pefee. 2018 [Cochrane nsɛmmisa](https://www.cochrane.org/CD011073/DEMENTIA_art-therapy-people-dementia) hwehwɛɛ nsɛnkyerɛ ayaresa ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia. Ɛhunuu nhwehwɛmu ketewa mmienu pɛ, a nnipa 88 nyinaa wɔ mu. Nsɛmmisafo no kaa sɛ nsɛm a ɛdɔɔso no nni hɔ sɛ wobɛka sɛ nsɛnkyerɛ ayaresa yɛ adwuma anaa. Wɔde nhwehwɛmu no bɔɔ sɛ ɛyɛ ketewa paa.

Ɛno ka nhwehwɛmu ho asɛm, ɛnyɛ nea ɛma wo nsɛnkyerɛ anigye twe ahyɛ. Wo ne obi bɛtumi aka nsɛnkyerɛ ne nnwom ho anigye wɔ ne ankasa mu, a wɔrenhwɛ ayaresa nsɛm bi. [Alzheimer's Association nsɛnkyerɛ ne nnwom asɛm](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) yɛ baabi a wobɛhyehyɛ wo ho ne ɔkenkan kɔ so. Sɛ wopɛ nneyɛe a ɛfata nkɔso biara, hwɛ [yɛn nkaebɔ a ɛfa nkaeɛ ho](/activities/reminiscence/), a ɛyɛ bɔ mu ne wiase mfonini anaa nnwom adwuma.
