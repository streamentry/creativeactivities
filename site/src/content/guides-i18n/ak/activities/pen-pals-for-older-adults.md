Nkyerɛwde yɛ adwuma a ɛfata ka bom, efisɛ ɛnam onipa a ɔrekyerɛw no ntɛm so na ɛkɔ. Ɛnhia sɛ wobɛtua ntɛm. Wobetumi aka kaadi no mpɛn abien, de akyerɛ obi a obeba, na wɔadie wɔ adaka mu. Saa kratafa yi ka nea wɔbɛyɛ: hwan na wobɛkyerɛw kyerɛw, sɛnea wobɛhwehwɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ mfasoɔ, dɛn na wobɛtumi asoma sɛ nsɛm yɛ den, ne sɛnea obeboafo bɛtumi aboa nkyerɛwde no.

## Dɛn nti na nkyerɛwde yɛ adwuma a ɛfata?

Nkyerɛwde ma onipa tumi di adwuma wɔ ne ntɛm ara mu. Paper a wobepaw, sitem a wobɛde ma, ne kyerɛwde baako a wobɛkyerɛw de ako wɔ tebea mu yɛ adwuma ketewa a ɛyɛ nokware, na ɛwɔ awiei a ɛda so. Alzheimer's Association [akwankyerɛ a ɛfa adwuma ho](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) kyerɛ sɛ adwuma nsɛ nkɔ so na ɛnyɛ nea ɛde ɔkwan ba, na nkyerɛwde fata saa afotuo yi koraa. Nkyerɛwde nhia sɛ ɛyɛ tenten sɛ ɛbɛyɛ biribi.

## Hwan na obetumi ayɛ nkitahodie?

- **Ba anaa ba a ɔwɔ ne mma,** titiriw deɛ ɔresua sɛ ɔbɛkyerɛw.
- **Nnamfo bi anaa adwumayɛfo a wɔyɛɛ adwuma wɔ ne nkyɛn,** obi a onipa no ara ne no.
- **Sukuu kilaas bi wɔ kuropɔn mu.** Bisa ɔkyerɛkyerɛfo sɛ kilaas no bɛtumi ayɛ nkyerɛwde nsesa.
- **Ɔfamfo bi,** a ɔbɛpɛ sɛ ɔde nkyerɛwde tiaa bi kɔ ɔfasu a ɛwɔ ɔkwan so anaa ɔdan a ɛwɔ ɔkwan so.
- **Asɔrefie anaa kɔkɔbɔ bi a onipa no yɛe bi bi a atwam.**

Nkyerɛwde a wɔkyerɛw kɔ abusuaa mu yɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mfiase a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, efisɛ onipa no ne nea ɔbɛkenkan no no ara ne no ahyia.

## Sɛnea wobɛhwehwɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ mfasoɔ?

Fa nhyehyɛe a ɔman a woahu no ara yɛ, sɛdeɛ sukuu, kɔkɔbɔ, asɔrefie, mpanyimfo ɔdan anaa ɔhwɛfo fie. Ansa na wobɛka wo ho, bisa:

- Hwan na ɔde nhyehyɛe no yɛ, na wobetumi afrɛ wɔn wɔ fon so wɔ nombɛr a wɔatwerɛ so?
- Wɔ ɔhwɛfo ho dwumadi mu, wɔhwehwɛ nkɔso anaa wɔhwɛ nkɔso wɔ nnipa a wɔkyerɛw nkyerɛwde no so?
- Ɛwɔ ɔdwumayɛfo bi a wobetumi afrɛ sɛ biribi nni yiye no?

Mma wo nsa nka obi a ɔfrɛ wo wɔ intanɛt so de, na ɔbisa adresi, mfonini anaa sika. Paw nhyehyɛe bi na tena so. Nhyehyɛe a wɔhwɛ so wɔ nnipa a wobetumi afrɛ.

## Ahobammɔ mmara ne dɛn?

- Nnkyerɛw banka nsɛm, kaadi nɔma anaa paswɛdi wɔ nkyerɛwde mu.
- Nnye sika, sika kaadi anaa cheque kɔma nkitahodie, ɛmfa ho sɛ nsɛm no te sɛ ɛho hia ntɛm.
- Kora wo fie adresi ho ho. Fa nhyehyɛe no abɔkɔ anaa ɔkwan a ɛfa adwumayɛfo adresi so sɛ wɔma wo.
- Onipa no na ɔpaw nea ɔbɛka. Din kɛse ne kurow bi nkutoo ara bɛtumi ayɛ.
- Kora nkyerɛwde no nnansa bi, na ka biribi a ɛhaw wo kyerɛ abusuaa mu obi.
- Ka biribi a ɛyɛ nea ɛnteɛ kyerɛ nhyehyɛe no. Sɛ sika ahyɛ mu a, frɛ wo bank.

## Dɛn na wobɛtumi asoma sɛ nsɛm yɛ den?

Nkyerɛwde nhia nsɛm pii. Sɔ hwɛ:

- **Mfonini anaa adaka,** a ɛwɔ kyerɛwde baako a ɛwɔ ase, sɛdeɛ "Yei ne yɛn turo wɔ Ɔsɔnkɔ mu."
- **Nhwiren anaa ahaban a wɔapɛ.** [Press leaves and flowers](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/) kaadi no kyerɛkyerɛ sɛnea wɔbɛpɛ wɔn wɔ buuk a ɛyɛ duru mu.
- **Mfonini a ɛwɔ kyerɛwde baako.** Paw mfonini a onipa no ani gye, na mo mmienu kyerɛw nkyerɛkyerɛmu no.
- **Kaadi a wɔyɛɛ ne nkɔ.** [Make a greeting card](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) kaadi no kyerɛ kaadi a wɔsisi, mfonini abien anaa baako, ne nkyerɛwde tiaa.
- **Mfonini ketewa a wɔabɔ ho.** [Make a memory collage](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/) kaadi no kyerɛ nkratafa a ɛbɛtumi aba ntoma mu.

Kenkan nkyerɛwde biara a wunya no kyerɛ no, na ma onipa no ka ne mmuae.

## Sɛnea obeboafo bɛtumi aboa nkyerɛwde no?

Tena ho ne wo ho pɛn, na bisa nsɛmmisa a ɛma onipa no ka biribi: "Dɛn na wopɛ sɛ wobɛka kyerɛ no?" Kyerɛw pefee nea onipa no ka, ɛwom sɛ ɛyɛ tiaa anaa ɛsan ba. Kenkan nkyerɛwde no baako, na bisa sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔsesa biribi anaa. [Ɛfiase nkɔ ansa na wɔayɛ nkɔsoɔ](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) nso wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛmu wɔ nsɛmmisa a ɛma onipa no ka biribi ho. Ma onipa no de ne din anaa ne nsɛnkyerɛnne toto so.

Abusuafo bi yɛ nkyerɛwde no karakyɛ, sɛ [kyerɛw nkyerɛwde bom](/activity/write-a-letter-together/) wɔ ɔprɛko mu. Fon ne video frɛ nso yɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ, na [fon ne video frɛ akwankyerɛ](/together/staying-connected-from-afar/) kyerɛkyerɛ sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ no. NHS [adwuma kratafa](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) de video frɛ ka adwuma a nnipa pii betumi aka ho nsɛ, na eyi kyerɛ wɔn sɛ nkyerɛwde nkutoo nyɛ ɔkwan a ɛma obi nya nkitahodie.
