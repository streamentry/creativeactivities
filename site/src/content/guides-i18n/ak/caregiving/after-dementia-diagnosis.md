Dementia nkyerɛkyerɛ bi bɛtumi de nsɛmmisa aba ahoɔfɛ, adwuma a wɔyɛ da biara, mmoa ne daakye ho. Nnipa di ano nkɔ wɔ akwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ so: obi bi pɛ bere ma ɔdwene asɛm no, na obi foforo pɛ sɛ wɔfi nhyehyɛe ase. Biribiara nni ɔkwan koro a ɛsɛ sɛ wodi so anaa bere a wɔhyɛ mu. Fi nea ɛho hia obi no nnɛ ase, na san kɔ nsɛmmisa foforo bere a wɔapɛ.

[Alzheimer's Association nkyerɛwde a ɛfa nkyerɛkyerɛ akyi nkwa ho](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/life-after-diagnosis) kyerɛ nnipa kwan so nkɔ nkɔsoɔ ahwɛyiyɛ, mmoa a ɛwɔ wɔn mpɔtam hɔ ne daakye nhyehyɛe. [Dementia Australia afotu a ɛfa nkyerɛkyerɛ ho ayaresa](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/coping-your-diagnosis) kyerɛ sɛ nnipa di ano nkɔ kɛse, na wotumi hwehwɛ mmoa bere a wɔpɛ. Saa asɛnka yi yɛ mfitiase, ɛnyɛ obi ankasa ayaresa, mmara anaa sikasɛm nhyehyɛe.

## Ɛdeɛn na ɛboa koraa a wobɛyɛ kan?

Paw asɛmmisa biako a ɛbɛma wo nnawɔtwe yi ayɛ wo yie. Ebia ɛyɛ sɛ wobɛhwehwɛ hena na ɔbɛhwɛ obi no nkɔsoɔ ahwɛyiyɛ, sɛ wobɛhwehwɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛteɛ wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ no ho, anaa sɛ wobɛhwehwɛ obi a obi no de ho to so. Obi no bɛtumi apaw sɛ ɔbɛdi ananmu, ɔbɛsrɛ obi sɛ ɔboa no, anaa ɔbɛtena bere ansa na ɔnsi ɔkwan.

Sɛ obi no pɛ a, monkyerɛw nsɛmmisa kakraa bi ka ho nkitahodie a edi hɔ ansa na ɛbɛba. Ebia wubebisa nkyerɛkyerɛ no kyerɛ dɛn, nea ɛnnim pefee, bere a ɛsɛ sɛ wodi nkɔsoɔ, ne hena na wobɛfrɛ sɛ ɛba bere a ɔhaw foforo ba. Sɛ wobisa ɔyaresa no sɛ ɔnkyerɛkyerɛ asɛm anaa nhyehyɛe biara a ɛnte aseɛ no, ɛbɛboa. Ɛbɛboa sɛ wokyerɛw nsɛm no ansa na woreba ayaresa no.

Obi no nhia sɛ ɔsua dementia nyinaa ɛberɛ biako. Paw nsɛmfua a ɛteɛ wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ a wɔde maa no ho, na kyerɛw nsɛmmisa a ɛba akyiri no. Nnɛ nni ɔkwan a ɛfata sɛ wɔka nsɛm a ɛfa ayaresa, ɔkwan a wɔfa so yɛ ayaresa, anaa nsɛm a wɔka sɛ obi nyinaa nni ayaresa koro biara so.

## Ɛhe na yɛbɛhwehwɛ mmoa?

Bisa obi sɛ mmoa bɛn na ɛboa no, ne hena a ɔpɛ sɛ wɔka ho. Ɔfamfo anaa ɔbusuafo a wotumi de ho to so bɛyɛ asɛmka a edi kan a ɛyɛ. Nnipa bi pɛ akuo a wɔboa wɔn, ɔsɛnkafo a ɔboa wɔ asɛm ho, dementia adwumayɛfo a ɛwɔ mpɔtam hɔ, anaa nkɔmmɛ a wɔbɛka kyerɛ wɔn ayaresa adwumfo. Ɛhwɛ nea ɔpɛ ne nea ɛwɔ bɛyɛn no.

Mmoa yɛ ma obi a wɔde nkyerɛkyerɛ no, ne ma ɔfamfo ne ɔbusuafo nso. Obi a ɔhwɛ no bɛtumi ahia bere a ɔbɛtena, anaa mmoa wɔ adwuma a ɛwɔ da so; obi no nso bɛtumi apɛ nimdeɛ anaa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a ɔbɛhyia nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia nso. Nsɛm a wɔhwehwɛ no ka ho bɛyɛ adi. Bisa ansa na wobɛhyehyɛ akuo bi, wobɛhyɛ nhyiamu bi, anaa wɔka wo ho nsɛm kyerɛ nnipa.

Yɛn [ho ahwɛyiyɛ afotu ma nea ɔhwɛ obi](/creative-calm/creative-self-care-for-caregivers/) wɔ nneɛma a wobɛtumi ayɛ de abisa nnipa sɛ wɔnna mmoa. Sɛ obi no pɛ mmoa kɛse wɔ da mu a, mmisa a ɛwɔ akyi yi ka nsɛm a wobɛtumi abisa wɔ mpɔtam hɔ adwumayɛfo ho.

## Ɔkwan bɛn so na obi no bɛtumi aka ho wɔ mmuaeɛ mu?

Nkyerɛkyerɛ a wɔde ama obi no nkyerɛ ankasa nea obi tumi de mmuaeɛ yɛ anaa ɔpɛ sɛ ɔka ho sɛnea. Bisa obi no ankasa. Ma no bere, kyerɛkyerɛ nkitahodie no wɔ nsɛmfua a ɛyɛ mmerɛ mu, na bisa nea ɛbɛboa sɛ nkitahodie no yɛ mmerɛ. Nkitahodie bi bɛyɛ no yie sɛ wɔka ho wɔ baabi a ɛnyɛ nnipa dodow, anaa wɔ nkitahodie pii mu.

Sɛ obi no pɛ sɛ ɔhyehyɛ daakye ho ayaresa, sika anaa baabi a ɔbɛtra no, fi nea ɛho hia no ne hena a ɔde ho to so ase. [Dementia Australia nhyehyɛe afotu](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead) kamfo sɛ wɔnkasa ho wɔ wɔn a wɔde ho to so ne ɔyaresa a ɛfata so. Mmara nhwɛsoɔ ne mmuaeɛ mmara ho nsɛm yɛ tɔ wɔ mpɔtam biara mu, enti bisa ɔsɛnkafo a ɔnim adwuma wɔ mpɔtam hɔ ansa na wode afotu a ɛyɛ nea ɛfrɛ ankasa no. Sɛ wɔpɛ sika boa ne nsɛm a ɛfa nnipa a wɔrepɛ wo ho ho a, kasa kyerɛ wo bank anaa obi a wotumi de ho to so.

### Ɛdeɛn na yɛbɛtumi abisa wɔ nkitahodie a edi kan ho wɔ daakye ahwɛyiyɛ ho?

Bisa obi sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔka ho seesei anaa, na afei fa asɛm biako biako ba. [Alzheimer's Association kamfo sɛ nkitahodie nketewa pii, nsɛmfua a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, ne sɛ wotie obi no pɛ, nsusuwii ne nneyɛe a ɔpɛ](https://www.alz.org/professionals/health-systems-medical-professionals/management/advanced-care-planning). [Alzheimer's Society kyerɛ sɛ nnipa di ano nkɔ wɔ sɛnea wɔn ho te sɛ wɔbɛsusuw daakye ho](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/planning-ahead). Ɛnyɛ ɔhaw sɛ wobɛdi ananmu na woagye ne ho akyiri ɛda foforo.

Sɛ obi no pɛ nkyerɛaseɛ a, ɛbɛyɛ yie sɛ wobisa nsɛm yi:

- “Nneyɛe bɛn na wopɛ sɛ wukeka wo nkwa mu?”
- “Hena na wopɛ sɛ ɔba yɛn nkitahodie a yɛreyɛ wɔ daakye ahwɛyiyɛ ho?”
- “Ɛdeɛn na ɛma wo ho yɛ fɛ na wuhu sɛ wɔda wo ni wɔ bere a mehia boa?”
- “Ɛwɔ biribi a wopɛ sɛ wo ayaresa adwumfo ho nim wɔ nea ɛho hia wo ho?”
- “Wopɛ sɛ mekyerɛw nea woaka no ne hena na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔhwɛ ho?”

Tie sɛnea obi no reka no nkyɛ, na nhwɛ kɔ mmuaeɛ a wɔrehwehwɛ ntɛmntɛm. [National Institute on Aging kyerɛ sɛ nkitahodie a ɛfa nsusuwii ne nea ɛho hia ho yɛ nea ɛho hia wɔ daakye ahwɛyiyɛ nhyehyɛe mu](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf). Sɛ obi no gyina so a, kyerɛw nea ɔka, da no, ne hena a na wɔwɔ hɔ. Bisa ne nkyɛn ansa na wɔbɛkyerɛ nkyerɛwde no. Nkyerɛwde yi bɛboa nkitahodie a ɛbɛba akyiri, nanso ɛnyɛ mmara nhyehyɛe anaa ayaresa mmara.

Mma wonyi nsusuwii wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ no nko ara anaa nkitahodie koro so. Obi no nsɛpɛ bɛtumi asesa, na ɔbɛtumi asrɛ sɛ wɔda nkɔsoɔ ano anaa ɔbɛsan akyi ɔkwan a ɔfa so akyi. Ma ɔnka ho wɔ nneyɛe a ɛka ne ho bebree sɛnea ɔpɛ.

## Hena na ɛsɛ sɛ ɔnim nkyerɛkyerɛ no?

Obi no na ɔbɛpaw sɛ ɔbɛka nkyerɛkyerɛ no kyerɛ nnipa, bere bɛn, ne sɛnea ɔbɛyɛ no, gyige sɛ ɔhwehwɛ ɔsɛnkafo anaa ɔhwehwɛ mmoa a ɛfa asɛm bi a ɛho hia ho. Bisa obi no hena na ɔpɛ sɛ ɔka kyerɛ, ne nea ɔpɛ sɛ wɔnim. Ɔbɛtumi ayɛ ade sɛ ɔnka kyerɛ obi koro, anaa ɔpɛ sɛ ɔnkyerɛ nnipa dodow.

Sɛ obi no pɛ boa wɔ nkasa a ɛbɛba a, bisa no nea ɔpɛ sɛ woka anaa sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔwɔ hɔ. Kyerɛ nea ɔapene so kɔ hɔ nko ara. Sɛ wunnim nea ɛsɛ sɛ woyɛ a, kasa wɔ ɔsɛnkafo, osuafo a ɔboa nnipa, anaa ɔbuafo a wotumi de ho to so no ho.

## Ɛdeɛn na yɛbɛyɛ na nkwa a yɛreyɛ da biara ka ho bere a yɛreyɛ nsesae?

Kɔ so de nneyɛe ne nkɔmhyɛ a ɛho hia obi no wɔ baabi a ɔka ho sɛ ɔnnya ahodie. Dementia nkyerɛkyerɛ nkyerɛ sɛ anigyeɛ, mmɔfra ntam nneyɛe, anaa adwuma a wɔyɛ fie nyinaa ɛsɛ sɛ wɔtwe ase. Sɛ biribi aba mmerɛ a, bisa obi sɛnea ɔpɛ sɛ ɔsesa. Ebia sɛ ɛbɛyɛ ntɛm a ɛtɔ bere tiaa, nnwom a ɔnim, mmoa wɔ anammɔn biako mu, anaa bere foforo a ɛfata no sen.

Obi no nso bɛpɛ bere sɛ ɔbɛdwene asɛm no ho ansa na wɔrebɔ nhyehyɛe ho asɛm. Hwɛ mu bere nyinaa, na mma ɛnyɛ nnipa nhyiamu a ɛfa ayaresa ho nyinaa. Ɛyɛ yie sɛ wɔgyae nhyehyɛe ho asɛm na wɔde bere ba ne ho wɔ akwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ so.

## Ɛberɛ bɛn na yɛbɛbisa ɔyaresa anaa obi bɛn?

Bisa obi no ɔyaresa nsɛm a ɛfa nkyerɛkyerɛ, ayaresa, nnuro anaa ahosɛmkɔm ho. Sɛ ɛyɛ mmara anaa sika mmuaeɛ a, kasa kyerɛ ɔsɛnkafo a ɔnim mmara a ɛwɔ baabi a obi no te no. Ɛnyɛ ɔwebsaet a ɛnyɛ nnipa nyinaa kyerɛ wo ayaresa, nkyerɛwde anaa adwuma a ɛfata obi bi.

Ntɛmntɛm ahosɛm anaa sɛ ayaresa suro ntɛmntɛm nso hia ntɛmntɛm ayaresa. [NHS afotu a ɛfa ntɛmntɛm ahosɛm ho](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) kyerɛ sɛ wɔnhwehwɛ ayaresa ntɛmntɛm. Fa wo mpɔtam hɔ ntɛmntɛm ayaresa anaa ntɛmntɛm ayaresa nhyehyɛe so, na nnyina ho sɛ ntɛmntɛm nsesa bi yɛ obi no ayaresa a ɛyɛ ne nneyɛe.

Sɛ nhyehyɛe no ma wo ho ayɛ duru a, paw obi anaa adwumayɛ biako a ɔbɛboa wo ɛnsɛ nsɛm no mu, de ahyɛ nea ɛho hia ntɛmntɛm ne nea ɛbɛba akyiri. Obi no tumi hyɛ bere no ase na ɔpaw nea ɔpɛ sɛ wɔboa no ho. Nkyerɛkyerɛ yɛ nsɛm a ɛho hia, nanso ɛnkyerɛ obi no ahoɔfɛ, nkitahodie anaa nkwa a ɔtra.
