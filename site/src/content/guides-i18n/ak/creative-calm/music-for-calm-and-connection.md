Muziki nhia obiara. Nsɛm biara nni ho ne mmara biara a wɔbɛdi so. Nnwom a wo ho ayɛ wo nwonwa betumi de anigye, nsa a ɛbɔ ntɛmntɛm, anaa nsɛm bi a wɔka ne ho wɔ bere a ɛyɛ bere a ɛnyɛ hyew mu. Eyi yɛ adwuma a yɛde yɛ, na ɛnyɛ ayaresa. Sɛ wo ho anaa ɔpɛ a wo boa no wɔ ahoɔyaw bi a, kasa kyerɛ wo dɔkota anaa ɔbɔadeyɛ a ɔwɔ nhyehyɛe ho kan ansa na wode muziki bɛdi dwuma ma biribi a ɛboro anigye.

## Hena na ɛfa ho?

Muziki fata obiara a ɔpɛ tie, ka, anaa sen ka nnwom a ɛwɔ mmerɛ. Ɛfata agyanom ne mma a wɔne wɔn ka nnwom bi, abusuafoɔ a wɔne mmofra hwehwɛ plate tɛtrɛw bi, ne nnipa a wɔabrɛ nanso wɔpɛ akyɛdeɛ. Ɛfata nso obi a wɔayɛ ayaresa foforo nanso ɔpɛ ade a ɛyɛ mmerɛ a wɔde bɛkyekyɛ. Eyi yɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ. Muziki nnyina ayaresa ho.

## Wohia dɛn?

- Nkɔmhyɛ bi anaa foon a bateri ne ho yɛ, ne ɔhyehyɛ bi a ɛbɛn
- Nnwom ɛnum anaa anan a ɛfa ho, baako a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ne baako a ɛyɛ anigye
- Krataa ne nsɔnsɔn, sɛ wopɛ sɛ wo ne ɔpɛ no bɛkyerɛw nhyehyɛe no
- Ɛyɛ bi: ntoma ketekete bi, nkaaɛ bi, anaa mpɛn abien a wɔyɛ aduru

## Ɔkwan bɛn so na ɛbɛyɛ nnwom nhyehyɛe fi obi nkwa mu?

Ade a ɛyɛ mmerɛ a wobetumi ayɛ ne nnwom nhyehyɛe a ɛkyerɛ ade a ɛwɔ ɔpɛ no ho. Bisa no, anaa bisa nnipa a wɔnyinyinyin no bere a ɔyɛ abofra wɔ bere a wɔte.

- **Wɔn mmerɛ ne wɔn mmofra mmere.** Dɛn na ɛwɔ radio, sɛ wɔrekɔ ahoɔfɛ ne sini mu bere a wɔyɛ abofra?
- **Nkɔso akɛse.** Nnwom a wɔde di ayaresabea anidasoɔ, nnwom a wɔde di wɔn ɔdɔ anidasoɔ, anaa wɔn abusuabɔ anidasoɔ.
- **Gyidi ne amammere.** Nnwom a wɔde ma Onyankopɔn, gospel, nnwom kronkron, anaa nnwom wɔ wɔn kasa mu.
- **Adwuma ne asetena.** Nnwom a wɔde toa so, adwuma nnwom, anaa nnwom a ɛfa beae anaa bere bi ho.
- **Nea abusuafoɔ kae.** Bisa wɔn nuanom, mma ne nnamfo a wɔn ho ayɛ wɔn nwonwa dɛn na wɔde yɛ ko anaa wɔka daa.

Sɛ wonnim baabi a wobɛhyɛ aseɛ a, sɔ nnwom a ɛyɛ mmerɛ nkaa nsɛm kakra a wɔde bɛhwɛ ɔkwan a ɔno nso bɛtaa so. Hwɛ nea ɛma ɔno ani di so anaa ɔno nsa bɔ ntɛmntɛm. Kora nea ɛma ɔno ani di so, na gyae nea ɛma ɔno ani agu. Fa nhyehyɛe abien: baako a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ma ɔno ahomegyebɔ, ne baako a ɛyɛ anigye ma nnwom anaa asaw.

## Nneɛma a wobetumi ayɛ ne ho bom

1. **Tie bom.** Fa wo ne ɔpɛ no tena mu, ɛnyɛ hwee a ɛrekɔ so. Tie ka ho adwuma.
2. **Ka nnwom ne ho.** Fi ase ka wo ho. Hum anaa mmoa nsɛm no nso yɛ adwuma.
3. **Bɔ ntɛmntɛm.** Pɛ aporɔ, bɔ abɛnkyɛn anaa de shaker. [Tap a rhythm together](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/) kaad no ka nneɛma foforo ho.
4. **Sɔ anaa sɛe.** Kyere nsa kɔ ɔdan mu, anaa sɛe nsa ne ho bere a wo ho tenaa mu.
5. **Ka muziki no ho asɛm.** Frɛ, na nnhwɛ so. Ka "Ka me afahyɛ hall no ho nneɛma" ma sɛ "Wokae saa nnwom yi?"
6. **Yɛ nnwom mpɛnsɛm ankasa wo ho.** Kyerɛw nnwom a wɔpɛ no nsɛm wɔ krataa kɛse mu, di kwan a wobɛka nkɔsoɔ. [Make a personal songbook](/activity/make-a-personal-songbook/) kaad no wɔ kwan ho.
7. **Yɛ nnwom nhyehyɛe bom.** Fa bere too nnwom. [Make a playlist together](/activity/make-a-playlist-together/) kaad no ma wo nhyehyɛe.
8. **Twe muziki ho.** Ma nsɛnsɛn ne nsuo nndi kwan wɔ nnwom no ho sɛ ɛrekɔ so. [Draw to music](/activity/draw-to-music/) kaad no kyerɛ sɛnea wobɛyɛ.

## Bere tiaa a yɛbɛyɛ bom

Simma enum kɔsi simma aduanan no ɛyɛ. Gyaeɛ TV no na to pono no mu. Bɔ nnwom baako, ɛnnɔ kakra. Fa beae a mo ani betumi ahyia ho, na ma ɔpɛ no nnwen nnwom a ɔtɔ so sɛ ɔpɛ. Frɛ bere a ɛyɛ nnwom a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, gyae nnwom no, na fa ho ntena ho bere tiaa. Nnwom a ɛnni nsɛm no nso yɛ adwuma a ɛwɔ mu.

## Ma nkwakwa anaa obi a ɔwɔ dementia

Muziki yɛ biribi a ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ mmerɛ sen biara a yɛbɛkyɛ. Ma nneɛma a wɔpɛ nnyɛ den, na ma ɔpɛ no nni asɛm. Alzheimer's Association ka sɛ wɔpaw nnwom a wɔn ho ayɛ wɔn nwonwa na wɔpɛ, ne nnwom a wɔrenhyɛ wɔn mu wɔ nkɔsoɔ ho, a ɛbɛma ɔyɛ basabasa. Fa muziki hyehyɛ ahomegyebɔ a wopɛ: nnwom a ɛma ahomegyebɔ ma ahomegyebɔ, ne nnwom a wɔpɛ ma anigye.

Dementia nkɔsoɔ mu no, [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ka sɛ nneɛma a yɛbɛyɛ no hia sɛ ɛnam nsɛnkyerɛnne so, na muziki betumi ayɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ yie. Tena ho ntena, ma nnwom a wɔdɔ paa no nnyɛ mmerɛ, na kyere nsa anaa sen ka mu. Sɛ wopɛ nsɛm a ɛfata nkɔsoɔ a ɛda so no, hwɛ [late-stage activities](/activities/late-stage/).

## Akwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ

- **Wɔtena anaa wɔdaa mpa so.** Fa ɔhyehyɛ bi a ɛbɛn de, na nnwom baako nkyɛ baako.
- **Tie nsɛm anaa ɔhyehyɛ.** Nkɔmhyɛ bi pɛ tie nsɛm, a ɛtwe nsɛm nyinaa. Afoforo hu sɛ ɛyɛ basabasa. Fa ɔhyehyɛ hwɛ kan na wo ne ɔno betumi atie bom.
- **Ani a ɛnhu yie.** Kyerɛw nnwom din wɔ krataa kɛse mu na kenkan ma wɔn.
- **Tiatia boa.** Hwɛ sɛ wɔwɔ mu na wɔyɛ adwuma ansa na woafi ase, na ma ɔhyehyɛ no yɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.
- **Nneɛma a ɛnyɛ pii.** Ma nnwom baako, anaa nnwom nhyehyɛe a ɛwɔ nnwom abiɛsa.

## Nsɛm a ɛfa ahobammɔ ho

- Ma ɔhyehyɛ no nnyɛ den. Sɛ ɛyɛ den sɛ ɛbɛyɛ kasa a, sɛe no ase.
- Nnwom bi de awerɛhyem, ayera anaa asɛm a ɛyɛ basabasa ba. Sɛ ɛte saa a, boa ɔpɛ no, sesa nnwom no, na mma ɔpɛ no ntwe nsɛm no ho ka.
- Mmfa nnwom nsɛm nkyerɛw kaad anaa nsɛm a wɔde kɔ ho nkyerɛwde mu, sɛ wonni tumi a wobɛyɛ saa. Tie ne ka nnwom bom yɛ kɛse.
- Sɛ wode shaker anaa aporɔ di dwuma a, yi ɔno a ɛnwɔ nneɛma kakraa a ɛbɛma wɔnnan anaa wɔadi.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ adwuma a

Sɛ ɔpɛ no te sɛ ɔyɛ basabasa, ɔnnye anaa ɔyɛ ɔyare a, gyae muziki, hwehwɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛ: ɔhoni, nsuo hia, aduane hia, nkaaɛ a ɛma ɔnnye, abɔfirɛ, anaa ɔdan a ɛyɛ hyew. Sɔ ntɛmntɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, anaa fa ne ho tena wɔ ahomegyebɔ mu. Simma enum a wɔde muziki pɛ yɛ nnwom nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ yie, na gyaeɛ no nso yɛ.

## Nhwehwɛmu no ka dɛn?

Nsɛm a wɔahwehwɛ ho no nni mmoa. [Cochrane review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003477_does-music-based-therapy-help-people-dementia) a ɛfa nnwom nhyehyɛe a wɔayɛ ho no kyerɛɛ nhwehwɛmu 30 ne nnipa 1,720 a wɔwɔ dementia wɔ mmoa 15 mu, ɛboro nnipa a wɔwɔ nkwakwa baabi mu. Ne nsɛm no yɛ soronko sɛnea nsɛm no te, na ɛka sɛ nokware a ɛwɔ nhwehwɛmu no mu yɛ ntɛm wɔ nsɛm pii mu. Saa nhwehwɛmu yi fa nnwom nhyehyɛe a ɔbɔadeyɛ a wɔatete hyehyɛ ho, ɛnyɛ tie fie kwan.

Yɛde muziki kyerɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ anigye, a ɛnhia adwene pii, de bere kɔ ho. Ɛnyɛ ayaresa, na ɛnnyina dɔkota anaa ɔbɔadeyɛ a ɔwɔ nhyehyɛe ho ayaresa ho.
