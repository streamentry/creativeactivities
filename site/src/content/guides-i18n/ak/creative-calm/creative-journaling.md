Adwene nkyerɛwee yɛ beaeɛ a wobɛtow nneɛma ketewa ahyɛ pɛpɛ mu: ɔsoro a wuhuu, nhahan, dwom mu asɛm, anaa nkyerɛwee a ɛka ntɛm no. Ɛyɛ adwene adwuma, ɛnyɛ ayaresa. Ɛnhyɛ wo sɛ wukyerɛkyerɛ wo ho. Sɛ wo anaa obi a woyɛ ho no wɔ ayaresa ho ahohia, kɔka wo ankasa dokota anaa obi a wonim ayaresa ho adwuma.

## Hwan na ɛfa yei?

Adwene nkyerɛwee fata nkorɔfo a wɔpɛ adwuma a wɔde nsa yɛ na wɔpɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ den a wɔde ankɔ akyiri. Ɛfata nkorɔfo a ɛyɛ den sɛ wɔkyerɛw nanso wɔpɛ mfonini, ne nkorɔfo a wɔpɛ nkyerɛwee ketewa sen nkyerɛwee tenten. Ɛtumi ayɛ adwuma a obiara bɛyɛ ka ho, sɛdeɛ ebinom bɛyɛ collage nkorɔfo a obiara bɛka ho. Ɛnyɛ nhyehyɛe a wɔde bɔ ho ban; yɛde ma nkorɔfo. Fa deɛ ɛyɛ wo yie.

## Dɛn na wohia?

- Nkyerɛwee a ɛwɔ nkyɛmu pɛpɛ anaa nkyɛmu a ɛyɛ den, anaa mpapahwɛ a wɔde ahyɛ fael mu
- Glue stick, nkɔtɔ a ɛyɛ sɛ nkyɛ a ɛyɛ mmɔdenbɔ, ne masking tape
- Magazine nkaebɔ, catalog, mpapahwɛ a wɔde pa nneɛma, fabric ahoma, anaa mfonini a wopɛ
- Pen, pensil, crayon anaa marker
- Nhaban a awu, tikiti mpapahwɛ anaa nneɛma a ɛyɛ mpɔmpɔn a wowɔ dada

## Nkorɔfo nsia a ɛyɛ mmerɛ

**Collage nkyerɛwee.** Mpapahwɛ biara wɔ asɛm bi, sɛ nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmɔdenbɔ, nneɛma a ɛyɛ bibiri, anaa nneɛma a wotumi tie. Tu anaa twa nkɔsoɔ fi mpapahwɛ a wowɔ dada mu, na hyehyɛ. Gyina hɔ a wopɛ sɛ ebesi hɔ. Ɛwɔ [make a color collage](/activity/make-a-color-collage/) kaad mu nhyehyɛe a wɔde ma.

**Nkyerɛwee a wɔde nsɔhwɛ.** Hwɛ ade biako da biara, de pensil anaa pen kyerɛ, de nsɔhwɛ kakra ne ahosɔfɛ kakra. Ɛho nni hia sɛ ɛyɛ pɛpɛ. Kaad [sketch what is on the table](/activity/sketch-what-is-on-the-table/) yɛ adwuma wɔ ha.

**Nkyerɛwee a ɛwɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ ne nsɔhwɛ.** Kyerɛw nkyerɛkyerɛ ketewa, sɛdeɛ nneɛma abien a wutee nnɛ, anaa sɛ wobɛhwɛ ahuhuru bi a wuhwɛ fi windo mu. Nsɛnkyerɛnne baako anaa mmienu nko ara ye.

## Nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛnsrɛ sɛ wobɛkyerɛ adwene

Yi baako ahyɛ a wobue nkyerɛwee no, na gyae nea aka no ma ɛberɛ foforɔ.

1. Hwɛ ade a edi kan a wuhwɛ bere a wosɔre no kyerɛw anaa kyerɛ.
2. Gu mpapahwɛ anaa fabric ahoma baanu, biara a ne ahoma yɛ soronko, na de ka ho sɛ wohu mu ne asɛm.
3. Kyerɛ mmara ahoɔfɛ baasa a wuhuu wɔ dan no mu, wɔ ahuhuru anaa wɔ kwan so a wodi fi adwuma so.
4. Sɔre nsɔhwɛ ade baako mu, sɛ ɔsoro baako, ɛfɔ baako anaa aburoɔ baako.
5. Fa wo nkyɛmu, ɛwɛ baako ne mpapahwɛ baako ko, na hyehyɛ wɔ nkyerɛwee baako so.
6. Kyerɛw dwom baako a wutee nnɛ ne baabi a ɛfiri.
7. Yi mfonini baako a wopɛ, na kyerɛ nkɔsoɔ a ɛwɔ mu nko ara.
8. Tɔ wo nsa nkyerɛwee, na de ɔkwan a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ den ma mu.

Sɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne bi yɛ wo fɛ sɛ ɛyɛ adwuma a wohia, fa foforɔ anaa mma nsɛnkyerɛnne biara.

## Hyɛ no mu

Hyɛ wo ho ho ye. Fa nkyerɛwee no ne baabi a wo nko ara bɛtumi akɔ so, na kyerɛ wo abusuafoɔ anaa nnamfoɔ sɛ ɛyɛ wo dea. Sɛ wode fon anaa tablet de kyerɛw nsɛm a ɛwɔ elektronik mu, fa passcode ma so, na hwɛ nneɛma a wobɛtumi ahu wo mfonini. Sɛ wobɛka nkyerɛwee no ho ne obi, ka ho pɛpɛ sɛ dɛn na ɛbɛba mu ne hwan na ɛbɛhwɛ. Obiara nhyɛ wo sɛ wɔnkyerɛ wo nkyerɛwee a wampɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ.

## Nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ mmerɛ

- **Sɛ wotena anaa wɔ mpa mu.** Fa nkyerɛwee no wɔ lap tray anaa clipboard so, na fa glue ne mpapahwɛ wɔ nkotidɔɔ ketewa mu.
- **Sɛ ɛyɛ den sɛ wobɛkyere.** Fa marker a ɛyɛ tenten, glue stick a ɛyɛ kɛse ne nkɔtɔ a ɛyɛ mmɔdenbɔ a mmofra nni ho asiane ne ntwa, anaa tɔ mpapahwɛ mmom sɛ wobɛtwa.
- **Sɛ wuhu yie nni.** Fa ahoɔfɛ a ɛyɛ dɔ, mpapahwɛ kɛse ne marker a ɛyɛ tuntum ka abɔnten anaa mpapahwɛ a ɛyɛ fitaa.
- **Sɛ nneɛma pii nni hɔ.** Ma mpapahwɛ ahoma abien ne asɛm baako. Fa glue ne mpapahwɛ de si ɔboɔ so ansa na woreyɛ.
- **Sɛ ɛnsɛ sɛ ɛtenten.** Ade baako, ahoma baako anaa nkyerɛkyerɛ baako ye ma nkyerɛwee a ɛyɛ pɛpɛ.

## Nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛfa ahobammɔ ho

- Fa glue stick anaa glue a ɛnnɛ ɔboɔ. Gyina nneɛma a ɛyɛ ketewa a ebetumi awe ɛkɔ mu, sɛdeɛ mmɔdenbɔ ne bɔtan, sɛ obi a ɔwɔ hɔ bɛtumi de nneɛma aka ne ano.
- Hwɛ ntwa ne mpapahwɛ ntoma. Sɛ ɛyɛ den sɛ wobɛtwa, tɔ mpapahwɛ.
- Fa nsuo a ɛwɔ ahoɔfɛ anaa glue a ɛyɛ den kɔ nkyerɛwee no mpɔtam sɛ obi wɔ ahome ho ɔhaw anaa ayaw.
- Nka nkorɔfo foforɔ mfonini ho, sɛ wɔamma wɔn ho ntam.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie

Sɛ mpapahwɛ bi yɛ wo ahonyadeɛ, gyae. Wobɛtumi adan no, de sie ɔfa mu, anaa afi foforɔ de asɛm foforɔ ahyɛ mpapahwɛ foforɔ. Sɛ wɔ asɛm bi a ɛde yaw ba, gyae. Ma nkyerɛwee no nkɔ, na yɛ ade a ɛyɛ dɛ na ɛyɛ wo dɔ, sɛdeɛ nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew anaa nantew. Sɛ adwene no ka wo ho, frɛ obi a wotumi de wo ho to so anaa boafoɔ kɔ.

[Finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) ɛyɛ akwan foforɔ a ɛyɛ dɛ, na wobɛtumi de nkyerɛwee ahyɛ mpapahwɛ so a mmisa a ɛyɛ den. Sɛ ɛba mu no, kae sɛ wotumi gyina adwene a ɛnyɛ pɛ mu. Nkyerɛwee bi a wɔammɔ ano koro ye mu.

Sɛ wowɔ ahohia anaa wo ankasa wowɔ adwene sɛ wobɛpira wo ho, mma wo ntease. Fa kwan a ɛda ha wɔ saa nkyerɛwee yi so, anaa frɛ wo beaeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ ma ntumi ntoa so.
