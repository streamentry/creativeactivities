Nneyɛe a ɛfa nsa so, bo, nteaseɛ ne ahwɛ fa nsa so, bo, nteaseɛ ne ahwɛ. Yɛmma aduane nni mu, enti biribiara a ɛwɔ mu nnyɛ ma wɔdi. Ɛyɛ akwahosɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, a wɔtwe adwene so sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ ade biako wɔ tebea ɔkwan so, te sɛ sɛ wobɛdwene mpɔtam a ɛyɛ kɛse anaa sɛ wobɛhwɛ nsuo mu nsuo anaa sɛ wobɛte mmɔnsɔ. Ɛyɛ nneyɛe a ɛfa akɔnnɔ ho, na ɛnyɛ ayaresa. Nneyɛe a ɛyɛ kakraa ho ahobammɔ ho mmuae betumi ama obiara ani agye. Sɛ wo anaa obi a wo hwɛ so wɔ akwan a ɛyɛ den sɛ nsɔre a ɛyɛ den, honam yare, anaa ahome, kasa wo ankasa dɔkotafo anaa obi a ɔwɔ tumi a ɔbɛboa wo ansa.

## Hena na eyi ma?

Nneyɛe a ɛfa nsa so, bo, nteaseɛ ne ahwɛ fata akenkan a wɔwɔ nkwa mu bere bebrebe. Ɛyɛ papa ma obi a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔbɛdi ade, ma obi a ne tumi sua na ɔpɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, ne ma obi a ɔtena ntamadan anaa mpadan so da nyinaa. Wɔbetumi nso ahyɛ ne nkɔ wɔ mmusuakuo nyinaa, te sɛ ɔba ne ɔpanin a wɔde nhoma a ɛyɛ nwi bi di adwuma. Eyi yɛ mmuae, ɛnyɛ nsusuwii a wɔayɛ wɔ ayaresa mu.

## Dɛn na wohia?

- Akwadaa anaa bokɔ a wɔbɛde nneɛma no ahyɛ mu, a ɛwɔ ntwa sɛ wopɛ sɛ ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ
- Nneɛma a ɛyɛ kɛse a ɛyɛ safe: nhoma a ɛyɛ nwi, sini a wɔde ayɛ kɛse, ɔdɔtɔ a ɛyɛ nwi, ɔfofɔ a ɛyɛ nwi
- Sopɔnkyea a bo nni mu anaa a ɛnyɛ den, sɛ honam yɛ papa na obi no nnyɛ ahoɔfɛ
- Dan a ɛmmɔ dwodwoɔ, a telefon no nni hɔ
- Ntadeɛ a ɛyɛ hyew, nsuo kuruwa ne ntamadan a obi bɛtena ho

## Nneyɛe a ɛfa nsa so, bo, nteaseɛ ne ahwɛ, ade biako biako

**Nsa so**

- **Nsa so a wɔde ahye.** Hye sopɔnkyea a bo nni mu kakraa wɔ wo nsa mu, na fa nsa so kɔ akyire. Nkyerɛkyerɛmu a ɛwɔ [give a hand massage](/activity/give-a-hand-massage/) kaad no mu.
- **Nhoma a wɔde ahyɛ mu.** Nhoma kɛse a ɛyɛ velvet, corduroy, fleece, satin ne wool a wɔbɛfa so ne wɔabɔ. [Make a texture board](/activity/make-a-texture-board/) kaad no ka saa adwene koro yi ho, na wɔde ayɛ ade a wɔbɛkora.
- **Ɔpɛ ne ɔpɛ a wɔde bɔ.** Nsuo mu nhoma anaa napkin a wɔahohoro a wɔbɛbɔ, a wɔbɛkyekye na wɔasan abɔ bio. [Fold towels together](/activity/fold-towels-together/) kaad no yɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.
- **Nsuo hyew nhoma.** Nhoma a ɛyɛ hyew a wɔde ma nsa wɔ awɔw bere mu. Hwehwɛ ɔhyew no wɔ wo ankasa nsa so ansa.

**Bo**

- **Nhwiren a ɛyɛ nhwiren.** Pirim mint, rosemary anaa basil ahahan wɔ wo nsa ntam, na fa bɛn wo hwene.
- **Ɛfie bo.** Kɔfi a ɔrehyɛ, paanoo a ɔreto, anaa ɔfifi a wɔrepɛ ɛbɛn ho.
- **Lavender a wɔahwie.** Sachet kakraa a wɔadeɛ aka. [Make a lavender sachet](/activity/make-a-lavender-sachet/) kaad no kyerɛ sɛnea wɔyɛ no.

**Nteaseɛ**

- **Nnwom a obi pɛ.** Nnwom a wo ne wɔn ahyia, a nne ntɛm. Hwɛ [music for calm and connection](/creative-calm/music-for-calm-and-connection/).
- **Asaase nteaseɛ.** Osu, nsuo anaa nkyene a wɔrehyɛ no ase kakra.
- **Nkyeɛ a wɔbɛwɔ mu.** [Make a sealed shaker](/activity/make-a-sealed-shaker/) kaad no ka sɛnea wɔbɛyɛ nkyeɛ a ɛyɛ safe a wɔde bɛgyina nnwom so.

**Ahwɛ**

- **Windo so hwɛ.** Mmoa a wɔde nnuane aduane bɛba windo so, anaa ɔkwan a ɛfa ɔkwan so ahwɛ. [Watch birds from a window](/activity/watch-birds-from-a-window/) kaad no wɔ nsusuwii.
- **Ahoɔden ne ahodoɔ.** Hyɛ nhoma anaa nhoma kakraa a wɔde ahyɛ mu ayɛ ahoɔden, de [sort buttons by color](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/) kaad no. Fa nhoma kɛse nko.
- **Nnua.** [Arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) kaad no yɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ de hwɛ, de bɛfa nnua a ɛyɛ foforɔ so.

## Dɛn na wɔde yɛ sopɔnkyea a wɔde nneɛma ahyɛ mu?

1. Yi ade a ɛfa obi a ɔwɔ nkwa mu ho: mpɔtam, akyirikyiri, aduane, adwumayɛbea anaa sini.
2. Fa nneɛma nnan anaa enum a ɛyɛ kɛse a ɛyɛ safe, te sɛ ɔkɔkɔ a ɛyɛ kɛse, ɔdɔtɔ a ɛyɛ nwi, ntoma a ɛyɛ hyew, lavender sachet a wɔahwie anaa nkyene a ɛyɛ nwi.
3. Hwɛ nneɛma biara sɛ ɛwɔ akakaa, ntɛntɛn, sini a ɛdwane anaa ade a wɔbɛbu.
4. Fa ade biako pue. Ma obi no nhwɛ no, ka ho asɛm, na ma obi no nhwehwɛ.
5. Afei, yi sopɔnkyea no nkɔ hɔ, na hwɛ nneɛma no bio ansa na wɔde bedi adwuma foforɔ.

## Ɛyɛ wo ho adwuma wɔ obi a ɔyɛ panyin anaa obi a ɔwɔ dementia ho?

Nneyɛe a ɛfa nsa so, bo, nteaseɛ ne ahwɛ yɛ ade a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ara wɔ dementia a ɔrekɔ akyiri mu. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) ka sɛ saa bere yi mu no, obi hwehwɛ wiase nyinaa ɔkwan a ɛfa honhom so, na wobetumi akyerɛ adwene pa wɔ nsa so, nteaseɛ, ahwɛ, nne ne bo so. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ka sɛ nneyɛe a wɔyɛ wɔ akyiri mu no, gyina honhom so, te sɛ nnwom a wɔgyina, nneɛma a wɔde ahyɛ mu ne nsa so a wɔde hye.

Bɛba hɔ firi anim, frɛ obi din, na bisa anaa ka ansa na woreka obi: "Mewɔ sopɔnkyea bi. Wopɛ sɛ mehye wo nsa?" Fa adwuma biako nkɔ. Hwɛ sɛ ne anim yɛ ɔdɔ, ne nsa tɔ, anaa ɔseesei. Sɛ ɔyi ne nsa, ɔbɔ mmɔden, anaa ɔnnye ho dwiriw no, di hɔ. Ma nhyiamu no nnyina tebea mu, bere a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ɛbɛyɛ nkyɛn enum.

## Ahobammɔ mmuae

Nneyɛe a ɛfa nsa so, bo, nteaseɛ ne ahwɛ yɛ mmerɛ, nanso ɔhaw bi wɔ hɔ, titiriw sɛ obi a ɔbɛtumi de nneɛma aka ne ano anaa obi a ne nsã ntumi nnan yiye.

| Ɔhaw | Dɛn na wobɛyɛ |
| --- | --- |
| Ɛka mmɔ | Fa nneɛma a ɛyɛ kɛse a wɔrentumi nnyan nkɔ nko. Mma bɔtɔn, nkɔngua, sika anaa mmoa ketewa mmra mu. |
| Nneɛma a ɛte sɛ aduane | Kyɛ anaa hyɛ sopɔnkyea, sapɔ, shampoo ne ɛtɔ a ɛhwene wɔ hɔ, efisɛ ɛbɛte sɛ aduane anaa ɛbɛbɔ sɛ aduane. Yi aburow a wɔayɛ ɛfiri hɔ. |
| Ahodoɔ | Hwehwɛ ahodoɔ a ɛfa nkɔm, latex, wool, nnua anaa bo ho ansa na woreyɛ ade. |
| Bo a ɛyɛ den | Gyae ɔtɔ a ɛyɛ den ne ahoɔdaa ka bo a wɔbɛbɔ, titiriw sɛ obi wɔ asthma anaa ɔhaw foforɔ a ɛfa ahome ho. Bo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ne a ɛfiri nnuam so yɛ papa. |
| Honam | Hwehwɛ honam a ɛyɛ yaw, a ɛabubu anaa a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ansa na wobɛhye. Fa nsa so a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, na sɛ wɔbɔ mmɔden a, gyae. |
| Ɔhyew | Hwɛ nhoma a ɛyɛ hyew ne nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew wɔ wo nsa so ansa. |
| Nneɛma a ɛyɛ den anaa ɛyɛ nsɛe | Mma nneɛma a ɛyɛ den, ahyeɛ anaa nneɛma a ɛyɛ nsɛe a wɔde yɛ nneyɛe. |

## Aromatherapy boa anaa?

Ɛnyɛ adeɛ a wɔbɛtumi ahu ntease mu. [Cochrane review of aromatherapy for dementia](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003150_aromatherapy-dementia) hwehwɛɛ nhwehwɛmu 13 a nnipa 708 wɔ mu, a wɔn nyinaa wɔ aduane mu. Nhwehwɛmu no yɛ ketewa, n'ankasa nsɛm no nyɛ pɛ, na enni nhwehwɛmu a ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɛboa wɔ nsɛm a ɔhwehwɛɛ hɔ. Sɛ obi ani gye bo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ a obi nim, te sɛ lavender a wɔde firi afuw mu, ɛyɛ adwuma a ɛfata sɛ wɔkyekyɛ. Ɛnyɛ ayaresa.

## Nneyɛe a ɛnyɛ den

Obi bi hwehwɛ sɛ nneɛma pii nam so no ayɛ no mmerɛ. Nneyɛe a ɛnyɛ den yɛ ade biako, hann a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, abɛnkyekyɛ a ɛyɛ hyew ne bo biara nni mu. Mmoa wɔn ho bom, pɛ sɛ hann no bɛbɔ nkaaso a ɛyɛ mmerɛ wɔ ɔfaa no so anaa hyɛ ade biako wɔ nsa mu. Ma nne no nnyɛ den, na ma ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔbɛkɔ so ɛberɛ biara a wopɛ. Sɛ obi no ne ho dan anaa ɔto ne ani a, saa ne nhyiamu a ɛyɛ pɛ.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ adwuma

Sɛ obi no ani begyina, gyae, de nneɛma no ahyɛ hɔ, na hyɛ hɔ akyɛre. Sɔ ade foforɔ a ɛfa nsa so, bo, nteaseɛ anaa ahwɛ bio da foforɔ, anaa bere foforɔ wɔ da no mu. Obi bi pɛ nhyiamu a ɛbɛba anɔpa, na afoforɔ pɛ sɛ ɛba anwummere bere a fie no nni nteaseɛ. Ɛyɛ papa sɛ wobɛgyae ntɛm.

[Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) ntɔnbɔ yɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ de ama nhyiamu no awie, na obi biara nhome ɔhome biara a ɔnnye ho dwiriw, na wɔrenhia obi sɛ ɔyɛ ade foforɔ.
