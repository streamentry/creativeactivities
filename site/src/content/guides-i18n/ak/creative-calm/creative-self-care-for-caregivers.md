Ɔhwɛ obi so yɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ den, na wo ho hwɛ yɛ adwuma no mu nso. Saa kratafa yi de adwinnadeɛ ntɔkwa ntɛm akakra ma wo, na ɛkyerɛ wo boafo a ɛyɛ nokorɛ bere a ɛho hia sen ntɔkwa. Saa ntɔkwa yi yɛ adwinnadeɛ ntɔkwa, na ɛnyɛ otherapy. Sɛ wo anaa obi a wohwɛ so wɔ ayaresa ho ahohia a, kasa kyerɛ wo ankasa dɔkota anaa obi a ɔwɔ ayaresa ho nimdeɛ.

## Ɛdeɛn na miniti enum adwinnadeɛ ntɔkwa yɛ?

Ntɔkwa akakra nso ka. Biribiara nni ho sɛ ɔhaw bɛwie, nanso ɛbetumi ama wo abɛboa wo wɔ anwummere a ɛyɛ den.

- **Pae ahahan.** Fa ahahan biako de gu ɔkwan dwetɛ a ɛyɛ den mu, na san bɛhwɛ no akyiri. Kaa [press leaves and flowers](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/) kaad no mu, na wobɛhunu akwan.
- **Twerɛ nhwɛso a wɔhwɛ.** Twerɛ biribi a wohu wɔ windo no mu anaa wɔ ɔpon no so wɔ ɔpɛnsɛ ho. Obiara nhwehwɛ.
- **Tɔ ɔdan.** Tɔ biribi kakraa a ɛsan bɛba ntam wɔ ɔpepɛw ho. [draw repeating patterns](/activity/draw-repeating-patterns/) kaad no ma wo akwan a ɛdi kan wɔ mpɛn biara.
- **Tie dwom a wopɛ.** Tie wo ani, anaa bobɔ wo nsa ka dwom no ho nnyinaso. [make a playlist together](/activity/make-a-playlist-together/) kaad no betumi aboa wo ma wo ahyehyɛ biribi.
- **Kyerɛw kaad ketewa.** Nkyerɛwde nsɛm kakraa kɔma nnamfo, na fa nhwɛso a ɛyɛ kwan bi ka ho. [make a greeting card](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) kaad no yɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ vinn yɛ saa.

## Ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ ntɔkwa a ɛtenten?

Hyɛ da biako anaa mmienu a wɔbɛyɛ wɔ dapɛn mu sɛ wobɛyɛ ade a ɛnni botae. Dɔ bodoma, de nnuba a ɛwɔ kuruwa mu hyehyɛ nnuhwa, anaa yɛ ɛbɔfoɔ kakraa afiri ɛbɔnsuo mu. [paint a garden stone](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/), [arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) ne [shape a pinch pot](/activity/shape-a-pinch-pot/) kaad no nyinaa yɛ adwuma ma saa. Wobetumi ayɛ biribi wo ankasa, anaa wo ne obi a wohwɛ so no, sɛ ɔpɛ saa adwuma no.

Sɛ wo ntumi nnyin obi a wohwɛ so no nkyɛn a, ntɔkwa betumi aka ho nso. [sort buttons by color](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/) kaad anaa nhwɛso a ɛyɛ kwan wɔ pepa so betumi ayɛ de nkyɛn ano.

## Ntɔkwa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw

- **Da.** Miniti enum de wo ani agu so, na dwom a wopɛ no ka ho asɛm.
- **Nneyɛe a ɛyɛ kakra.** Fa ɔkɔkɔɔ kɔkɔɔ biako ne pepa biako. Ɛno nko ara ye.
- **Ahoɔden a ɛyɛ kakra.** Tena windo nkyɛn, hwehwɛ mmoa a wohu, anaa fa ɔbɔ a ɛyɛ pɛpɛɛpɛ biako.
- **Ahuntuo.** Fa kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ne pepa a ɛyɛ kɛse, na ka nea wɔreyɛ kyerɛ wo ho wɔ mmɔdenbɔ so.

## Ɛhe na wobetumi anya respite care?

Respite care yɛ ɔhwɛ a ɛyɛ ɛberɛ tiaa ma obi a wohwɛ so, de wo ho ahome. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/respite-care) kyerɛ mmienu a ɛwɔ mu:

| Ɔkwan | Nea ɛyɛ |
| --- | --- |
| Fie mmoa | Nnamfo, abusua, ɔboafo anaa ɔboafo a wotua ka sɛ wɔbɛba fie |
| Nnipa a wɔhyiam wɔ ɔhwɛ beaeɛ mu | Nhyehyɛe a ɛwɔ ɔkwan a wɔfa so yɛ adwuma wɔ nnipa mu, a ɛfa kwan ne aduane ho bio |
| Anadwo anaa ɛberɛ tiaa | Ɔhwɛ kuw bi hwɛ obi no anadwo biako anaa ɛberɛ tenten |

Abusua, nnamfo anaa ɔboafo no respite betumi anyɛ wo sika biara. Ɔhwɛ a wɔtua sika betumi ahyɛ no ɔberɛ, da anaa ɛberɛ a wɔbɛtena. Bisa Helpline ho asɛm a ɛfa sika ne ɔkwan a ɛwɔ wo mantam mu, ansa na ɛhia wo. Hwehwɛ ɔboafo bi wɔ ɛberɛ a ɔhaw nni hɔ, saa ara na wobɛyɛ no nnim sɛ ɔhaw bi bɛba.

## Ɛhe na wobetumi anya boafo?

- **Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline: 1-800-272-3900.** Saa frɛ yi wɔ US yɛ ade a wɔntua ka, ɛyɛ nkrasɛm, na ɛda so. Wobetumi ano wɔ kasa pii mu, wɔ nnipa a wɔkasa mmienu anaa ɔkasakyerɛ a wɔfa so. Wobetumi aka ɔhwɛ ahohia, respite ne mmeae a wɔwɔ wɔ hɔ ho asɛm. Adwumfoɔ no nnhwɛ ayaresa ho akwankyerɛ.
- **Eldercare Locator: 1-800-677-1116 anaa eldercare.acl.gov.** Ɛyɛ ɔman adwuma wɔ US a ɛboa wo ma wo nkɔ mmeae a wɔwɔ wɔ hɔ ma mpanyin ne wɔn abusua.
- **Alzheimer's Association nkitahodi.** Nkitahodi a wɔma ɔhwɛfo, wɔ baabi a wohyiam anaa wɔ intanɛt so, a wɔtena so nnipa a wɔatete ho yiye hyehyɛ. Bisa Helpline sɛ ɛhe na biara ɛwɔ wo nkyɛn.
- **Carers UK.** Nsɛm ne akwankyerɛ ma ɔhwɛfo wɔ UK, ne boafo wɔ nneɛma a ɛfa adwuma no ho.
- **Samaritans: 116 123 (UK ne Ireland).** Ɛnni bo, na ɛda so nnɔnhwerɛ 24 wɔ da biara, nnafua 365 wɔ afe biara, ma obiara a ɔhia sɛ ɔka ho asɛm.
- **Find A Helpline.** Ɛkyerɛ frɛ a wɔntua ka wɔ nkurɔfo mu, sɛ wonnim frɛ a wobɛfrɛ.

## Ɛdeɛn na wobisa abusua ne nnamfo sɛ wɔmmoa wo?

Ka asɛm no pefee. Nnipa pii pɛ sɛ wɔboa, nanso wonnim nea wɔbɛma.

- **Kyerɛw nkɔsoɔ** a nnipa betumi ahwɛ, na ma wɔnyi biako.
- **Fi ketewa.** Anwummere biako anaa frɛ biako dapɛn biara yɛ mmisa a ɛyɛ.
- **Hyɛ adwuma no ho.** Ɔfranka a ɔyɛ aduane betumi de aduane aba da Ɔbɔadeɛ. Ɔkyerɛkyerɛfoɔ a ɔyɛ papa wɔ nkɔsoɔ mu betumi aboa wo wɔ nwoma ho.
- **Kyerɛw anaa ma email** sɛ ɛyɛ den sɛ wobisa mmɔden.
- **Ɔbɛyɛ sɛ wɔka dabi.** Mma ɛnkɔ wo koma so, na bisa obiara foforo.

## Ɛberɛ bɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ wohwehwɛ mmoa ntɛm?

Sɛ wo ho nhyɛ sɛ wobetumi atena so, anaa wowɔ ahoyeraw, anaa wowɔ adwene sɛ wobedi wo ho ka a, frɛ nnipa ntɛm. Wɔ US mu no, frɛ anaa kyerɛw 988 de rebɛfrɛ [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/), a ɛnni bo na ɛda so nnɔnhwerɛ 24 wɔ da biara. Wɔ UK ne Ireland no, frɛ Samaritans wɔ 116 123. Afiri baabi a aka no, [Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com/) kyerɛ boafo a ɛnni bo wɔ mmeae nyinaa. Sɛ obiara wɔ ɔhaw a ɛbɛba ntɛm a, frɛ wo beaeɛ ho ntɛm ɔhaw ho nnipa.

Kasa kyerɛ wo dɔkota sɛ ahohia no ba daa daa, sɛ wo ho yɛ fɛ nnawɔtwe, anaa sɛ ɛyɛ wo den sɛ wobɛtena so. Ka sɛ wo yɛ ɔhwɛfo. Wɔbetumi aboa wo ma wohyehyɛ mmoa ma wo ne obi a wohwɛ so no nyinaa.

## Nkɔsoɔ a ɛfa ahobammɔ ho

- Fa nkataa ne nneɛyɛ mfidie no so yiye, na sie wɔn afiri obi a wohwɛ so no nkyɛn.
- Gyina nnuba a ɛyɛ foforo, nhaban ne nhwiren a wɔatwa mu so sɛ obiara wɔ nkwadaran anaa ahome ho haw. Hwehwɛ nnuba ansa na woakɔ so.
- Nkɔkɔ ne mfiri ɔkɔ ɛnyɛ aduane. Hohoro nsa wɔ nneyɛe no akyi.
- Fa ntɔkwa no mu ntɛm ka wo ho nso. Adwinnadeɛ ntɔkwa ntɛm nyɛ adwuma foforo.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie a

Sɛ adwinnadeɛ ntɔkwa rehyɛ wo sɛ ɛyɛ adwuma foforo a, fa biribi a ɛyɛ kakra bɔ, anaa de wo ho tena. Sɛ ahohia no san ba daa daa a, boa ɔhwɛ ahohia no ankasa, na ɛnyɛ ntɔkwa no nko ara. Nkitahodi, respite ɛberɛ bi anaa nkɔmmɔ wo dɔkota nkyɛn betumi ama ɛyɛ nkɔso sen ade biara. Ɔhome nyɛ sɛ wobɛnya ɛkyɛ a wo ara wo ho ka. Ɛyɛ karaa a ɛma wo ntumi nkɔ so.
