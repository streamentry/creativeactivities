Adwene so adwuma yɛ kwan a wɔfa so yɛ nkyerɛwee ntɛmntɛm. Wopɛ biribi a ɛyɛ mmerɛ anaa biribi a wobɛyɛ bi, na wodi so tirim kakra kakra, wohwɛ yiye wɔ nkyerɛwee biara ntam. Ɛnsɛ sɛ biribiara yɛ biribi a ɛwɔ hɔ. Nea ɛho hia ne adwene, mpaapaemu ne nneɛma a wodi, na nea ɛnyɛ adwuma a wɔawie. Eyi yɛ adwuma a wɔyɛ ma anigye, na ɛnyɛ ayaresa. Sɛ wo anaa obi a woboa no wɔ yare ho ahohia, kasa kyerɛ ɔdɔkotafo anaa ɔbɔadeɛ a ɔwɔ tumi ansa na woreyɛ biribi foforo.

## Hwan na ɛyɛ ma?

Adwene so adwuma fata nnipa akɛse a ɛpɛ sɛ wɔyɛ nneɛma nanso wɔnnim nkyerɛwee yiye. Ɛfata nso nnipa a wɔpɛ mpaapaemu, nhyehyɛe ne nneyɛe a ɛyɛ mmerɛ a wɔnnyɛ ntɛmntɛm. Wutumi ayɛ no wo ho, anaa wo ne adamfo, ɔdamfo anaa obi a woboa no bom. Ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ tebul so, anaa wɔ ɔpon so a wɔde tray ahyɛ wo atifi. Yeinom yɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ, nnyɛ yareɛ bɔ. Obi bi bɛpɛ, na obi a ɛnyɛ saa.

## Dɛn na ehia?

- Pampamu a ɛyɛ pɛpɛ, fitaa printer paper a ɛyɛ fa, anaa sketch pad a ɛyɛ kakra
- Pen a ɛyɛ kakra, pencil a ɛyɛ nwini ne eraser
- Colored pencil anaa fine marker a ɛyɛ kakra
- Nneɛma baako a wobɛdi, te sɛ kɔp, ɔhaban, ɔpɛnsa anaa kii
- Clipboard, sɛ tebul no nni mu anaa wopɛ sɛ wɔde nkyerɛwee bɛyɛ ɔpon so

Nneɛma a ɛwɔ fie yɛ mfaso. Fa biribiara a wowɔ.

## Ɛdeɛn na wobɛfi ase ayɛ kɔnturu a ɛyɛ ntɛmntɛm?

1. Fa nneɛma no to tebul so wɔ hann a ɛyɛ pɛpɛ, ɛnyɛ adwene ne wo ho ntam.
2. Yi nneɛma no mu baako, te sɛ ɔkuruwa no ntoma anaa ɔhaban baako no ntoma.
3. Kyerɛw ntɛmntɛm, na hwɛ nneɛma no yiye sen pampamu no. Ma pen no nkɔ baabi a ɛpɛ.
4. Wɔ nkyerɛwee kakra akyi, gyae, hwɛ nkyerɛwee no, na hwɛ nneɛma no bio.
5. Wɔ pampamu no baabi foforo, fi mpaapaemu ase: mpɔmpɔn nketewa, mmɛnsɛm a ɛyɛ mmɔdenbɔ, anaa mpɛnsa. San de ɛfa sɛnkyerɛnne a ɛdi kan ho, na sɛ ɛho nni ɛhia no, nkyerɛw no bio.
6. Fa kɔla ka ho sɛ ɛyɛ anigye. Fa kɔla baako bɛdi, na ma ɛbɔ baabi baako ma.
7. Gyae bere a wopɛ. Fa pampamu no sɛ ɛwɔ baabi a ɛwɔ hɔ, na san bra da foforo.

Kɔnturu nkyerɛwee, a wotwe pen no firi pampamu so, na wɔnhwɛ nnwa koraa, yɛ anamɔn pa a ɛdi kan. Nkyerɛkyerɛ a wɔbɛprint wɔ [mpaapaemu a wɔsan de ayɛ](/activity/draw-repeating-patterns/) ne [nkyerɛwee a ɛwɔ tebul no so](/activity/sketch-what-is-on-the-table/) mu.

## Ɛdeɛn na wobɛsɔ hwɛ akyire

- **Mmɔdensɛm a wɔsan de ayɛ.** Fa nkyerɛwee baako, te sɛ mpɔmpɔn anaa ɔsankɔfa nketewa, na ma wɔ pampamu baabi biara mu. Sesa biribi koro, te sɛ ɔkɛse anaa mmɔdensɛm no ntam kɔ.
- **Nkyerɛwee a ɛwɔ mmɔdensɛm.** Kyerɛw mmɔdensɛm, na ma wɔ mmɔdensɛm biara mu bɔ nkyerɛwee baako a ɛyɛ mmerɛ. Obi nhia sɛ ɔbɔ mmɔdensɛm no wɔ ɔhyɛ so.
- **Kɔla wɔ mmeae.** Di kɔla baako, de ɛbɛyɛ tɛ, na ma ɛka anaa ɛbɛka ho. Eyi yɛ yie nso sɛ wode [ɔsɛeɛ nsuo](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/) rekɔla.
- **Nkyerɛwee a ɛdi nnwom so.** Tɔ nnwom baako, na ma nkyerɛwee biara nni so. [Draw to music](/activity/draw-to-music/) kaad no kyerɛkyerɛ sɛnea wɔyɛ.
- **Nsɛnkyerɛnne a wɔde ahyɛ.** Fa pampamu gu nhahan anaa tile a ɛwɔ nsɛnkyerɛnne so, na fa crayon ɛpɛ ntwa. Hwɛ mpaapaemu a ɛba mu.

## Nneyɛe a ɛyɛ mmerɛ

- **Wɔatena anaa wɔ ɔpon so.** Fa pampamu gu clipboard anaa tray a ɛwɔ wo nkyɛn, na ma pen no ka wo nsa ho.
- **Ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wɔbɛkyere.** Fa marker a ɛyɛ kɛse anaa fa pencil a ɛyɛ kakra no ma foam tape. Kyerɛw mmɔdensɛm kɛse sen nketewa.
- **Ɛnhwɛ yie.** Fa marker a ɛyɛ tuntum di pampamu a ɛyɛ fitaa so, fa nneɛma a ɛyɛ kɛse, na hann pɛpɛ. Ka mmɔdensɛm no ho kɔ nkyerɛwee a woreyɛ no.
- **Nneɛma a ɛyɛ kakra.** Fa pen baako ne mpaapaemu baako, na gyae kɔla no.
- **Ɛyɛ tiaa.** Kyerɛw mpɔmpɔn baako wɔ nkyerɛwee no so, na gyae. Mmerɛ abien nso ye.
- **Ɛwɔ hɔ dedaw.** Fi nkyerɛwee kɔnturu a wɔakyerɛw dedaw ase, na ka nsɛnkyerɛnne anaa kɔla ho wɔ mu.

## Ɔkwan a ɛfata ahobammɔ

- Kyɛ pen mu ahyɛ, pencil sharpener ne eraser a ɛyɛ kakra fi mmofra ketewa ne obiara a ɔbɛtumi de nneɛma ahyɛ ne ano no ho.
- Fa washable marker ne kɔla a ɛnnyɛ ɔhyɛ, na kenkan label no.
- Sɛ nneɛma no ma wo nsa yɛ yaw anaa nsa nni hɔ mu yie, gyae. Fa tool a ɛyɛ kɛse, wrist rest anaa da foforo.
- Yɛ adwuma wɔ tebul a ɛho ntɛm, a nsuo anaa tinta remi so.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie a

Sɛ obi no ayɛ no mmrɛ, abɔ no fo, anaa ɔnnhwɛ so, sesa mpaapaemu a ɛyɛ mmerɛ anaa gyae. Sɛ ɔka sɛ wantumi nkyerɛw a, fa mmɔdensɛm a ɛnni nsɛnkyerɛnne biara dwene so: mpɔmpɔn, nsuo ne mpɛn, a ɛnni nea ɛteɛ anaa nea ɛyɛ bɔne. Sɛ adwuma no ma obi tete a ɛyɛ yaw no ba, gyae, ma no nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew, na yɛ biribi a ɔbɛhu. Gyae yɛ mmuae a ɛyɛ yie, na pampamu no bɛtena hɔ.

[Coloring](/games/coloring/) agoro no ma adwuma a ɛte saa a ɛdi ntɛm, a ɛwɔ kɔnturu a wɔaye dedaw. Ɛyɛ baabi a ɛyɛ mmerɛ a wobɛfi ase sɛ nkyerɛwee yɛ den wo nnɛ.

Wɔ 2019 mu, WHO Regional Office for Europe yɛɛ scoping review a ɛhwehwɛɛ nhwehwɛmu a ɛboro 3,000 mu, na ɛkyerɛkyerɛ nkitahodi a ɛda nneyɛe a ɛfa nnwinnade ho ne ahoyare ne ahomegyebere ntam. Fa no sɛ nsɛm a ɛboa ma hu nsɛm, na ɛnyɛ bɔhyɛ sɛ biribiara bɛboa obiara.
