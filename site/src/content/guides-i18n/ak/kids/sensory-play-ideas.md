Sensory play ye ntease a ɛfa tɔ, hũ, ahyew, ahoni ne ntam. Ɔba a ɔtwa nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew, a ɔpete nsuo a wɔde ahyɛ mu sponge, anaa ɔwosow ɔsɔnkyɛ no yɛ sensory play. Ɛnhia nneɛma a wɔtɔ wɔ sika mu. Nneɛma a ɛwɔ saa nkyerɛwde yi mu kɛse no wɔ nʼaduane mu, nʼefie anaa nkɔfa mu.

Saa nkyerɛwde yi ye ma awofo, nana ne akwansrafo a wɔhwɛ mmofra a wɔn afe ye afe 1 ase. Ɛka sɛnea ɛbɛyɛ ma obi biara ne nneɛma a ɛboa ne fie ne bere a ɔbɛyɛ ntease no, na ɛma wo ho to nea ɛfata wo fie.

## Hwan na ɛfata

Sensory play fata mmofra a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔhwehwɛ nneɛma, na ɛbetumi nso ayɛ ntease a ɛyɛ dodow a ɛnnɛ. Ebinom pɛ sɛ wɔfa wɔn nsa ahwɛ nneɛma, ebinom nso pɛ sɛ wɔsen sɛ ɛyɛ nneɛma. Ebinom pɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ anaa wɔtie. Saa nneɛma yi nyinaa yɛ papa. Nhwehwɛmu yi nkyerɛ mmofra ntam, na ɛma mo nkurɔfo nhwɛ nneɛma. Wo ara ne wɔn bɔbɔ nnim wɔn ankasa.

## Ahobammɔ ne ɛkan

- **Ɔpanyin wɔ nkyɛn.** Wɔ nsuo ntease bere nyinaa, hyɛ ɔba a ɔwɔ mfe 5 ase nkyɛn. Ɔba betumi awu wɔ nsuo kakra mu, enti nnyɛ ɔba a wɔbɛbɔ nkwa kɛkɛ, ɛnnɛ ɛnyɛ bere tiaa.
- **Nneɛma ketewa nnyɛ ma mmofra a wɔn afe ye 3 ase.** Fa ntwerɛ, nkyɛmu, nkɔkɔɔ, aburo, pasta ne nneɛma ketewa a wɔwɔ kwan a wɔntumi nnya so. Ɔsɔhwɛ KidsHealth Nemours ahyɛ choking ho nkyerɛwde ka sɛ nneɛma ketewa a wɔde ma ntease, sika ne batiri yɛ nneɛma a ɛma obi gu ho.
- **Nkɔkɔɔ ne aburo ma mmofra a wɔn afe ye 5 ne ɔsoro nko.** Nkɔkɔɔ ntease tete yɛ ma mmofra a wɔn afe ye 5 ne ɔsoro, na ɔpanyin wɔ nkyɛn. Fa ahobammɔ bɔ pasta ne nkɔkɔɔ a wɔde ahyɛ mu.
- **Nnyɛ nneɛma a wɔbɛdi.** Nnyɛ ntease a wɔde fufu, cloud dough anaa salt dough ma mmofra a wɔbɛnnyɛ nneɛma, na nnyɛ nneɛma biara sɛ nneɛma a wɔde ntease. Ɔbɔ, ɛbɔ ne nufɔ nkyerɛ nneɛma ahyɛde a ɛwɔ nkyerɛwde so sɛ Food Allergy Research and Education ka. Ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ ansa na wode fufu anaa nufɔ ahyɛ mu. Bisa abusua no ansa na wode nnuane biara ahyɛ mu.
- **Fa nsuo a ɛwɔ nkɔsoɔ mu nko.** Nsuo bo a ɛwɔ ahoɔfɛ, nsuo ne ntwa a wɔatwa mu no nnyɛ mmofra a wɔn afe ye 3 ase. Fa ntoma a ɛyɛ den ato ntwa no so, na hwɛ sɛ ɛyɛ den ansa na woda so.
- **Nnyɛ nkotodwe a ɛyɛ nkɔkɔ.** Nnyɛ nkotodwe a ɛyɛ nkɔkɔ ma nneɛma a wɔwɔ sɛ wɔbɛtow wɔ wɔn anofa mu, ɛmfa ho mmofra no mfe.
- **Hwɛ nneɛma a ɛbɛtumi ayɛ obi ayaw.** Ebinom pɛ sɛ wɔnnye agorɔsoɔ. Fa nsuo a ɛwɔ ahoɔfɛ anaa nsa nkyɛnkyɛnkyɛn a ɛnyɛ nnuane de ahyɛ mu. Hwɛ sɛ sopo, bubble bath anaa lotion a wɔde di ɔba no ho yɛ ahobammɔ.
- **Kora nneɛma a ɛyɛ nnwuma sɛ bɛsoa.** Nneɛma a wɔde ho nsuo, bubble bath ne dish soap betumi ayɛ ɔhohoro sɛ wɔnom. Hyɛ wɔn anaa wɔ baabi a wɔntumi nnya. HealthyChildren.org nkyerɛwde a ɛka ho ahobammɔ wɔ ɔhohoro ho no wɔ nsɛm pii ho.

## Nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew ne nsuo a ɛyɛ fuw

**Nneɛma a wohia:** nkotoku abien a ɛyɛ ketewa; ɔkɔkɔɔ a ɛwɔ nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew (wɔsɔ no wɔ wo nsa akyi); ɔkɔkɔɔ a ɛwɔ nsuo a ɛyɛ fuw; tawuo; kɔp ne ntɔnsɛnsɛn ketewa.

**Nkɔsoɔ:**

1. Fa ɔba no tena fam anaa ntoma ketewa so, na fa tawuo wɔ nkotoku no ase.
2. Ma ɔnsa nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew nka, anaa fa ntɔnsɛnsɛn nka sɛ ɔpɛ. Ka nea ɔrenya ho asɛm: "Ɛyɛ hyew."
3. Fa nsuo a ɛyɛ fuw ma no. Bisa, "Ɛte sɛ, anaa ɛyɛ soronko?"
4. Ma ɔnsɔ nsuo kɔ kɔp ne kɔp mu, anaa nʼakyi nsuo kɔ nkotoku foforɔ mu.
5. Gyae sɛ ɔba no antwa nsa. Nsuo no nka kɔ ɔwɔ mu, nnyɛ sink mu, na pɛ ntɛmpa no.

**Ɔkwan a ɛnyɛ mmɔ fie bɔ:** Fa nkotoku baako ne nsuo kakra. Fa nkotoku no wɔ tray a ɛwɔ nkɔsoɔ mu.

## Ntoma a wɔde ahyɛ mu

**Nneɛma a wohia:** ntoma anaa ɔdaa a wɔde ahyɛ mu; nneɛma anan anaa enum a ɛyɛ kɛse sen toilet roll tube, te sɛ ɔkwansrafo, ɔsoro soft toy, sponge, bath toy ne pine cone.

**Nkɔsoɔ:**

1. Fa nneɛma no mu wɔ ntoma no mu, na to ntoma no. Hwɛ sɛ biribi ketewa biara ntumi nnwane.
2. Ma ɔba no ahyɛ nsa mu, na ɔnhwɛ, ɔnkyere, ɔpaw baako.
3. Bisa, "Ɛte sɛ dɛn?" na tie. Ma nsɛm te sɛ "ɔsoro," "nkɔkɔ" anaa "ɛyɛ fɛ."
4. Ma ɔba no nyane nneɛma no sɛ ɔrehwɛ.
5. San de no ma mu bio, anaa gyae.

**Ɔkwan a ɛnyɛ mmɔ fie bɔ:** Fa nneɛma a wɔawo nkodɔ nko. Fa ntoma no wɔ wo nkotɔ so, na ma ɔba no nka ntoma no, na ɛnyɛ sɛ ɔbɛhyɛ nsa mu.

## Bubble wrap a wɔbɛtow so

**Nneɛma a wohia:** bubble wrap a ɛyɛ dwamu bi, ɛyɛ metre baako nnan; fa too ntoma a ɛyɛ den so. Sɛ wopɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛnnyɛ bɔbɔ, fa krataa a ɛnnɛ ɛnni mu gu so.

**Nkɔsoɔ:**

1. Fa bubble wrap no too ntoma a ɛyɛ pe so, bubble no to so.
2. Sɛ ɔba no pɛ a, yi ne pata ne nkɔkɔɔ ntwa. Bisa no sɛ ɔntena, ɔntow, anaa ɔntoa.
3. Ka ɔkwan a ɛte: "Pop, pop." Ma ɔpaw sɛnea ɔbɛtena.
4. Pɛ dwamu no na yɛ bio sɛ ɔba no pɛ sɛ ɔyɛ bio.
5. Ɔba no ayɛ no, guan bubble wrap no na yi firi ntoma no so.

**Ɔkwan a ɛyɛ hɔ:** Fa tawuo gu bubble wrap no so, na nne no bɛyɛ fɛ. Tow so brɛoo, anaa ma ɔba no bɔ bubble baako baako ne nsa so.

## Nsuo a wɔde ahyɛ mu ne wɔtɔ nkɔsoɔ

Nsuo a wɔde ahyɛ mu yɛ papa ma mmofra a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ nneɛma a ɛtutu. Yɛ mu wɔ ɔpanyin ne wo nkyɛn, na ma mmofra a wɔn afe ye 3 ne ɔsoro nni so.

**Nneɛma a wohia:** kɔp a ɛyɛ nkɔkɔɔ ne ɔkɔkɔ a ɛyɛ den a wɔbɛtow so; nsuo; ntɔnsɛnsɛn a wɔde ahyɛ mu a wɔbɛsa; ntwerɛ anaa sequins a ɛyɛ kɛse sen sɛ wɔbɛgu so; ngyinamu a ɛyɛ den a wɔde ato ntɔnsɛnsɛn no so.

**Nkɔsoɔ:**

1. Hyɛ nsuo wɔ kɔp no mu, bɛyɛ abɛɛ mu ɔkyɛmu mmiɛnsa, na fa ntɔnsɛnsɛn ne ntwerɛ ka ho.
2. To ɔkɔkɔɔ no yie, na fa ngyinamu a ɛyɛ den to ntɔnsɛnsɛn a ɛwɔ ɔkɔkɔɔ ne kɔp no ntam, na ma ɛyɛ den.
3. Ma ɔba no mpo kɔp no na ɔnhwɛ ntwerɛ no ne ho.
4. Sɛ wɔde di awie a, hyɛ no bɔɔ, na kora no wɔ baabi a mmofra a wɔn afe ye 3 ase ntumi nnya.

**Ɔkwan a ɛnyɛ mmɔ fie bɔ:** Fa kɔp ketewa, na ma ɔba no nkɔ so de sɛ nʼasa wɔ tray so. Hwɛ ɔkɔkɔɔ no yie ansa na wode di, na sɛ ɛpɛ a, tow kɔp no.

## Ɔhwehwɛ ne nhyehyɛe

Hyehyɛ ahoɔfɛ a wɔbɛyi ansa na wɔaduru. Fa ntoma, abɔ ne bakan wɔ wo nkyɛn. Fa nsuo a ɛyɛ fuw ne a ɛyɛ hyew a wɔde ahyɛ mu wɔ tray so, na wotumi nkɔ sink so wɔ mmere baako mu. Pɛ tuo no ne ntoma a ɛyɛ nsuo, na ma ɛtɔ. Ma mmofra no nnwene nnwene anaa wɔn adwuma kakra, te sɛ ɔde sipuun ahyɛ kɔp mu, na ɛbɛma ntease no awie no nso ayɛ ntease no ankasa.

Sɛ ahoɔfɛ yɛ ɔhaw ma wo ba no a, fi nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ ketewa so ase, na ka nneɛma foforɔ ho bio akyire. Fa ntoma a ɛyɛ nsuo gu ɔba no ntebea so, na ɔbɛtumi apɛ ne nsa wɔ bere biara.

## Ntease a wɔde agyanom ne wo

Agyanom betumi de nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew ne fuw ahyɛ sɛ ɔba no bɛpaw baabi a ɔbɛtena. Agyanom a wɔwɔ bere pii betumi ate fam ma nneɛma a ɛyɛ ntoma no, na wɔka nneɛma no ho asɛm. Agyanom a ɛnyɛ den nkɔ so betumi atena ntebea so, na wɔde nneɛma ama ɔba no. Sɛ wopɛ nkɔsoɔ foforɔ a mo nyinaa betumi asɔ, hwɛ [nkɔsoɔ a ɛfa mo nyinaa ho](/together/) wɔ saa nkyerɛwde yi mu.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie a

- **Ɔba no ntwi anaa ɔtu afi hɔ.** Gyae, pɛ ne nsa wɔ tawuo so, na ma ɔba no ntease a ɛyɛ dwodwoo. Sɔ hwan bio da foforɔ, na nneɛma ɛnnyɛ dodow a wɔbɛtow.
- **Ɔba no de nneɛma to ne nno mu.** Yi nneɛma no ne nneɛma ketewa no firi hɔ. Fa nneɛma kɛse a ɛyɛ papa nko, na ma ntease no ne ɔpanyin no nyinaa ntena wo nkyɛn.
- **Ɔba no pɛ sɛ ɔhwɛ nko.** Ɛyɛ papa. Kyere no nneɛma no, anaa ma ɔhwɛ fi sohwɛ mu.
- **Ɛfa fɛ kɛse.** Fa tray a ɛwɔ nkɔsoɔ mu, anaa kɔ fie so sɛ ewiei ma.

Sɛ wo mfa nni ahoɔden a ɛfa ɔba no nhoma, nnwuma a ɛyɛ nnuane anaa asɛm biara a ɛfa ahoyare ho no, bisa ɔyarefo a ɔhwɛ ɔba no yiye.
