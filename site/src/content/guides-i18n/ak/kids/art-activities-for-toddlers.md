Mmofra yɛ adwuma pa wɔ nkɔkɔɔ mu. Wɔde nkyerɛ nsɛm, wɔde nsu bɔ, wɔyi sticker ahyɛ mu, na wɔhwɛ nea ɛbɛba. Nea ɛnhia ne sɛ wɔyɛ adwuma a wɔawie, na nneɛma kakra ne obi a ɔwɔ tenten na ɛbɛtumi ayɛ. Saa nkyerɛkyerɛ yi yɛ ma awofoɔ, agyanom ne wɔn a wɔhwɛ mmofra a wɔn mfe ɛyɛ 1 kɔsi 3. Ɛka adwuma abiɛsa: kɔkɔɔ kɛse, nsu bɔ, ne sticker dwuma.

Eyi yɛ nneɛma a wobɛsɔ hwɛ, na ɛnyɛ nkɔso a wɔbɛsi so. Mmofra biara yɛ adi. Ɔba a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyere kɔkɔɔ no nko, anaa ɔpɛ sɛ ɔde nsu bɔ na ɛnyɛ sɛ ɔbɛtɔ wɔ so, ɔreyɛ adwuma no yie.

## Hwan na nneɛma yi fata?

Nneɛma yi fata mmofra a ɛdɔ sɛ wɔkyere nneɛma, wɔsɛe wɔn nsa, na wɔhwɛ nea ɛsesa. Ɛfata mmofra a wɔn mfe ɛyɛ 1 kɔsi 3, obi a ɔte wɔn ho. Mmofra binom pɛ adwuma baako nko, na wɔn ani nnye nneɛma foforo; eyi yɛ mmerɛw. Fa adwuma baako pɛ ma ɔba, na ma bere no nyɛ tenten.

## Ahobammɔ ansa na woafi ase

Kenkan saa nkyerɛkyerɛ yi ansa na woafi adwuma biara ase.

- **Tena wo nsa ase.** Mmofra ntɛm paa. Hwɛ sɛ obi wɔ wo nsa ase wɔ adwuma biara mu, na mma ɔba nka nsu ho nkoa.
- **Hwɛ sɛ ɔbɛtrɛw nneɛma ano.** Mmofra de nneɛma ka wɔn ano. Nemours KidsHealth choking guide kyerɛ sɛ mpompɔn a wɔayi, sika, nneɛma a wɔde yɛ adwuma ne nneɛma ketewa a ɛwɔ akɔnnɔ ho ka nneɛma a ɛbɛtrɛw mmofra ano wɔ fie. Sɛ wobɛhwɛ nneɛma a ɛtɔ mu, biribiara a ɛtumi atwa toilet roll tube mu no yɛ ketewa ma ɔba a ɔwɔ mfe 3 ase.
- **Yi nneɛma ketewa afiri ano.** Ma mmofra a wɔn mfe ɛyɛ 3 ase nnye bɔtɔn, akyekyedeɛ, nkɔnkɔm, sticker ketewa anaa kapu biara a ɛnyɛ den.
- **Mma nkɔsoɔ.** Mma mmofra a wɔn mfe ɛyɛ 3 ase nnye nkɔsoɔ, latex anaa foil. Nkɔsoɔ a ɛbuabua no nkyɛn yɛ nneɛma a ɛbɛtrɛw ɔba ano.
- **Fa nneɛma a ɛnnyɛ nnuro, a wɔbɛtumi asa, na wɔnsoɔ.** Hwehwɛ label a ɛkyerɛ sɛ ɛnnyɛ nnuro. Art and Creative Materials Institute de Approved Product seal ma nneɛma a wɔahwɛ, na Cautionary Labeling seal sɛ akwankyerɛ wɔ hɔ. Kenkan label no ansa na woyɛ dwuma.
- **Hwɛ sɛ obi nni aduane ho ntoɔ.** Bisa abusua no ansa na wode biribiara a ɛwɔ aduane mu di dwuma, te sɛ dough a wɔyɛ fi aduane mu. Abusua binom pɛ sɛ wɔnnye aduane kɔkɔɔ, enti fa paint a wɔsa ahinika na ɛnyɛ aduane, anaa non-food kɔkɔɔ, kɔ food coloring so.
- **Mma nneɛma nkɔ ano.** Yi kɔkɔɔ ne sticker a ɛwɔ akyi ne mpapahwɛ ketewa firi ano ntɛmntɛm bere a wɔrentumi nyɛ dwuma bio.

## Kɔkɔɔ kɛse wɔ pampa kɛse so

Kɔkɔɔ kɛse yɛ mmerɛw ma ɔba sɛ ɔbɛkyere, na ɛma ɔyɛ kɔkɔ yiye, enti ɛfata mmofra mu biara.

**Nneɛma a wohia:** kɔkɔɔ kɛse abien anaa abiɛsa a ɛnyɛ nnuro, a wɔsɔ ho; pampa kɛse anaa ɛdwa a wɔde yɛ wallpaper; masking tape; tray anaa beaeɛ kɛse wɔ fam; wet wipes ma nsa.

**Anammɔn:**

1. Fa pampa no to ɔpon anaa fam so, na ɛnsɔre.
2. Te wo ho ɛbɛn ɔba no. Yɛ kɔkɔ tenenenen wɔ pampa no so, na ka nea wɔreyɛ: “Ase, na ɛtrɛw.”
3. Ma ɔba no kɔkɔɔ baako, na ma ɔnyɛ kɔkɔ sɛnea ɔpɛ.
4. Sɛ ɔdi wo akyi a, kɔ so. Sɛ ɔde kɔkɔɔ no to ne ano a, yi bio na de kɔkɔɔ foforo ma no.
5. Frɛ agorɔ no din bere a wɔreyɛ: “Eyi yɛ fitaa.” Kyerɛ kɔkɔ bi nso na ka sɛ: “Eyi yɛ kɔkɔ kɛse.”
6. Gyae bere a ɔba agyae. Hohoro nsa ne ho, na gyaw pampa no sɛ wobɛhwɛ bio.

**Ɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ fɛ:** Fa pampa no to tray a ɛwɔ mpɛn kɛse so. Fa kɔkɔɔ no to ɔba nsa mu, na fa wo nsa kyekyɛ ɔba nsa so bere a ɛreyɛ nkɔkɔɔ mmienu mmienu, na afei gyae.

**Ɛdwodwo ɔkwan:** Yɛ agorɔ baako pɛ. Fa wo ho to fam so, pampa no wɔ dwoma so, na yɛ kɔkɔ ntɛmntɛm bere a ɔba no rehwɛ.

## Nsu bɔ

Nsu bɔ yɛ ɔkwan a ɛnyɛ hwee a ɛyɛ fɛ. Kɔkɔ no pue, ɛsesa, na ɛfiri hɔ; na nneɛma no nhia sɛ wɔpopa wɔ ho ntɛmntɛm.

**Nneɛma a wohia:** bokɔ anaa ɔpɛnsɛ nsu a ɛyɛ ketewa; brush kɛse a ɛyɛ nwiaa anaa sponge a ɛwɔ ɔdan a ɛyɛ den; pampa, fɛns anaa fam a wɔayɛ, anaa card kɛse a wobɛtumi asanka; atade ma fam.

**Anammɔn:**

1. Paw fam a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, te sɛ fam a wɔayɛ anaa kɔkɔ a ɛwɔ abɔfra. Sɛ ɛwɔ fie a, fa card kɛse so wɔ tray so.
2. Fa nsu to ɔpɛnsɛ no mu kakra, na ɛnyɛ fã a ɛyɛ mmerɛw. Gyina ɛbɛn wo, nea wɔbɛtumi ati wo nsa ase.
3. Fa brush anaa sponge no to nsu mu, na kyerɛ ɔba sɛnea wɔbɛpɛ so wɔ nkɔkɔɔ so. Ma no ka kwan a ɔpɛ.
4. Hwɛ nkɔkɔɔ no ahyɛ nsu ... dwiri na ɛyera. Bisa sɛ: “Nsu no kɔɔ he?” na ma ɔba no mmɔ mmuo a ɔpɛ.
5. Sɛ brush no awow a, fa nsu bio ma no. Sɛ ɔba no pɛ sɛ ɔde mfasoɔ foforo di dwuma a, ma no brush anaa sponge foforo.
6. Sɛ ɔba no ayɛ sɛ ɔbɛgyae a, tu nsu no kɔ fam na sɔ brush no.

**Ɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ fɛ:** Fa sponge a ɛwɔ ɔdan a ɛyɛ den so, ɛyɛ mmerɛw sen brush a ɛyɛ ɛkakra. Sɛ ɛhia a, fa too sponge no ma ɔba no, na to ɛwɔ nkɔkɔɔ so.

**Ɛdwodwo ɔkwan:** Ma ɔba no nnɔ nsu firi kɔp ketewa mu kɔ tray so, bere a wo na woretwe bokɔ no. Nnɔ nsu yɛ adwuma koraa.

## Sticker dwuma

Sticker bɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛboa ma ɔba bɛyɛ mfonini na ɔasua sɛnea wɔbɛfa nneɛma na wɔde ako fam. Nanso ma mmofra no, sticker hwehwɛ nkɔkɔ.

**Nneɛma a wohia:** sticker kɛse abien anaa abiɛsa a ɛnyɛ nnuro, a ɛyɛ fɛw sɛ wɔyi; pampa a ɛnni nkɔkɔ anaa windo; tray ma sticker sheet no.

**Anammɔn:**

1. Yi sticker baako firi sheet no mu, na ma ɔba no.
2. Boa no ma ɔhyɛ sticker no pampa so, anaa windo so wɔ ɔba no kɔkɔ so.
3. Frɛ mfonini no din: “Ɛyɛ nkɔkɔɔ kɔkɔ,” anaa “Ɛyɛ nsoromma.”
4. Hyɛ sticker foforo kakra, na ma ɔba no ye sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɛkɔ he.
5. Yi sticker sheet no firi hɔ bere a adwuma no awie, na kɔ hɔ a ɛnnya ho nka.

**Ɛyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ fɛ:** Yi sticker no ansa wɔ pampa no so, na ɔba no nyɛ hyɛ. Eyi yi nea ɛyɛ den no firi hɔ.

**Ɛdwodwo ɔkwan:** Ma sticker abiɛsa pɛ. Adwuma ketewa a ɛwɔ awiei a ɛda adi ... ɛbɛtumi ayɛ dodoɔ sen sticker sheet a ɛma ɔba yi ahyɛ mu.

Mma sticker sheet ketewa nyinaa nkɔ mmofra nkyɛn. Sticker yɛ nneɛma ketewa, na sɛ wɔnam ɔba no ano a, yɛ ahobammɔ ho asɛm.

## Yɛ no wɔ agyanom ne wo ho

Agyanom bɛyɛ mmoa pa wɔ saa adwuma yi mu. Wɔbɛtumi atena ɔba nkyɛn, wɔde brush pɛ no nso pampa wɔn ankasa so, na ɔba no bɛtumi asɛe wɔn. Agyanom binom nso wɔ bere a ɛyɛ dodo, na mmofra dodoɔ pɛ saa. Sɛ wopɛ nkɔnsɔ foforo ma agyanom ne wɔn mmawa sɛ wɔbɛyɛ ho bom, hwɛ [nkɔnsɔ a wɔyɛ bom](/together/) mu.

Agyanom binom suro mpɔtam anaa ahobammɔ ho. Kyerɛ wɔn saa kratafa yi ansa na woafi ase, na ka nea obiara bɛhwɛ ɔba no wɔ anammɔn biara mu.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie

- **Ɔba no de nneɛma nyinaa to ne ano.** Ma no biribiara a ɛyɛ den a ɔbɛkyere. Sɛ ɔtɔ mu bere nyinaa a, gyae adwuma no, na nkyɛ bio kɔsi ɛda foforo anaa afe foforo.
- **Ɔba no tow kɔkɔɔ anaa brush.** Eyi yɛ nea ɛbaa ho bere nyinaa. Hohoro nneɛma no, na ma baako baako. Sɛ wɔtow hɔ bio a, gyae adwuma no.
- **Ɔba no te anaa ɔkɔ akyiri.** Ma no kɔ. Yi bio bere a ɛbɛba anaa ɛda foforo, anaa tena nkyɛn na woyɛ nsu wɔ fal no so wo ho.
- **Ɔba no pɛ adwuma no bere kakra.** Ɛyɛ adwuma pa. Ka nea ɔyɛe, na gyae.

Sɛ wonnim sɛ nneɛma a woreyɛ dwuma no ahobammɔ wɔ mu, hwɛ label no. Na sɛ ɔyɛ asɛm a ɛfa yare ho, bisa wo ba no ayaresafo ankasa.
