Mmere bi ara na wowɔ simma nsia, mmere foforo na ɛyɛ anwummere, na nhyehyɛe nni hɔ. Saa kratafa yi de nneɛma a ɛnhwɛ screen ba, wɔahyehyɛ sɛnea bere a wowɔ ye. Ɛbarima pii hia paper, ikyiri anaasɛ nneɛma kakra a ɛwɔ fie. Nneɛma kakra hia sɛ wɔkɔ abɔnten kɔnnantew.

Saa kratafa yi ma awofo, agyanom ne wɔn a wɔhwɛ mmofra a wɔn afe ɛyɛ 3 anaasɛ ɛboro so. Mmofra a wɔn ka ho ketewa betumi aka ho sɛ wobɛboa wɔn, nanso ɔpanyin no hia sɛ ɔwɔ nkyɛn wɔ nneɛma biara mu. Nneɛma biara kyerɛ nea wohia ne nea wobɛyɛ.

## Hwan a ɛfata

Nneɛma yi fata mmofra a wɔpɛ akyinkyin, ayɛ nneɛma, nantew ne ka. Ɛboa nso sɛ screen na ɛyɛ wɔn ade a wɔde di dwuma kɛse, na wopɛ sɛ wɔwɔ foforɔ. Bere a wɔfa nneɛma yi nkyɛ screen nni hɔ hia sɛ ɛyɛ bere a wɔde yɛ biribi foforɔ, na wo nso betumi aka ho.

## Ahobammɔ ne ade a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ kan

- **Ɔpanyin wɔ nkyɛn.** Ma mmofra a wɔn afe ɛyɛ 5 ase, ɔpanyin no hia sɛ ɔwɔ nkyɛn wɔ nneɛma a ɛwɔ nneɛma ketewa, nsuo, nkrataa anaasɛ abɔnten ho.
- **Nneɛma ketewa.** Mmofra a wɔn afe ɛyɛ 3 ase, wɔrenhyɛ wɔn nneɛma ketewa te sɛ aboɔfoɔ, ahinkotoo, sika, kɔka anaasɛ nneɛma ketewa a ɛwɔ akɔnnɔwa ho. Nneɛma a ɛte saa ka ho wɔ nneɛma a ɛbɔ mmerɛw a wɔhwɛ wɔ [Nemours KidsHealth choking guide] mu.
- **Abɔnten.** Hwɛ owia, de ahoɔfɛ ka anibuei ho, na tena kwan so bere a ɛwɔ nsuo nkyɛn. Sɛ mo tu abɔnten a ɛwɔ ntɛm mu akyi a, hwɛ mmofra nsa ho sɛ ticks wɔ hɔ.
- **Nnua.** Mma mmofra nnnnwa nnua anaasɛ aba biara a ɛwɔ abɔnten. Hwɛ, kyerɛ ne ho na kyekyɛ, nanso mma wɔn ndi.
- **Nkrataa ne gɔm.** Fa nkrataa a ɛyɛ blunt ma mmofra a wɔn afe ɛyɛ 6 ase, na fa gɔm a ɛnnyɛ nnwuma.

## Simma nsia

Fa saa nneɛma yi di dwuma bere a wowɔ simma nsia akɔnnɔ, te sɛ bere a wo ne aduane wɔ ɔkwan so anaasɛ bere a worehwɛ ɔkɔ.

- **Me hwɛ biribi a ɛyɛ mpɔmpɔmpɔ.** Bɔ mmɔden sɛ mo bɛka biribi a mohwɛ a ɛyɛ mpɔmpɔmpɔ anaasɛ ɛyɛ ɔkɔlɔr bi. Ma ɛnkɔ so kɔsi sɛ ɛyɛ anigye.
- **Bɔ mmoa, na ɔba no ne wo bɔ.** Bɔ mmoa kakra te sɛ bɔ, bɔ, bɔ. Ɔba no bɛtu wo bɔ. Ɛnna, ma ɔba no nso ayɛ wo bɔ. Ɛyɛ den ma mmofra nyinaa, efi ababaa a ɔreyɛ mmoa kɔsi ɔba a ɔrebɔ bɔ ɛtɔ mmerɛw.
- **Yɛ sɛ mframa.** Frɛ mframa anaasɛ osutɔ, te sɛ mframa, osutɔ, owia, na ma ɔba no yɛ saa. Sesa asɛmfua no bere biara a ɛbɛyɛ sɛ simma abien.
- **Kan akyi.** Sɔre so ne ɔba no, kan mmɛɛ nkyerɛ mmɛtiatwa. Ɛnna sɔ fa akyi.

## Simma aduonu

Saa nneɛma yi hia nhyehyɛe kakra, te sɛ krataa anaasɛ nsuo a ɛwɔ abɔdeɛ mu.

- **Paper-plate faces.** Ma ɔba no paper plate ne ɔkɔlɔr pen anaasɛ crayon kakra. Ma ɔnkyerɛw ne ho anifuro, na ka sɛnea anifuro no yɛ ho. Fa pɛnsel sɛ wonni pen.
- **Tear-paper pictures.** Tear ɔkɔlɔr paper ayɛ nʼakyi, na ma ɔba no de gɔm kɔ krataa so. Ɛnni ɔfa mfonin a ɛyɛ mpɔmpɔmpɔ. [Tear paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/) wɔ akwankyerɛ a ɛyɛ ma nkɔso.
- **Nsuo a ɛyɛ hyew ne nsuo a ɛyɛ ahoɔ.** Fa abɔ mmienu, biako hyew, biako ahoɔ, na hwɛ wɔ wo nsa so. Ma ɔba no nsɔ nsuo afi ha kɔ ha, na ka nea ɔte. Ma ɔpanyin wɔ mpɔ ahoɔ.
- **Draw to music.** Gyina nnwom a ɛwɔ beat a ɛda mu kɛse, na ma ɔba no nsɛe bere a ɔretie. Kaad a ɛkyerɛ sɛnea wɔyɛ no [draw to music](/activity/draw-to-music/) kyerɛkyerɛ.

## Dɔnhwere baako

Nneɛma a ɛfata bere kɛse ma ɔba no betumi ahyehyɛ, ayɛ ne nsesa nneɛma.

- **Thieta a wɔde kɔkɔɔ ayɛ.** Fa kɔkɔɔ kɛse, na ɛwɔ hɔ dinn baabi a wɔatwa. Tɔ ne mu ɔkɔlɔr, na yɛ puppet afi paper anaasɛ sɔsɛ mu. Yɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ tew, na ɔpanyin yɛ audience.
- **Paper-bag puppets.** Ma mmofra a wɔn afe ɛyɛ 3 anaasɛ ɛboro so, kyekyɛ paper bag wɔ crayon so, na de maso ne nwiniwini bɔ. Yɛ ɔkyerɛkyerɛ tiawa ne puppet no. Ma ɔpanyin nkura aduru ne gɔm.
- **Tena kwan so, boa nneɛma.** Kɔ tenaa, boa aduro, ɛboɔ anaasɛ pine cones, de akɔ mmoa ketewa. Wɔ fie, hyehyɛ nneɛma no sɛnea ɛte ne ɔkɔlɔr, kɛseyɛ anaasɛ ɛte. Ɛnsɛ sɛ mma ɔba no nnnnwa biribi a wɔboaboa afi abɔnten.
- **Yɛ playlist a wo ne ɔba no bɛyɛ.** Paw nnwom abien abien, na ma ɔba no kyerɛ nea ɛmaa ɔpaw no. Ɛyɛ yie ma mmofra a wɔn ayɛ kɛse, wɔn a wɔbɛtumi akyerɛw no.

## Anwummere nyinaa

Ma osu rɛtɔ anaasɛ bosome a ɛyɛ dɛ, anaasɛ mmere a ɛyɛ dɛ pii.

- **Den.** Fa nsɔre di abatɔ so anaasɛ ntɔ mmienu ntam, na yɛ baabi a ɛwɔ mu de ntoma ne nwoma. Ma nneɛma no nnyɛ kɛse, na ma nsɔ ne nneɛma ketewa nnkɔ mmofra a wɔn afe ɛyɛ 3 ase nkyɛn.
- **Adansedie a wɔhwehwɛ mu wɔ fie.** Yɛ mfonin anaasɛ nsɛm a ɛyɛ list, te sɛ sɔsɛ a ɛyɛ ɔkɔlɔr, sɛnkɔ anaasɛ nwoma a ɛwɔ mmoa. Hyɛ nneɛma no, anaasɛ ma ɔba no nhwehwɛ nneɛma a ɛwɔ fie. Gyae bere a ɔrehunu.
- **Aduane a wɔnyɛ kwan so.** Ma mmofra a wɔn afe ɛyɛ 5 anaasɛ ɛboro so, ɔpanyin wɔ nkyɛn, yɛ aduane a ɛyɛ tew fi nneɛma kakra a abusua no betumi adi. Hwɛ mmoa ho ntɛm kan. Ma ɔba no mpaw nhyehyɛe a ɛde kɔ so.
- **Abɔ anaasɛ veranda mu aduaba.** Duaba nkakrankakra wɔ abɔ mu, na nsuo mu da biara. Hwɛ akontaabu a ɛwɔ nkyɛw so, na sa nsa bere a woakyiri ɛse.

## Nneɛma a ɛyɛ ɛkɔ ne ɛyɛ nwoso

- **Ɛyɛ ɛkɔ:** Fa nkyɛmu ne nneɛma kakra. Yɛ kan yɛ wo ho, na ma ɔba no awie.
- **Nwoso:** Paw nneɛma a ɛyɛ nwoso te sɛ nsɛmbɔ anaasɛ kenkan, na ma ɛnyɛ nnwom anaasɛ nneɛma a ɛyɛ fɛw.
- **Kakra:** Gyae bere a ɔrebɛyɛ kan ntam. Mmofra pii ani gye bere a ɛyɛ simma kakra.
- **Ɛtena:** Paw nneɛma a wɔyɛ wɔ ntɛm so, na ma wɔn ahwɛ ɛbɔ a ɛyɛ agyinatuo anaasɛ ntoma a wɔde ka ho.

## Sɛ wo ne agyanom reyɛ

Agyanom betumi asesa nneɛma pii yi. Agyanom betumi akenkan "I spy" ho nsɛm, anaasɛ wɔatena ɔba no akyi de paper ne pen ayɛ wɔn ankasa anifuro, bere a ɔba no nso reyɛ ne ho. Agyanom a ɔtena akyirikyiri betumi aka ho de telefon anaasɛ video call. Mo nyinaa de nneɛma koro di so, na mma mo nkɔ nwoma a mowɔ no. Kɔhwɛ [together ideas](/together/) ti no nkyerɛ nneɛma foforɔ.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie

- **Ɔba no pɛ screen.** Dwo wo ho, na ma wɔn ɔkwan baako a ɛnhwɛ screen. Wɛnhia sɛ woaka ho. Ka sɛ, "Yɛbɛyɛ eyi ansa na," na ka ho.
- **Ɔba no nyɛ anigye wɔ nea ɛdi kan no ho.** Ma wɔn nea ɛdi foforɔ fi bere korɔ mu. Nsakrae ketewa betumi aboa.
- **Ɔba no mpɛ sɛ ɔka ho.** Ma ɔhwɛ, anaasɛ ma ɔpaw nea ɛbɛdi so. Ɛhwɛ nso yɛ anigye no mu tɛkrɛma.
- **Nneɛma no fata bere pii.** Gyae wɔ nea ɛdi kan no akyi, na wɔ da foforɔ mu awie.

Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wohyehyɛ screen bere ma abusua no, American Academy of Pediatrics kyerɛkyerɛ sɛnea wobɛyɛ abusua media plan. Sɛ wowɔ biribi a ɛfa ɔba no ahoyare ho nsɛm bi, bisa ɔba no ankasa ɔdɔkotafoɔ.
