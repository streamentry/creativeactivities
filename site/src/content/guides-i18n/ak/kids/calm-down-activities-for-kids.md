Mmofra bi pɛ nnwuma a ɛyɛ bebrebre, a wɔyɛ wɔ da a awie, anaa bere a wɔpɛ biribi a ɛyɛ mmerɛ nko ara. Saa kyerɛwsɛm yi de nsusuiɛ enum a ɛnhia ɛfɛ paper, kɔlɔ, play dough kakra ne adwo beaɛ bi pɛ. Biribiara yɛ paw. Ɔba no betumi agye, asesa anaa apo, na sɛ ɔgyae a, wo nso gyae.

Saa kyerɛwsɛm yi yɛ ma awofoɔ, agyanom ne wɔn a wɔhwɛ mmofra, mmofra a wɔwɔ mfe 3 so. Mmofra a wɔyɛ nketewa betumi aka bom wɔ obi a ɔboa wɔn ho, nanso ma nnwuma no nyɛ den na ɛbɛn wo.

## Hena na ɛfata?

Saa nsusuiɛ yi fata mmofra a wɔpɛ kɔlɔ, nkyerɛ, paper a wɔbubu ne dough a wɔbɔ mu, ne mmofra a wɔpɛ nnwuma a ɛyɛ tiaa a wɔn ho ntim. Ebetumi afata mmofra a abrɛ, a wɔayɛ nnwuma kɛse, anaa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔbɛn wo nanso nkasa nnyɛ dɔɔso. Wo ara wunim wo mmofra yie sen obiara. Ma nsusuiɛ baako wɔ bere biara, na hwɛ nea wɔpaw.

Saa nsusuiɛ yi nyɛ ayaresa, na wɔnnhyɛ bɔ sɛ ade bi bɛba. Ɛyɛ akwan a wɔfa so tena adwo bere mu bom.

## Mmara a ɛfa nnwuma ho

- **Fa nnwuma no ma paw, na mmfa ahyɛ.** Ka sɛ, “Wopɛ sɛ wobɛkɔlɔ kakra?” ɛnyɛ sɛ, “Ɛsɛ sɛ wodwo ahome.”
- **Ɔba no betumi aka daabi.** Sɛ ɔpo a, ma no ade foforɔ anaa gyae de kɔ bere foforɔ.
- **Gyae sɛ ɔba no agyae.** Nkɔ so ɛfiri sɛ woahyehyɛ bere tenten.
- **Ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔde di so sɛ mmuaeɛ.** Nnwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ yɛ ade a ɔba no betumi anya anigye, na ɛnyɛ ade a ɔbɛhwe.
- **Obi wɔ hɔ.** Tena nkyɛn wɔ nnwuma biara a ɛfa nsuo, dough, straw anaa nneɛma kakraa ho.
- **Ɛnyɛ nnuonyam na ɛtumi hohoro.** Hwehwɛ label a ɛka sɛ ɛnyɛ nnuonyam wɔ kɔlɔ, pɛnt ne dough so. The Art and Creative Materials Institute de Approved Product seal ma art materials a wɔahwɛ.

## Kɔlɔ nnwuma ne dwom a ɛyɛ mmerɛ

**Ɛsɛ sɛ wowɔ:** paper akɛse kakra anaa picture a wɔde kɔlɔ; kɔlɔ nnwuma anaa kɔlɔ pensil; dwom a ɛyɛ mmerɛ anaa dwom a wɔde ka brɛoo.

**Akwan:**

1. Paw picture anaa paper a ɛnni nsɛnkyerɛnne. Ma ɔba no mpaw kɔlɔ.
2. Gyina dwom a ɛyɛ mmerɛ brɛoo, anaa tena ase a dwom nni hɔ.
3. Kɔlɔ ne ɔba no bom, na sɛ wopɛ a, yɛ wo ankasa picture.
4. Bisa daabi bi a ɛnyɛ ‘yes’ anaa ‘no’, te sɛ “Kɔlɔ bɛn na dwom no yɛ?” na twɛn.
5. Gyae sɛ ɔba no agyae, na mo nyinaa sie kɔlɔ nnwuma no mu wɔ nkɔsoɔ.

**Ɛyɛ mmerɛ kɛse:** Fa picture kɛse a ɛyɛ tẽẽ, nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛyɛ den. Ma kɔlɔ nnwuma mmiɛnsa, na ɛnyɛ kɔlɔ nnwuma a ɛyɛ mmoaa.

**Ɛyɛ adwo kɛse:** Fa kɔlɔ baako ne paper a wɔnkyerɛwee. Ma ɔba no ntwe nea ɔpɛ, anaa ma no mmɔ shape baako nkɔsoɔ wɔ paper no so.

## Paper a wɔbubu bebrebre

**Ɛsɛ sɛ wowɔ:** paper a ɛwɔ kɔlɔ, tissue paper anaa thin craft paper; apɔ anaa baket ma nea wɔabubu; card ne glue stick sɛ ɔba no pɛ sɛ ɔyɛ picture.

**Akwan:**

1. Bubu paper no ayɛ nkwa tenten, baako baako. Ma ɔba no sie bere no.
2. Hyehyɛ nkwa no wɔ apɔ mu sɛ ɛyɛ kɔlɔ, anaa gyae sɛ ɛnyɛ.
3. Sɛ ɔba no pɛ sɛ ɔyɛ picture a, gluu nkwa no wɔ card no so wɔ akwan biara so.
4. Gyae sɛ ɔba no agyae. Nkwa no betumi asie ma da foforɔ.

**Ɛyɛ mmerɛ kɛse:** Bubu paper no ayɛ nkwa ansa, na ma ɔba no gluu no.

**Ɛyɛ adwo kɛse:** Bubu kɔlɔ baako nko ara, na bubu bebrebre wɔ nsa mmienu so.

## Dwom a wɔde ka picture

**Ɛsɛ sɛ wowɔ:** paper; kɔlɔ nnwuma anaa markers; dwom a ɛwɔ mmoa a ɛyɛ mmerɛ anaa dwom a ɛyɛ ayaw.

**Akwan:**

1. Gyina dwom no brɛoo.
2. Ka kyerɛ ɔba no sɛ ɔnkyerɛ bere a ɔtie dwom no. Wo ara nkyerɛ, anaa tena ase na wo ara.
3. Bisa, “Dwom no kɔ soro anaa kɔ fam?” Ma ɔba no ka daabi denam kwan (line) so.
4. Gyae wɔ dwom baako anaa mmienu akyi, anaa sɛ ɔba no ayɛ.

Sɛ wopɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ pii, hwɛ [Dwom a wɔde ka picture](/activity/draw-to-music/).

**Ɛyɛ mmerɛ kɛse:** Kɔlɔ kwan baako ma sɛkɔnd kakraa biara a dwom no kɔ, na ma ɔba no ma nea ɛremfa mmɔ ahyɛ ma.

**Ɛyɛ adwo kɛse:** Gyina dwom a ɛnyɛ den, anaa dwom biara nni hɔ, na kyerɛ nea ɛwɔ paper no so.

## Play dough

Play dough yɛ nnwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ ma mmofra bebrebre, nanso ɛhia nhwɛso. Dough a wɔyɛ wɔ fie yɛ ɔfifi, nnuro ne nsuo, na ɔfifi no yɛ wheat. Wheat yɛ bi a Food Allergy Research and Education ka sɛ ɛwɔ nnuane a ɛbaa mmoa mu. Bisa abusua no ansa na woyɛ anaa de dough biara di so, na sɛ wopɛ a, tɔ dough a wɔayɛ a wɔaka sɛ non-toxic.

**Ɛsɛ sɛ wowɔ:** dough kakra wɔ tray anaa mat so; rolling pin anaa ade a ɛnyɛ den; nneɛma a ɛyɛ safe te sɛ plastic cookie cutter.

**Akwan:**

1. Fa dough no to tray so. Kyerɛ ɔba no sɛ ɔbɛdi bɔ, ɔbɛtwa, ɔbɛkyekye.
2. Ma no mmɔ shape bi wɔ dough mu, anaa ma ɔnyɛ bɔɔl.
3. Bisa no sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔyɛ shape bɛn, na ma no paw.
4. Sɛ ɔba no awie a, fa dough no to airtight container mu.

**Nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛfa ahobammɔ ho:** Play dough nyɛ ma wɔde kɔ ano anaa wɔde ano. Mma ɔba a ɔnnyɛ mfe 3 ase nnye no, ɛfiri sɛ ɔbɛtumi ada ano. Kora nneɛma nketewa ne nneɛma a wɔde yɛ adwuma akɔ akyirikyiri mfe 3 ase mmofra, na hohoro nsa wɔ nnwuma akyi.

**Ɛyɛ mmerɛ kɛse:** Fa dough kɛse kakra ne cutter kɛse baako. Ma ɔba no de ne nsa ntɛ dough no.

**Ɛyɛ adwo kɛse:** Kɔlɔ dough no baako. Tena ase na fa wo ankasa dough kakra a wɔbɛbɔ mu.

## Ahome a ɛyɛ mmerɛ wɔ bubble ho

Ɛyɛ anigye sɛ wobɛfa straw bɔ bubble wɔ kapɛ a nsuo wɔ mu. Ɛhia sɛ obi wɔ hɔ bere nyinaa.

**Ɛsɛ sɛ wowɔ:** kapɛ a ɛyɛ tiaa, a nsuo ayɛ fã; straw a ɛyɛ plastic; obi a ɔbɛtena ɔba no nkyɛn.

**Akwan:**

1. Hyɛ nsuo kakra wɔ kapɛ no mu. Fa straw no to kapɛ no mu, na ne ano nka nsuo no ase kakra.
2. Kyerɛ ɔba no sɛ ɔbɛfa straw no ahome yie, na ɛma bubble ketewa.
3. Ma no mmɔ kakra, na gyae. Gyina bere kakra ntam ntam mmɔ ntam.
4. Sɛ nnwuma no awie a, gu nsuo no, na sɛ straw no nni dwuma a, kyerɛ ne ano nnkɔ ɔba no anom.

**Nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛfa ahobammɔ ho:** Tena ɔba no nkyɛn bere nyinaa. Fa straw a ɛyɛ plastic ketewa nko ara ne kapɛ nsuo. Mma ɔba no nnom kapɛ no mu nsuo, na mma no nnhome wɔ straw no mu ɛnyɛ sɛ obi hwɛ.

**Ɛyɛ mmerɛ kɛse:** Ma obi nhyɛ kapɛ no mu wɔ bere a ɔba no rehome. Gyae straw no, na hom bubble soft wɔ plate so.

**Ɛyɛ adwo kɛse:** Ma ahome baako anaa mmienu, na gyae. Bubble baako nso betumi ayɛ.

## Nnwuma a wobɛyɛ wo ne agyanom bom

Agyanom betumi akenkan wɔ kwan so bere a ɔba no rekɔlɔ, anaa wɔn nso bɛdwa wɔ tebil no afã foforɔ so. Agyanom a wɔwɔ akyirikyiri betumi akɔlɔ picture koro ara bere a wɔrekasa wɔ telefon so, na ɔba no bɛma wɔn paper no wɔ so. Sɛ wopɛ nneɛma foforɔ a wobɛyɛ bom, hwɛ [nneɛma a wobɛyɛ bom](/together/) akyiri no.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie

- **Ɔba no ppɛ.** Gye atom. Ma nnwuma foforɔ bere foforɔ, anaa mmom mo nyinaa nna ho bom.
- **Ɔba no bɛyɛ ahohia kɛse.** Gyae nnwuma no, na ma no kwan. Tena nkyɛn na ka asɛm wɔ ahomegyeɛ mu. Sɛ eyi ba daa a, ka kyerɛ wo mmofra ankasa ayaresa adwumfoɔ.
- **Ɔba no de dough anaa kɔlɔ nnwuma kɔ ano.** Yi ade no fi ne nsa, na gyae. Ma no ade a ɛyɛ safe a ɔbɛkyere.
- **Ɔba no pɛ sɛ ɔkɔ so.** Gyae bere a woahyehyɛ, na ka nea ɛbɛba. Gyaa nneɛma no ma bere foforɔ.

Sɛ wowɔ mmisa wɔ mmofra ho ayaresa ho a, bisa wo mmofra ankasa ayaresa adwumfoɔ. Saa nnwuma yi nnyɛ saa kasa no baabi.
