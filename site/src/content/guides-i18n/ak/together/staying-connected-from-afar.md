Frɛ a ɛfiri akyirikyiri ba bɛyɛ obi nkwa dapɛn mu anigye, na ɛbɛtumi nso ayɛ den. Nnipa bebree bisa "Wo ho te sɛn?" ne "Wo yɛɛ dɛn nnɛ?", na nsɛm yi de kɔ ma obi sɛ ɔnkae na ɔnkyerɛ asɛm. Saa nhwehwɛmu yi yɛ akwan foforo. Hyehyɛ biribi kakra a mobɛka, na ma frɛ no nkyerɛ ho. Ɛkyerɛkyerɛ vidio ne fon frɛ, nkrataa, nkra a wɔakodi ne adwuma a mo bɛyɛ bom a wɔde kɔ. Ɛnyinsɛn sɛnea akyirikyiri ba bɛboa obi a ɔbɛn wo nso.

## Akwan a ɛfa vidio frɛ

Vidio yɛ yie sɛ mohwɛ biribi a ɛda so anaa moyɛ bom.

- **Kyerɛ na ka.** Kɔ kamera no ho kɔ afuw mu brɛoo, ma wɔhu aboa, di nea woreyɛ ano, anaa fa mfonini a wɔayɛ no ma no. Ka nea wuhu wɔ nsɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛw mu, na ma ɛbɛyɛ bere ama wɔaka ho.
- **Hwɛ mfonini.** Fa mfonini a wɔaprint no bɛda kamera no ho, anaa kyerɛ ne mfonini kɛse kakra bi ɔkɔɔɔ. Ka sɛ, "Hwɛ Agya no kɔfi," mmom sɛ, "Ɔno ne hwan?"
- **Kenkan.** Nkyerɛwde tiaa, anaa asɛm a ɔpɛ, anaa nkrataa a obi a mo di mu ada. Ɛma ɔnipa no tie biribi a ɛyɛ dɛ.
- **Dwom anaa tie.** Dwom a ɔte no nyinaa, anaa dwom a ɔpɛ sɛ mo nam. Dwom mu mmoa ɛyɛ mmerɛw sen nkasa.
- **Di nneɛma koro.** Hyehyɛ frɛ no wɔ fie mo mmienu. Ɔnipa a ɔwɔ hɔ bɛtumi ahyɛ nnuruyɛ anaa abɔ bo, bere a wo nso wodi nnɔbae a ɔde ba.
- **Hwɛ biribi koro bere koro.** Vidio tiaa, TV a wɔahyehyɛ kɔsi, anaa agoro bɛtumi ayɛ no wɔ afie a wɔ mu nyinaa, bere a mokasa.

Sɛ obi no wɔ hɔ a, obi bɛtumi abue agoro wɔ tablet so, na ɔbɛma wɔn no. Agoro a ɛyɛ yie wɔ screen a wɔbɛhwɛ koro so ne [finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/), [picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/), [conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) ne [photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/), a ɛde mfonini a woapaw wɔ wo ankasa device so no.

## Fon frɛ

Dementia Australia [visiting guide](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/visiting-someone-dementia) ka sɛ, frɛ koro bere koro, na nnyɛ ntɛm, efisɛ nnipa bɛtumi de bere atwe adwene anaa atɔ mmuae. Wɔ fon so:

- Ka wo din ne wo abusua ntɛm: "Papa, ɛyɛ Jenny, wo babaa."
- Kasa brɛoo, de nsɛm tiaa, na bisa nsɛm baako bere biara.
- Ka nea woreyɛ, te sɛ nnuruyɛ anaa nantew kɔ nkrataa asɔfo.
- Gyae frɛ no bere a ɛda so yɛ dɛ, na ka bere a wobɛfrɛ bio.

## Nkrataa ne nkra a wɔakodi

Nkrataa tumi tena ase bere a frɛ yɛ den. Wɔbɛtumi akenkan nkrataa bio bere a ɔno ankasa pɛ, na obi a ɔbɛn no bɛtumi akenkan ma no. [Write a letter together](/activity/write-a-letter-together/) ma nkrataa ayɛ adwuma a mo bɛyɛ bom: obiara de asɛm baako, nwoma anaa ahahɔn a wɔadwene ana ɔdan a wɔasi ka. [Make a photo postcard](/activity/make-a-photo-postcard/) yɛ nkrataa tiaa a ɛwɔ mfonini wɔ ɛpon so.

Sɛ frɛ a ɛda so ba yɛ den a, kɔ vidio tiaa: ɛbɛyɛ ayɛ, dwom, anaa nantew wɔ fie. Obi a ɔwɔ hɔ bɛtumi abue no wɔ bere a ɔno ankasa pɛ, bere bebree sɛnea ɔpɛ.

## Adwuma a mo bɛyɛ bom wɔ nkrataa so

Adwuma a ɛkɔ ne ɛba ma nnipa mmienu wɔ biribi a wɔrehwɛ anidasoɔ.

- **Dwom nhwehwɛmu.** Obiara paw dwom na ɔkyerɛw nsɛm kakra a ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ no. Hwɛ [make a personal songbook](/activity/make-a-personal-songbook/).
- **Aduane nhoma.** Obi a ɔwɔ hɔ ka abusua aduane ne asɛm a ɛfa so, na wo nso dwen wo nsakrae ho. Hwɛ [start a family recipe book](/activity/start-a-family-recipe-book/).
- **Abusua dua a ɛyɛ nsa.** Obiara twe ne nsa wɔ krataa so, na ɔde kɔ ma obi separ, na wɔakɔ ne nsa a ɛyɛ dua baako. Hwɛ [make a family tree of hands](/activity/make-a-family-tree-of-hands/).

## Boa akyirikyiri

Sɛ wo wɔ akyirikyiri a, wubetumi aboa wɔ nneɛma a ɛfata, sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wo boa no. Fi ase bisa nea ɔpɛ na ɔpɛ sɛ nnipa bɛn no. Afei dwene saa nnwuma yi ho:

- **Nnansa ahyɛde.** Boa de kalenda anaa nkɔ kwan hyehyɛ, sɛ ɔnam so.
- **Nhwehwɛmu.** Hwehwɛ mmoa a ɛwɔ mantam mu, te sɛ aduane a wɔde ma, akwantu, anaa mmofra a wɔboa wɔ bere a wɔn nnwuma ayɛ. Hwɛ nsɛm no ne obi a ɔyɛ adwuma no, efisɛ ɛsɛ sɛ mpɛnsa ne mantam.
- **Krataa nnwuma.** Hyehyɛ nkrataa nkyɛ sɛ ɔpɛ no. Ayaresa ne sika nsɛm ho sɛ ɛhia nnyinasoɔ pii. Ebia adwumfoɔ hia sɛ wɔnya ɔpɛ a wɔatwerɛ ansa na wɔaka ho asɛm kyerɛ abusua, enti bisa wɔn nea wɔhia.
- **Bere ma obi a ɔwɔ hɔ.** Ka sɛ wobɛba anaa wobɛyɛ adwuma bi, na obi a ɔwɔ hɔ no atena ase.

Alzheimer's Association [long-distance caregiving guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving) kyerɛkyerɛ nnwuma pii a ɛwɔ saa mu. Gyina ho sɛ obi koro na ɔbɛka nsɛm a ɛfa da biara ho kyerɛ, di ne bɔmma kɔ so, na kyerɛ sɛnea nsɛm a ɛyɛ ntɛmntɛm bɛba. Mma obi nkɔ asɛm a ɛfa ayaresa mu ɛfiri sɛ wɔn nso wɔ hɔ.

## Ma obi a ɔwɔ dementia

Sɛ obi a ɔwɔ dementia ne wo nnɛ, ma frɛ no nyɛ tenten, na ɛyɛ nea ɔhu. Fa bere koro ne asɛm a ɔte so, na ma no ahyɛ kwan sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔka asɛm foforo. Sɛ ɔnte ase sɛ ɔhunu wo wɔ screen no so a, ka wo din ne nea woreyɛ, na mma no nhwehwɛ sɛ ɔbɛka wo din. Adwuma a mo bɛyɛ bom bɛyɛ adwuma koro.

## Ahobammɔ ne ahobrɛaseɛ

- Bisa ansa na wo bɛkyɛ nfoto a ɛwɔ afoforo mu, na ntow abusua ayaresa nsɛm kɔ intanɛt so.
- Hwɛ sɛ obi no ama wɔn kwan ansa na obiara bɛka frɛ no mu anaa ɔbɛte nsɛm a ɛfa ho.
- Fa vidio frɛ no kɔ dan a ɛnyɛ nsɛm, na hyehyɛ kamera no ma ɔnhu wo anim ne wo nsa.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie a

Ma frɛ no nyɛ tenten, sesa kɔ audio nko, anaa fa nkrataa sɛ wo bɛyɛ. Sɛ frɛ no kɔ kwan a ɛnyɛ yie a, gyae no ne animuonyam, na san bɔ bio nna foforo. Boa a ɔwɔ hɔ bɛtumi atena ne ho wɔ frɛ a ɛdi so no, na aboa ɔno ma ɔnnyin ase.
