Nnipa pii pɛ sɛ wɔwɔ adwuma a ɛyɛ anigye a wɔbɛyɛ ne ɔpanyin anaa agyanom a ɔte wɔ fie, a ɔte wɔn ho bio, anaa a wɔsra wɔn dapɛn biara. Saa kratafa yi yɛ saa bere no ma. Nneɛma no hyehyɛ sɛnea ɔhoɔden ne tumi te, sɛnea n'nsa, ani anaa n'aso yɛ adwuma no. Ɛyɛ nkɔmhyɛ nko ara. Wo na wonim ɔno yie sen obiara.

## Dɛn na wobehia?

Nneɛma pii yi yɛ fie mu nneɛma: pɛpa ne ntɛm, radio anaa fon a nnwom wɔ mu, ɛsaase a wɔabɔ, bɔtan a ɛwɔ mu, kaad, nnuhuo kakra, anaa ɔhwene bi a wɔde ahyɛ fie, anaa dirubɔ a nkɔko wɔ hɔ. Biribiara a ɛwɔ link no ho kaad kyerɛ nneɛma a ɛho hia. Kenkan nneɛma no ansa na woafi ase, na woremfa mfe nhwehwɛ nneɛma wɔ adwuma no mfiase.

## Nneɛma ma da a ɔhoɔden yɛ kakra

Sɛ ɔhoɔden yɛ kakra da bi, nipa no betumi ayɛ nneɛma kakra. Nneɛma yi gyina miniti du ne aduonu, na wɔbɛtumi ayɛ bere a wɔtena.

1. **Tie nnwom bom.** Paw nnwom mmienu anaa mmiɛnsa a ɛfiri ne mmerante mu, na ma ɔpaw nea ɛdi so. [Nsɛmpa wɔ nnwom so](/activity/draw-to-music/) de pɛnsel ma sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔbɛkyerɛ ɔkwan bi bere a ɔretie.
2. **Hwɛ mfonini albɔm mu.** Bisa sɛ, "Ka ho asɛm ma me," na mfa "Hwan ne ɔno?" nnbisa. [Kaad a ɛfa mfonini albɔm](/activity/look-through-a-photo-album/) wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛboa ma.
3. **Kyekyere ɛsaase bɔɔl.** Kyɛ ɛsaase no wɔ wo nsa ntam bere a wokyekyere. Ɛyɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, a ɛwɔ awiei a ɛda so. Hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛkyekyere ɛsaase bɔɔl](/activity/wind-a-ball-of-yarn/).
4. **Hwɛ nkɔko fi windo so.** Tena baabi a hann yɛ no, na ka nea wuhu kyerɛ no. Hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛhwɛ nkɔko fi windo so](/activity/watch-birds-from-a-window/).
5. **Bɔ rhythm bi bom.** Di nkyɛn sɛ mo nyinaa bɛbɔ tabɛl no so. Ma ɛnyɛ tenten. [Bɔ rhythm bom](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/) wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ.

Sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ wɔbɔ no nsa so a, [ma wɔnhyɛ nsa mu nsa](/activity/give-a-hand-massage/) sɛ awiei a ɛdi asɛm so. Bisa ansa na woayɛ, na gyae ntɛm sɛ ɔtwe ne nsa fi wo nsa mu.

## Nneɛma ma da a ɔhoɔden wɔ mu

Sɛ ɔhoɔden wɔ mu da bi, ma adwuma a ɛwɔ nneyɛe pii mu.

- **Hyehyɛ nkwadaa ho bom.** Twa nkɔtɔ, paw ɔfuw, na ma ɔkwan biara nkɔ ne kwan. Hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ nkwadaa](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/).
- **Yɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne wɔ nsuo so.** Fa kɛse kɛse pɛpa ne brɔsɛ kɛse, na ma nsuo ntrɛw nkɔ so. Hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛyɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne wɔ nsuo so](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/).
- **Pɛ nkataa bom.** Adwuma a ɛboa, na ɛwɔ awiei a ɛda so. Hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛpɛ nkataa bom](/activity/fold-towels-together/).
- **Yɛ mfonini a wɔkyekyere bom** fi mfonini ne nkataa so, ɔfoforo biako biako. Hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛyɛ mfonini a wɔkyekyere](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/).
- **Hyehyɛ bɔtan sɛnea wɔn ɔkɔm te** wɔ nkyɛn ketewa, ma nipa a ɔpɛ nhyehyɛe ne adwuma ketewa. Hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛhyehyɛ bɔtan](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/).
- **Yɛ nnwom nhyehyɛe bom** wɔ fon anaa speaker so, de nnwom baako baako ka ho. Hwɛ [sɛnea wobɛyɛ nnwom nhyehyɛe bom](/activity/make-a-playlist-together/).

## Sesa ma nsa mu tumi, ahunu, ntease ne nantew

Hwɛ nea ɛreyɛ ɔhaw ansa na woasesa adwuma no.

**Nsa mu tumi.** Fa ntɛm a ɛyɛ kɛse, kuruyɛ a ɛyɛ kɛse, brɔsɛ a ne tiri yɛ kɛse, anaa ɛsaase a wɔde kyekyere ntɛm a ɛyɛ tiri. Di pɛpa no so ntɛw ansa na ɛbɛsɔre. Nsesa a wɔatwa ansa na ɛbɛboa sɛ wɔde mfa nkyerɛ tɛkɛremu.

**Ahunu.** Fa nneɛma a ɛdi mu pefee, te sɛ ntɛm tuntum wɔ pɛpa fitaa so. Fa nkyerɛwde kɛse ne hann a ɛfiri fam bra. Tabɛl ntadeɛ pɛ ma nneɛma ketewa ahunu yie.

**Ntease.** Tena wo ne nipa no ano. Ka asɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, na gyina ase ntɛm a ɛwɔ wo ntease mu. Sɛ radio anaa tiviɛn ɛreyɛ nea ɛbɛtwe wo nne ase a, to.

**Nantew.** Ma adwuma no nkɔ kɔnhyɛ anaa mpaboa a ɔtena so, na ɛnyɛ sɛ ɔbɛkɔ. Hyɛ nsuo ne mpaboa ahyɛ baabi a ɔnam so bɛtumi atwe. Hwɛ sɛ tabɛl no ne ɔtena no bɔ ho bo.

**Kasa.** Ma nneyɛe a wɔde ahunu anaa nsɛnkyerɛnne ka ho. Bo sɛ woreyɛ adwuma no nkyɛn na wo ne ɔno ka nea mohyɛ ase ho asɛm. Nkɔm anaa nsɛnkyerɛnne nso yɛ adwuma wɔ nhyiamu no mu.

## Sɛ ɔpanyin no ne dementia wɔ no

Nneɛma bebree a yɛakyerɛkyerɛ yi wɔ de nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia so. Nsakrae kakra boa paa: ma baako baako, ma ɔpaw ntam mmienu, de nneɛma a ɔnim ne nnwom, na mfa ho sɛ adwuma no betena bere pii anaa ɛbɛsesa wɔ mfiase. Sɛ abakɔsɛm bi kɔ baabi a wɔnsuro no a, di so anigye na bisa ho ahoɔfɛ: "Ɛte sɛ ɛhyɛɛ wo den."

Alzheimer's Association [kratafa a ɛfa nneɛma ho](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) kyerɛ sɛ wobu nipa no nneyɛe ho ntease, di nneɛma a wɔapɛ daa so, na sesa wɔn sɛnea ɛho hia. NHS [kratafa a ɛfa nneɛma ho](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ka sɛ adwuma a nnipa abien yɛ ne ho betumi ama wɔn nyinaa anigye kɛse na ɛboa wɔn ma wɔdi bere no so.

Sɛ wopɛ nneɛma foforɔ, hwɛ [nneɛma ma nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia](/activities/ideas/) ne [kɔnnɛkyɛn a ɛfa dementia nneɛma ho](/dementia-activities/).

## Ahobammɔ nkyerɛaseɛ

- Kora nneɛma ketewa, te sɛ bɔtan, mpaboa, aba ne nsɛnsɛn, wɔ mpaboa anaa nkyɛn a wobetumi akata. Yi wɔn fi hɔ bere a woregyae.
- Kora nkyɛn ne akɛseɛ wɔ akyirikyiri, gye sɛ wo ne nipa no tena ntam na adwuma no hia wɔn.
- Fa nneɛma a wobetumi ahohoro na ɛnnyɛ nnubɔne. Hwɛ sɛ nipa no nni ayaresa ansa na wɔde nnuhuo, nkɔkɔsɛ anaa nneɛma a ɛwɔ nhwɛanim.
- Kora nsuo nkorɔfo fi pɛpa no ho, na mma wɔnnwene nsuo ɔtwa sɛ ɛyɛ nsuo a wɔnom.
- Sɛ nipa no bɛyɛ ɔwɔ ɔhaw a, gyae, de tee ma no, na sesa adwuma no. [Nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛfa bere a wɔka sɛ daabi](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) wɔ nsɛm pii ho.

## Sɛ ɛnyini yiye

Sesa biribi biako: adwuma no, bere a wɔyɛ, tabɛl no, bere a ɛbɛtena anaa nneɛma a wɔpaw ma no. Sɛ nipa no toa so se daabi a, mfa mmɔ no ano. Mmom, tena wɔ dan no mu ne wo ne ɔno, radio no ngyina, nso ɛyɛ bere a mo nyinaa de ma wo ho. San sɔ hwɛ da foforo. Nnipa pii san ba adwuma a wɔpoo no da a atwam no.

## Wo nso ara

Wo ne adwuma no mu. Nhyiamu ketewa a mo nyinaa anigye wɔ mu yɛ nea ɛboa sen ɔberɛ tenten a ɛma mo nyinaa ayɛ ɔhoɔden a ɛbrɛ. Ma nsɛnkyerɛnne no nyɛ kɛse: nnwom baako, mfonini baako, nkataa baako a wɔapɛ. Ɛyɛ yie sɛ wobɛgyae wɔ hɔ.
