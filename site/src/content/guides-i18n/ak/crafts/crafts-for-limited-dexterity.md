Nsa ahoɔden, ahoɔden ne ntease pɛpɛɛpɛ betumi asesa wɔ nneɛma pii nti. Arthritis, yare a ɛba foforo, akyiwadeɛ, bere tenten a wɔda, ne nkɔsoɔ a ɛba onipa so nso betumi ama peni, nkyene anaa pɛpɛ kakraa a ɛyɛ den sɛ wɔbɛfa. Adwuma bi betumi aka ho anigye, na nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ yie no hia sen adwuma ankasa. Saa kratafa yi kyerɛ nkɔso a ɛboa adwuma pii, ne nneɛma a wɔapaw wɔ saa beaeɛ yi, ɛfiri sɛ ɛnhwɛ ntease pɛpɛɛpɛ kakraa.

Saa afotu yi yɛ ma nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wobɛyɛ biribi, nanso nsa adwuma bi yɛ wɔn den. Ɛnyɛ nsɔhwɛ, ɛnyɛ ayaresa afotu. Sɛ tool bi ma wo nsa yaw a, gyae. Dokota, physiotherapist anaa occupational therapist betumi ahyɛ nsa ayaresa ne nneɛma ho nkyerɛkyerɛ.

## Nhyehyɛe bɛn na ɛboa paa?

Nsakrae kɛse bi fi sɛdeɛ wɔhyehyɛ nneɛma no ho, na ɛnyɛ adwuma ankasa.

- **Hyɛ paper ho tape.** Fa masking tape to asɛm no mpɔtam so, ama ɛntena mu a ɔnsɛ nsa foforo.
- **Fa non-slip mat anaa nsuo a ɛnyɛ den** ase wɔ adwuma no ase, ama board anaa tray no ntwe.
- **Fa tray a ɛwɔ mpɔtam a ɛsɔre.** Nneɛma no ntena tray mu, na onipa no betumi afa no wɔ baabi baako.
- **Hyɛ nneɛma nyinaa wɔ hɔ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw.** Hyɛ nneɛma no wɔ onipa no anim, wɔ ne nsa a ɛyɛ den fam, ama ɛnsɛ sɛ ɔtrɛw ne nsa kɔ akyiri.
- **Twa ne hyehyɛ ansa.** Twa, pae na hyehyɛ nneɛma no ansa na onipa no atena ase, ama adwuma no ne ayɛ, na ɛnyɛ nhyehyɛe.

## Sɛnea wobetumi ayɛ nneɛma ayɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛkyere?

- **Ma handle no ayɛ kɛse.** Kata foam tape ho wɔ ɔpen kakraa, anaa brush, anaa ɔpen, anaa fa foam tube kata so. Handle a ɛyɛ kɛse no hia sɛ wɔkyere den kakraa.
- **Fa nneɛma kɛse.** Jumbo crayon, marker a ɛyɛ kɛse ne brush kɛse a handle no yɛ tenten na ɛtrɛw no yɛ mmerɛw sen nneɛma a ɛyɛ kakraa.
- **Sɔ palm grip hwɛ.** Obi betumi ahu sɛ nneɛma a wɔkyere wɔ nsa nyinaa mu yɛ mmerɛw sen nneɛma a wɔkyere ntam. Sɔ hwɛ ansa na woreyɛ adwuma.
- **Fa nneɛma foforo.** Glue stick betumi ayɛ mmerɛw sen glue a wɔde wɔ bottle mu. Sponge anaa roller betumi ayɛ mmerɛw sen brush. Stamp betumi ayɛ mmerɛw sen sɛ wobɛtɔ.
- **Fa tape ma ɛnyɛ nkɔtɔ.** Tape anaa clip betumi akyere ribbon, kaad anaa mpɔtam bi wɔ beaeɛ so, nsa baako nko na ɛrehwehwɛ.

[Arthritis Foundation](https://www.arthritis.org/partnership/ease-of-use) wɔ Ease of Use program a ɛma wɔsɔ hwɛ nneɛma a wɔde di da biara sɛ ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛde di dwuma, ebinom mu ne joint protection nneɛma ne nneɛma a wɔbɛbue ntɛm. Wɔn nsɛnkyerɛnne yɛ baabi a ɛboa sɛ wobɛhyɛ ase bere a wotɔ nneɛma, nanso ɛnsakra sɛ wobɛhwɛ nneɛma no ne onipa no nkyɛn ansa.

## Adwuma bɛn na ɛhwehwɛ ntease pɛpɛɛpɛ kakraa?

Paw adwuma a ɛwɔ nneɛma kɛse, palm grip anaa ɔkwan baako a ɛyɛ pefee. Saa kaad yi wɔ saa beaeɛ yi fata nsa ahoɔden a ɛnni so, na biara wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw:

- [Tear-paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/). Wɔntwa paper no, wɔde nsa ntwitwa, enni scissors. Kaad no wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ne ɔkwan a wobetumi ayɛ wɔ mpaboa so.
- [Arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/). Wɔtwa nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn a wɔde hyɛ abɔ kɛse a ɛyɛ den mu. Kaad no wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ne ɔkwan a wobetumi ayɛ wɔ mpaboa so.
- [Tap a rhythm together](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/). Wɔbɛtow nsa nyinaa so wɔ table anaa tray so, enni tool.
- [Make a texture board](/activity/make-a-texture-board/). Wɔde nkataa ne paper ahyɛ kaad so, na nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛda so pefee wɔ hɔ.
- [Make a greeting card](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/). Wɔbɛyɛ kaad no ansa, na wɔde mfonini baako ahyɛ so, enti adwuma no ne kyerɛwde no nko.
- [Make a sealed shaker](/activity/make-a-sealed-shaker/). Obi boa kata kɔfi no, enti onipa no de nneɛma ahyɛ mu na ɔde ahyehyɛ.
- [Make a lavender sachet](/activity/make-a-lavender-sachet/). Ɛnhia sɛ wobɛdoa, na kaad no wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ne ɔkwan a wobetumi ayɛ wɔ mpaboa so.

Kaad bi wɔ nneɛma a ɛyɛ kakraa, te sɛ bɔtɔn anaa mpuntuo. [Sort buttons by color kaad](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/) de bɔtɔn kɛse di dwuma wɔ tray mu, na ɛyɛ ma nnipa akɛse ne mmofra akɛse nko, ɛfiri sɛ bɔtɔn bɛyɛ ɔhaw ma mmofra a wɔn mfe nni mmiɛnsa.

## Nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wobetumi asɔ hwɛ?

- **Ɔte anaa wɔ mpa so.** Kaad pii wɔ ɔkwan a wobetumi ayɛ wɔ ɔte anaa wɔ mpa so. Fa tray no to beaeɛ a ɛyɛ den a ɛnsɛ sɛ wobɔ mu.
- **Ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛkyere.** Fa tool a wɔakata ho ne nneɛma a ɛyɛ tenten a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ. Paw abɔ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛbue, anaa abɔ a wɔbɛma ayɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛtwa.
- **Nsa baako.** Fa tape, clamp anaa non-slip mat, ne tray a ɛwɔ mpɔtam a ɛsɔre. Brush a wɔde ayɛ ansa anaa glue stick a wɔde adwene ayɛ ansa boa.
- **Nneɛma kakraa a wɔbɛpaw.** Ma mmienu, anaa nneɛma mmienu, na ma onipa no ne ho nhwɛ ne ho nkɔ.
- **Bere tiaa.** Ma ɔbɛtwa baako, ɔnkɔnsɔn baako anaa kaad baako. Adwuma tiaa a ɛba ayie yɛ adwuma pa.
- **Ɛnyɛ nneɛma pii.** Yɛ adwuma wɔ tray a ɛwɔ kɔtɔn ase. Paw adwuma a ɛnni nsuo, te sɛ ntwitwa anaa nhyehyɛe.

## Nsɛm a ɛfa ahobammɔ ho

- Fa nneɛma kakraa, te sɛ bɔtɔn, mpuntuo ne aba, wɔ tray mu. Mmofra a wɔn mfe nni mmiɛnsa betumi aguaguaa so.
- Fa scissors a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ anaa nneɛma a wɔatwa dedaw. Craft knife yɛ ma nnipa akɛse nko.
- Fa paint ne glue a ɛnni toxic, na wɔawɔ wɔn mu nkyerɛwde. [Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety) kyerɛkyerɛ AP seal a ɛwɔ art materials so.
- Hwɛ sɛ grip aid, te sɛ foam tape, nnyina, nnkɔ nsa mu anaa ɛnhyɛ ase.
- Gyae sɛ onipa no yaw, nsa ayɛ nkɔmmɔ, anaa ɔnhyɛ ase bere a wɔreyɛ adwuma, na kasa kyerɛ dokota anaa physiotherapist.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie

- **Tool no yaw wo nsa.** Gyae, na sɔ tool a ɛyɛ kɛse anaa a ɛyɛ mmerɛw. Anaa paw adwuma foforo.
- **Paper no kɔ so twam.** Fa tape bio, anaa clamp abɔ no ho wɔ table no so.
- **Nneɛma no kɔ fam.** Fa tray a ɛwɔ mpɔtam a ɛsɔre, anaa fa nneɛma kakraa.
- **Onipa no ayɛ abufuw.** Gyae bere a ɔtwa baako. Adwuma no betumi aba awieɛ da foforo, anaa wobetumi agyaw.
- **Ɛnyɛ yie koraa.** Paw adwuma a ɛnni nsa ahoɔden kɛse, te sɛ ntoto nnyigyei anaa ahwɛ window bi ho wɔ ho.

## Adwuma wɔ ɔkɛse anaa onipa a ɔwɔ dementia ho yare

Nkɔso a ɛwɔ ngyinaeɛ a atwa ho yi bi fata ɔkɛse, nanso nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia ho yare, a nsa ahoɔden nso yɛ wɔn den. Fa adwuma no nyinaa to nkyekyɛ ketewa mu, na ma onipa no yɛ nea ɔbetumi ayɛ, te sɛ paw ne nsusuwii anaa dwane mpɔtam bi. Wo ankasa yɛ nneɛma a ɛyɛ den, na ma onipa no nso ahwɛ sɛ ɔpɛ sɛ ɔbɛka adwuma no mu kakra anaa.

Creative Activities nyɛ ayaresa, na nkɔso yi nyɛ ayaresa. Ɛma adwuma yɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛsɔ hwɛ, na onipa betumi agyae bere nyinaa.
