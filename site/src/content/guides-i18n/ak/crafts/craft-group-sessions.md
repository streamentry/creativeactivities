Nnwuma nkitahodie a wɔyɛ ma nnipa pii bɛma nnipa abɛboa ɛberɛ a wɔatena pono ho, sɛ pono no wɔ buukuo, ahoɔfɛ fie, klabu, sukuu anaa abusua nhyiam wɔ fie kwan so. Ɛho hia sɛ wɔwɔ ɔbɔadeyɛfo a ɔyɛ adwuma yie anaa nneɛma a ɛyɛ adwuma. Ɛho hia sɛ wɔwɔ nhyehyɛe a ɛyɛ nea wɔahu, ɛyɛ tenten, na obiara tumi aka mu, ne ɔkyerɛkyerɛfo a ɔma nnipa hu sɛ wɔn biara yɛ adwuma a ɛho hia. Saa asɛm yi kyerɛ sɛdeɛ wobɛhyehyɛ ne sɛdeɛ wobɛyɛ nnwuma nkitahodie a ɛte saa, ne sɛdeɛ wobɛsesa nnwuma biako ma nnipa a wɔwɔ nneɛma a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ so.

Nhyiam a ɛte saa boa buukuo a wɔyɛ pono a wɔbɛyɛ nnwuma nkitahodie wɔ bosome biara, ahoɔfɛ fie nnipa a wɔhwɛ wɔn so, ɔboafoyɛ wɔ klabu mu, sukuufo a wɔwɔ nnipa a wɔyɛ nneyɛe nyinaa, ne abusua a wɔbɛboaboa ɛberɛ a wɔbɛhyiam. Ɛyɛ ma nnipa a wɔpɛ akyɛdeɛ ne nkitahodie, ne ma ɔkyerɛkyerɛfo a wɔboa wɔn. Nhwehwɛmu a wɔde aka ho yi kyerɛ nneɛma a ɛma nhyiam yɛ adwuma yie, na ɛnyɛ atosɛm ho ntease wɔ obiara ho wɔ nhyiam no mu.

## Dɛn na ɛma nnwuma nkitahodie yɛ adwuma wɔ nnipa pii mu?

Nnwuma nkitahodie a ɛyɛ adwuma yie yɛ nea wɔahu, ɛyɛ tenten, obiara tumi aka mu wɔ ne tumi so, na ɛnnhyɛ nnipa so. Ɛkyerɛ sɛ:

- **Nea wɔahu ye sen nea wɔnnim.** Yi nnwuma a nnipa no nim dada, anaa nnwuma a ɛde nneɛma a wɔhu sɛ ɛyɛ wɔn dwumadi, te sɛ mpapa, lɛn anaa mfonini ade a wɔda wɔ wɔn nkyɛn.
- **Tenten ye sen tenenee.** Hyehyɛ bere a nnipa no tumi de ahyɛ adwene so. Ma ɛwɔ hɔ ɛberɛ a wɔbɛtra ase kakra wɔ mfitiase ne awiei mu.
- **Nneyɛe ye sen hwehwɛ.** Ma obiara nkɔ ne nsa mu, na ɔnhwɛ anaa ɔnhwɛ ade, bɛhyɛ ade ase, tua ade, yi ade, anaa ka asɛm.
- **Nneyɛe pii, adwuma biako.** Hyehyɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ ɛdedaw ne ɔkwan a ɛyɛ den a nnipa bɛtumi de aka mu, na obiara ntwe ase.
- **Obi nni nkonim, na mmuae nni hɔ.** Nnwuma a nnipa bɛpere ɔkwan so de ahoɔfɛ ma obiara bi a ɔbɛhu sɛ waware. Nnwuma a wɔbɛyɛ bom no yi ɔhaw yi afi hɔ.

Alzheimer's Association [nhyehyɛe a wɔde hwɛ dementia so](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/1a0020aa-0bfb-47ac-a545-1dd8a3ee1444/alzheimers-dementia-care-practice-recommendations.pdf) yɛ mmoaso a ɛboa akwankyerɛfo a wɔyɛ adwuma wɔ ahoɔfɛ mmeae.

## Nnipa dodow bɛn na ɛyɛ mmerɛw?

Nea ɛyɛ mmerɛw ne ɛkakra. Nnipa anan kosi mmienu, ɔkyerɛkyerɛfo baako ne aboafo baako ma pɔ biara, yɛ mfitiase a ɛyɛ. Obi a ɔhu sɛ nnipa kɛse nhyiam yɛ no den no, ebia ɔbɛtumi aka mu yiye wɔ nhyiam a nnipa yɛ kakra mu.

Sɛ wobɛhwehwɛ nea ɛyɛ: obiara tumi hu ɔkyerɛkyerɛfo no anim, tie no yie, na aboafo tumi abɛn no wɔ nkyɛn mmere kakra mu? Sɛ ɛnte saa a, kyekyɛ nnipa no ano, fa aboafo ka ho, anaa kɔ dan a ɛyɛ ketewa mu. Sɛ ɛyɛ nhyiam kɛse a, fa aboafo nkɔ nnipa no mu, na ɛnyɛ wɔ mpɔtam so.

## Dɛn na wobɛhyehyɛ dan no yie?

- **Akyiri anaa ɔsɔrɛ.** Nnipa no tena ase fɛn ketewa a wɔn nyinaa tumi de nneɛma bɔ mmoa, na ɔkyerɛkyerɛfo no wɔ nkyɛn anaa wɔ ɔsɔrɛ no mu.
- **Ɔkyerɛkyerɛfo bɛn wɔ, nna ne ani hɔ.** Fa wo ho to fam sɛ nea ɛyɛ, sɛ wotumi yɛ saa.
- **Tie ne hu nni kwan so kɔsi.** Ɛnye nnipa a wɔn tie yɛ den no nkyɛn wɔ ntwa a ɛyɛ den no ano. Hwɛ sɛ ahuampa ne nkwansrafo ahyɛn wɔn ho.
- **Ɔwɔ ɛpono a ɛyɛ mmerɛw.** Ma nnipa no nni bɔ ahyɛ ntwa anaa mpɔ mmeae a ɛyɛ den a nneɛma no bɛhyɛ wɔn ho.
- **Fa nnyɛ ahoɔfɛ anaa dwom.** Gyae ahuampa anaa radio, na ma nneɛma a ɛdɔ ntwa nyɛ mmerɛw.
- **Ɔkasa baako nkyɛn.** Ɛyɛ den ma nnipa pii nteɛ nneɛma a wɔka ɛberɛ a nnipa bɛka bom, enti ɔkyerɛkyerɛfo no tumi kɔ nnipa baako ka ne ho.
- **Mpɔtam a ɛyɛ pefee ne abɔfra a ɛyɛ dwodwo.** Ma nnipa a wopɛ sɛ wofiri nhyiam no tumi kɔ ho yie. Fa ɔsɔrɛ a ɛdɛn no ne aboafo bi a ɔbɛtumi akɔ ne nkyɛn.

## Nnwuma nkitahodie a ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ nnipa pii mu

Nnwuma yi wɔ mmuae a nnipa nyinaa bɛka bom, enti obiara de bi ka ho. Nkrataa a ɛwɔ hɔ yi wɔ anammɔn ahyehyɛde ne nneɛma a ɛboa ma ɛbɛyɛ:

- [Pen mfonini a nnipa nyinaa bɛka bom](/activity/paint-a-shared-picture/). Mfonini kɛse biako a wɔde ahyɛ mpapa so a wɔde tɛp ahyɛ pono no so, na obiara de bi ka ho wɔ ne ntam. Ɛfata nnipa kakraa ne nnipa a mmofra ne akɛseɛ wɔ hɔ.
- [Yɛ mpapa abɔdeɛ a wɔde ahyɛ abɔ](/activity/make-a-paper-chain-garland/). Obiara yɛ mpapa abɔdeɛ, na nnipa nyinaa de hyɛ wɔ dan no mu. Ɛyɛ yie ma nnipa pii a wɔbɛyɛ bere koro.
- [Mpapa a wɔbubu ayɛ mfonini](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/). Obiara bubu mpapa a ɔpɛ, na ɔde agu ntoboa biako so. Wɔnhwehwɛ nnwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw.
- [Yɛ abusua dua a nsa ayɛ](/activity/make-a-family-tree-of-hands/). Obiara de ɔpene ka ne nsa ho wɔ dua a wɔde tu ahyɛ so. Mframa biara nni ɔhɔho so, enti ɛfata nnipa nyinaa.
- [Kyerɛ nhaban ne nnwiinwii](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/). Nnipa a wɔbɛboaboa tumi bɛboaboa nhaban ne nnwiinwii, sie wɔn, na wɔayɛ kaad afi mu.
- [Yɛ wo ara dwom nhoma](/activity/make-a-personal-songbook/). Obiara ka dwom a ɔdɔ ne asɛm a ɛfa so. Ɛboa asɛm kɛse ne nnwuma.
- [Bɔ nkɔaa ɔkyɛnkyɛn bom](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/). Obiara bɔ ne nsa, de di dwom a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, na wɔsesa nnipa a wɔdi kan.

## Dɛn na wobɛsesa nnwuma biako ma nnipa a wɔwɔ nneɛma a ɛyɛ nneɛma a ɛsɛ sɛ wohwɛ so?

Hyehyɛ ɔkwan a ɛboro mmienu ma nnwuma biara a obiara bɛka bom. Nkyerɛaseɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ yi kyerɛ sɛdeɛ mpapa abɔdeɛ a wɔde ahyɛ nnipa nyinaa bɛyɛ adwuma wɔ ɔkwan mmiɛnsa so:

| Ɔkwan | Nea ɔnipa no yɛ | Nea ɔboafo no yɛ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ɔtumi di ne ho so kɛse | Ɔpaw ɔhoɔfɛ na ɔpene ɔfa bi | Ɔma pen ne nneɛma no bɛn ne nsa, na ɔka mfonini no ho asɛm |
| Ɔhia boafo kakra | Ɔbɔ nsuo a ɔboa ayɛ ne akyɛ dwoowa, a ɔboafo no paw no ɔhoɔfɛ | Ɔkyerɛ dwoowa no kwan, na ɔtra mpapa no so |
| Ɔhia boafo kɛse | Ɔpaw ɔhoɔfɛ abien mu, anaa ɔhwɛ na ɔka ho asɛm | Ɔkyen pen anaa dwoowa, na ɔpene ɛfa no ho wɔ ne ho ne no |

Saa ara nso na ɛyɛ adwuma wɔ mpapa abɔdeɛ mu (yɛ abɔdeɛ nyinaa, de abɔdeɛ baako ka ho, anaa hwɛ abɔdeɛ no ɛreyɛ dwoodwoo), mpapa a wɔbubu ayɛ mfonini mu (bubu ntoboa, de ntoboa baako si so, anaa paw ɔhoɔfɛ) ne dwom nhoma mu (kyerɛw asɛm, ka asɛm, anaa kyerɛ baako).

## Dɛn na akwankyerɛfo kasa de ma nnipa pii?

- Ka wo din wɔ nhyiam biara mu, na frɛ obiara wɔ din a ɔpɛ.
- Kasa ntɛmntɛm, ma nkyerɛ ɛnyɛ anammɔn baako, na kyerɛ ade ansa na wobɛka.
- Bisa nnipa biara ma ɔka ne nkɔso, te sɛ "Ka me ho asɛm wɔ eyi ho," anaa paw kakra. Mma mmisa nsɛm te sɛ "Wo kae?"
- Mma nnipa ano nnyɛ ɔkwan a ɛnteɛ. Ma obiara nyinaa ayɛ ne tumi nnwuma ayɛ ahyɛde a ɛyɛ pɛ.
- Yɛ nhyiam a wɔde bɛhyɛ nkyɛ ne awiei no ahyɛde pɛpɛɛpɛ, na ɛnyɛ nea wɔsesa ho, ma ɛnyɛ nea wɔahu.

Yɛn [nkitahodie a ɛfa nnwuma a nnipa pii yɛ ho akwankyerɛ](/activities/group-art-activities/) wɔ ade pii a ɛfa nhyiam a wɔyɛ nnwuma wɔ nnipa a wɔwɔ nneɛma a ɛyɛ nneɛma ho.

## Dɛn na wobɛyɛ awiei yie?

Ɛyɛ papa sɛ wɔsiesie nhyiam no. Hwɛ nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛkyerɛ sɛ obi abrɛ, ɔnni ahome anaa ɔabɔ mmoden. Ma aboafo nhyɛ nnipa no ahyɛde sɛ wɔnkɔ nantew anaa sɛ wɔntena fɛn ketewa bi. Ma nnipa no nkɔ a wɔn ani nnye, na wɔnka asɛm biara. Obi a ɔdaa ɛberɛ a nhyiam no reyɛ no, ɛnyɛ ɔkwan bi a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ so, ɛnam so ɔrehyɛ ɔdɔ.

Ma nhyiam biara awiei nkɔ ɔkwan koro, de dwom a wɔn nyinaa ani gye anaa asɛm a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ka ho, sɛdeɛ awiei no bɛyɛ komm na ɛbɛyɛ nea wɔrehwɛ kwan. Akyiri yi, kyerɛw nea ɛyɛ adwuma ne nea obiara ani gyee. Nsɛm yi ma nhyiam a ɛbɛba akyiri no yɛ yie. Kora adwuma a wɔawie no nyinaa de kɔ ɔno a ɔyɛe no nkyɛn, na bisa ne nkɔso ansa na wobɛda so.

## Sɛdeɛ wobɛyɛ wɔ afiase a wɔhwɛ nnipa so anaa wɔ ɛbɛ a wɔhwɛ nnipa a wɔn adwene nni yie so

Wɔ afiase a wɔhwɛ nnipa so anaa ɛbɛ a wɔhwɛ nnipa a wɔn adwene nni yie so, ɛkwan koro nso yɛ adwuma, nanso nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw bi ka ho. Paw nnwuma a ɛde tirim, nneɛma a wɔhu wɔ mmere a wɔatena ase no mu. NHS [nkratɔkyerɛ a ɛfa nnwuma ho](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) kyerɛ sɛ nnwuma dodow no sɛ wɔbɛyɛ ɛdedaw, na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔbɛhwɛ tirim, ahunu, nne ne ahuhuo wɔ mmere a ɛda so. Ɛberɛ kakra a wobɛka ne ɔno nkyɛn wɔ nhyiam mu anaa akyiri yi, ebia ɛboro nhyiam no ara ma ɔbɔ. Bisa abusua sɛ nea obiara ani gyee, na fa ne nkɔso no yɛ wɔn ahyehyɛde.
