Adwuma a wɔsan yɛ fi nneɛma a ɛwɔ fie biara mu: bɔkɔ a wɔde di aduane, atoko kɔkɔɔ, mpɔtam bɔkɔ, yogɔt bɔkɔ, tin, ne ntama a wɔtwa. Ɛnna sika biara, ɛyɛ nea ɛbɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛhwehwɛ, na sɛ wode bɛyɛ ade a ɛyɛ mfaso, ɛbɛma wo ani agye sen sɛ wobɛtɔ nneɛma a wɔde ayɛ adwuma a wɔayɛ dedaw. Saa kratafa yi kyerɛkyerɛ nneɛma a ɛyɛ yie, sɛnea wobɛhyɛ nneɛma no ho ho ahoɔfɛ, ne sɛnea wobɛsesa adwuma biara.

Saa adwuma yi yɛ fie a nneɛma kakra wɔ hɔ, sukuu, abusuafo a wɔwɔ mmofra akɛse, anaa beaeɛ a wɔboa nnipa na nneɛma no bɛba da biara da. Ɛsɛ de nso ma nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔka nneɛma ho, anaa nnipa a wɔpɛ nneɛma a wɔwɔ nsɛmfua ho. Nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛwɔ ha kyerɛ adwuma biara ne nneɛfo a ɛho hia. Ɛnyɛ nkɔmhyɛ fa obi ho.

## Hyɛ w'nneɛma no ho ho kan

Nneɛma no ho ho ye nneɛma a ɛfa ahoɔfɛ ho dodow, na ɛsɛ sɛ wɔyɛ saa adwuma yi ansa na ɔno ara ada.

1. **Hohoro nneɛma biara nsuo ne sini.** Fa nsuo ahunu ne sini a ɛyɛ hyew mu, hohoro nsuo ano yie, na ma ɛnwie pɛpɛɛpɛ. Nneɛma a nsuo ka no, ebia ɛbɛyɛ hwen wɔ mu wɔ adwuma a wɔakata no so.
2. **Hwɛ nea ɛde ogyaa.** Nnye nneɛma a wɔde gu nnuro, aduaba, nsuo a ɛyɛ yaw, aboa aduane anaa biribiara a wunnwie yie.
3. **Yi tin ne mpɔtam a ɛyɛ den.** Hwɛ tin ne mpɔtam. Fa tiri, fa tape kata so, anaa yi nneɛma no mu.
4. **Ɔkata ansa na wɔde di dwuma.** Twa bɔkɔ, twa nkɔnkɔn, ne twa ahɔhɔ wɔ ne ho ka ho. Kata akɛse no kɔ fam, sɛ ɛbɛwɔ akyiri sɛ wobɛde beaeɛ a ɛyɛ den no nsa, na wɔde kɔ hɔ bere a wodi no akyi.
5. **Hyɛ nneɛma ketewa wɔ aseɛ.** Bɔtɔn, aduaba ne nkɔnkɔn a ɛyɛ mpɔn no ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde kɔ sɛ wɔbɛhyɛ wɔ ɔsɛnkyekyɛ a ɛwɔ ne mpuntuo. Mma wɔnhyɛ no wɔ tebɔl so nkoa.

## Nneɛma bɛn na ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛhyɛ ase?

### Atoko kɔkɔɔ nkɔkɔ

Twa atoko kɔkɔɔ no mu nkɔkɔ, ma ntaban ketewa nka mu. Hyɛ peinti, ma ɛnwie, afei twa nsɛnkyɛnkyɛn bi na fa ɔfa tiri anaa dua ketewa bi nsɔ ho. Nkɔkɔ bi a wɔde ka ɔdan mu bɛyɛ tebɔl a ɛhyerɛn. Nkɔkɔ a wɔatwa kan no yɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔbɛkyere sen atoko kɔkɔɔ a ɛyɛ nyinaa.

### Bɔkɔ a wɔde yɛ ntoma ɔdan

Twa ɛdan ketewa no wɔ bɔkɔ a wɔde yɛ nneɛma no mfimfini, fa ntoma anaa kaad bi nsɔ ne akyi, na fa kaad ntakra bi nkyekyɛ ama ɛnyɛ nkɔnsɔnkɔnsɔn. Nhwɛso a ɛwɔ [decorate a keepsake box card](/activity/decorate-a-keepsake-box/) kyerɛ sɛnea wobɛfa nneɛma yi di dwuma sɛ wobɛkata bokɔ so ne ntoma.

### Yogɔt bɔkɔ a wɔde yɛ ɛpɔtam

Hohoro bɔkɔ no, ma ɛnyɛ ɛpɔtam a wɔde nsoma anaa nsɛpɔ twa nsuo a ɛbɛfiri mu (obi kɛse nko ara na ɔbɛyɛ), kata ne fam peinti anaa ntoma, afei fa aboɔde ne ɔdua bi ka ho. [Kaad a wɔde yɛ ɛpɔtam no](/activity/decorate-a-plant-pot/) kyerɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ no ne ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛdi so.

### Nneɛma a wɔde yɛ nkonkon

Fa nsuo ɛkɔ nneɛma a ɛyɛ ketewa a wɔahohoro, a ɛyɛ plastik, mu na fa ɛmo anaa nkrato a wɔayɛ kɔ mu, twa ne kata so ne ntɛ anaa tape a ɛyɛ den, afei kata. Obi a ɔboa wo ne nea ɔbɛto nneɛma no kata. Kaad [sealed shaker](/activity/make-a-sealed-shaker/) no kyerɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ ne ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ma bere a ɛyɛ dwodwo.

### Bɔtɔn mfonini

Kyekyɛ bɔtɔn no sɛnea ɛyɛ bɔne anaa kɛse, na fa ɛkɔ kaad so sɛ wobɛyɛ mfonini a ɛyɛ mmerɛw. Bɔtɔn yɛ nneɛma a ɛde mmofra a wɔn afe yɛ abiɛsa ase kɔ ahoɔfɛ ase, enti saa adwuma yi yɛ ma akɛse ne mmofra a wɔyɛ kɛse nko ara. [Kaad mfonini a wɔde bɔtɔn yɛ no](/activity/make-a-button-picture/) wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw nyinaa.

### Ntama ɔdan a wɔde ka ho

Fa ntama, tiri, ne mpapahwɛ a wɔatwa ka kaad a ɛyɛ den so. Ɛnsɛ sɛ wɔtwa ho kwan, na ɛboa sɛ wɔde nneɛma a wɔatwa di dwuma yiye. Hwɛ [texture board card](/activity/make-a-texture-board/).

## Ɛdɛn na wobɛtumi asɔ hwɛ nneɛma a ɛyɛ mmerɛw?

- **Ɛtena ase.** Saa adwuma yi nyinaa yɛ ade wɔ tebɔl so. Hyɛ nneɛma no wɔ ɔkwan a ɔbɛtumi atua so, na ɔnhia sɛ ɔsɔre.
- **Ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛkyere.** Fa nneɛma a ɛyɛ kɛse, a ɛyɛ hare nanso ɛnyɛ den, na kata nsɛnkyɛnkyɛn a ɛyɛ kakra anaa tape anaa ntɛ so sɛ wɔbɛyɛ den. Yi ɔsɛnkyekyɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔbɛbɔ.
- **Nsa baako nko.** Fa nneɛma no kata tebɔl so, anaa hyɛ wɔ tray a ɛwɔ ntɛ so, na ɛbɛtena ase.
- **Ahuhu ahuhuhuhu.** Yi nneɛma a ɛwɔ nkyerɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw, na fa peinti a ɛyɛ nea ɛyɛ dɔɔ ɛkata nneɛma no so. Hyɛ adwuma no wɔ dade a ɛyɛ hyew na ɛnyɛ den so.
- **Nneɛma a ɛkakra.** Yi adwuma a ɛwɔ nneɛma baako anaa mmienu. Ɛpɔtam baako a wɔakata anaa mfonini baako yɛ adwuma a ɛma wotumi ka sɛ ɛyɛ baabi a wɔasa.
- **Ɛbɛyɛ kakra.** Fa peinti nneɛma no kan na ma wɔtena ase, sɛnea ɔbɛde ntoma anaa kata nko ara.

## Akwankyerɛ a ɛfa ahoɔfɛ ho

- Nnye ahonyadeɛ, na nnye tin a ɛwɔ nkyɛn anaa a wɔatwa.
- Ma akɛse nko ara nni kaad a wɔde twa, na de bɔɔdi a ɛyɛ den so ɔsɛnkyekyɛ a ɛyɛ den hyɛ ɛkwan a nnipa nsa nkɔ so.
- Ɛsɛ sɛ nneɛma a ɛde nneɛma ketewa wɔ mu nyinaa hyɛ ahohohahɔ. Fa ntɛ a ɛyɛ den anaa tape a ɛyɛ den kata so, na hwɛ sɛ ɛntumi nnue.
- Kɔ fa bɔtɔn, aduaba, nkɔnkɔn ne nkɔkɔ ne nsɔ a wɔagye ato mu nkɔ akyi. Mmofra a wɔn afe yɛ abiɛsa ase nso ntumi nnye ho. [Nkyerɛwde a HealthyChildren.org de kyerɛ sɛnea wobɛkɔ akyiri nkɔnkɔn ho](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/choking-prevention.aspx) kyerɛ sɛ mpuntuo bɔl, kyɛr ntakra ne bateri a ɛyɛ kakra nso wɔ nneɛma a ɛde nkɔnkɔn ba mu.
- Fa peinti ne ntɛ a wɔaka sɛ ɛnyɛ aduane. [Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety) kyerɛ sɛ AP seal yɛ nkyerɛwde a ɛkyerɛ sɛ adwuma nneɛma no ho nhia, na akɛse a wɔnkenkan nkyerɛwde no ɛsɛ sɛ wɔde nneɛma a ɛnyɛ aduane di dwuma.
- Hwɛ sɛ ntɛ, aduro ne nneɛma a ɛfiri aduru mu nkɔ mu nkɔ no nni yare.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie

- **Nneɛma no bɔ nkɔ anaa ɔkɔ yɛ tebɔl so.** Hyɛ no wɔ tray mu, anaa kata ne ase kaad a ɛyɛ kɛse.
- **Ɛfã no nyɛ den.** Kata tape anaa rabaa nkɔnkɔn no so sɛ ɛbɛyɛ den, anaa yi nneɛma foforo.
- **Adwuma no yɛ kɛse.** Fa nneɛma ketewa, anaa yɛ ɛpɔtam baako na gyae.
- **Ahoɔfɛ anaa nsuo.** Sa nneɛma no bio ansa na woregyina so. Nnye nneɛma a ɛyɛ nsuo anaa a ɛwɔ ogyaa a ɛwɔ mu.

## Adwuma yi wɔ akɔnhwɛfo anaa mmofra ho

Adwuma a wɔsan yɛ ka abusuafo a wɔwɔ mmerɛ ahodoɔ. Akɛse, ɔnam so ɔyɛ ɔdwamfo a ɔwɔ dementia yare, ebia ɔbɛpɛ sɛ ɔkyekyɛ bɔtɔn, ɔpaw peinti a ɔbɛde ayɛ wɔ ho, anaa ɔbɛkata ɛpɔtam ne sɔfa. Ma no nkɔ adwuma a ɔka ho ɔyɛ ɛpɛ, na wo ankɔ kɔtwa mu. Sɛ ɛyɛ mmofra, ɛsɛ sɛ akɛse wɔ hɔ daa, de nneɛma a ɛfata wɔn mfe nko ara di dwuma, na hyɛ nneɛma ketewa wɔ mmofra a wɔn ho yɛ kakra no nkyɛn.

Nnipa binom hu sɛ nneɛma a wɔahu no, te sɛ kɔn a wɔfi aduan mu, yɛ nea ɛyɛ anigye pefee. Bisa ansa na wɔde di dwuma, na ma ɛnkɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛw.
