Nnipa pii kyerɛ sɛ wontumi nyɛ nkyerɛwee, nsɔhwɛ anaa nneɛma. Sɛ wɔka saa a, ɛtɔ so wɔrenhwɛ ntetee wɔ sukuu mu, anaa wɔsuro sɛ nea wɔayɛ bɛyɛ nea ɛkyerɛ sɛ wɔyɛ mmofra anaa ɛnyɛ yiye. Saa nhwɛso yi ma obiara a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔne obi a ɔwɔ ne ho ka ho bɛyɛ biribi, nanso onni adetɔ a ɔbɛkyerɛ. Adwuma a ɛwɔ so yi yɛ mmerɛw, fa nneɛma a ɛwɔ fie de ayɛ, na biribiara nni mmuae a ɛteɛ.

Nneɛma a ɛwɔ saa nhwɛso yi yɛ mmerɛw ma ɔbaa kɛse, nnamfo, obi a ɔhwɛ onipa, anaa ɔkwan mu boafo. Ɛfata nso ma nnipa a wɔwɔ ahoɔden kakra, nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔtena, anaa nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔde wɔn nsa ayɛ adwuma bere tiaa. Eyinom yɛ nhyehyɛe a ɛfa adwuma no mu ho, na ɛnyɛ atemmu wɔ nea obiara betumi ayɛ ho.

## Dɛn na wohia?

Adwuma ntetee pii no hia nneɛma a ɛwɔ fie kɛkɛ, ne nneɛma kakra a wobetumi anya fi dwadie mu:

- Ɛdan a wɔabɔ plastic kɔbɔ anaa ntama a aka so
- Pampa a ɛyɛ den anaa kaad a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ, sɛdeɛ kɔkɔ bɔkɔ, pleti a wɔde ayɛ paper, anaa pampa a wɔaprinte so
- Glue stick, a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sen liquid glue
- Watercolor nsuhwɛ kakra anaa dye a ɛyɛ dwuma wɔ aduane mu, brush kɛse a ɛyɛ nwini, ne nsuo kuruwa abien
- Mfonini a wɔfa afi magazine a wɔde kɔ dwadie, kaad a wɔde ma nnipa, anaa adidi nkɔsoɔ
- Nnua a wɔahwie anaa wɔapɛ, nhaban, anaa nkɔnsonsɔnsɔn afi aduaba mu

Sɛ wɔnni nneɛma biara a, [nhwɛso a ɛfa nneɛma a wɔde di adwuma](/activities/art-supplies-for-older-adults/) kyerɛ nneɛma du a ɛnnwe sika.

## Adwuma ɛhe na ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔbɛfi ase?

Adwuma yi biara wɔ anan a ɛda so pefee. Paw baako, na fa ne so de ayɛ adwuma no mu.

### Kɔlɔ collage

Paw kɔlɔ baako, na twitwa mfonini a ɛwɔ saa kɔlɔ no afi magazine mu. Twitwa yɛ mmerɛw sen ntwa, na ɛnhia nneɛma a ɛyɛ nsɛe. Gyigyi nkyɛn no wɔ kaad so. [Kaad a ɛwɔ kɔlɔ collage](/activity/make-a-color-collage/) wɔ anammɔn a ɛma wohu nea wɔyɛ.

### Watercolor a wɔde nsuo ayɛ

Fa nsuo a ɛho tew bɔ pampa a ɛyɛ den ano, afei ma onipa no de brush kɛse ahyɛ kɔlɔ so. Kɔlɔ no bɛtrɛw ne ho ne ho. Biribiara nni mmuae a ɛteɛ. [Kaad a ɛfa watercolor ho](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/) ka pampa, nsuhwɛ, ne sɛdeɛ wobɛpopɛ.

### Kaad a wɔapɛ ano

Pɛ pampa a ɛyɛ kɔkɔɔ mfinimfini. Gyigyi mfonini baako so, afei kyerɛw nkyerɛwee a ɛyɛ tiaa ma obi a onipa no dɔ. Bisa sɛ ɛnam hɔ na wɔbɛyɛ kaad no ansa na woafi ase, ɛfiri sɛ ɛyɛ anigye sɛ wɔyɛ biribi ma ɔbi a ɛnam ho. [Kaad a ɛfa asɛm ho](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) ma nkyerɛkyerɛ.

### Nnua wɔ abɔde mu

Tew nnua ahyɛ tɛkɛɛ, na fa biara fa sɛ wobɛhyɛ abɔde mu. Afei hwɛ nhyehyɛe no mu bom. [Kaad a ɛfa nhyehyɛe ho](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) wɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw ma nnua baako anaa abɔde ketewa.

### Nhaban ne nnua a wɔapɛ

Boa nhaban anaa nnua nketewa, de ahyɛ buukuw kɛse mu nnapɔw baako, afei gyigyi kaad a wɔapɛ ano so. [Kaad a ɛfa pɛpɛ ho](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/) kyerɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ no.

### Ntamadan a ɛwɔ ahodoɔ

Gyigyi ntama, ribbon ne pampa a aka so de ayɛ kaad a ɛyɛ den so. Sɛ wodi ntamadan a wɔayɛ no so a, ɛyɛ adwuma no mu ho akyinnye, enti ɛfata nnipa a wɔpɛ sɛ wɔkyɛ nneɛma. Hwɛ [kaad a ɛfa ntamadan ho](/activity/make-a-texture-board/).

### Dɔteɛ a ɛyɛ mmerɛw

Yɛ abɔde ketewa a wɔde dɔteɛ a ɛhwɛ mframa ahyɛ, de nsa nkɔm na wobɛyɛ. Ɛnhia nneɛma biara, na ɛhwie wɔ anadwo. Hwɛ [kaad a ɛfa pinch pot ho](/activity/shape-a-pinch-pot/).

## Ɛdeɛn na wobɛyɛ adwuma no?

1. **Hyehyɛ ansa.** Hyehyɛ nneɛma baanu anaa abien, ntama ne ntama a wɔabɔ nsuo mu ma nsa. Tew anything a wohia ansa na woreyɛ adwuma no.
2. **Kyerɛ anammɔn a edi kan.** Hyɛ brush mu, twitwa tua a edi kan anaa sɛe nnapɔw a edi kan, na fa ma onipa no nso yɛ saa.
3. **Yɛ adwuma wɔ ɔkwan baako so.** Kɔ so tena wo abɔde ho, sɛdeɛ adwuma no bɛyɛ nea wɔne no bɔ mu, na ɛnyɛ nea wɔrehwɛ no.
4. **Ka nea wuhu ho asɛm.** Ka kɔlɔ, nhyehyɛe ne sɛdeɛ ɛte ho asɛm. Gyae nsɛnkyerɛnne sɛ "Ɛyɛ dɛn?" ne ayamye sɛ "Wayɛ yie", efiri sɛ eyi betumi ama ɛnyɛ anigye.
5. **Gyae ansa na ahoɔden no regyae.** Simma du kɔsi aduonu nkutoo betumi aboa. Adwuma no betumi awie da foforɔ, anaa wobetumi agyae so.

Alzheimer's Association [kyerɛ](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) sɛ adwuma a ɛfa nneyɛe ho betumi ama nnipa ahu sɛ wɔayɛ biribi a ɛyɛ mfaso, na ɛma wɔka wɔn ho. NHS [adwuma nhwɛso](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) kyerɛ sɛ adwuma bi yɛ yie bere a ɛyɛ mmerɛw na ɛde ahoɔfɛ a wɔde ahu ne nsa ahyɛ mu.

## Ɛdeɛn na ɛyɛ mmerɛw a wobetumi asɔ hwehwɛ?

Biara a ɛwɔ anim yi betumi ayɛ mmerɛw. Paw nsakrae a ɛfata onipa no ɛnnɛ.

- **Ɛtena.** Biara wɔ saa adwuma yi so tena ɛdan so. Tena ɛfiri ho, nnyɛ sɛ wobɛgyina onipa no so.
- **Ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛkyere.** Fa brush kɛse, kɔlɔ kɛse anaa glue stick a ɛyɛ kɛse. Kyekyere ahyɛ sɛ wɔbɛgyina nsa mu. Dɔ pampa no ka, na ɛremmfa.
- **Ɛhu ankasa.** Fa pampa a ɛyɛ nkɔkɔɔ ahyɛ mfonini a ɛwɔ mmɔnsɛn a ɛyɛ tuntum, kɔlɔ kɛse baako baako, na de ahoɔfɛ a ɛyɛ den ahyɛ ɛdan no so. Ma nea wɔayɛ no ahu.
- **Nkɔrɔfo kakra.** Fa kɔlɔ mmienu anaa mfonini anan a wɔatwitwa dedaw ma. Ma onipa no ayi biribi afi ho, na ɛnyɛ nneɛma a ɛwɔ ɛdan so nyinaa.
- **Ɛyɛ tiaa.** Gyigyi nea ɛdi kan ansa, sɛdeɛ onipa no betumi agyigyi nea ɛto so baako nkutoo. Kaad a ɛwɔ mfonini baako betumi awie simma enum mu.

## Nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛfa ahobammɔ ho

- Fa nneɛma a ɛnyɛ nsɔhwɛ, na hwehwɛ sɛ nsuhwɛ ne nsɔre wɔ mfonini a ɛfata ho. [Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety) kyerɛ AP seal, a ɛkyerɛ sɛ nneɛma a wɔde di adwuma no nni nsɔhwɛ a ɛdɔɔso. Hwɛ kyerɛwee no, ɛnyɛ kɔlɔ a ɛwɔ abɔde no so nkutoo.
- Fa scissors a ɛnye no, anaa de ntwa mmom. Sɛ wɔrehyɛ nneɛma a wɔde ntwa, ankasa wo ara na wɔayɛ ansa na ɛreba.
- Hwɛ sɛ obiara nnwene nsuhwɛ, dɔteɛ, nnua anaa nneɛma ketewa. Kɔ nneɛma a ɛyɛ nketenkete wɔ ahyɛ mu.
- Hwɛ latex a ɛwɔ gloves mu, ne ahoɔfɛ biara a ɔnsɛ glue, dye anaa nnua nsã.
- Nnua ne nkɔnsonsɔnsɔn bi nyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wobɛkyɛ anaa wɔbɛdi. Fa nnua a wunim nkyerɛ nko.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yie a

- **Onipa no to nneɛma no afi ne ho.** Gyae, na dai adwuma no wɔ beaeɛ a ɛyɛ ahobammɔ. Fa tiyaa anaa kɔ nantew ma no. Ɛyɛ nneyɛe a ɛyɛ mmerɛw sɛ wɔbɛsan da foforɔ.
- **Ahosɛe da adi.** Yɛ anammɔn a ɛyɛ den no wo ara, na fa ma wɔatoa so yɛ nea ɛdi so. Adwuma a ɛyɛ kakra anaa ɛwɔ ɔkyinkyin a wɔayɛ ansa, betumi aboa.
- **Wɔka sɛ wontumi nyɛ.** Ma wɔnte ase sɛ nea wɔyɛ no nni hia. Fi nkyerɛwee a edi kan ase, na ma ɛbɛka wɔn ho sɛ wɔbɛkɔ so. Sɛ wɔka ho ka a, gyae.
- **Wunnim nea wɔbɛyɛ ɛdi hɔ.** Fa abɔde baako ne anammɔn baako toa so. Ɛnyɛ yie sɛ ɛyɛ kɛse.

## Sɛdeɛ wobɛyɛ no ne onipa a ɔwɔ dementia

Adwuma a ɛte saa betumi afata ɔpanyin, ɛwɔ mu nso nnipa a wɔwɔ dementia. Nsakrae a ɛboa sen biara ne sɛ wobɛka adwuma no sɛ adwuma a ɛyɛ nokwar, te sɛ kaad ma nnamfo. Ma anammɔn no nyɛ tenten, na ma onipa no nyɛ nea ɔbetumi ayɛ, te sɛ ɔbɛpaw kɔlɔ anaa ɔbɛdi tua a ɛwɔ hɔ. Wɔ mmere a ɛtwam so no, watercolor a wɔde nsuo ayɛ anaa dɔteɛ a wɔde nsa ayɛ betumi aboa ma ɔnnwene adwuma no. Sɛ wohwɛ sɛdeɛ wuyɛ wo ankasa adwuma, na wɔka kɔlɔ ho asɛm no, saa nso yɛ adwuma. Sɛ wopɛ nkɔso wɔ saa asɛm yi ho a, hwɛ [nhwɛso a ɛfa adwuma a wɔyɛ bom ho](/activities/group-art-activities/).

Sɛ onipa no no ntɔ so ma wɔ ne ho a, gye too sɛ ɛnyɛ yie. [Nhwɛso a ɛfa bere a wɔka sɛ yoo](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) kyerɛ sɛdeɛ wobɛdi akyi a ɛnyɛ ɔhaw.

Creative Activities nyɛ ayaresa. Art therapy yɛ adwuma kwan a ɛyɛ ayaresa, na ɔyareɛ adwumfoɔ a wɔawɔ ho ban de ayɛ. Adwuma ntetee yi yɛ ma anigye ne bere a wobɛtena mu.
