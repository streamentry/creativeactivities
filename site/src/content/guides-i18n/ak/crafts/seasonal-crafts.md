Bere ma yɛ nneɛma a yɛbɛyɛ. Nhahan bɛsɔ wɔ osutɔ, na ɛbɛma krataa a wɔbɛsɛe dan wɔ hɔ. Nnwawa a ɛwɔ owia mu ma nkyene ntoma ma dan no yɛ anomaa. Ahyeɛ mu no, ɛyɛ bere a ɛfata sɛ wobɛdua aba wɔ nkɔkɔ mu a ɛbɛgu nkɔkɔ mu. Bere nnwuma yɛ den sɛ wobɛhyehyɛ, na nneɛma no dodow ntɔ tɔ. Ɛha, yɛde nnwuma ahorow ma bere biara mu, ne afahyɛ bebree a wɔde di ho, ne sɛdeɛ wɔbɛyɛ nnwuma no wɔ ahobammɔ mu na ɛbɛyɛ tenten.

Eyi fata ma abusua, nnamfo, ahyehyɛde a wɔhwɛ nnipa, ne nkurɔfo a wɔboa wɔn ho. Ɛfata ɔpanyin a ɔpɛ bere ahorow no, ɔpanyin a ɔwɔ afahyɛ a ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyerɛ, ne ɔpanyin a ɔpɛ sɛ wotena ase wɔ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ dɛ so, na wɔayɛ krataa anaa nnwuma bi. Nnwuma no ho ɛnyɛ atemmu wɔ ne tumi ho, na ɛyɛ nea ɔpɛ.

## Dɛn na ɛma wopaw bere nnwuma?

Fi nea ɛwɔ wo ho ase. Nantew a wobɛboaboa nhahan anaa wuhu nea ɛrepɛpɛ no, ɛbɛma wunya adwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ. Afei susuw ɔbarima anaa ɔbaa no ho:

- **Ɔpɛ akwan a ɛgyina wɔ mframa ase anaa?** Paw nea ɛde ɔsɛnkafo ahyehyɛde, te sɛ nhahan krataa anaa aba a wɔde di nsuo.
- **Ɔpɛ krataa ne kaad anaa?** Kaad a wɔde ma obi a ɔdɔ no, anaa krataa ntoma, ebetumi aba.
- **Ɔpɛ anwonwade ne mmoa anaa?** Wɔbɛtumi ayɛ nkyene ntoma anomaa ma wɔahyɛ wɔ sɛ ɛbɛtumi ahwɛ wɔ dan kɔnkyɛn.
- **Ɔpɛ nea ɛyɛ dwodwoo anaa?** Ɔsɛe mpo, anaa boɔda a wɔde nneɛma ahorow ahyɛ mu, de ma ɛyɛ dwodwoo ne nea wɔde nsa ayɛ.

## Afahyɛ nnwuma wɔ bere nyinaa

### Ɔsutɔ

- **Dua aba wɔ nkɔkɔ mu.** Aba kɛse a ɛyɛ mmerɛ wɔ nkɔkɔ mu a wɔde asaase ahyɛ, na wɔde nsuo bɔ mmɔden. [Aba nkɔkɔ kaad](/activity/plant-seeds-in-a-pot/) no wɔ ɔkwan a wɔtena ase ne a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.
- **Pena abo ma ɔbɔ.** Abo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ a wɔde nkaebɔ anaa ahoma bi bɔ, de ma ɛbɛyɛ ɔkwan anaa ɔpon a wɔde ahyɛ. Hwɛ [abo ɔbɔ kaad](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/).

### Osuman

- **Yɛ lavender nkɔsoɔ.** Hyɛ lavender a wawo wɔ kakra mu, de si ɛbɔ kakra mu. Ɛnhia sɛ wobɛdeɛ, na wowɔ akwan a ɛbɛyɛ mmerɛ wɔ mpaboa ne a ɛnnwene. Hwɛ [lavender nkɔsoɔ kaad](/activity/make-a-lavender-sachet/).
- **Didi nhahan so.** Pan nhahan akyi, de ne nkyɛn to krataa so de ma nhahan no nkwa ahyɛ. Wobɛtumi ayɛ eyi wɔ abɔde mu anaa wɔ abɔde so. Hwɛ [nhahan didi kaad](/activity/print-with-leaves/).

### Osutɔ (Ɔsutɔ bere a nhahan retena)

- **Pae nhahan ne nnuaba.** Pae wɔn wɔ nwoma kɛse mu nnanson, afei sɛe wɔn kyɛ kaad so. Hwɛ [pae kaad](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/).
- **Yɛ nkaeɛ akuraa.** Akuraa bere mu a wɔde krataa anaa nkyɛnkyɛn ahyɛ, de ma ɛbɛyɛ ɔpon a wɔbɛtena so. Hwɛ [nkaeɛ akuraa kaad](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/).

### Owia

- **Yɛ krataa nkyene ntoma.** Sɛe krataa kɔnkɔn ahoma, baako baako. Wobɛtumi ahyɛ wɔ dan mu, na ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wobɛkyɛ obiara. Hwɛ [krataa nkyene kaad](/activity/make-a-paper-chain-garland/).
- **Yɛ pine cone anomaa nkɔsoɔ.** Hyɛ ayaayaa ne aba anomaa mu wɔ pine cone no mu, afei hyɛ no wɔ beaɛ a wubetumi ahwɛ anomaa no. [Anomaa nkɔsoɔ kaad](/activity/make-a-pine-cone-bird-feeder/) no wɔ ɔkwan a nkɔsoɔ nni mu.
- **Yɛ akyɛdeɛ kaad.** Kaad a wɔde ma afahyɛ a ɛwɔ owia mu, a wɔde ma obi a ɔdɔ ɔpanyin no. Hwɛ [akyɛdeɛ kaad kaad](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/).

## Afahyɛ wɔ amammerɛ bebree mu

Nnipa bebree di afahyɛ wɔ bere yi mu, na bebree nnyɛ saa. Afahyɛ bi di nkrataa ntoma, nkrataa a wɔtwa, nkrataa a ɛyɛ fitaa, anaa nhyehyɛe a wɔde ahyɛ. Afahyɛ bi nni biribiara. Saa ebsite yi nnhwɛ afahyɛ biako, usuo biako anaa abusua baako.

Sɛ wo rehyehyɛ afahyɛ nnwuma:

1. **Bisa ansa.** Bisa nea ɔpɛ sɛ ɔkyerɛ, ne nna a ɛho hia ma no. Ma ɔpaw afahyɛ no, anaa ɔmfa biara nnpaw.
2. **Fa nneɛma, nnfa nkyerɛaseɛ.** Krataa, nhahan, atade ne aba yɛ adwuma wɔ bere biara. Mma nkyerɛaseɛ a ɛfa usuo anaa amammerɛ ho, sɛ wonnim a ne ntease, anaa sɛ ɔpanyin no nnpɛ sɛ wɔde di.
3. **Bisa obi a ɔfiri saa amammerɛ no mu.** Sɛ wopɛ sɛ wɔyɛ nnwuma a ɛfa afahyɛ bi ho, bisa obi a ɔnim no yiye sɛdeɛ wɔde kyerɛ no ne nea ɛfata. Kasa a wɔde sesa no nkutoo nni ho adeɛ.
4. **Ma ɔpaw ho ahobrɛaseɛ.** Ma nhwɛso abien anaa abiɛsa, na gye sɛ ɔka dabi.

## Dɛn na mɛtumi ayɛ sɛ nnwuma a ɛyɛ mmerɛ?

- **Wɔ ntebea so.** Nnwuma bebree wɔ ntebea so. Fa nneɛma no bɛn ne nsa.
- **Ɛyɛ mmerɛ sɛ wɔbɛkyɛ.** Fa krataa a ɛyɛ den ne nneɛma a ɛyɛ kɛse. Pae kaad no kane, anaa twa nkyene ntoma no ansa.
- **Ahwɛ kɔ kɛse.** Fa krataa a ɛyɛ nwini a ɛyɛ mmerɛ, na ma kaad biara nyɛ baako ne kɛse.
- **Nneɛma kakra.** Ma nkyene abien anaa baako ne nhahan baako ne ntɛa baako. Fa nwoma a wɔayɛ so ma ɔnhwɛ.
- **Tenten.** Sɛe nkyene ntoma no ansa anaa nhahan no ansa, na ɔde nkyɛn a ɛka akyi ayɛ.
- **Nkasa.** Ma nhwɛso abien a wɔayɛ, na hwɛ sɛ ɔbɛtena anaa ɔbɛhwɛ wo.

## Ahobammɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ wɔ afahyɛ nnwuma mu

- **Nsɔhwɛ.** Fa LED anaa batiri nsɔhwɛ wɔ krataa nsɔhwɛ ne nkyene ntoma mu. Nnyɛ ogya bɔ krataa, atade anaa nnuaba a awu ano ho.
- **Nneɛma a wɔde di.** Nnyɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ no nna anaa nneɛma a ɛyɛ aduane. Nkyerɛkyerɛ a ɛte saa a ɛma nnipa bi susuw sɛ ɛyɛ aduane. Alzheimer's Association [afahyɛ nkyerɛkyerɛ](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) ka sɛ nsɔhwɛ a ɛhyɛ-ɛhyɛ betumi ama ɛnyɛ nea wɔtɔ so.
- **Nneɛma kakra.** Ma bɔtan, ahoma ketewa, ahyɛde ketewa ne aba a ɛnnyinaa nnyɛ mmofra a wɔadi mmiensa so nkɔnkɔ. [HealthyChildren.org nkɔsoɔ a ɛyɛ ahobammɔ nkyerɛkyerɛ](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/choking-prevention.aspx) kyerɛ sɛ nneɛma kakra yi ara ne nsɛnkyerɛnne a ɛma nnipa bi ɔboa.
- **Nnuaba.** Nnnoa nnuaba anaa nkyene a wunnim din. Hohoro nsa wɔ wo tɔ fidie anaa atade so.
- **Wɔ baabi a ɛwɔ mframa mu.** Wɔ wo tɔ fidie akyi, hwɛ sɛ abɔde nkɔ ne nsɛnkyerɛnne nni wo ho.

## Sɛ ɛnyɛ yiye a

- **Da no yɛ basabasa.** Fa nnwuma no kɔ dan a ɛyɛ dwodwoo, na ma ɛnyɛ tenten. Nhahan baako anaa krataa baako yɛ tɔ a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.
- **Anifere pue.** Gyina, na ma wɔnantew anaa wɔntɔ kwan. Nnwuma a wɔyɛ wɔ bere mu betumi aka nna foforo.
- **Ɔbarima anaa ɔbaa no ampɛ sɛ ɔyɛ biribiara.** Mmom fa ho to ntebea so, na hwɛ bere no. Ka nea wuhu wɔ abɔde mu. Eyi nso yɛ adwuma.

## Ma da no nyɛ basabasa

Afahyɛ bi betumi ama nnipa atwasɛm, nkɔneɛ ne sɛ ɛnsɛn nhyehyɛe a ɛte sɛ bere nyinaa. Hyehyɛ da no sɛdeɛ ɛfata wɔn daa nhyehyɛe, na ma da no nyɛ kɛse sɛ wobɛtumi. Ma wɔn adwuma ketewa, te sɛ nkɔ ntoma anaa ɔhyɛ nhwiren, na ma dan a ɛyɛ dwodwoo nni hɔ sɛ da no ye den. Video frɛ betumi ama abusua a ɛwɔ akyirikyiri nso nni nkɔmhyɛ no mu anaa nkurɔfo a ɛyɛ mmerɛ.

## Nnwuma a ɛfa ɔpanyin anaa ɔbarima/ɔbaa a ɔwɔ dementia ho

Afahyɛ nnwuma betumi ayɛ ɔkwan a ɛyɛ mmerɛ de kyerɛ bere a ɛwɔ hɔ ne ɔpanyin, ɛjɛ ɔbarima/ɔbaa a ɔwɔ dementia nso. Paw nnwuma a wɔn ho ayɛ nnwuma, te sɛ kaad anaa nhahan didi, na ma ɛnyɛ anammɔn biako ara. Ma wɔapaw nkyerɛaseɛ anaa wɔnpɛ nhahan no. Nkaeɛ a ɛfa bere a atwam nwoma mu ebetumi aboa nkɔmmɔ, na [nkaeɛ kwan](/activities/reminiscence/) kyerɛ sɛdeɛ wobɛkasa wɔ nkaeɛ ho nkɔ nsɛnkyerɛ. Sɛ wobɛdwene dwom a ɛfa bere ho a Alzheimer's Association [kyerɛ](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays), ɛyɛ ɔkwan foforo a wɔbɛfa so.

Sɛ wɔakyerɛ ɔbarima/ɔbaa no dementia ntɛm, anaa ɔrenya nkɔsoɔ foforo wɔ ahyɛde hɔ, [dementia akyerɛkyerɛ akyi](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) nkyerɛkyerɛ no betumi aboa.

Creative Activities nyɛ ayaresa. Afahyɛ nnwuma yɛ ɔkwan a yɛfa so hwɛ bere a ɛwɔ hɔ, na yɛyɛ nneɛma bi bom.
