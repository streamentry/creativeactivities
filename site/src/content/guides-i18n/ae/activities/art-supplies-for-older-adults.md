Vāstra-kit khšaθra-aēta, mashya-dasa. Ahmi page yaēṭ: kadā kiṭa ānhaiti, substitute-aṣ, adaptation arthritis, low vision, bed-ā; kadā nāi vaēnā; kadā kit ahurā box-aēta.

## Kadā ānhaiti vāstra-kit-ā?

Table-aēta core items. Each common, cheap, useful.

| Item | Why it helps | Low-cost substitute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thick brushes, 1 inch or wider | Big strokes, easy to hold | Foam paint brushes |
| Watercolor pan set, 12 colors | Colors ready, no mixing | Store-brand pan set or a few tubes |
| Heavy paper, 140 lb or 300 gsm | Holds water without buckling | Heavy card or a mixed-media pad |
| Glue stick | Clean, no-drip gluing | Any non-toxic glue stick |
| Soft pencils, 2B or 4B | Dark marks with little pressure | Standard graphite pencils |
| Oil pastels | Rich color, no water or brush needed | Chunky crayons |
| Air-dry clay | Squeezing and shaping, no kiln | Modeling dough, labeled non-toxic |
| Masking tape | Holds paper still | Painter's tape |
| Tray with a rim | Keeps supplies together, catches spills | A baking sheet with sides |
| Apron or old cotton shirt | Protects clothing | An old shirt worn backward |

Labels-ā glue, clay, paint allergens-ā check; non-toxic mark-ā look. Activity cards [Tear paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/) aṣ [Paint with watercolors](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/) basics-ā only.

## Kadā adaptations arthritis, low vision, bed-ā?

**Arthritis.** Handles foam tape-ā wrap; thicker, easier grip. Light brushes, markers. Clip or tape paper-ā hold; press nāi. Forearm table-ā support.

**Low vision.** High-contrast colors: black marker, white paper. Bold simple subjects, single flower. Good lamp side-ā, glare dūrə. [Arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) card low-vision version ahmi.

**Bed-ā working.** Lap tray, clipboard pillows-ā. Non-slip mat paper-ā nīce. Clamp light headboard-ā. Kit few items: oil pastels, glue stick, card.

## Kadā avoid?

- Glitter, loose beads, small stickers: mouth-ā vaēnā.
- Solvent paints, spray adhesives, varnish, ventilation nāi.
- Craft knives, hot glue guns. Cutting ahead, yourself.
- Open liquid jars, tip-able.
- Children's cartoon art sets. Adults off-putting.
- Large kits, full-use pressure.

## Kadā store kit, minute-aēta ready?

One clear lidded box, tray. Brushes cup-ā, bristles up. Pencils, pastels shoebox section-ā; paper flat under lid. Box reachable, pets, small children dūrə. Clay, paint monthly check; dried replace. Short list lid-inside tape; missing visible. Paint time: lift tray, start.
