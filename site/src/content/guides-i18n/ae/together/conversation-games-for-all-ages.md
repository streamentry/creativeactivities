Ṣaiθi-zanōiš ahūm vāca-sūtrā vaxšaiti, yaθā nara ṣaiθiyā nōi quiz. Vīsaiti frašnā ya ahū mąθrā-hiša ṣaiθi-gaēθā vaxšaiti, ahmāi hāθrā ṣaiθi-raēvā nōi. Ahū xšaθrā-kāraiti: nara ṣaiθi-maiðyā, ahū frašnā-kāraiti. Paθrā ahū-maiðyā ṣaiθi-gaēθā vaxšaiti: frašnā-ahū, ṣaiθi-paθrā, mąθrā-hiša, gaēθā ṣaiθi-raēvā. Mąθrā ṣaiθi-zanōiš ahmāi vaēδa, ṣaiθi-gaēθā hāθrā ṣaiθi-kāraiti.

## Caiθrā ahū xšaθrā-kāraiti?

Quiz-ahū, ahū ṣaiθi-maiðyā frašnā kāraiti, ṣaiθi nara paθrā raθwā, nōi ṣaiθi-hiša. Yadi nara ṣaiθi-kāraiti mąθrāṃ, ṣaiθi ahū vaēδa. Ahū-gaēθā, ahū ṣaiθi-paθrā, ahmāi ṣaiθi-raēvā. Alzheimer's Association [activities page](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) vaxšaiti: ṣaiθi nara vaēδa ṣaiθi-gaēθā, ahū hāθrā. Vāca-sūtra ṣaiθi-vaēδa.

## Kaēšcit vīsaiti frašnā ṣaiθi-kāraiti

1. **Ṣaiθi ṣaiθi-mąθrā** ṣaiθi nara zaṃ-ahū: ṣaiθi-maiðyā, gaēθā, paśu, samudra.
2. **Nara ṣaiθi-kāraiti** nōi, vā ṣaiθi-paθrā ṣaiθi-zanōiš, yaθā nōi ṣaiθi-srūta.
3. **Ṣaiθi ekā frašnā ā-nōi.** "Ṣaiθi xraθwā ahū?" "Ṣaiθi-vaēδa ṣaiθi ... ?"
4. **Kaēšcit raθwā.** Nōi ṣaiθi, nōi āpō, vā ṣaiθi-hāθrā, ṣaiθi-gaēθā.
5. **Ṣaiθi-gaēθā nara ṣaiθi.** Yadi raθwā, ṣaiθi-hāθrā.
6. **Ṣaiθi ṣaiθi-paθrā** yadi nara kaēšcit stāyeiti: "Ṣaiθi maiðyā-kāraiti."
7. **Nōi frašnā-ahū, nōi ṣaiθi-raēvā.**

Ṣaiθi-zanōiš: ṣaiθi-maiðyā, paśu, ahū, gaēθā, raθwā, ṣaiθi-vaēδa. Nōi nōmōi ṣaiθi-zanōiš, nōi ṣaiθi-xraθwā.

## Kaēšcit mąθrā-hiša ṣaiθi-kāraiti

Mąθrā-hiša ṣaiθi ahū mąθrā ṣaiθi-hiša, ya ahū vaēδa. Ṣaiθi nara vaxšaiti, ṣaiθi-hāθrā, nōi ṣaiθi-srūta.

1. **Nara ṣaiθi-zanōiš** ṣaiθi-gaēθā, ahū ṣaiθi.
2. **Ṣaiθi ahū-paθrā** ṣaiθi-kāraiti.
3. **Ṣaiθi-paθrā, kaēšcit.** Ṣaiθi-gaēθā dvā ṣaiθi.
4. **Ṣaiθi ṣaiθi-raēvā.** "Ṣaiθi-vaēδa, ahū-gaēθā," vā "Nōi ṣaiθi-xraθwā," ṣaiθi ahū.
5. **Vaxšaiti ṣaiθi-mąθrā.** Ṣaiθi nara ṣaiθi, ṣaiθi yaθā ṣaiθi ahū ahmāi vaēδa.
6. **Ṣaiθi ṣaiθi-raēvā** yadi nara ṣaiθi-hiša.

Ahū mąθrā: "Rain before seven..." (fine by eleven); "Too many cooks..." (spoil the broth); "Home is where the heart..." (is); "April showers bring..." (May flowers). Ṣaiθi nara ṣaiθi-maiðyā yadi kaēšcit. Online version: [finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/).

## Frašnā ahū-gaēθā

Gaēθā ṣaiθi-zanōiš ahū-gaēθā, ahū ṣaiθi: ṣaiθi-srūta, ṣaiθi-mąθrā, ṣaiθi-gaēθā, ṣaiθi-maiðyā. Ahū ṣaiθi: frašnā ṣaiθi-zanōiš, nōi ṣaiθi-xraθwā.

- Vaxšaiti "Tā ṣaiθi..." vā "Kaēšcit ṣaiθi...?"
- Nōi vaxšaiti "Kō ahū?", "Kadā ṣaiθi?" vā "Nara ṣaiθi nāma?"
- Ṣaiθi-raēvā. Yadi ṣaiθi-gaēθā ṣaiθi, ṣaiθi-zanōiš.
- Yadi gaēθā ṣaiθi-zāna, nōi raθwā. Vaxšaiti: "Tā ṣaiθi-zāna ṣaiθi. Ṣaiθi nara vaxšaiti ṣaiθi-srūta?" Ṣaiθi ṣaiθi-maiðyā.

Ahū frašnā: Kaēšcit ṣaiθi nara-paθrā ṣaiθi? Kaēšcit ṣaiθi-gaēθā vaxšaiti? Kaiθā ṣaiθi-maiðyā ṣaiθi? Online: [reminiscence](/activities/reminiscence/).

## Vīsaiti frašnā, nōi quiz

Ahū nōi quiz. Nōi kaēšcit ṣaiθi, nōi ṣaiθi-raēvā. Dvā vā θrī vaxšaiti ṣaiθi, ṣaiθi-paθrā. Frašnā ṣaiθi-srūta, ṣaiθi-zanōiš.

| Frašnā | Vaxšaiti | Ṣaiθi-zanōiš |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kō uzjaiti 88 kaēθā? | Guitar, **piano**, trumpet | Ahū nara ṣaiθi-zāna vaēδa? |
| Kō ṣaiθi Lucy "I Love Lucy"-hiša? | **Lucille Ball**, Doris Day, Betty White | Kā mąθrā ṣaiθi-zanōiš? |
| Kō ṣaiθi Model T? | **Ford**, Chevrolet, Buick | Ṣaiθi-gaēθā ṣaiθi-raēvā. |

Yadi nōi kaēšcit vaēδa, ṣaiθi-raēvā, ahū. Gaēθā ṣaiθi-zāna. Nōi ṣaiθi ṣaiθi: ṣaiθi about ten frašnā.

## Ahū vāca-sūtrā ṣaiθi

- **Ahū vā ahū.** Dvā vaxšaiti: caiti vā kāfa, ṣaiθi vā samudra. Nōi ṣaiθi-wrong.
- **Would you rather.** "Ṣaiθi picnic vā ṣaiθi-xšaθra?"
- **Ṣaiθi-nāma.** Ṣaiθi ṣaiθi-maiðyā, gaēθā vā ṣaiθi-raēvā.
- **Mąθrā-hiša.** [Conversation cards](/games/conversation-cards/) ṣaiθi-mąθrā, ṣaiθi-maiðyā.

## Ṣaiθi-zanōiš nara ṣaiθi-kāraiti ṣaiθi-maiðyā

Ahū sūtrā ṣaiθi-mąθrā ṣaiθi-zanōiš, ahū nara ṣaiθi. Ṣaiθi frašnā-ahū, ṣaiθi-raēvā. Yadi nara ṣaiθi-zanōiš, ṣaiθi-srūta vā ṣaiθi-mąθrā. Ṣaiθi-hāθrā ṣaiθi-gaēθā hāθrā ṣaiθi-zanōiš, ṣaiθi nara-zaṃ-hāθrā. Ṣaiθi-gaēθā ṣaiθi-kaθā vaxšaiti: [how to play games together](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).

Ṣaiθi nara tarasaiti vā ṣaiθi-xraθwā? "Caiti ṣaiθi-aēδa." Ṣaiθi-paθrā ṣaiθi-raēvā. Ahū-gaēθā ṣaiθi ṣaiθi.

## Yadi nōi kāraiti

Ṣaiθi-mąθrā, ṣaiθi-maiðyā, vā ṣaiθi-srūta. Kaēšcit ṣaiθi-vāyu ṣaiθi vaxšaiti. Ṣaiθi-srūta ahū, ṣaiθi-paθrā ṣaiθi-gaēθā.

## Ṣaiθi vā ṣaiθi-raēvā

- Ṣaiθi frašnā ṣaiθi-xraθwā, ṣaiθi ṣaiθi-maiðyā, nōi ṣaiθi.
- Ṣaiθi ṣaiθi-kāraiti ṣaiθi-paθrā, ṣaiθi-srūta.
- Nōi kaēšcit ṣaiθi-gaēθā ṣaiθi. Ṣaiθi-zanōiš.
