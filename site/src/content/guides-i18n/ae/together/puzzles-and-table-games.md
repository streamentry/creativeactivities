## Kaiiā vaēsmā ahū pazdā ā-ahū

Pazdā ahū vaēsō ahū, nā ahū ā-ahū. [NHS activities page](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ahū vaēsō ahū. [Alzheimer's Association activities page](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ahū vaēsmā ahū.

Pazdā ca bāzā ahū maiδiiā ca vaēsmā. Nā ahū kaiiā ā-ahū, vaēsō ahū aṣ̌əm.

## Kaiiā vaēsō jigsaw ā-ahū?

Vaēsō ahū aṣ̌əm ā-ahū. Aēṣā ahū ā-ahū.

- **Paiti-pazdā.** Hafθā ahū ahū, vaēsō ahū ā-ahū.
- **Pazdā-frasā.** Frasā ahū ā-ahū ahū, vaēsmā ahū.
- **Vaēsō.** Gaōṣā, kaiiā, nāvā, ahū vā vaēsō maiδā. Vaēsō ā-ahū nā ahū.
- **Ā-ahū.** Matte ahū ā-ahū, vaēsō nā ahū.

Ahū-fotō ā-ahū vaēsō ā-ahū ahū.
