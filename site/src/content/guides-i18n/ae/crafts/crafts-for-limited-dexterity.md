Tōyuš, ḫvarəšta, duš-gərədā ahūiš hvaṇtā̊ bərəzaṇi kāmā ahūm. Arthritis, nā ahū, aēnahā ahūiš, pərəšā tōyuš, nā ahū, cā bərəzaṇi ahuirā, yā̊ kārəm, aēvā ahū, yā̊ pərəsaiti. Kārəm hvaṇtā̊ aēvā ahū; ašnā dastā̊ kārəm pārəštā ahū, yā̊ kārəm ahuirā. Aēnahā yasna ahū yā̊ kārəmā hvaṇtā̊, cā tā̊ kārəmā ahū, yā̊ aēvā-dastā tōyuš ahū.

Aēnā ahūiš, yā̊ kārəm vaēdā̊ ahū, cā tōyuš ahū. Nā ahū, nā ahurā̊ hvaṇtā̊; yadi taptā̊ pərəsaiti, pāta. Doctor, physiotherapist vā occupational therapist ahū, yā̊ ahurā̊ hvaṇtā̊ dastā̊ ahū.

## Kā ahūiš hvaṇtā̊ bərəzaṇi?

Bərəzaṇi tōyuš ahū, nā ahū kārəmā, aēnahā ahūiš.

- **Taptā̊ pāta.** Masking tape ā aiwī-kərənā ahū, yā̊ pərəšā aēvā-dastā.
- **Non-slip mat vā damp cloth** ahū, yā̊ tāpaiti nā sarəm.
- **Tray hvaṇtā̊ bərəzaṇi-kənā.** Pərəšā tray ā, yā̊ aēnahā ahūiš.
- **Ašnā ahūiš, yā̊ dastā̊ hvaṇtā̊.** Ahū ā, cā ahurā̊ dastā̊ bərəzaṇi, nā ahū yā̊ dūrəm.
- **Pre-cut vā pre-prepare.** Kārəm, fold vā set ahū, yā̊ kārəm nā aēnahā.

## Kathā tōyuš hvaṇtā̊ vaēdā̊?

- **Handle bərəzaṇi-kərə.** Foam tape ā pen, brush vā pencil āpaiti. Bərəzaṇi handle nā pinča.
- **Wide tōyuš.** Jumbo crayon, chunky marker vā large brush hvaṇtā̊.
- **Palm grip.** Tōyuš ā tā̊ dastā̊ vaēdā̊ hvaṇtā̊. Pərəšā ahū, yadi kārəm ahū.
- **Aēvā tōyuš.** Glue stick vā sponge vā roller hvaṇtā̊ ahū.
- **Tape nā tying.** Tape vā clip ā ribbon ā card ā hvaṇtā̊.

[Arthritis Foundation](https://www.arthritis.org/partnership/ease-of-use) Ease of Use program ā ahū, yā̊ tōyuš ā hvaṇtā̊ vaēdā̊. Aēnā label ahū, yā̊ shopping ā vaēdā̊, nā ahū, yā̊ tōyuš ahurā̊ hvaṇtā̊ pərəšā aēvā-dastā.

## Kā kārəmā ahū, yā̊ fine movement nā?

Kārəmā ahū, yā̊ larger movement ā, palm grip ā, vā single action ā. Aēnā card ahū, yā̊ hvaṇtā̊ ahū:

- [Tear-paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/). Tearing ā fingers ā; scissors nā. Card ā easier vā bedside versions.
- [Arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/). Trimmed stems ā wide jar ā. Card ā easier vā bedside version.
- [Tap a rhythm together](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/). Whole hand ā table vā tray ā; tōyuš nā.
- [Make a texture board](/activity/make-a-texture-board/). Fabric vā paper ā card ā glued; first step clear.
- [Make a greeting card](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/). Card pre-folded, picture pre-glued; message ahū.
- [Make a sealed shaker](/activity/make-a-sealed-shaker/). Helper lid seal; person fill vā decorate.
- [Make a lavender sachet](/activity/make-a-lavender-sachet/). Sewing nā; card easier vā bedside versions.

Kāhicit card ā tiny pieces ahū, yathā buttons vā beads. [sort buttons by color card](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/) large buttons tray ā hvaṇtā̊; adults vā older children ahurā̊, buttons under-threes ā choking hazard.

## Kā easier versions ahū?

- **Seated vā bedside.** Card hvaṇtā̊ seated vā bed ā. Tray stable surface ā.
- **Easier grip.** Built-up tōyuš, wide lightweight container. Lid twist vā pop open.
- **One-handed.** Tape, clamp vā non-slip mat; tray raised edges. Pre-loaded brush vā pre-spread glue.
- **Fewer choices.** Two colors vā two pieces; person choose.
- **Shorter.** One piece, one stem, one card. Short session good session.
- **Less mess.** Tray ā cloth underneath. Dry crafts: tearing vā arranging.

## Safety notes

- Small pieces (buttons, beads, seeds) tray ā. Under-threes choke.
- Blunt scissors vā ready-cut pieces. Craft knives adults only.
- Non-toxic, labelled paints vā glues. [Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety) AP seal explains.
- Grip aid (foam tape) nā slip, trap, come loose.
- Pain, numbness vā swelling activity ā pāta; doctor vā physiotherapist ā speak.

## Yadi nā kārəm ahū

- **Tōyuš pərəsaiti.** Pāta, wider vā lighter tōyuš, vā aēvā activity.
- **Paper ahū tāpaiti.** Tape ā; corner table ā clamp.
- **Pieces paitiš.** Higher tray edges, vā fewer pieces.
- **Person tired.** One piece ā pāta. Kārəm another day vā aēvā.
- **Nā ahū vaēdā.** Less fine movement activity: tapping rhythm vā window ā looking.

## Older adult vā person living with dementia ā kārəm

Adaptations ahūiš older adults, person living with dementia, limited grip ahū. Kārəm small steps ā; person parts ahū ā manage (colors choose, piece press). Fine work āpaiti tā̊; person decide participation.

Creative Activities therapy nā ahū, adaptations treatment nā ahū. Kārəm easier try ā; person pāta ahū.
