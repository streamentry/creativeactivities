Isikhwama esincane nesingabizi silula ukusisebenzisa kunestudiyo egcwele, futhi abantu abaningi abadingi ngaphezu kwezinto ezicishe zibe eziyishumi. Leli khasi likhombisa okufanele ukuthenge, okungathathwa esikhundleni okungabizi, izilungiselelo zezifo zamalunga, ukubona kancane nokusebenza emubhedeni, okufanele kugwenywe, kanye nendlela yokugcina isikhwama ukuze silungele noma ngasiphi isikhathi.

## Yiziphi izinto ezifanele isikhwama esiyisisekelo sokudweba?

Ithebula elingezansi likhombisa izinto eziyisisekelo. Ngayinye ivamile, ayibizi kakhulu, futhi iyasiza emisebenzini eminingi. Okungathathwa esikhundleni kungatholakala ezitolo ezinemikhiqizo engabizi noma ezitolo zezakhiwo.

| Into | Kungani isiza | Okungathathwa esikhundleni esingabizi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amabhulashi amakhulu, 1 intshi noma ngaphezulu | Imishayo emikhulu, ilula ukuyibamba | Amabhulashi epeyinti e-foam |
| Isethi yamapende e-watercolor, emibala engu-12 | Imibala isilungele ukusetshenziswa, akudingeki ukuxuba | Isethi yamapende yesitolo noma amathubhu ambalwa |
| Iphepha elinzima, 140 lb noma 300 gsm | Liyayimunca imanzi lingagobi | Ikhadibhodi elinzima noma i-mixed-media pad |
| Ugamu wentonga | Ukunamathisela okuhlanzekile, akuchitheki | Noma yiluphi ugamu wentonga ongenawo ubuthi |
| Amapensela athambile, 2B noma 4B | Amamaki amnyama ngocindezelo oluncane | Amapensela wegraphite ajwayelekile |
| Oil pastels | Imibala ecebile, akudingeki amanzi noma ibhulashi | Ama-crayon amakhulu |
| Udaka oluoma ngokwemvelo | Ukufinyeza nokwakha, akudingeki i-kiln | Udaka lokumodela olumakwe njengolungenawo ubuthi |
| Ithephu yokufihla | Ibamba iphepha lime | Ithephu yomdwebi |
| Ithreyi enomphetho | Ihlanganisa izinto, ibambe ukuchitheka | Ishidi lokubhaka elinemiphetho |
| Ibhulukwe lokugqoka noma ihembe lakudala lokotini | Livikela izingubo | Ihembe elidala elifakwe ngemuva |

Hlola amalebula kugamu, udaka nopende ukuze uthole izinto ezingathinta, futhi ubheke uphawu oluthi okungenawo ubuthi. Amakhadi omsebenzi afana no-[Tear paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/) no-[Paint with watercolors](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/) asebenzisa izinto eziyisisekelo kuphela.

## Yiziphi izilungiselelo ezisiza ngezifo zamalunga, ukubona kancane noma ukusebenza emubhedeni?

**Izifo zamalunga.** Faka ithepu yefoam ezibambweni ukuze zibe zikhulu futhi zilule ukubambeka. Khetha amabhulashi nezimaki ezilula. Sebenzisa iclip noma ithephu ukubamba iphepha, ukuze umuntu angadingi ukucindezela. Sekela ingalo ngaphezulu etafuleni.

**Ukubona kancane.** Sebenzisa imibala ephezulu yokuphambanisa, njengesimaki esimnyama ephepheni elimhlophe, nezihloko ezilula nezikhulu, njengembali eyodwa. Beka isibani esihle ngasohlangothini ukuze kuncishiswe ukukhanya okuqhaqhazelayo. Ikhadi [Arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) linenguqulo yokubona kancane.

**Ukusebenza emubhedeni.** Sebenzisa ithreyi yasemadolweni noma ibhodi lokubhala elibekwe phezu kwezigqoko. Beka imethi engashayi ngaphansi kwephepha. Isibani esincane esingabanjwa ekhanda lombhede noma ethekeni siyasiza. Gcina isikhwama sincane, sinezinto ezimbalwa, njengama-oil pastels, ugamu wentonga nekhadi lokusebenza kulo.

## Yini okufanele ugweme?

- I-glitter, amapheshana amancane nezitika ezincane, ezingangena emlonyeni.
- Amapende anesisekelo se-solvent, ugamu wokupompa nevarnish ngaphandle komoya ohambayo kahle.
- Imela yobuciko nezibhamu zogamu oshisayo. Sika wena ngaphambilini.
- Izitsha zoketshe ezivulekile ezingaphuka kalula.
- Amasethi obuciko wezingane anemiklamo yamakhathuni. Abantu abadala bavame ukungazizwa kahle ngawo.
- Amasethi amakhulu ozizwa ukucindezeleka ukuwasebenzisa wonke.

## Ungayigcina kanjani isikhwama ukuze silungele ngemizuzu embalwa?

Sebenzisa ibhokisi noma ithreyi eyodwa ecacile enesivalo ngayo yonke into. Ama-brashi ayawumisa ebhodleleni ngamacici aphezulu. Amapensela nama-pastel agcinwa esigabeni sebhokisi lamashuzi, namaphepha ayalalwa phansi ngaphansi kwesivalo. Beka ibhokisi lapho lilula ukufinyelela khona, kude nezilwane zasekhaya nezingane ezincane. Hlola udaka nopende njalo ngenyanga, bese ushintsha noma yini eyomile. Namathisela uhlu olufushane ngaphakathi esivalweni, ukuze noma ubani athole ukuthi yini engekho. Uma sekuyisikhathi sokupeyinta, phakamisa ithreyi, bese usulungele ukuqala.
