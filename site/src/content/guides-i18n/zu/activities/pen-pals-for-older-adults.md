Incwadi yisenzo esihle sokwabelana ngoba ihamba ngesivinini somuntu ozibhalayo. Akudingeki ukuphendula masinyane. Ikhadi lingafundwa kabili, likhonjiswe umvakashi, futhi lingagcinwa edilowa. Leli khasi limayelana nokuthi ubhalela bani, ukuthi uthola kanjani uhlelo ngokuphepha, yini ongayithumela uma amagama enzima, nokuthi umsizi angasekela kanjani ukubhala.

## Kungani izincwadi zilungele?

Izincwadi zivumela umuntu ukuthi abambe iqhaza ngesikhathi sakhe. Ukukhetha iphepha nesitembu, ukubhala umugqa oyodwa, nokuwuthumela kuyimisebenzi emincane yangempela enesiphetho esicacile. I-[activities guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ye-Alzheimer's Association iyeluleka ukugcina imisebenzi ilula nokugxila enqubweni kunasemphumeleni, futhi incwadi ihambisana kahle naleyo ncazelo. Umbhalo akudingi ukuba mde ukuze ubaluleke.

## Ngubani angaba umngane wokubhalelana?

- **Umzukulu noma umzukulu omkhulu,** ikakhulu oqala ukufunda ukubhala.
- **Umngane wakudala noma umsebenzi owayesebenza naye,** umuntu omaziyo kakade.
- **Ikilasi esikoleni sasendaweni.** Buza uthisha ukuthi ikilasi lingasungula yini ukushintshana ngezincwadi.
- **Umakhelwane,** oyojabulela inothi elifushane ngaphesheya komgwaqo noma ezansi kwendlela.
- **Umphakathi wenkolo noma iklabhu** umuntu ayejoyine esikhathini esedlule.

Izincwadi zomndeni ziyisiqalo esilula kakhulu, ngoba umuntu usazi ukuthi ubani ozozifunda.

## Ungathola kanjani uhlelo ngokuphepha?

Khetha uhlelo oluphethwe yinhlangano ongayihlola: isikole, ilabhulali, umphakathi wenkolo, isikhungo sabantu abadala noma indlu yokunakekela. Ngaphambi kokujoyina, buza:

- Ubani ophethe uhlelo, futhi ungamfinyelela ngocingo ngenombolo epapashiwe?
- Abantu ababhala izincwadi bahlolwa noma bagadwa isisebenzi?
- Ukhona yini isisebenzi ongaxhumana naso uma kukhona okungahambi kahle?

Ungasebenzisi umuntu ongaziwa oxhumana nawe ku-inthanethi acele ikheli, izithombe noma imali. Khetha uhlelo bese uhlala nalo. Izinhlelo ezihleliwe zinabantu ongabashayela ngocingo.

## Yimiphi imithetho yokuphepha?

- Ungabelani ngemininingwane yebhange, izinombolo zamakhadi noma amaphasiwedi encwadini.
- Ungathumeli imali, amakhadi esipho noma ama-cheque kumngane wokubhalelana, ngisho nasendabeni enodaba olusheshayo.
- Gcina ikheli lekhaya lingaziwa. Sebenzisa ibhokisi leposi lohlelo noma ikheli lenhlangano uma linikezwa.
- Umuntu uyanquma ukuthi yini ayabelana ngayo. Igama lokuqala nedolobha kuvame ukwanela.
- Gcina izincwadi isikhathi esithile, futhi utshele ilungu lomndeni noma yini ekukhathazayo.
- Bika noma yini esolisayo ohlelweni. Uma kubandakanyeke imali, xhumana nebhange lakho.

## Yini ongayithumela uma amagama enzima?

Incwadi ayidingi amagama amaningi. Zama:

- **Umdwebo noma upende,** nomugqa owodwa ngaphansi kwawo, njengokuthi, "Yile ngadi yethu ngoJuni."
- **Imbali noma iqabunga elicindezelwe.** Ikhadi le-[Press leaves and flowers](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/) lichaza ukuthi ungakucindezela kanjani encwadini enzima.
- **Isithombe nomugqa owodwa.** Khetha isithombe umuntu asithandayo, bese nibhala ichasiso ndawonye.
- **Ikhadi elenziwe ndawonye.** Ikhadi [Make a greeting card](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) limboza ikhadi elipheshulwe, izithombe ezimbalwa nomlayezo omfushane.
- **I-collage encane yezithombe.** Ikhadi [Make a memory collage](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/) lenza ikhasi elilingana nenvelopu.

Funda ngokuzwakalayo noma yiluphi uhlelo olutholile, bese uvumela umuntu akhulume impendulo.

## Umsizi angasekela kanjani ukubhala?

Hlalani ecaleni komunye nomunye bese nibuza imibuzo evulekile: "Ungathanda ukumtshela ini?" Bhala phansi ngokunembile amagama umuntu awashoyo, noma abe mafushane noma ephindwa. Funda incwadi kabusha kanye, bese ubuza ukuthi kukhona yini okufanele kushintshwe. Ukuze uthole okwengeziwe ngokubuza imibuzo evulekile, bheka [izinyathelo zokuqala ngemuva kokuxilongwa](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/). Vumela umuntu asayine ngegama lakhe noma ngophawu.

Amanye amakhaya enza incwadi ibe yingxenye yeseshini evamile [yokubhalela incwadi ndawonye](/activity/write-a-letter-together/), mhlawumbe ntambama yezinsuku zokusebenza. Izingcingo nevidiyo nazo zingaba ukwengeza okuhle, futhi [umhlahlandlela wamakholi wefoni nevidiyo](/together/staying-connected-from-afar/) uchaza ukuthi uzisetha kanjani. Ikhasi le-NHS [lemisebenzi](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) lidla amakholi evidiyo phakathi kwemisebenzi abantu abaningi bangaqhubeka besiyijabulela, futhi kuyisikhumbuzo esiwusizo sokuthi incwadi ayisona kuphela indlela yokuxhumana.
