Abantu abaningi abakhulu bathi "Angikwazi ukudweba" bese beyeka ngaphambi kokuqala. Leli khasi lenzelwe imindeni nabasebenzi bokunakekela abasiza abantu abakhulu abazizwa kanjalo, kubandakanya nabantu abaphila ne-dementia. Injongo ukuba imizuzu embalwa yokwenza izimpawu ndawonye, ngaphandle kwempendulo efanele. Akuyona isifundo sokudweba, futhi akudingeki ukuthi noma ubani abe muhle kukho.

## Yini ukwenza izimpawu, futhi kungani kuqala ngakho?

Ukwenza izimpawu kusho ukwenza imigqa, amachashazi, izigijimo nezigoqo ephepheni, ngaphandle kokuzama ukudweba into ethile. Siqala ngakho ngoba akukho mpawu engalungile. [Ikhasi lobuciko nomculo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) le-Alzheimer's Association liphawula ukuthi imisebenzi yobuciko ingaletha umuzwa wokufeza nenjongo, nethuba lokuzivezela. Ukwenza izimpawu kukunika lokho ngaphandle kokuhlolwa. Zama:

- imigqa emide kuwo wonke amaphepha ngesimaki esikhulu noma ikhalika
- izixhobo zamachashazi, ucindezela ngesiphetho sethuluzi
- izigoqo nezigqegqe, eyodwa ngesikhathi, nisebenzisa iphepha elifanayo ngokuphendukana

Uma izimpawu ezivulekile zizwakala zingakuphathi kahle, [Tear paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/) inikeza umphumela omuhle obukeka usuqediwe ngaphandle kokudweba neze.

## Ungadweba kanjani ngomculo?

Dlala ingoma enomculo ocacile noma umuzwa ocacile. Buza, "Lo mculo uyakhuphuka noma wehla?" Vumela umuntu adwebe ngesikhathi elalela, futhi nawe udwebe ndawonye naye. Izinyathelo eziphelele nenguqulo elula zisekhadini [Draw to music](/activity/draw-to-music/). Izingoma ezijwayelekile zingaba yimpendulo emnandi ikakhulu. Gcina ivolumu iphakathi nendawo, negumbi lithule, ukuze umsindo ungaphazamisi ingxoxo.

## Ungazama kanjani ukugeza ngemibala ye-watercolor?

Ukugeza ngemibala yindlela elula kakhulu yokuthola umphumela ngaphandle kokudinga ikhono lokudweba. Sebenzisa iphepha elinzima le-watercolor, liwumanzise ngamanzi ahlanzekile, bese uvumela umuntu aphathise ibhulashi elifakwe umbala ephepheni. Imibala iyasakazeka ihlangane yodwa. Ikhadi [Paint with watercolors](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/) linezinyathelo zokulungiselela. Sebenzisa upende ongenawo ubuthi, futhi gcina izitsha zamanzi zingafinyeleleki kunoma ubani ongazisela.

## Yiziphi izinkambo ezilula ongaziphinda?

Izitayela eziphindaphindekayo ziyaduduza ngoba isinyathelo esilandelayo sihlala sicacile. Dweba umugqa wezimo bese ucela umuntu ukuthi afake eyelandelayo. Noma gcwalisa igridi ngesimpawu esiphindaphindiwe, njengecork elicwiliswe kupende. Yima lapho umuntu eselungele ukuyeka. Ilitshe nalo lingapeyintwa ngendlela efanayo, ngedoti elilula noma igagasi eliphindaphindekayo; bheka [Paint a garden stone](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/).

## Ungasiza kanjani ngokubamba okuthambile?

Sebenzisa izinto ezinkulu: ama-crayon amakhulu, izimaki ezinkulu, noma ibhulashi elikhulu esibambweni esifushane. Goqa isibambo esincane nge-foam tape noma ibhande lenkanyeza ukuze kube lula ukusibamba. Bophelela iphepha etafuleni ngethephu yokufihla ukuze lingashayi. Iphepha elikhulu linika ingalo indawo eningi yokunyakaza. Uma ukubamba ithuluzi kubangela ubuhlungu, libeke phansi.

## Umsizi uqala kanjani umshayo ndawonye?

Hlala eceleni komuntu, hhayi ngaphambi kwakhe. Beka isandla sakho phezu kwesakhe, noma phezulu kancane, bese nidweba umshayo wokuqala ndawonye. Bese ukhipha isandla sakho uvumele aqhubeke. Abantu abaningi baqhubeka ngemuva kokuthi ukunyakaza sekuqalile. I-Alzheimer's Association ithi umuntu ophendula ukupeyinta angase adinge usizo ukuqala ukunyakaza kwebhulashi. Ngemva kwemishayo embalwa, buyela emuva bese ukhuluma ngalokho obona.

## Yini okufanele uyisho, nokufanele ukugwema?

Sho izinto ezivula impendulo ngaphandle kokuhlolwa:

- "Lowo mbala oluhle eduze kophuzi."
- "Ngitshele kabanzi ngalena."
- "Mbala muni okufanele ufake okulandelayo?"

Gwema imibuzo efana nokuthi "Yini lena?", izibongo ezifana nokuthi "Wenze kahle", ezingase zizwakale zingokwezingane, nanoma yiliphi iphimbo lokulungisa imigqa. Ngezinyathelo ezilandelayo ngemuva kokuxilongwa, bheka [izinyathelo zokuqala ngemuva kokuxilongwa](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/). Ikhasi le-NHS [lemisebenzi](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) lithi ezigabeni zakamuva imisebenzi ngokuvamile idinga ukulula futhi ingagxila ezinzwa. Ibhulashi elifakwe umbala, lithintwe ephepheni, lingase lanele.

Uma umuntu engenamdlandla namuhla, zama [ukugcwalisa ngemibala](/crafts/coloring-pages-for-adults/) ngelinye ilanga, noma nje hlalani ndawonye nikhulume. Ukuyeka kusenesikhathi kuyinto evumelekile.
