Ukwelapha ngezindlela zobuciko kuyinhlobo yezingcweti zezempilo yengqondo. Ngayinye isebenzisa uhlobo olwahlukile lobuciko ebudlelwaneni bezokwelapha: ubuciko, umculo, ukudansa nokunyakaza, umdlalo nezinkondlo. Ngayinye inokuqeqeshwa kwayo, futhi umelaphi ngamunye unesitifiketi esihambisana nensimu yakhe. Leli khasi lichaza izinhlobo, ukuthi ukwelapha okuhlanganisiwe kuhlanganisa kanjani izindlela, ukuthi zihlukile kanjani emisebenzini yansuku zonke yokudala, kanye nokuthi ucwaningo lwe-dementia lukhombisa ukuthini.

## Yiziphi izindlela ezinhlanu zokwelapha ngezindlela zobuciko?

[I-National Endowment for the Arts](https://www.arts.gov/initiatives/creative-forces/clinical-program/type-of-creative-arts-therapies) ichaza izindlela zokwelapha ngezindlela zobuciko. [I-National Coalition of Creative Arts Therapies Associations](https://nccata.org/) ihlanganisa izinhlangano zochwepheshe.

### Ukwelapha ngobuciko

Ukwelapha ngobuciko kusebenzisa ukwenza ubuciko, njengokupeyinta, ukudweba, ukugundaza noma ukwakha ngodaka, ngaphakathi kobudlelwane bezokwelapha. Umelaphi usebenza nomuntu nobuciko bakhe ukuze afinyelele izinhloso ezibekwe ohlelweni. Abelaphi bobuciko baqinisekiswa ngezinhlangano zabo zochwepheshe. Bheka [ukuthola nokuhlola umelaphi wobuciko](/activities/finding-an-art-therapist/) ukuze uthole indlela yokumhlola.

### Ukwelapha ngomculo

Ukwelapha ngomculo kusebenzisa umculo, njengokulalela, ukuhlabelela, ukudlala izinsimbi nokubhala izingoma, ukuze kufinyelelwe izinhloso zezokwelapha. [I-American Music Therapy Association](https://www.musictherapy.org/about/musictherapy/) ichaza le ngcweti. Ezweni laseMelika, abelaphi bomculo bathola isitifiketi se-MT-BC.

### Ukwelapha ngokudansa nokunyakaza

Ukwelapha ngokudansa nokunyakaza kusebenzisa ukunyakaza komzimba nokudansa ukuhlola imizwa nezinjongo. Umelaphi oqeqeshiwe uholela iseshini futhi ubheka ukuthi umuntu unyakaza kanjani, uphendula kanjani futhi uxhumana kanjani nabanye.

### Ukwelapha ngomdlalo

Ukwelapha ngomdlalo kusebenzisa ukudlala izindima, ukulandisa nezindlela zethiyetha ngaphakathi kwesinqumo sezokwelapha. Iholwa umelaphi womdlalo oqeqeshiwe.

### Ukwelapha ngezinkondlo

Ukwelapha ngezinkondlo kusebenzisa ukufunda nokubhala izinkondlo, nezinye izinhlobo zokubhala, ngaphakathi kobudlelwane bokwelapha. Iholwa umelaphi wezinkondlo oqeqeshiwe.

## Yini ukwelapha okuhlanganisiwe?

Ukwelapha okuhlanganisiwe kuhlanganisa izinhlobo ezimbalwa zobuciko eseshini eyodwa. Umelaphi angadlulela ekudwebeni kuye ezinkondlweni ezimfushane, bese kuthi emithanjeni yokunyakaza. Kuyindlela yokwelapha, hhayi umsebenzi weqembu ojwayelekile. Njengezinye izindlela zokwelapha ngezindlela zobuciko, yenziwa ochwepheshe abaqeqeshiwe.

## Kuhluke kanjani emculweni wokuhlabelela noma emini yobuciko?

Ukuhlabelela, iqembu lokupeyinta noma ikilasi lokudansa kuyimisebenzi yokudala. Kuyokujabulela, ukuba nomngane nokuthile okwenzayo ndawonye. Akukho injongo yezokwelapha, akukho ukuhlolwa, futhi akukho uhlelo lokwelashwa.

Umehluko usemqeqesheni nasenjongweni. Umelaphi wobuciko oqeqeshiwe uqeqeshelwe ukusebenza nezidingo zezempilo yengqondo nokuhlela ukuya ezinjongweni. Umholi weqembu ohlabelela izingoma zakudala neqembu lasendlini yokunakekela uyenza into enhle nehlanganisa abantu, kodwa lokho akuyona ukwelapha. Akukho okubi kokunye. Yehlukile nje.

Imihlahlandlela yethu [yomculo](/creative-calm/music-for-calm-and-connection/) nomhlahlandlela wethu [wezobuciko nezandla](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/) ichaza imisebenzi yokudala yasekhaya nasezindaweni zokunakekelwa. Imisebenzi efana [nokudweba ngomculo](/activity/draw-to-music/) noma [nokwenza incwadi yezingoma yomuntu siqu](/activity/make-a-personal-songbook/) iyimibono yokudala evamile, hhayi ukwelapha.

## Ucwaningo lwe-dementia lukhombisa ukuthini?

Ucwaningo lulinganiselwe. Ukubuyekezwa kwe-[Cochrane](https://www.cochrane.org/CD011073/DEMENTIA_art-therapy-people-dementia) ka-2018 kubheke ukwelapha ngobuciko kubantu abaphila ne-dementia. Kwathola izifundo ezimbili ezincane kuphela, kubantu abayi-88 ngokubalwa konke. Abahlaziyi baphetha ukuthi kwakungekho ubufakazi obanele bokutshela ukuthi ukwelapha ngobuciko kuyasebenza yini. Babubheka njengobuphansi kakhulu ngekhwalithi.

Lokho kuyisitatimende ngocwaningo, hhayi isizathu sokugwema ubuciko ojabulisayo. Ungasajabulela ubuciko nomculo ndawonye ngenxa yalokho okuyikho, ungalindele umphumela wezokwelapha. Ikhasi le-[Alzheimer's Association mayelana nomculo nobuciko](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) liyisiqalo sokufunda kabanzi. Ukuze uthole imibono efanele izigaba ezahlukene, bheka [umhlahlandlela wethu wokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/), oqhubeka kahle nomsebenzi wesithombe noma womculo.
