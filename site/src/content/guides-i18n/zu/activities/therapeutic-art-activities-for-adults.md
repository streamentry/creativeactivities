Lezi yimisebenzi yobuciko yokudala ephumulisayo yabantu abadala ongayizama ekhaya. Azilona ithherapi yobuciko, futhi azelashi, aphulukise noma avimbele noma yisiphi isimo. Ziyindlela ejabulisayo yokwenza okuthile ngezandla zakho, wedwa noma nomuntu omunakekelayo. Uma ufuna ukwazi ukuthi ithherapi yobuciko iyini njengomsebenzi, funda kuqala [ithherapi yobuciko noma umsebenzi wokudala](/activities/art-therapy-or-creative-activity/), bese ubuyela lapha ukuze uthole imibono.

## Yiziphi izimisebenzi ezinhle zokudala ephumulisayo yabantu abadala?

Umbono ngamunye ongezansi uchazwa ngemisho eyodwa noma emibili. Iningi lixhunywe ekhadini eliphelele lomsebenzi, elinezinyathelo, izinto ezidingekayo nezindlela ezilula zokwenza kube lula.

1. **Ukugeza imibala yamanzi.** Phonsa imibala ephepheni elimanzi bese uvumela isabalale. Akukho mphumela ongalungile. Bona [yenza imibala yamanzi](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/).
2. **Imosaic yephepha eladatshulwe.** Datshula iphepha elinemibala libe izicucu ezincane bese uzincamathisela ukuze wenze isithombe. Akudingeki izikelo. Bona [yenza imosaic yephepha eladatshulwe](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/).
3. **Amatshe egadini apenthiwe.** Penda itshe elibushelelezi ngemibala elula noma izitayela, bese uliyisa phandle lapho nonke ningalibona khona. Bona [penda itshe legadi](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/).
4. **Amaqabunga nezimbali ezicindezelwe.** Cindezela amaqabunga nezimbali ezithambile phakathi kwephepha ebhukwini elisindayo isonto elilodwa, bese uzisebenzisa ekhadini. Bona [cindezela amaqabunga nezimbali](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/).
5. **Ukuhlobisa izimbali.** Sika izinduku ezimbalwa bese uzibeka embizeni, eyodwa ngesikhathi. Bona [hlobisa izimbali ndawonye](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/).
6. **Ikholaji yezinkumbulo.** Namathisela izithombe nezithombe zamamagazini ekhadini, bese nikhuluma ngakho ngakunye njengoba niqhubeka. Bona [yenza ikholaji yezinkumbulo](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/).
7. **Ibhodi lokuthinta.** Namathisela izicucu zendwangu, uchungechunge nezinye izindwangu ekhadini eliqinile ukuze wenze into ongayithinta futhi ongayibheka. Bona [yenza ibhodi lokuthinta](/activity/make-a-texture-board/).
8. **Ukudweba ngomculo.** Vula umculo nonke aniwuthandayo bese uvumela imigqa nezimo zilandele isandiso. Bona [dweba ngomculo](/activity/draw-to-music/).
9. **Amakhadi okubingelela.** Goqa ikhadi, wengeze isithombe nomlayezo omfushane wabantu nonke abaziwayo. Bona [yenza ikhadi lokubingelela](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/).
10. **Isithombe ngezinkinobho.** Hlukanisa izinkinobho ngemibala bese uzinamathisela ekhadini. Gcina izinkinobho kude nalowo ongaziginya. Bona [yenza isithombe ngezinkinobho](/activity/make-a-button-picture/).

## Ngiyisetha kanjani isikhathi sokudala?

Lungisa konke ngaphambi kokuhlala phansi. Sika iphepha, thela amanzi, bese ubeka amabhulashi ngendlela yokuthi kungabi khona olinda ngoba befuna ukuzula izinto. Mboza itafula ngeshidi lepulasitiki noma ilaka elidala.

Hlalani eceleni komunye nomunye niqale umsebenzi wakho. Qala umugqa wokuqala ndawonye uma kusiza. Khuluma ngemibala, izindwangu nezinkumbulo njengoba niqhubeka, futhi gwema imibuzo yokuhlola efana nokuthi "Lokhu yini?" Ukuze uthole imibono eminingi ngezigaba, bona [umhlahlandlela wobuciko nezandla](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/), nokuze uthole izindlela zokwabelana ngemisebenzi, bona [indlela yokudlala ndawonye](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).

## Ngingayenza kanjani umsebenzi ube lula?

Iningi lalezi zinhlelo lingalenziwa lula ngezindlela ezintathu. Nikeza imibala noma izinto ezimbalwa. Yenza ukusika, ukuhlobisa noma ukunamathisela wena. Fingqa isikhathi sibe imizuzu eyishumi noma eyishumi nanhlanu.

Uma izandla noma amehlo ekhathala kalula, khetha iprojekthi ehlezi phansi, enkulu, njengokugeza imibala yamanzi ephepheni elikhulu. Gcina ukukhetha kuvulekile: umuntu angakhetha isihloko, imibala noma ukuthi uqale yini.

## Kufanele siyeke nini?

Yeka uma umuntu ebonakala ekhathele, engasathokozi, ehluleka noma ekhathazekile. Beka iprojekthi endaweni ephephile, bese unikeza okuthile okuphumulisayo, njengengilazi yetiye noma uhambo oluthulile. Umsebenzi ongaqediwe kahle kuhle, futhi ungawubuyela ngolunye usuku noma ungawubuyeli nhlobo.

Ukuyeka kusenesikhathi kuyisiphumelo esihle, hhayi ukwehluleka. Injongo ukuba nesikhathi esabelanwayo nesijabulisayo, futhi imizuzu embalwa yokwenza okuthile ndawonye ingase yanele.
