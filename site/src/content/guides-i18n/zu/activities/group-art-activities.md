Iseshini yobuciko beqembu inika isiyingi esincane sabantu into okuyenza ndawonye, ngesivinini esifanele umuntu ngamunye. Leli khasi lenzelwe abahleli bemisebenzi, abavolontiya nemindeni abaphethe itafula labantu abathathu kuya kwabayisishiyagalombili. Lichaza ukukhetha iphrojekthi, ukulungisa indawo, nokunika wonke umuntu ingxenye, kubandakanya nabantu abakhetha ukubukela.

Inothi elingaphambili ngamagama. [Ukwelapha ngobuciko](/activities/art-therapy-or-creative-activity/) yingcweti yezempilo yengqondo eyenziwa ochwepheshe abaqeqeshiwe, abaningi babo banezitifiketi zalo msebenzi. Leli khasi lihlobene nemisebenzi yokudala evamile. Akukho okwelapha lapha.

## Ukhetha kanjani umsebenzi wobuciko beqembu?

Khetha okudinga amakhono amancane, okunezinyathelo ezimbalwa, futhi okungayekwa noma yisiphi isikhathi. I-[activities guide](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) ye-Alzheimer's Association iyeluleka ukuthi imisebenzi ihambisane namakhono abantu nezintshisekelo zabo zangaphambili, igcinwe ilula, futhi igxile enqubweni kunasemphumeleni. Izinketho ezinhle zisebenzisa itafula elilodwa nezinto ongazihlanza emizuzwini embalwa. Ukuze uthole okwengeziwe ngokuqhuba amaqembu ezindaweni zokunakekela, bheka [umhlahlandlela wamaqembu asendlini yokunakekela](/crafts/craft-group-sessions/) nalo [umhlahlandlela wemibono yemisebenzi](/activities/ideas/).

## Igumbi netafula kufanele kulungiswe kanjani?

- Hlala abantu bebhekene, noma esiyingini esilula, ukuze wonke umuntu akwazi ukubona phakathi nendawo.
- Sebenzisa itafula elilodwa kubantu abangaphezu kwabane. Kwabayisishiyagalombili, sebenzisa amatafula amabili amancane eceleni komunye nomunye.
- Beka izinto zomuntu ngamunye endaweni efanayo njalo, ukuze bazi lapho izinto zikhona.
- Mboza itafula ngendwangu yepulasitiki, usebenzise ukukhanya okulinganayo okungenakukhazimula ephepheni.
- Gcina indlela ecacile eya emnyango, futhi unikeze isihlalo esinezingalo noma yimuphi umuntu odinga ukuvuka.
- Geza izandla ngaphambi nangemva, futhi gcina indwangu emanzi eduze.

## Kufanele wonke umuntu enze isiqeshana esisodwa noma esakhe?

Zombili zisebenza. Isiqeshana esabelwanayo, njengeketanga lephepha noma i-mosaic, sinika abantu isizathu sokukhuluma nomphumela abawenze ndawonye. Izingxenye ezimele zivumela umuntu ngamunye alawule isivinini sakhe. Amaqembu amaningi enza zombili: umuntu ngamunye wenza ithayela noma ikhadi, bese amathayela exhunywa ndawonye abe yisibonisi esisodwa ekugcineni. Buza ngaphambi kokubonisa umsebenzi womuntu, futhi ukhombise kuphela lapho lowo muntu efuna ukubonwa khona.

## Yiziphi izindima ezisiza abantu abakhetha ukubukela?

Akuzona zonke izinhlobo zabantu ezifuna ukubamba ibhulashi, futhi lokho kulungile. Nika izindima ezingokoqobo, hhayi ezimpisi:

- **Umkhethi wombala.** Phakamisa izimbiza bese ubuza ukuthi umbala olandelayo kufanele ube muphi.
- **Umgcini wethreyi.** Gcina iphepha elingeziwe nogamu eduze kweqembu.
- **Umfundi.** Funda isinyathelo esilandelayo, noma inkondlo emfushane phakathi kwezingxenye.
- **Umbukeli.** Ukubukela nokuphawula umsebenzi kuyindima egcwele. Yisho lokho ngokuzwakalayo ukuze ukungabi khona kungamenzi umuntu azizwe esuswe ngaphandle.

Nika umbukeli isihlalo esisesiyingini, hhayi ngasemuva kwegumbi.

## Uyiphelisa kanjani iseshini ngokuthula?

Nika isixwayiso esicacile ngaphambi kokuphela, ngezwi elithulile: "Sisele cishe imizuzu eyishumi." Bese uvumela abantu baqede umugqa noma ukugoqa, esikhundleni sokuyeka phakathi kokushaya. Beka umsebenzi oqediwe lapho wonke umuntu angawubona, noma uvumele umuntu ngamunye athathe isiqeshakhe sibuyele ekhaya. Bonga iqembu ngesikhathi sabo, hhayi ngomphumela.

Ukuyeka kusenesikhathi kuyinto evumelekile. Uma umuntu ethukuthela, misa, nikeza isihlalo esithule nesinkomishi setiye, bese uzama futhi ngolunye usuku. Iseshini eqeda ngengxoxo emfushane nomoya wokuthula iyiseshini enhle.
