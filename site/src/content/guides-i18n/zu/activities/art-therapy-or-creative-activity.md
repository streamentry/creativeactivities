Ukwelapha ngobuciko nomsebenzi wobuciko wokudala kungabonakala kufana ngaphandle. Zombili zisebenzisa iphepha, upende nodaka. Zihlukile ngenjongo, ngabaholi bazo nangokuqeqeshwa okuzisekela. Ukwazi umehluko kusiza ukukhetha uhlobo olufanele losizo, futhi kukugcina ungabizi intambama ejabulisayo yokupeyinta ngokuthi yisimo esingasiyo. Leli khasi lichaza umehluko nokuthi ungenzani ekhaya namuhla.

## Yini ukwelapha ngobuciko?

Ukwelapha ngobuciko yingcweti yezempilo yengqondo. Abelaphi bobuciko bayizingcweti eziqeqeshiwe ezisebenzisa ukwenza ubuciko ngaphansi kobudlelwane bezobungcweti, futhi basebenzela izinhloso ezibekwe nomuntu. Umsebenzi wabo ngokuvamile uyingxenye yohlelo olubanzi lwezempilo yengqondo noma lokunakekelwa.

Ezweni laseMelika, abelaphi bobuciko bangathola izitifiketi ezifana ne-ATR noma ATR-BC, ezidinga imfundo yesidanga esiphakeme nokuqeqeshwa okuhlolwayo. [I-American Art Therapy Association](https://arttherapy.org/credentials-and-licensure/) ichaza lezi zitifiketi. E-UK, amagama athi "art therapist" kanye "art psychotherapist" avikelekile emthethweni, futhi ochwepheshe kufanele babhalisiwe. [I-British Association of Art Therapists](https://www.baat.org/art-therapy/what-is-art-therapy/) ichaza le ngcweti.

## Yini umsebenzi wobuciko wokudala?

Umsebenzi wobuciko wokudala yinto owenza ngenjongo yokujabula nokuba nomngane. Awukho uhlelo lokwelashwa, akukho ukuhlolwa, futhi awunayo injongo yezokwelapha. Ukupeyinta isithombe ndawonye, ukugundaza iphepha ukuze wenze umsaiko noma ukwenza ikhadi lokuzalwa kuyimisebenzi yokudala.

Imisebenzi kulo mkhiqizo imisebenzi evamile yalolu hlobo. Yenzelwe isikhathi esihlanganyelwe, hhayi ukwelashwa. Ayelashi, ayiphilisi, futhi ayivimbeli noma yisiphi isimo, futhi ayithuthukisi inkumbulo.

## Ukwelapha ngobuciko nomsebenzi wokudala kuqhathaniswa kanjani?

| | Ukwelapha ngobuciko | Umsebenzi wobuciko wokudala |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ongumholi | Umelaphi wobuciko oqeqeshiwe nogunyaziwe | Ilungu lomndeni, umngane noma umholi womsebenzi |
| Injongo eyinhloko | Injongo yezokwelapha, ebekwe nochwepheshe | Ukujabula, ukuba nomngane nokuthile okwenzayo |

| Ubudlelwane | Ubudlelwane bobungcweti, bezokwelapha | Ubudlelwane bansuku zonke |
| Lapho kwenzeka khona | Emitholampilo, ezibhedlela, ezikoleni noma ezindaweni zabucwebi zomuntu ngamunye | Ekhaya, esikhungweni sokunakekelwa noma endaweni yomphakathi |
| Ngubani ongahlola | Izinhlangano zezelayisense noma zokubhalisa | Akekho odinga ukuhlola |

## Yini okufanele ufune umelaphi wobuciko nini?

Khuluma nodokotela kuqala. Udokotela angakutshela ukuthi ukudluliselwa kochwepheshe wezempilo yengqondo ogunyaziwe kuyinto enengqondo yini. Uma umuntu onakekelayo enokukhathazeka okuqhubekayo, ukucindezeleka, ukuhlukumezeka noma ezinye izinkinga zemizwa, ochwepheshe bangasiza ngezindlela itafula lobuciko okungenakwenza ngazo.

Ukuze uthole usizo lokuthola nokuhlola umelaphi, funda [ukuthola nokuhlola umelaphi wobuciko](/activities/finding-an-art-therapist/). Ukuze uthole incazelo elula yokuthi lobu bungcweti nemisebenzi yethu kuhlukile kanjani, bheka [ukwelapha ngobuciko nemisebenzi yokudala](/art-therapy-and-creative-activities/). Uma umuntu ekhuluma ngokuzilimaza, noma ukhathazekile ngokuphepha kwakhe, ungalindi. Xhumana nodokotela, nomugqa wosizo lwenkinga noma nezinsizeni eziphuthumayo ngokushesha.

## Yini ongayenza ekhaya namuhla?

Akudingeki umelaphi ukuze ujabulele ubuciko ndawonye. Khetha okuthile okulula, ukulungiselele kusengaphambili, bese uyeka noma nini lapho kungasaphathi kahle. Lezi zindawo ezinhle zokuqala:

- [Paint with watercolors](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/), uvumele imibala igeleze ephepheni elimanzi.
- [Make a torn paper mosaic](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/), ngaphandle kwezikelo nangaphandle kwendlela eyodwa efanele yokubeka izingcezu.
- [Make a memory collage](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/) ngezithombe nemifanekiso yemagazini.
- [Press leaves and flowers](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/) phakathi kwamakhasi encwadi enzima.

Ukuze uthole imibono eminingi, bheka [umhlahlandlela wethu wezobuciko nezandla](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/). Ubuciko bungaba yingxenye emnandi yesonto lakho. Akudingeki bubizwe ngokuthi ukwelapha ukuze bufanele ukwenziwa.
