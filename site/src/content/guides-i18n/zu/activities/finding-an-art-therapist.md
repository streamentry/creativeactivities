Ukuthola umelaphi wobuciko ofanele kudinga ukuhlola. Isihloko asifani zonke izindawo, futhi akuwona wonke umuntu onikeza iseshini zobuciko oqeqeshelwe ukwelapha. Leli khasi lichaza indlela yokuhlola izitifiketi e-United States nase-United Kingdom, ukusesha irejista esemthethweni, nokubuza ngaphambi kweseshini yokuqala. Libuye lichaze okungenzeka ekhaya ngenkathi usesha.

## Ubuza kubani kuqala?

Buza udokotela noma ochwepheshe wezempilo yengqondo ogunyaziwe. Bangakutshela ukuthi ukwelapha ngobuciko kufanele yini umuntu onakekelayo, futhi bangakunikeza isiphakamiso. Uma umuntu enokulahleka kwenkumbulo noma i-dementia, udokotela oyazi umlando wakhe angaphakamisa umelaphi onolwazi kulesi simo.

Uma ungakwazi ukufinyelela ochwepheshe ngokushesha futhi umuntu enosizi noma eyingozi, xhumana nodokotela, nomugqa wosizo lwenkinga noma nezinsizeni eziphuthumayo.

## Ngihlola kanjani izitifiketi e-United States?

Bheka izitifiketi ezinikezwa yi-Art Therapy Credentials Board. I-[American Art Therapy Association](https://arttherapy.org/credentials-and-licensure/) iyazichaza:

- **ATR** (Registered Art Therapist) idinga imfundo yesidanga esiphakeme sokwelapha ngobuciko nokuqeqeshwa okuhlolwayo emva kwesidanga.
- **ATR-BC** (Board Certified) itholwa ngokudlula ukuhlolwa kwezwe lonke, ngaphezu kwezidingo zemfundo nokuqeqeshwa.

Ezinye izifundazwe zikhipha nezelayisense kubelaphi bobuciko. Ibhodi lezelayisense lesifundazwe sakho lingaqinisekisa ukuthi umelaphi unelungelo lokusebenza lapho ohlala khona. Cela umelaphi anikeze igama lesitifiketi nenombolo yakhe, bese uyihlola ngokwakho.

## Ngihlola kanjani irejista e-United Kingdom?

E-UK, "art therapist" kanye "art psychotherapist" ngamagama avikelekile. Ochwepheshe kufanele babhaliswe ku-Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC). Ikhasi le-[GOV.UK lokubhalisa abelaphi bobuciko](https://www.gov.uk/art-therapist-registration) lichaza lesi sidingo.

Ukuze uhlole umelaphi:

1. Cela igama lakhe eliphelele nenombolo yakhe yokubhalisa.
2. Sesha ku-[irejista ye-HCPC](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/check-the-register/), esebenzisa ku-inthanethi mahhala.
3. Qinisekisa ukuthi igama nesihloko kuvumelana nalokho umelaphi akutshele kona.

Uma ungakwazi ukuthola umelaphi kurejista, ungabhukeli iseshini. Cela ayichaze.

## Yini okufanele ngiyibuze ngaphambi kweseshini yokuqala?

Uhlu olufushane lwemibuzo lukusiza uqhathanise izinketho:

- **Ulwazi.** Usebenze nabantu abakhulu yini? Nabantu abaphila ne-dementia? Bangaki?
- **Indawo.** Iseshini zenzeka kuphi: emtholampilo, esikhungweni sokunakekela, ekhaya lakho, noma ku-inthanethi?
- **Izindleko.** Iseshini eyodwa ibiza malini? Uyayamukela yini i-inshuwarensi noma ezinye izinsizakalo? Kukhona yini inkokhelo ehambisana nemali yomuntu?
- **Ifomethi.** Iseshini evamile ibukeka kanjani? Ithatha isikhathi esingakanani, futhi abantu bavame ukuba neseshini zingaki?
- **Umndeni.** Amalungu omndeni azohilelwa yini? Inqubekelaphambili izoxoxwa kanjani?
- **Ukuyeka.** Yenzekani uma umuntu engafuni ukuqhubeka, noma uma iseshini zikhathaza?

Umelaphi omuhle uzophendula lezi mibuzo ngokucacile. Uma uzizwa usheshiswa noma ucindezelwa, lokho kuyilwazi oluwusizo.

## Yini esingayenza ekhaya ngenkathi sisesha?

Ukulinda isiphakamiso akusho ukuthi akukho lokhu okungenzeka. Imisebenzi yokudala ekhaya ingaba yingxenye ejabulisayo yesonto lakho, futhi ayidingi ukubizwa ngokuthi ukwelapha. Ukuze uthole umehluko phakathi kwale ngcweti nemisebenzi yethu, bheka [ukwelapha ngobuciko nemisebenzi yokudala](/art-therapy-and-creative-activities/). Lezi zindawo ezilula zokuqala:

- [Draw to music](/activity/draw-to-music/), uvumele imigqa nemibala ilandele umsindo.
- [Make a greeting card](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) womuntu eniyaziyo kobabili.
- [Arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) embizeni etafuleni.

Ukuze uthole imibono eminingi efanele izigaba ezahlukene, bheka [umhlahlandlela wethu wezobuciko nezandla](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/). Ukuze uthole ukuthi ukwelapha ngobuciko kuhluke kanjani emsebenzini wokudala, funda [ukwelapha ngobuciko noma umsebenzi wobuciko wokudala](/activities/art-therapy-or-creative-activity/). Ukunakekela omunye umuntu nakho kudinga amandla, ngakho yenza isikhathi sokuphumula kwakho. Bheka [umhlahlandlela wethu wokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/creative-calm/creative-self-care-for-caregivers/).
