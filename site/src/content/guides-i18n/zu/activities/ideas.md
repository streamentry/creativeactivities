Umsebenzi omuhle kakhulu womuntu ophila ne-dementia ngokuvamile umsebenzi asazi ukuwenza, owenziwe kancane futhi wenziwe ngakanye nomuntu amethembayo. Ngezansi kunemibono engu-101, ehlelwe ngezinhlobo, ngayinye inokuthi ungayilungiselela kanjani nokuthi ungayenza kanjani ilula uma iba nzima. Awudingi izinto ezikhethekile, futhi awudingi ukuqeda lutho. Ntambama enhle ingaba yibhasikidi lamathawula eyodwa nenkomishi yekhofi ndawonye.

## Yiziphi izinto okufanele uzikhethe ngazo umsebenzi ofanele?

Khetha ngomuntu, hhayi ngohlu: qala ngalokho ayekwenza njalo nalokho anokukwenza ngokukhululeka namuhla.

- **Cabanga ngomsebenzi wakhe nezintshisekelo zakhe.** I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) iphakamisa ukuthi umuntu owayesebenza ehhovisi angathanda ukuhlunga izinhlamvu noma ukuhlanganisa izincwadi, nomlimi angathanda umsebenzi wegceke.
- **Qaphela ayekwenza ngokwemvelo.** Uma ubaba wakho eshanela amaqabunga ekuseni ngaphandle kokucelwa, khona-ke wakha lokho esikhathini sakho.
- **Hlela ngokucabanga imikhawulo.** Ukukhathala, ukubona, ukuzwa, izandla ezibuhlungu nokulinganisela konke kubalulekile.
- **Qaphela imizwa yakhe.** Imisebenzi enomsindo nenhlokoya ilungele abanye abantu, kanti abanye bayesaba.
- **Yinika inhloso.** Ukugoqa amanapkini esidlweni sesonto kuzwakala kungcono kunomsebenzi womzimba wokuzivocavoca ngawo wodwa.

### Isikhomba esijwayelekile ngezigaba

I-dementia ivame ukuchazwa ngezigaba zokuqala, zasemaphakathi nezakamuva. Lezi zilebuli ezingaqinile, hhayi amabhokisi, futhi abantu abaningi banezinsuku ezingcono nezinzima, ngakho hambisana nalokho obona namuhla. [Uhlu lwe-Alzheimer's Foundation of America ngezigaba](https://alzfdn.org/therapeutic-activities-for-3-main-stages-of-alzheimers-disease/) lulandela iphethini efanayo.

| Isihloko | Isigaba sokuqala | Isigaba sasemaphakathi | Isigaba sakamuva |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ongakubona | Ikakhulu uzenzela; ukuhlela, amagama amasha nezindawo ezinomsindo kuba nzima | Udinga usizo ekuqaleni nasezinyathelweni; amagama afika kancane | Udinga usizo emsebenzini wansuku zonke; izinzwa zibaluleke kakhulu |
| Imisebenzi evame ukulingana | Amakhadi, imidlalo yebhodi, ukupheka, ukulima, ukubhala idayari, uhambo, amaklabhu | Ukugoqa izingubo, ukuhlunga, izinyathelo zokupheka, ukucula, ama-albhamu wezithombe, ukuhamba okuholwayo | Umculo awuthandayo, ukuhlikihla izandla, izinto ezithambile zokubamba, ukufundelwa, ukuhlala ngaphandle |

## Uqala kanjani umsebenzi nomuntu one-dementia?

Qala kancane, qala naye, futhi yenza kube lula ukuthi athi yebo. Abantu abaningi basafuna ukubamba iqhaza kodwa kubanzima kubo ukuhlela noma ukuqala.

1. **Khetha isikhathi sakhe esihle,** ngokuvamile ekuseni.
2. **Beka konke phansi kuqala** ukuze ukwazi ukuqala masinyane.
3. **Cela usizo, hhayi umbukiso:** "Ungangisiza ngalezi?"
4. **Qala wena.** Goqa ithawula lokuqala bese uvumela naye ajoyine. Ukukhombisa kungcono kunokuchaza.
5. **Nikeza isinyathelo esisodwa ngesikhathi.**
6. **Thatha ingxenye enzima.** I-Alzheimer's Association iyeluleka ukuthi wena ukale izithako, bese unikeza isipuni ukuze axove.
7. **Vumela ukuthi kwenziwe ngendlela yakhe.** Amathawula agoqwe ngendlela ehlukile ayalungile.
8. **Yeka lapho kungasaba mnandi.** Ukuqhuzuka, ukuqhimama noma ukuthi "Angikwazi" yizimpawu zokushintsha noma zokuphumula.

Bheka futhi [ukuthi ningadlala kanjani ndawonye](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/) no-[izinyathelo zokuqala ngemuva kokuxilongwa](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).

## Imisebenzi yansuku zonke ekhaya

Imisebenzi yasekhaya ijwayelekile futhi iwusizo, okuyenza ibe yindawo yemvelo yokuqala. I-[National Institute on Aging](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ibala ukugeza izitsha, ukubeka itafula, ukushanela, ukuhlunga iposi nokugoqa izingubo phakathi kwemisebenzi abantu abangathanda ukuyenza.

1. **Goqa amathawula afudumele.** Thulula ibhasikidi elisuka kumshini womisa etafuleni bese ugoqa ndawonye. Kamuva, ukubheka nokubeka ngoluhlu nakho kuyasiza.
2. **Hlanganisa amasokisi.** Qala ngemibala ehlukile kakhulu. Kamuva, ukuhlunga ukukhanya nokumnyama kwanele.
3. **Beka itafula.** Nikeza uhlobo lwento olulodwa ngesikhathi. Beka indawo eyodwa wena njengesibonelo.
4. **Geza noma omisa izitsha.** Amanzi afudumele, isipontshi nezitsha ezingaphuli. Ukomisa uhlezi kulula emilenzeni.
5. **Sula itafula.** Nikeza indwangu emanzi ngemva kokudla. Kuwumsebenzi wangempela kwezandla ezingahleli phansi.
6. **Hlunga iposi.** Amakhathalogi nemeyili yokungabalulekile ibe "gcina" noma "phindaphinda". Gcina amabhili angempela ngaphandle.
7. **Sika amakhuphoni.** Izikele zomphetho ojikelezayo nezikhangiso zokuthenga. Kamuva, ukujikelezisa izinto ezithandwayo zokudla nakho kuyasiza.
8. **Hlunga izinhlamvu.** Hlunga ushintshi embizeni noma ugoqe ngamaphepha. Gwema izinto ezincane uma umuntu efaka izinto emlonyeni.
9. **Sula noma uthuthuke.** Umbhroshi olula esiseleni, noma i-duster ende esetshenziswa esihlalweni.
10. **Siza ukulungisa ukudla.** Ukugeza imifino, ukuhlephula ulethisi, ukugovuza. Wena uphatha izinkemba nokushisa.
11. **Bhaka iresiphi yomndeni.** Sebenzisa ikhadi elibhalwe ngesandla sakhe uma unalo. Kamuva, angaba yisihlolisisi.
12. **Wenze umsebenzi wehhovisi.** Goqa izincwadi, gcwalisa futhi ubeke amasitembu ezinvelopu, noma nibhale uhlu lokuthenga ndawonye.

## Umculo

Umculo ojwayelekile ungafinyelela abantu noma uma amagama enzima. Amathiphu omculo we-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) aphakamisa izingoma ezijwayelekile, akukho izikhangiso, nethelevishini icishwe. Bheka [imisebenzi yomculo](/creative-calm/music-for-calm-and-connection/).

13. **Yakha uhlelo lomculo lomuntu siqu.** Umculo wakhe wasemasheni, wasemshadweni, wesonto nesiko lakhe.
14. **Hlabelela naye.** Qala ukuhlabelela wena. Ukubhadazisa noma ukulalela kubalwa.
15. **Gcina ukunyakaza kwamaqhezu.** Hlanza izandla, shaya amadolo noma ushaye idrumu lesandla. Kusebenza uhlezi nasezigabeni zakamuva.
16. **Dansa.** Dansa kancane ekhishini, noma phuphuma uhlezi uphethe izandla.
17. **Dlala i-albhamu ephelele.** Irekhodi elithandwayo kusukela kuqala kuze kuphele, nekhofi nokungabi nokunye okwenzekayo.
18. **Khuluma ngomculo.** Mema izindaba, hhayi izinto eziqinisekisiwe: "Ngitshele ngalezo zingoma ozidansile."
19. **Bukela umdlalo womculo owafilimiwe noma ikhonsathi.** Okuthandwa yiso kwenza ukuhlwa okuhle. Ungayima noma nini.
20. **Dlala ithuluzi lomculo elijwayelekile.** Umpiyanisti wangaphambili angathanda izingoma ezilula; kamuva, amanothi ambalwa ndawonye.
21. **Lalela umculo ophilayo.** Ikhonsathi emfushane yomphakathi noma ukuhlolwa kwekwayala. Hlala eduze kwendlela yokuphuma.

## Ukukhumbula: izithombe, izinto nezindaba

Jabulelani esikhathini esedlule ndawonye ngaphandle kokukuhlola. Gwema "Uyakhumbula...?" no-"Lo ngubani?" bese usebenzisa izimemo ezivulekile. Bheka [imisebenzi yokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/).

22. **Bheka i-albhamu yezithombe.** Chaza lokho obona: "Yiyo le ngubo! Ngitshele ngalolo suku." Vumelana namagama noma yiwaphi asebenzisayo.
23. **Yenza ibhokisi lenkumbulo.** Ibhokisi lamashuzi elinezinto zempilo yakhe, njengebheji lomsebenzi noma ikhadi leresiphi. Khipha izinto okulodwa ngesikhathi.
24. **Qala incwadi yendaba yempilo.** Izithombe ezimbalwa ekhasini ngalinye namagama amakhulu. Isiza abasizi abasha ukwazi umuntu.
25. **Sebenzisa amakhadi engxoxo.** [Amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/) ahlanganisa isithombe nombuzo ovulekile.
26. **Yenza umdlalo wezithombe zomndeni.** [Izithombe ezifanayo](/games/photo-pairs/) zishintsha izithombe zakho zibe umdlalo wokuhlanganisa.
27. **Bukela amavidiyo wasekhaya noma i-TV endala.** Khulumani ndawonye esikhundleni sokubuza imibuzo.
28. **Bamba izinto zempilo yakhe yokusebenza.** Isikulufo, isistethoskopu, incwadi yokugcina izimali. Izandla zivame ukuholela indaba.
29. **Pheka isidlo sasebuntwaneni.** Angaxova, anambitha, noma ahlale nje ekhishini nawe.
30. **Bheka imephu yezindawo abahlala kuzo.** Landela imigwaqo ejwayelekile ku-athlas enkulu nalapho ukhuluma.

## Imisebenzi yobuciko

Ubuciko kuyisenzo, hhayi into eqediwe. Gcina izinto zabantu abadala zivikelekile. Bheka [ubuciko nezandla](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/).

31. **Peyinta ngama-watercolor.** Iphepha elinzima, imibala embalwa. Uma ukuqala kunzima, yiqondisa imishayo yokuqala ndawonye.
32. **Gcwalisa ngemibala imiklamo yabantu abadala.** Izimbali noma amakhwilithi, hhayi amakhathuni. Amakhasi wethu [okugcwalisa ngemibala](/games/coloring/) asebenza ku-screen noma ephrintiwe.
33. **Yenza i-collage.** Dweba izithombe zemagazini zezingadi, izimoto noma ukudla bese uzinamathisela. Kamuva, ukukhetha izithombe kwanele.
34. **Hlela izimbali.** Iqembu lezimbali esitolo sokuthenga nomphongolo. Kamuva, izimpande ezimbalwa embizeni.
35. **Hluka noma ukhwizele ndawonye.** Imigqa elula ngezinaliti ezinkulu. Kamuva, ukugoqa uzi kube amabhola.
36. **Yenzela umndeni amakhadi.** Faka isitembu, sayina noma uhlobise amakhadi okuzalwa nokuphulukiswa zabantu bangempela.
37. **Bumba udaka oluoma ngokwemvelo.** Cindezela isitsha esincane. Sebenzisa izinto ezingenawo ubuthi bese uhlala eduze.
38. **Hlikihla bese uqeda ukhuni.** Ibhokisi lezinyoni elisikwe kuqala nephepha lesihlikihli, ngaphandle kwezixhobo zikagesi.
39. **Bhala noma nikhulume incwadi.** Kumzukulu noma kumngane wakudala. Uma ukubhala kunzima, yena akhulume, wena ubhale.

## Ngaphandle nokulima

Umoya omusha nokukhanya kwemini kuyinkampani enhle. Bheka [imisebenzi yangaphandle nokulima](/together/outdoor-and-garden-activities/).

40. **Nisela izitshalo.** Ikani elincane nezimbiza ezimbalwa. Ukunisela nsuku zonke kunika usuku ukuhambisana.
41. **Tshala isitsha.** Sibeke etafuleni ukuze kungabi nomuntu oguqa ngamadolo. Izintshalana zilula kunembewu.
42. **Hlakula noma ususe izimpande.** Izigqoko zigqokiwe, indawo encane ngesikhathi.
43. **Hluza amaqabunga.** Indawo encane eyodwa. Kamuva, ukubamba isikhwama sivulekile yindima yangempela.
44. **Hamba ngendlela ejwayelekile.** Ezungeze ibhlogo noma eya ebhokisini lamaposi. Okufushane kuyanele.
45. **Hlala ngaphandle ndawonye.** Isihlalo esiseserenkeni ngosuku olufudumele. Bukela amafu noma abadlulayo.
46. **Dla izinyoni.** Gcwalisa isitsheli ndawonye bese ubukela esihlalweni sefasitela.
47. **Geza imoto.** Ibhakede, isipontshi, umsebenzi wasemini ofudumele. Ukuhlanza amakhava wamavili kuyisinyathelo esisodwa esihle.

## Imisebenzi yezinzwa

Ukuthinta, iphunga, ukunambitha, umsindo nokubona kubaluleke kakhulu njengoba i-dementia iqhubeka. I-[NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ikhankasela umculo, izinto zokubamba nokuhlikihla izandla kwizigaba zakamuva. Hlola izinto eziyisisekelo zokungezwani nengozi yokuhefuzela kusengaphambili; bheka [imisebenzi yezinzwa](/creative-calm/sensory-activities-for-adults/).

48. **Nika izandla ukuhlikihla.** Cela kuqala bese usebenzisa amafutha amancane. Khetha engenaphunga uma ungaqinisekile ngesikhumba noma ngezingozi zokungezwani.
49. **Zizwe amalaphu.** Ibhasikidi levelvet, i-corduroy, uboya nesatin ukuze uhlikihle futhi ugoqe.
50. **Nukela izimbali nekhofi.** Hlikihla imint noma i-rosemary entsha phakathi kweminwe. Gwema amaphunga anamandla uma kukhona izinkinga zokuphefumula.
51. **Hluza izinwele zakhe.** Kancane futhi kube mnene, uma ezithanda.
52. **Abelana ngendwangu yokugezela efudumele.** Ngezandla nobuso ngosuku olubandayo. Hlola izinga lokushisa kuqala.
53. **Hlola i-activity apron.** Izinkinobho ezinkulu nezipho zokuvala ezibanjelwe ngokuqinile. Hlola ukuthi akukho okungakhululeka.
54. **Nambitha into ayithandayo.** Isipuni esincane, ngendlela angayiphatha ngokuphepha.
55. **Dlala imisindo yemvelo.** Imvula, amagagasi noma ukucula kwezinyoni ngevolumu ephansi.
56. **Bukela okuthile okuhamba kancane.** Ithangi lezinhlanzi, ividiyo yemvelo noma ukuwa kweqhwa.

## Imidlalo nama-puzzle

Imidlalo isebenza uma kungekho sikhathi, amaphuzu noma uxhaniso. Uma eyodwa iqala ukuzwakala njengovivinyo, yiyeke. Bheka [ikhasi lemidlalo](/games/) no-[imisebenzi ye-dementia ekhaya](/dementia-activities/).

57. **Hlanganisa izithombe.** [Izithombe ezifanayo](/games/picture-pairs/) zivumela izithombe ukuthi zihlale zivulekile, ngakho kuyokubona, hhayi ukukhumbula.
58. **Thola isithombe esifanayo.** [Thola okufanayo](/games/find-the-same/) inikeza ukukhetha okungezimbili kuya kwezine. Xoxa ngesithombe ngasinye.
59. **Hlunga ngamabhasikidi.** [Hlunga ndawonye](/games/sort-together/) kufana nokuhlunga amasokisi ku-screen.
60. **Yenza i-puzzle yezingxenye ezinkulu.** Izithombe zabantu abadala, inani elilula lezingxenye. [I-picture puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) igcina isithombe sonke sibonakala. Okwengeziwe ku-[amapuzzle nemidlalo yetafula](/together/puzzles-and-table-games/).
61. **Dlala umdlalo wamakhadi wakudala.** Noma yimuphi ayewudlala iminyaka. Kamuva, ukuhlunga ideki ngombala kuyumdlalo ngokwawo.
62. **Dlala ama-dominoes.** Hlanganisa izinhlangothi. Kamuva, ukuhlela amathayela kwanele.
63. **Zama ukusesha amagama anezinhlamvu ezinkulu.** [Ukusesha amagama](/games/word-search/) kwethu kunamagama ngokuqondile nangokuphansi kuphela, futhi kuyaphrintwa.
64. **Qeda izaga ezijwayelekile.** [Qeda isaga](/games/finish-the-saying/) ikuvumela ukuthi ukhombise isiphetho noma nini.
65. **Dlala i-picture bingo.** [Picture bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) ilungele imindeni namaqembu. Siza ngokukhululeka ukumaka amakhadi.
66. **Dlala i-checkers.** Gcina imithetho ilula. Ukuhambisa izingcezu ngesikhathi uxoxa kubalwa.

## Ukunyakaza

I-NIA iphakamisa ukuhamba, ibhayisikili elimile, amabhande okwelula, ukuphonsa ibhola elithambile noma ibhaluni, ukuphakamisa amakani esobho nama-video wokuzivocavoca abantu abadala. Buza udokotela ngaphambi kokuqala into entsha, ikakhulu uma kunezinkinga zokulinganisela.

67. **Phonsa ibhaluni.** Hlalani nibhekene. Lihamba kancane, ngakho kulula ukulibamba.
68. **Landela ividiyo yokuzivocavoca ehlezi** eyenzelwe abantu abadala, nibe ngasohlangothini olulodwa.
69. **Zelula ngebhande.** Ukudonsa ngezingalo ngobumnene ngebhande elilula uhlezi.
70. **Phakamisa amakani esobho.** Ukuphakamisa izingalo kancane, mhlawumbe emculweni.
71. **Gibela ibhayisikili elimile.** Imizuzu embalwa elula, nawe eduze.
72. **Dansa ngamasikhafu.** Vuthela amasikhafu akhanyayo emculweni, uhlezi noma ume.
73. **Hamba unenhloso.** Ukuthumela incwadi noma ukuthenga ikhofi. Isizathu sokuphuma sizwakala sivamile.
74. **Yabelana ngokuphefumula okuthulile.** Yehlisani ndawonye ngokuphefumula [okuthulile](/games/calm-breathing/).

## Imisebenzi yokuxhumana

Ubuhlobo buyabaluleka noma amagama enzima, futhi amaqembu amancane alula kunamakhulu. I-NIA ithi umuntu angase angakhumbuli ukuthi umvakashi ungubani, kodwa kusengenzeka ajabulele ubuhlobo. Bheka [ukuvakashela](/together/visiting-someone-in-care/).

75. **Thola izivakashi ezimbalwa ngesikhathi.** Izivakashi ezimfushane egumbini elithulile, nezithombe zokubheka.
76. **Xhumana ngevidiyo nomndeni.** Amakholi amafushane, izwi elilodwa ngesikhathi. Bheka [amakholi wefoni nevidiyo](/together/staying-connected-from-afar/).
77. **Zama indawo yokuxoxela yenkumbulo.** Amahlangano avulekile kubantu abaphila ne-dementia nabanakekeli babo. Buza i-Area Agency on Aging yakho ukuthi kukhona yini eduze.
78. **Dla ngaphandle ngesikhathi esithulile.** I-NIA iphakamisa indawo yokudlela ejwayelekile nethulile ngamahora angajwayelekile, nethebula eduze kwendlu yangasese.
79. **Vakashela isikhungo sabantu abadala noma uhlelo lwansuku.** Ubuhlobo kubo nokuphumula kuwe.
80. **Chitha isikhathi nezingane.** Ukuvakasha okufushane nokuthulile kwezingane, noma ukufundela ingane encane incwadi yezithombe.
81. **Yabelana ngephephandaba ngekhofi.** Funda izihloko ngokuzwakalayo. Umkhuba ubaluleke kunezindaba.
82. **Siza omunye umuntu.** Goqa izinhlelo zesonto noma wenzele omakhelwane ikhadi.

## Imisebenzi yenkolo nokomoya

Landela isiko lomuntu siqu, noma lingabi lilo. I-NIA iphakamisa izinsizakalo ezijwayelekile, imibhalo, umculo ongcwele, umthandazo, ukuzindla nezimiso zemikhosi.

83. **Yiya esontweni elijwayelekile.** Khetha elithulile, hlala eduze kwendlela, futhi wazise abantu lapho ngodementia.
84. **Bukela isonto ku-inthanethi** ezinsukwini lapho ukuphuma kuyinkinga.
85. **Thandaza noma nifunde ndawonye.** Amagama ajwayelekile, afundwa kancane. Angajoyina ezingxenyeni azaziyo.
86. **Hlabelela amahubo noma umculo ongcwele** wesiko lakhe.
87. **Gcina amasiko emikhosi.** Amakhandlela asebenza ngamabhethri, isibusiso, izimpahla zokuhlobisa azisebenzisa njalo.
88. **Yabelana ngesikhathi sokuthula.** Hlalani egcekeni noma nibukele ukushona kwelanga ndawonye.

## Izilwane zasekhaya nezilwane

I-NIA iphakamisa ukudla, ukunakekela, ukuhamba, noma ukuhlala nesilwane sasekhaya nokusibamba.

89. **Hlikihla inja noma ikati elizolile** ngemizwazwana emide nethambile.
90. **Dla isilwane sasekhaya ndawonye.** Ukala wena; uthele yena.
91. **Hambisa inja ndawonye.** Ubamba ilesi.
92. **Bamba isilwane esihlezi esinqeni.** Ikati elizolile ebhulangeteni lingakhululeka kakhulu.
93. **Amukela izilwane ezivakashelayo.** Ezinye izindlu zokunakekela nezinhlelo zansuku zibamba izivakashi zezilwane. Buza endaweni.
94. **Nika isilwane esithambile esifana nesangempela.** Uma ungekho isilwane sasekhaya, abanye abantu bayathanda ukubamba ikati noma inja yeplastiki elingana nesangempela. Yinikeze ngokulula, ungayinikezi njengethoyizi, bese wamukela ukuthi cha.

## Imisebenzi yezikhathi zonyaka

Gcina amasiko, kodwa ngokulula. I-NIA iphakamisa ukuba ngabantu abangempela ngezinhlelo nokuba nendawo ethulile yokuphumula ngezikhathi zamaholide anokudlala okuningi.

95. **Hlobisa ngesikhathi sonyaka.** Ithanga, isongo noma izimbali ezintsha etafuleni.
96. **Yenza into emnandi yamaholide.** Gingqa inhlama noma uhlobise amakhukhi. Wena uphatha i-oven.
97. **Phachamisa izihlobiso.** Izihlobiso ezingaqhekeki, ezinikezwa ngayinye ngesikhathi.
98. **Shayela ukubona izibani.** Ukuhamba kwakusihlwa okufushane akucelii lutho ngaphandle kokuba nomngane.
99. **Tshala ama-bulb wasentwasahlobo.** Cindezela ama-bulb esitsheni ekwindla bese ubukela ukuphuma kwezithombo.
100. **Hluba ummbila noma ukhiphe amaphizi emagobolondweni.** Umsebenzi wasehlobo esiseleni nesitsha esisesinqeni.
101. **Bhala amakhadi amaholide.** Bona bayasayina, bafake isitembu noma bavale; wena uphatha amakheli.

## Uma umsebenzi ungasebenzi?

Misa, bese uzama okuhlukile ngesikhathi esizayo. I-NIA iphakamisa ukubheka ukuthi kukhona ukuqhuma yini, futhi kuvumelekile ukuthi umuntu abukele nje uma ekuthanda kanjalo. Ukuthi cha kuvame ukusho ukuthi umsebenzi unzima kakhulu, isikhathi asifanele, noma kukhona okunye okumhlupha, njengobuhlungu, ukulamba noma umsindo. Yenza kube ncane, zama ekuseni kakhulu, noma uqale wena bese ubona ukuthi uyajoyina yini. Bheka [uma besithi cha](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) no-[imisebenzi yasemini ethulile](/creative-calm/calm-afternoon-activities/).

Ukuze uhlele imisebenzi phakathi kokudla nokuphumula, bheka [imisebenzi yasemini ethulile](/creative-calm/calm-afternoon-activities/). Ngezinto ozilungiselele, bheka [okuphrintwayo mahhala ekhasini lemidlalo](/games/), futhi uma uhola iqembu, [imisebenzi yamaqembu ezindlu zokunakekela](/crafts/craft-group-sessions/). Ukuphumula kwakho nakho kubalulekile: bheka [ukuzinakekela komnakekeli](/creative-calm/creative-self-care-for-caregivers/).
