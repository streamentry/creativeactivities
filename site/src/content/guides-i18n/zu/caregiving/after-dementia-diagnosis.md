Ukutholwa yi-dementia kungaletha imibuzo mayelana nempilo, impilo yansuku ngansuku, ukusekelwa nekusasa. Abantu basabela ngezindlela ezahlukene: abanye bafuna isikhathi sokwamukela izindaba, kuyilapho abanye befuna ukuqala ukuhlela. Azikho indlela eyodwa efanele yokusabela noma isikhathi esifanele. Qala ngalokho okubalulekile kumuntu manje, bese ubuyela kwezinye imibuzo lapho esekulungele.

[Umhlahlandlela we-Alzheimer's Association ngempilo ngemva kokutholwa](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/life-after-diagnosis) uqondisa abantu ekunakekelweni okulandelayo, ekusekelweni kwendawo nasekuhleleni isikhathi esizayo. [Iseluleko se-Dementia Australia ngokubhekana nokutholwa](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/coping-your-diagnosis) siqokomisa ukuthi abantu basabela ngezindlela ezahlukene futhi bangacela usizo uma befuna. Leli khasi liyisiqalo, hhayi uhlelo lomuntu lwezempilo, lwezomthetho noma lwezezimali.

## Yisiphi isinyathelo esisodwa esiwusizo?

Khetha umbuzo owenza leli sonto libe lula kakhulu. Kungaba ukuthola ukuthi ubani ozohlela ukunakekelwa komuntu okulandelayo, ukubuza lapho kungatholakala khona ulwazi oluthembekile mayelana nokutholwa kwakhe, noma ukuthola umuntu amethembayo okungakhuluma naye. Umuntu angase afune ukuhola, acele omunye ukuthi asize, noma alinde ngaphambi kokuthatha isinqumo.

Ngesikhathi sokuvakasha okulandelayo, bhala phansi imibuzo embalwa ndawonye uma umuntu efisa. Ungabuza ukuthi ukutholwa kusho ukuthini, yini engakaqinisekiswa, nini kufanele kulandelwe, nokuthi kufanele uxhumane nobani uma kuvela enye inkinga emisha. Cela udokotela achaze noma yiliphi igama noma iseluleko esingacacile. Ukubhala phansi ngaphambi kokuvakasha kungasiza.

Umuntu akudingeki afunde yonke into mayelana ne-dementia ngesikhathi esisodwa. Khetha ulwazi oluthembekile mayelana nokutholwa akutholile, bese ubhala phansi imibuzo eyovela kamuva. Qaphela izithembiso, izinkulumo zokuthi kukhona ukwelashwa okuphulukisayo, nezeluleko ezithi wonke umuntu kufanele alandele ukwelashwa noma uhlelo olufanayo.

## Singathola kuphi ukusekelwa?

Buza umuntu ukuthi yiluphi uhlobo lwesekelo olungasiza nokuthi ufuna ubani abandakanyeke. Umngane noma ilungu lomndeni elithembekile lingaba yingxoxo enhle yokuqala. Abanye bakhetha iqembu lokusekela, umeluleki, inhlangano yendawo ye-dementia noma ingxoxo nethimba lakhe lokunakekela. Inketho elungile incike kumuntu nasekutholakaleni eduze.

Ukusekelwa kungokomuntu otholwe yi-dementia kanye nomndeni nabangane. Umnakekeli angase adinge ukuphumula okwesikhashana noma usizo emisebenzini yansuku ngansuku; umuntu angafuna ulwazi noma ithuba lokuhlangana nabanye abaphila ne-dementia. Lezi zidingo zingahluka. Buza ngaphambi kokuhlela iqembu, ukuhlela isikhathi sokuhlangana, noma ukwabelana ngemininingwane yomuntu siqu.

Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuzinakekela komnakekeli [unemibono yokucela abanye ukuthi babambe iqhaza ekusekelweni](/creative-calm/creative-self-care-for-caregivers/). Uma umuntu efuna usizo olengeziwe emini, imibuzo mayelana nokunakekelwa esikhathini esizayo ngezansi ihlanganisa lokho okufanele kubuzwe ngezinsizo zendawo.

## Umuntu angahlala kanjani eyingxenye yezinqumo?

Ukutholwa okwodwa akutshongo ukuthi umuntu ungakhetha ini noma ufuna ukubamba iqhaza kanjani. Buza umuntu ngqo. Mnike isikhathi, chaza ukukhetha ngolimi olulula, bese ubuza ukuthi yiluphi usizo olwenza ingxoxo ibe lula. Ezinye izinqumo zingase zibe lula ukuzixoxa endaweni ethule noma ezingxoxweni ezimbalwa.

Uma umuntu efuna ukuhlela impilo yesikhathi esizayo, izezimali noma indawo ahlala kuyo, qala ngalokho okubalulekile kubo nabantu abamethembayo. [Umhlahlandlela we-Dementia Australia wokuhlela](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead) uweluleka ukuxoxisana ngezifiso nabantu abathembekile nochwepheshe abafanele. Imibhalo yezomthetho nemithetho yokuthatha izinqumo ihluka ngendawo, ngakho buza ochwepheshe basendaweni abaqinisekisiwe ngaphambi kokuncika kumhlahlandlela jikelele. Ngosizo lwemali yansuku ngansuku nokukhathazeka mayelana nokukhwabanisa, xhumana nebhange lakho noma umeluleki oyethembayo.

### Yini ongayibuza engxoxweni yokuqala mayelana nokunakekelwa esikhathini esizayo?

Buza ukuthi umuntu ufuna ukukhuluma manje yini, bese uletha isihloko esisodwa ngesikhathi. [I-Alzheimer's Association incoma izingxoxo ezimbalwa ezincane, ulimi oluhlanzekile nokulalela izifiso, izinkolelo nezinqubo zokucabanga zomuntu](https://www.alz.org/professionals/health-systems-medical-professionals/management/advanced-care-planning). [I-Alzheimer's Society iphawula ukuthi abantu bahluke ngokuzwa beselungele ukucabanga ngakusasa](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/planning-ahead). Kulungile ukumisa isikhashana nokubuyela ngolunye usuku.

Uma umuntu efuna imibuzo yokuqalisa ingxoxo, zama enye yalezi:

- "Yiziphi izingxenye zansuku zakho ozifisa kakhulu ukuzigcina?"
- "Ufuna ubani ahlanganyele uma sikhuluma ngokunakekelwa esikhathini esizayo?"
- "Yini esenza uzizwe ukhululekile nehlonishwa uma udinga usizo?"
- "Kukhona okufunayo ithimba lakho lokunakekela lilwazi mayelana nalokho okubalulekile kuwe?"
- "Ungathanda ngibhale phansi lokho okushilo, nokuthi ubani okufanele akubone?"

Lalela ungacelanga isinqumo masinyane. [I-National Institute on Aging ichaza izingxoxo ezinencazelo mayelana nezinqondo nezifiso njengengxenye ebalulekile yokuhlela ukunakekelwa kusengaphambili](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf). Uma umuntu evuma, qopha amazwi akhe, usuku nabantu ababekhona. Cela imvume ngaphambi kokwabelana nencwadi yokuqopha. Lezi zinqophe zingasiza izingxoxo zesikhathi esizayo, kodwa azingona amadokhumenti omthetho noma imiyalo yezokwelapha.

Ungenzi imibono ncamashi ngokuncike kokutholwa kuphela noma engxoxweni eyodwa. Izifiso zomuntu zingashintsha, futhi angacela ukumisa noma ukubuyela esihlokweni ngokuhamba kwesikhathi. Mbandakanye umuntu ezinqumweni ezimthinta kakhulu njengoba efisa.

## Ngubani okufanele awazi ngokutholwa?

Umuntu unquma ukuthi uzotshela abanye yini, nini nokuthi kanjani, ngaphandle uma kukhona isimo esithile sokuphepha noma somthetho esidinga iseluleko sochwepheshe. Buza ukuthi ufuna ukutshela bani nokuthi ufuna laba bantu bazi ukuthini. Angakhetha umuntu oyethembayo kuqala, noma angathanda ukungabelani kabanzi.

Uma umuntu efuna usizo engxoxweni, buza ukuthi ufuna ukuthi usho ukuthini nokuthi ufuna ukuba khona yini. Yabelana kuphela ngolwazi avumele ukuthi lwabelwane. Xoxa ngakho nodokotela, umsebenzi womphakathi noma omunye ochwepheshe oyethembayo uma ungaqinisekile.

## Impilo yansuku ngansuku ingaqhubeka kanjani ngenkathi nizijwayeza?

Gcina izinjwayelo nemisebenzi ebalulekile kumuntu lapho sekusenza azizwe ekhululekile khona. Ukutholwa akusho ukuthi zonke izithakazelo, izizathu zokuphuma nezindima ezindlini kufanele zime. Uma into ithatha isikhathi eside, buza umuntu ukuthi ufuna ukushintsha ini. Ukuvakasha okufushane, umculo ojwayelekile, usizo kwesinyathelo esisodwa noma isikhathi sosuku esihlukile kungase kumlungele kangcono.

Umuntu angase futhi adinge isikhathi sokuqonda izindaba ngaphandle kokuxoxa ngezinhlelo. Qhubeka uhlola kodwa ungaguquli yonke ingxoxo ibe yinhlangano yokunakekela. Kulungile ukuphumula ekuhleleni futhi nihlale ndawonye ngendlela ejwayelekile.

## Yini ukuthi sicele ingcweti nini?

Buza udokotela womuntu imibuzo ethinta ukutholwa kwakhe, impilo yakhe, imithi noma izimpawu zakhe. Ezinqumweni zomthetho nezezimali, khuluma nochwepheshe abaqinisekisiwe abaqonda imithetho endaweni ahlala kuyo. Iwebhusayithi jikelele ayikwazi ukukutshela ukuthi ukwelashwa, idokhumenti noma isevisi ilungele umuntu othile.

Ukudideka okuzumayo noma ushintsho olusheshayo lwezempilo kudinga usizo lwezokwelapha ngokushesha. Umhlahlandlela we-[NHS wokudideka okuzumayo](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) uthi kufanele uthole usizo lwezokwelapha masinyane. Sebenzisa uhlelo lwezimo eziphuthumayo noma lokwelashwa okusheshayo lwendawo lapho umuntu ahlala khona; ungacabangi ukuthi ushintsho olusheshayo kuyingxenye yesimo sakhe ajwayelekile.

Uma ukuhlela kukhathaza kakhulu, khetha umuntu oyethembayo noma isevisi eyodwa ukuze isize ihlukanise imibuzo ibe yizihloko ezidingeka manje nezezikhathi ezizayo. Umuntu angasetha isivinini futhi anqume ukuthi yini afuna usizo ngayo. Ukutholwa kuyiphuzu lolwazi elibalulekile, kodwa akuchazi izithakazelo zomuntu, ubudlelwano bakhe noma impilo yakhe yansuku ngansuku.
