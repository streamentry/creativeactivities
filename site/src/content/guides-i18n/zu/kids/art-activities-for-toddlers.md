Abantwana abancane bayakwazi ukwenza ubuciko. Bayakwenza izimpawu, bathele amanzi, bakhiphe izitika bese bebheka ukuthi kwenzekani kulandela. Lokhu akudingi umphumela ophelele, futhi iningi lalo lingenziwa ngezinto ezimbalwa ezilula kanye nomuntu omdala eseduze. Leli khasi lenzelwe abazali, ogogo nomkhulu nabanakekeli babantwana abancane, cishe abaneminyaka engu-1 kuya ku-3. Libhekana nemibono emithathu: amakrayoni amakhulu, ukupeyinta ngamanzi, nokudlala ngezitika.

Lezi yimibono yokuzama, hhayi izinhloso okufanele zifinyelelwe. Yilowo nalowo ngane uhlukile, futhi umntwana omncane ofuna ukubamba ikrayoni kuphela, noma othanda ukuthela amanzi kodwa angapeyinti ngawo, usenza umsebenzi kahle.

## Ubani lezi zimbono zifanele?

Lezi zimisebenzi zifanele abantwana abancane abathanda ukubamba izinto, ukunyakaza ngezingalo nokubukela ukuthi yini eshintsha. Zilungele kakhulu abantwana abaneminyaka engaba ngu-1 kuya ku-3, nomuntu omdala ehlezi eduze kwabo. Abanye abantwana abancane bathanda umbono owodwa bese belahlekelwa yisithakazelo ngezinye, futhi lokho kujwayelekile. Nikeza umbono owodwa ngesikhathi, bese ugcina isikhathi sifushane.

## Ukuphepha kuqala

Funda lesi sigaba ngaphambi kokuqala noma yimuphi umsebenzi.

- **Hlala ngaphakathi kobude besandla.** Abantwana abancane bayasheshisa. Gcina isandla esisodwa eduze nengane kuwo wonke umsebenzi, futhi ungashiyi ingane yodwa namanzi.
- **Qaphela ukuvinjwa.** Abantwana abancane bafaka izinto emlonyeni. Isikhungo se-Nemours KidsHealth sokuvinjwa sishiya kuhlu lwezingozi ezivamile zasendlini ibhaluni elingaphumile, izinhlamvu, izinto zobuciko nezingxenye ezincane zezidlalo. Njengokuhlola okulula, noma yini engena esiphongolweni sephepha lasendlini yangasese ilincane kakhulu kumntwana ongaphansi kweminyaka engu-3.
- **Shiya izingxenye ezincane.** Kwabangaphansi kweminyaka engu-3, ungasebenzisi amaphuzu, izinkinobho, amabhontshisi omile, izingcezu ezincane zobuciko noma isitika esincane noma noma yiliphi ikhava elikhululekile.
- **Awukho amabhaluni.** Ungamnikezi umntwana ongaphansi kweminyaka engu-3 amabhaluni, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi enziwe nge-latex noma nge-foil. Izicucu zebhaluni eliqhume ziyingozi yokuvinjwa.
- **Khetha izinto ezingenabuthi, ezigezekayo.** Funa ilebula elicacile elithi engenabuthi. I-Art and Creative Materials Institute isebenzisa isitembu se-Approved Product ezintweni zobuciko ezibukisisiwe, nesitembu se-Cautionary Labeling lapho kukhona isexwayiso. Funda noma yiliphi ilebula ngaphambi kokusetshenziswa kokuqala.
- **Hlola ukungezwa.** Buza umndeni ngaphambi kokusebenzisa noma yini equkethe ukudla, njengensimbi yokudla. Abanye emindeni bagwema imibala yokudla, ngakho sebenzisa upeyinti ogezekayo noma umbala ongewona owokudla esikhundleni se-food coloring.
- **Gcina izinto kude nomlomo.** Susa izingcezu zamakrayoni, isisekelo sezitika namalahle ephepha uma zingasasetshenziswa.

## Amakrayoni amakhulu ephepheni elikhulu

Amakrayoni amakhulu ayalula ukubamba futhi alula ukwenza ngawo izimpawu, yingakho alungele kakhulu abantwana abancane.

**Okudingekayo:** amakrayoni amakhulu amabili noma amathathu, angenabuthi, agezekayo; iphepha elikhulu noma ingemuva lomqulu wephepha lodonga; ithiyipu yokubopha; ithreyi noma indawo ebanzi phansi; amaswabhu amanzi wezandla.

**Izinyathelo:**

1. Bopha iphepha libe ngqo etafuleni noma phansi ukuze lingashelelezi.
2. Hlala eceleni kwengane yakho. Yenza umugqa omude ephepheni, bese uchaza ongakwenzayo: "Phansi, bese ngakwesokudla."
3. Nikeza ingane ikrayoni elilodwa. Vumela yenze izimpawu njengoba ifisa.
4. Uma ilingisa wena, qhubeka. Uma ifaka ikrayoni emlonyeni, liyithathe kancane bese unikeza elinye.
5. Qamba imibala njengoba iqhubeka: "Lokhu kubomvu." Chaza ngomunwe isimpawu bese uthi, "Lokhu kungumugqa omkhulu."
6. Yeka lapho ingane iyeka. Hlanza izandla ndawonye bese ushiya iphepha ukuze libukwe kamuva.

**Inguqulo elula:** Beka iphepha ethreyini enezinhlangothi eziphakanyisiwe. Faka ikrayoni esandleni sengane bese ubamba isandla sakho phezu kwesakhe emashiwongeni okuqala, bese uyayeka.

**Inguqulo ethulile:** Sebenzisa umbala oyodwa kuphela. Hlala phansi nephepha elibekwe phezu kwekhushini, bese wenza izimpawu ezithulile ngenkathi ingane ibukela.

## Ukupeyinta ngamanzi

Ukupeyinta ngamanzi yinketho enhle engangcolisi. Izimpawu ziyavela, zishintsha bese ziyanyamalala, futhi akukho okudingeka kuhlanzwe ngelaphu.

**Okudingekayo:** ibhakede elijulile noma isitsha samanzi; ibhulashi elikhulu elithambile noma isipontshi esinesibambo esijiyile; udonga, uthango, indawo eyakhiwe noma ishidi elikhulu lekhadibhodi elingamanzisa; ilaphu lasesitofu.

**Izinyathelo:**

1. Khetha indawo eyisicaba engaphandle, njengendawo eyakhiwe noma udonga lwegadini, noma sebenzisa ikhadibhodi ngaphakathi ethreyini.
2. Gcwalisa isitsha ngomthamo wengxenye eyodwa kwezintathu. Gcina sisembusweni wakho, singaphakathi kobude besandla.
3. Cwilisa ibhulashi noma isipontshi, bese ukhombisa ingane ukuthi ingakucindezela kanjani phezu komhlaba. Vumela yenze ngendlela yayo.
4. Bheka izimpawu zomile zinyamalale. Buza, "Amanzi aye ephi?" bese uvumela ingane iphendule ngendlela ethandayo.
5. Gcwalisa kabusha ibhulashi uma lomile, bese unikeza ibhulashi noma isipontshi sesibili uma ingane ifuna ukuthatha ithuba ngelinye ithuluzi.
6. Lapho ingane isilungele ukuyeka, chitha amanzi bese uhlanza ibhulashi.

**Inguqulo elula:** Sebenzisa isipontshi esinesibambo esijiyile, esilula ukusibamba kunebhulashi elincane. Cwilisela ingane uma kudingeka bese uyibeka phezu komhlaba.

**Inguqulo ethulile:** Vumela ingane ithele amanzi esitsheni esincane kuthreyi ngenkathi ubamba isitsha sizinzile. Ukuthela kuwumsebenzi ophelele ngokwawo.

## Ukudlala ngezitika

Izitika zingaba yindlela enhle yokwenza izithombe nokufundisa ukuthatha nokubeka izinto phansi. Kodwa kubantwana abancane, izitika zidinga ukunakekelwa.

**Okudingekayo:** izitika ezimbalwa ezinkulu, ezingenabuthi, ezikhishwa kalula; ishidi lephepha elijwayelekile noma iwindi; ithreyi yeshidi lezitika.

**Izinyathelo:**

1. Khipha isitika esisodwa ushidi bese usinika ingane.
2. Msize ukucindezela endaweni yephepha, noma ewindini ngaphezulu kwebanga lengane.
3. Qamba izimo: "Kuyisiyingi," noma "Yinkanyezi."
4. Namathisela ezinye ezimbalwa, bese uvumela ingane ikhethe lapho kumele kuye khona ngakunye.
5. Thatha ishidi lezitika uma umsebenzi uphelile, bese ulibeka ngaphandle kokufinyeleleka.

**Inguqulo elula:** Khipha isitika ngaphambilini, bese usibeka ephepheni ukuze ingane ikwazi ukusicindezela. Lokho kususa ingxenye enzima.

**Inguqulo ethulile:** Nikeza izitika ezintathu kuphela. Umsebenzi omfushane onesiphetho esicacile ungaba nokuthula kuneshidi eligcwele izinketho.

Ungasebenzisi amashidi ezitika ezincane nabantwana abancane. Izitika ziyizingxenye ezincane, futhi iphepha elisekuphazamisekeni lingaba yingozi uma lidliwa.

## Ukukwenza nogogo noma nomkhulu

Ogogo noma umkhulu uvame ukuba ngumlingani omuhle kulolu hlobo lokudlala. Bangahlala eceleni kwengane, bapeyinte iphepha labo ngebhulashi lamanzi lefanayo, bese bevumela ingane ibalingise. Ogogo noma umkhulu nabo bangaba nesikhathi sokuthula, okuthandwa yiningi labantwana abancane. Ukuze uthole imibono engeziwe yogogo nezinzukulwane okungazama ndawonye, bheka ingxenye ethi [imibono yokwenza ndawonye](/together/).

Abanye ogogo nabakhulu bakhathazeka ngokungcola noma ngokuvinjwa. Bonise leli khasi ngaphambi kokuqala, bese nivumelana ukuthi ubani uzobheka ingane ngesinyathelo ngasinye.

## Uma kungasebenzi

- **Ingane ifaka konke emlonyeni.** Yima, bese unikeza into enkulu, ephephile ukuze ibambe esikhundleni. Zama umsebenzi futhi ngolunye usuku, lapho ingane sesiyeke ukufaka izinto emlonyeni.
- **Ingane ilahla amakrayoni noma ibhulashi.** Lokhu kuvame ukwenzeka. Thatha izinto, bese unikeza elilodwa ngesikhathi. Yeka isikhathi uma ukulahla kuqhubeka.
- **Ingane ikhala noma ihamba.** Vumela ihambe. Nikeza umsebenzi futhi kamuva ngosuku noma ngelinye ilanga, noma hlala eduze bese uzama wena ukupeyinta amanzi odongeni.
- **Ingane inentshisekelo isikhashana kuphela.** Lokho kuyisikhathi esihle. Chaza ngokwenzile bese uqeda.

Uma ungaqiniseki ukuthi into iphephile yingane yakho, hlola ilebula, bese ubuza udokotela wengane yakho ngombuzo wezempilo.
