Ikholi evela kude ingaba yisikhathi esihle kakhulu sikamuntu ngeviki, kodwa futhi ingazwakala inzima. Abantu bavame ukubuza ukuthi, “Unjani?” nokuthi, “Wenzeni namuhla?”, okucela umuntu akhumbule abike. Leli khasi linikeza indlela ehlukile. Hlela into encane eyodwa yokwabelana ngayo, bese ikholi ihlale ngalokho. Limboza amakholi evidiyo nawefoni, izincwadi, imilayezo eqoshiwe nemiklamo eyabelwanwa ngeposi, bese libheka indlela umndeni okude ongasekela ngayo umuntu ohlala eduze.

## Imisebenzi yekholi levidiyo

Ividiyo isebenza kangcono lapho kukhona into ecacile yokubukela noma yokwenza ndawonye.

- **Khombisa nokutshela.** Hambisa ikhamera kancane egadini lakho, azisa inja, khombisa into opheka yona, noma phakamisa umdwebo. Chaza ongakubona ngamagama alula bese uyeka ukuze kube nokusabela.
- **Bukela izithombe.** Phakamisa isithombe esiphrintiwe ekhamerani, noma yabelana ngoskrini wakho ukhombise izithombe ezimbalwa ezinkulu ngakunye. Thi, “Bheka ingubo kaGogo,” esikhundleni sokuthi, “Ngubani lo?”
- **Funda ngokukhala.** Ipoyema elifushane, ihubo, isigaba esithandwayo noma incwadi evela kubantu bomndeni inika umuntu into ethulile yokulalela.
- **Cula noma lalela.** Cula iculo alaziyo, noma dlala irekhodi elithandwayo ohlangothini lwakho. Ukucula kulandela kulula kunokukhuluma.
- **Pheka iresiphi efanayo.** Lungisa ikholi ezindlini zombili. Umuntu oseduze angaxova noma anambitha ngesikhathi uwulandela ikhadi lakhe lendabuko lokupheka.
- **Bukela into efanayo ngesikhathi esifanayo.** Iklipu elifushane, uchungechunge lwakudala lwethelevishini noma umdlalo kungabukwa emakhaya ahlukene ngesikhathi nixoxa.

Uma umuntu eseduze, umsizi angavula umdlalo ku-tablet bese ethinta ngesikhathi uxoxa nawe. Imidlalo esebenza kahle eskrinini esabelwanwa yi-[qeda isisho](/games/finish-the-saying/), [amapheya ezithombe](/games/picture-pairs/), [amakhadi engxoxo](/games/conversation-cards/) nemidlalo [amapheya ezithombe zakho](/games/photo-pairs/), esebenzisa izithombe ozikhethele zona ngedivayisi yakho.

## Amakholi wefoni

Umhlahlandlela we-Dementia Australia [wokuvakasha](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/staying-connected/visiting-someone-dementia) uncoma ukushaya ikholi ngesikhathi esifanayo nsuku zonke, futhi ungaphuthumi, ngoba abantu bangathatha isikhathi ukuze bahlanganyele noma baphendule. Ku-fonu:

- Tshela igama lakho nobudlelwano bakho masinyane: “Sawubona Baba, ngingu-Jenny, indodakazi yakho.”
- Khuluma kancane, ngemisho emifushane, bese ubuza into eyodwa ngesikhathi.
- Chaza into oyenzayo, njengokupheka noma ukuhamba uya kubhokisi leposi.
- Phetha ngesikhathi kusaseyinjabulo, bese utshela ukuthi uzoshaya nini ikholi elandelayo.

## Izincwadi nemilayezo eqoshiwe

Izincwadi ziyaqhubeka nokusebenza lapho amakholi enzima. Incwadi ingafundwa futhi kamuva, ngesikhathi esithulile, futhi umsizi angayifunda ngokukhala. [Bhala incwadi ndawonye](/activity/write-a-letter-together/) ishintsha incwadi ibe umsebenzi owabelwanwa: umuntu ngamunye engeza umugqa, umdwebo noma igatsha elicindezelwe. [Yenza ikhadi lesithombe ngeposi](/activity/make-a-photo-postcard/) ngumlayezo omfushane onesithombe ngaphambili.

Lapho amakholi ebukeka enzima kakhulu, qopha ividiyo emfushane: isibingelelo, iculo, uhambo lwasegadini. Umuntu oseduze angayidlala ngesikhathi esithulile, kangangoba ayithanda ngalo.

## Imiklamo eyabelwanwa ngeposi

Iphrojekthi edlulisana iphe kokubili abantu okulindele.

- **Incwadi yamaculo.** Umuntu ngamunye ukhetha amaculo bese ebhala imigqa embalwa ngokuthi kungani ebalulekile. Bheka [yenza incwadi yamaculo yomuntu siqu](/activity/make-a-personal-songbook/).
- **Incwadi yamaresiphi.** Umuntu oseduze utshela iresiphi yomndeni nendaba yayo, bese wena wengeza inguqulo yakho. Bheka [qala incwadi yamaresiphi omndeni](/activity/start-a-family-recipe-book/).
- **Umlando womndeni wezandla.** Umuntu ngamunye uhlanza isandla sakhe ephepheni bese ulithumela komunye, ukuze kuhlanganiswe kube umlando oyodwa. Bheka [yenza umlando womndeni wezandla](/activity/make-a-family-tree-of-hands/).

## Ukusiza ukusuka kude

Uma uhlala kude, ungasiza ngezindlela ezisebenzayo, uma umuntu efuna ukuthi wenze njalo. Qala ngokubuza ukuthi ufuna ukwenzani nabani ofuna ukuthi abambe iqhaza. Bese ucabangela lezi ndima:

- **Izikhathi zokuvakasha.** Siza ngokugcina ikhalenda noma ngokuhlela ukuthuthwa, uma umuntu evumelana nalokho.
- **Ukucwaninga.** Funa izinsiza zendawo, njengokuletshelwa ukudla, ukuthuthwa noma izinhlelo zosuku zabantu abadala. Hlola imininingwane nomhlinzeki ngamunye, ngoba ukutholakala kuyahluka ngendawo.
- **Imibhalo.** Hlela amafomu kuphela ngemvume yomuntu. Imininingwane yezempilo nezezimali idinga ukunakekelwa okwengeziwe. Abahlinzeki bangadinga ifomu elisayinwe ngaphambi kokuthi basabelane ngolwazi nesihlobo, ngakho bebuze ukuthi badinga ini.
- **Isikhathi somsizi wendawo.** Nikeza ukuvakashela noma ukuthatha umsebenzi ukuze umuntu oseduze aphumule.

Ikhasi le-Alzheimer's Association [lokunakekela ukusuka kude](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/long-distance-caregiving) lichaza ezinye zalezi ndima. Vumelani ngoxhumana oyedwa ojwayelekile othumela izibuyekezo ezijwayelekile, qamba owesibili, bese nizinqumela ukuthi izinkinga eziphuthumayo zizokhulunywa kanjani. Ungafaki abantu enkulumweni yokunakekelwa ngoba bahlala eduze.

## Nomuntu ophila ne-dementia

Kumuntu ophila ne-dementia, gcina amakholi amafushane futhi ajwayelekile. Sebenzisa isikhathi esifanayo nokubingelela okufanayo, bese umvumela ahole uma efuna ukuxoxa ngento ehlukile. Uma umuntu engabonakali ukukubona esikrinini, tshela igama lakho nokuthi wenzani, ungamcele ukuthi akuqonde. Umsebenzi owabelwanwa usabalwa.

## Ukuphepha nobumfihlo

- Buza ngaphambi kokwabelana ngezithombe zabanye abantu, futhi ungaphostri imininingwane yezempilo yelungu lomndeni ku-inthanethi.
- Hlola ukuthi umuntu unikeze imvume ngaphambi kokuthi omunye umuntu ajoyine ikholi noma athole izibuyekezo.
- Gcina amakholi evidiyo ekamelweni elithulile, nenguqulo yekhamera esethelwe ukuze umuntu abone ubuso bakho nezandla zakho.

## Uma kungasebenzi

Fushanisa ikholi, shintshela emsindweni kuphela, noma zama incwadi esikhundleni. Uma ikholi iphazamise kabi, yiphethe ngobumnene bese uzama futhi ngolunye usuku. Umsizi wendawo angahlala eceleni komuntu ngesikhathi sekholi elandelayo, asize ukuthi ajoyine.
