Kubantu abaningi igadi yindawo abayaziyo kahle. Ukuhlaba, ukuthela amanzi nokukhetha izimbali kusebenzisa amakhono afunde kudala, nokuba phandle kuletha umoya omusha, ukukhanya nokukhala kwezinyoni. Igadi lingabelwana ngezizukulwane: ogogo angakhombisa ingane ukuthi kuthelwa kanjani amanzi, nomuntu omdala angahlala emthunzini abize izimbali. Leli khasi lihlanganisa okufanele kuzanywe, indlela yokwenza izindawo zangaphandle zisebenziseke kalula, nokuthi kuphephe kanjani.

## Okufanele kuzanywe egadini

Imisebenzi engcono ilula futhi inokuqala nokuphela okucacile. Khetha imisebenzi efanele amandla womuntu nalokho ayekudla kakhulu ngaphambili.

1. **Ukuthela amanzi.** Sebenzisa ikani elincane nelilula, noma inozzle yepiphu enobumnene, bese ugcina izitsha ezimbalwa eduze. Ukuthela amanzi kunzima ukukwenza kabi.
2. **Ukutshala imbewu ezitsheni.** Imbewu enkulu, njengobhontshisi, amahlamvu elanga noma amaertshisi, ilula ukuyiphatha. I-Alzheimer's Foundation of America incoma ukutshala imbewu ezitsheni ezincane nokubukela ikhule. Bheka [tshala imbewu ethini](/activity/plant-seeds-in-a-pot/).
3. **Ukususa izimbali ezibunile.** Ukubamba izimbali ezibunile ngesandla kumnene futhi kuphindaphindeka. Khetha izitshalo ezingenamahlwele.
4. **Ukukhetha amakhambi noma imifino.** Utamatisi omncane, ubhontshisi namakhambi kulula ukukhetha futhi kuhle ukuwasebenzisa ekudleni kwemini.
5. **Ukunuka nokuthinta amakhambi.** Iroziveli, iminti nelavenda zinephunga elinamandla. Hlikihla igqabunga ngeminwe yakho bese uliphasisa.
6. **Ukushaya uthuli endleleni noma esitebeleni,** uma indawo ilingana futhi yomile, nokulinganisela kumi kahle.
7. **Ukugcwalisa isitsha sokudlela izinyoni,** bese nihlala ndawonye nibone ukuthi ngubani ofika.

## Izinketho zangaphakathi

Uma ukuphuma kungenzeki, letha igadi ngaphakathi.

- **Isitsha samakhambi efasiteleni** lebhejili, iparsley noma ushizi wamasvi.
- **Ukutshala ethreyini elimbozwe.** Amathreyi asesitolo sokudlela agcina inhlabathi endaweni eyodwa, nezinkomishi ezincane zenza kube lula ukuthutha inhlabathi ibe epotini.
- **Ukuhlela izimbali** ngezimbali ezisikiwe namagatsha aluhlaza eyadini noma esitolo. Bheka [hlelani izimbali ndawonye](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/).
- **Izimbali ezicindezelwe** embhuqeni, bese kamuva ziyanamathiselwa emakhadini. Bheka [cindezela amagatsha nezimbali](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/).
- **Ukuphrinta ngamagatsha** ephepheni, usebenzisa umbala omncane. Bheka [phrinta ngamagatsha](/activity/print-with-leaves/).

## Ukuphuma

- **Uhambo oluncane** ozungeze ibhulokhi, ipaki noma isikhungo sezitshalo, ngesivinini esicwebezelayo nendawo yokuhlala.
- **Ukuhlala ngaphandle** esiqeshini noma ebhentshini, ukulalela izinyoni nokuzwa umoya. Lokhu kubalwa njengomsebenzi ophelele.
- **Ukuvakashela igadi lomphakathi** ngesikhathi esithulile sosuku. Amagadi amaningi anezindawo zezitshalo ezinephunga, ukuthungwa kwezinto namanzi. Buza kuqala ngezihlalo nemizila.
- **Ukondla izinyoni noma ukubukela amadada** echibini, ugcine ibanga elivikelekile emaphethelweni amanzi.

## Indlela yokwenza igadi lisebenziseke kalula

Lethe igadi eduze komuntu.

- **Imibhede ephakanyisiwe noma izitsha ezinde** ezingaba ngaphezulu kwesilinganiso sesisu, okusho ukungaguquki noma ukugoba.
- **Izitsha ezitafuleni** zifanela umuntu osebenzisa isihlalo sensimbi noma othanda ukuhlala.
- **Imizila ebanzi nelinganayo** engenamatshe akhululekile noma amapayipi ancibilikile phezu kwawo yehlisa ingozi yokukhubeka.
- **Izihlalo ezinezandla nomthunzi** endleleni, ukuze ukuphumula kube njalo okukhona.
- **Amathuluzi anezibambo ezijiyile nezithambile.** Gwema imisumo yokusika amahlamvu, imimese nanoma yini ebukhali.

## Yiziphi izitshalo okufanele uzigweme?

Khetha izitshalo ezivikelekile ukuthinta futhi, ngokufanelekile, ezingacasuli isikhumba. I-Alzheimer's Foundation of America iyeluleka ukusebenzisa kuphela izitshalo ezingaziwa ukuthi zibangela ukucasula isikhumba, ukuhlola izinkinga zokudla ngaphambi kokutshala, nokunakekela noma ubani ongase amangaze inhlabathi njengokudla.

- Ungacabangi ukuthi isitshalo siphephile ukudla. Izitshalo eziningi zegadi ezijwayelekile namajikijolo anobuthi uma zigwetshwa.
- Gcina amabhalbhu, imbewu, amapelethi nenhlabathi ephathwe ngemikhiqizo kude, ikakhulu ezinganeni ezincane nakunoma ubani ofaka izinto emlonyeni.
- Hlola izinkinga zokudla ngaphambi kokutshala noma ukusika izimbali, amakhambi namagatsha anephunga.

## Ukushisa, ilanga nokuzulazula

- Khetha izikhathi ezipholile zosuku, bese ugcina izikhathi zimfushane.
- Nikeza amanzi njalo, bese ugcina indawo emthunzini yokuphumula. Isigqoko siyasiza.
- Ungashiyi muntu eyedwa elangeni elishisayo.
- Uma umuntu evele wazulazula ngaphambili, gcina igadi liphephile ngesango elikhiyiwe bese uhlala ubonakala. Cela iseluleko eqenjini lokumnakekela.

## Izinguqulo ezilula

- **Ukutshala ngokuhlala phansi.** Beka ithreyi etafuleni bese nitshala isitshalo esincane ndawonye.
- **Ukuthela amanzi ngesandla esisodwa.** Sebenzisa ikani elikhanyayo elinomlomo omfushane, noma thela epotini umuntu angayiphakamisa.
- **Ukukhetha esitsheni.** Letha amakhambi noma izimbali esihlalweni bese umvumela akhethe, ahlele noma anuke.
- **Ukubukela igadi.** Hlala eduze kwefasitela noma emthunzini, bese nibiza ndawonye okubonayo.

## Amanothi okuphepha

- Gcina amathuluzi, izitsha nezinto ezibukhali kude nalapho ungazisebenzisi.
- Geza izandla emva kwenhlabathi, futhi ungavumeli muntu adle inhlabathi, imbewu noma izitshalo.
- Hlola umhlabathi ngaphambi kokuqala ngezinto ezingakukhubeka.
- Yeka uma umuntu ekhathele, eshisa kakhulu noma ekhathazekile.

## Uma kungasebenzi

Shintsha indawo, isikhathi sosuku noma umsebenzi. Igadi elinzima kakhulu ukulilawula lingancishiswa libe yisitsha esisodwa noma isitsha samakhambi etafuleni. Ukuhlala phandle ndawonye kwanele ngezinye izinsuku.

## Nomuntu ophila ne-dementia

Igadi elaziwayo lingaba yinto ejabulisayo kakhulu. Gcina imisebenzi ilula futhi ejwayelekile, bese uhlala eceleni komuntu esikhundleni sokuhlala ngaphambi kwakhe. Sebenzisa amathreyi nezinkomishi ezincane ukugcina inhlabathi ibekwe endaweni eyodwa. Gcina izitshalo ezivikelekile ukuthintwa bese ugwema noma yini umuntu angayibheka njengokudla. Lindela ukuthi bazodinga usizo ekuqaleni komsebenzi, futhi bavumele bayeke lapho sebehlelekile.
