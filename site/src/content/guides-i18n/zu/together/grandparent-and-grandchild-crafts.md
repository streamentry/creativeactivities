Ogogo nezingane zobuzukulu bavame ukufuna into okwenza ndawonye engesikrini. Umsebenzi wezandla unika ngamunye umsebenzi. Umdala uphathwa njengomuntu omdala onendima nombono wakhe, nengane ithenjwa ngomsebenzi wangempela. Leli khasi libeka izindima zomuntu ngamunye, uhlu olufushane lemisebenzi efanele amazinga ahlukahlukene eminyaka nokubamba, nezinyathelo zokuphepha zezingane ezincane. Ukuze uthole ezinye izibhangqwana, bheka [ikhasi lemisebenzi yogogo nezingane zobuzukulu](/grandparent-grandchild-activities/).

## Beka izindima ngaphambi kokuqala

Shono ngokuzwakalayo ukuthi ubani okhetha, ubani obamba, ubani obhala nobani ohlanza. Ingane enomsebenzi izohlala kuwo isikhathi eside. Umdala obuzwa umbono wakhe uzizwa esizakala, hhayi elawulwa.

| Umsebenzi wezandla | Ingxenye kagogo | Ingxenye yengane |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Umlando womndeni wezandla](/activity/make-a-family-tree-of-hands/) | Ukhetha isiqu bese eshilo igama ngalinye | Ilandela izandla ngopensela bese yengeza iminyaka |
| [Iposikhadi lesithombe](/activity/make-a-photo-postcard/) | Ushilo umlayezo nokuthi ngowubani | Ikhetha isithombe bese ibhala igama |
| [Isikebhe sephepha](/activity/fold-a-paper-boat/) | Ukhombisa ukugoqa ngakunye, kancane | Ilandela ukugoqa, bese ihambisa izikebhe |
| [Penda idwala legadi](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/) | Ukhetha imibala nephethini | Iyapenda, nomuntu omdala eduze |
| [Luka imicu yephepha](/activity/weave-paper-strips/) | Ubamba uhlaka bese ehlela iphethini | Ihlephula futhi ifake imicu |
| [Dweba amaphethini aphindwayo](/activity/draw-repeating-patterns/) | Udweba umugqa wokuqala | Iqhubeka nephethini |

Phatha ogogo njengomuntu omdala onendima. Mbuze umbono wakhe, futhi vumela emlungise. Izindaba zakhe yingxenye enkulu yokuvakasha. Umsebenzi wezandla yisizathu sokuhlala ndawonye.

## Imisebenzi yetafula elinabantu abahlukene eminyaka

Amakhadi amaningi kulesi siza asebenza kahle phakathi kwezizukulwane. Khetha elifanele isikhathi, igumbi neminyaka yengane.

- [Imosaic yephepha elidatshuliwe](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/) isebenzisa iphepha elidatshuliwe nogcobo. Ingane iyadatshula, umdala ubeka.
- [Yenza ikhadi lokubingelela](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) lingathunyelwa komunye emndenini. Sebenzisa izimo ezisikwe ngaphambili kogogo, uma kusiza.
- [Yenza ujeqe wamakhekhe ephepha](/activity/make-a-paper-chain-garland/) kuhle eqenjini. Ingane idatshula imicu, umdala aluke bese uxhumanisa.
- [Bethelela isigqi ndawonye](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/) ayidingi izinto ngakho, futhi iyindlela enhle yokuqala noma yokuphetha ukuvakashelana.

## Ukuphepha kwezingane ezincane

Gcina umuntu omdala eduze, nezinto zibe lula.

- **Ngaphansi kwaleminyaka emithathu: akukho zingxenye ezincane.** Shiya amagilo, amaqhosha, amatshe amancane, imbewu, izinhlanzi ezomile nanoma yini engadlula ethubeni lephepha lasendlini yangasese. Izinkomba eziningi kulesi siza zidwebe izingxenye ezincane, ngakho hlola izinto ngaphambi kokuqala.
- **Akukho mabhaluni** nakukho izingcezu ezincane zefoham noma zepulasitiki emsebenzini.
- **Izinto ezingahlanzeka, ezingenakho amakhemikhali** kuphela, nomuntu omdala ogada noma yimuphi umbala noma ugcobo.
- **Akukho ugcobo oshisayo noma amathuluzi ashisayo.** Sebenzisa intonga yogcobo noma ugcobo wesikole ohlanzekayo.
- **Izikele** zisetshenziswa nomuntu omdala kuphela, futhi kuzingane ezikhulile ezisezisebenzise ngaphambili. Ukuhlephula iphepha kuyindlela enhle esikhundleni sazo.
- **Akukho kokudla** njengento yezandla. Gcina okudliwayo kude netafula lapho usebenza khona.
- **Gcina amanzi okupenda ekomishini,** hhayi esitsheni esikhulu, bese uwasusa lapho ingane iqedile.
- **Geza izandla** emva kombala, ugcobo noma inhlabathi.

Ungathathi izithombe zezingane ukuze wabelane nazo, futhi ungabeki igama, iminyaka noma isikole sengane ku-inthanethi ngaphandle kwemvume yabazali. Gcina umlando womndeni wezandla, incwadi yokupheka nencwadi yamaculo ngaphakathi komndeni.

## Ukulungisa ukubamba noma ukubona kukagogo okunomkhawulo

- Sebenzisa izikhwama zemibala ezinkulu, amamakhala ajiyile namabhulashi amakhulu anezibambo ezimbozwe ngokuthambile.
- Thepisa iphepha etafuleni ukuze lingashuquzeli.
- Izimo ezisikwe ngaphambili, noma imicu ehlephulwe ngaphambili, ukuze ogogo ayibeke esikhundleni sokuyisika.
- Sebenzisa ukuphambanisa okukhulu, njengemakhala omnyama ephepheni elimhlophe, nombhalo omkhulu kuzo zonke izimpawu.
- Hlala uhlangothi nohlangothi ukuze ingane nogogo bobabili babone umsebenzi.

[Umhlahlandlela wezobuciko nomculo](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ovela ku-Alzheimer's Association uveza ukuthi imisebenzi yobuciko ingaletha umzwelo wokufeza nenjongo. Uphinde ncamisa ukusebenzisa izinto ezephepha nokugwema izinto ezinobuthi nezithuluzi ezibukhali. Lezo mithetho zifanela kahle itafula elinabantu abahlukene eminyaka.

## Uma ogogo enezinguquko enkumbulweni

Sebenzisa amagama alula, aqotho ngaphambi kokuvakasha. Chaza okushintshile nokuhlala kunjalo: Ukhokho angakhohlwa igama noma aphinde indaba efanayo, kodwa usathanda ukuba nabantu. Cela ingane ingxenye encane yendaba, hhayi ukuhlolwa. Mmemele ukuthi abuze, “Kwakunjani lapho wawusemncane?” Gwema ukubuza ukuthi umdala ukhumbula yini into ethile.

Uma ingane ibuza umbuzo ongawuphendula, yisho lokho. “Angazi, kodwa singabuza uGogo uma esekulungele.” Izimpendulo eziqotho zisiza izingane zizizwe zivikelekile.

## Uma kungasebenzi

Fushanisa umsebenzi, shintsha umsebenzi noma yeka. Ingane eselahlekelwe yisithakazelo ayidingi ukuqeda. Beka izinto ebhokisini bese ubuya ngolunye usuku. Uma ogogo ekhathele, yenza ingxenye yengane ibe nkulu bese uvumela ogogo abuke futhi ekhombe.

## Amathiphu wokuhlala okuhle

- Hlela zonke izinto ngaphambi kokuthi ingane ifike.
- Khetha itafula elinokukhanya okuhle nendwangu yokulivikela.
- Qala ngesinyathelo esilula kakhulu, ukuze ingane izizwe iphumelele ngokushesha.
- Phetha ngento yokugcina, ukuyikhombisa noma ukuyinika umuntu.
- Phetha ngokudla okuncane nokuhamba kancane, noma indaba ethulile.
