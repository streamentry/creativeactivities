Abantu abaningi bafuna into ejabulisayo yokwenza nomzali omdala noma nogogo noma nokhokho ohlala nabo, ohlala eduze noma ovakashelwa kanye ngeviki. Leli khasi lelo sikhathi. Imibono ihlelwe ngokuthi umuntu unamandla angakanani nokuthi izandla, amehlo noma izindlebe zakhe zenza kangakanani umsebenzi. Ziyizincomo nje kuphela. Wena umazi umuntu kangcono.

## Udinga ini?

Iningi lalezi zimbono lisebenzisa izinto ezisendlini: iphepha namapensile, iradio noma ifoni enomculo, ubhasikidi lentambo, ithreyi lezinkinobho, iphakethe lamakhadi, izimbali ezimbalwa, noma iwindi enezinyoni ngaphandle. Ikhadi ngalinye elixhunyiwe lidwebe izinto zalo. Funda uhlu ngaphambi kokuqala, ukuze ungasafuni into ngesikhathi useqhubeka.

## Imisebenzi yosuku olunamandla amancane

Ezinye izinsuku umuntu unamandla amancane kakhulu. Lezi zimbono zithatha imizuzu eyishumi kuya kwengamashumi amabili, futhi zingenziwa uhlezi.

1. **Lalelani umculo ndawonye.** Khetha izingoma ezimbili noma ezintathu zesikhathi sakhe sokuqala, bese emvumela ukuthi akhethe elandelayo. Umsebenzi [Dweba ngomculo](/activity/draw-to-music/) wengeza ipensile uma efuna ukwenza izimpawu ngesikhathi elalela.
2. **Bhekisisa i-albhamu yezithombe.** Buza, “Ngitshele ngalena,” esikhundleni sokuthi, “Ngubani lo?” Ikhadi le-[albhamu yezithombe](/activity/look-through-a-photo-album/) linemibuzo eengeziwe.
3. **Mpheluza ibhola lentambo.** Bamba iskein ngobunono phakathi kwezandla zakhe ngesikhathi ushikashika. Umsebenzi onokunyakaza okulinganayo, unesiphetho esicacile. Bheka [mpheluza ibhola lentambo](/activity/wind-a-ball-of-yarn/).
4. **Bheka izinyoni ewindini.** Hlala lapho ukukhanya kuhle, bese ukhomba ezikubonayo. Bheka [bheka izinyoni ewindini](/activity/watch-birds-from-a-window/).
5. **Bethelela isigqi ndawonye.** Shintshanani nibethelela etafuleni. Gcina kube mfushane. Umsebenzi [bethelela isigqi ndawonye](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/) unezinyathelo.

Kumuntu othanda ukuthintwa, [nikeza ukuhlikihla isandla](/activity/give-a-hand-massage/) kungaba yisiphetho esithulile. Buza kuqala, bese uyeka uma esusa isandla sakhe.

## Imisebenzi yosuku olunamandla

Ngosuku oluhle ungamnikeza into enokwenziwa okuningi.

- **Hlelani izimbali ndawonye.** Sika izinduku, khetha umphongolo bese ubeka iluba ngalinye ngesikhathi sawo. Bheka [hlelani izimbali ndawonye](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/).
- **Penda ngomibala yamanzi.** Sebenzisa iphepha elikhulu nebhulashi elikhulu, bese uvumela imibala ihambe. Bheka [penda ngomibala yamanzi](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/).
- **Goqani amathawula ndawonye.** Ngumsebenzi osizayo, unesiphetho esicacile. Bheka [goqani amathawula ndawonye](/activity/fold-towels-together/).
- **Yenza ikholeji yenkumbulo** ngezithombe namaphepha, isithombe esisodwa ngesikhathi. Bheka [yenza ikholeji yenkumbulo](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/).
- **Hlunga izinkinobho ngombala** zibe ezitsheni ezijulile, kumuntu othanda ukuhlelekile nemisebenzi emincane. Bheka [hlunga izinkinobho ngombala](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/).
- **Yenzani uhlu lomculo ndawonye** ku-fonu noma kwisipikha, nengeza ingoma eyodwa ngesikhathi. Bheka [yenza uhlu lomculo ndawonye](/activity/make-a-playlist-together/).

## Ukulungisa ngokubamba, ukubona, ukuzwa nokunyakaza

Cabanga ngalokho okuvimbela ngaphambi kokushintsha umsebenzi.

**Ukubamba.** Sebenzisa izimakhala ezinkulu, amakhrayoni amakhulu, ibhulashi elinesibambo esikhulu, noma ithepi yefoham ezungeza izibambo ezincane. Thepisa iphepha ukuze lingashuquzeli. Izimo ezisikwe ngaphambili zingathatha indawo yezikele.

**Ukubona.** Sebenzisa ukuphambanisa okukhulu, njengemakhala omnyama ephepheni elimhlophe. Sebenzisa imbhalo enkulu nokukhanya okuhle okuvela ohlangothini esikhundleni sokuvela emuva. Indwangu yetafula elingenamibala yenza izingxenye ezincane zibonakale kalula.

**Ukuzwa.** Hlalani nobuso bebhekene, khuluma kancane, bese uvala umabonakude noma iradio uma ilwa nezwi lakho.

**Ukuhamba.** Lethe umsebenzi esihlalweni noma embhedeni kunokuba umcele ukuthi anyakaze. Gcina amanzi namathishu eduze, bese uhlola ukuthi ubude betafula bufanele isihlalo sakhe.

**Ukukhuluma.** Nikeza izinketho ngezithombe noma ngokuvumelana ngezandla. Ukunqekeza ikhanda noma ukukhomba kubalwa njengokubamba iqhaza. Ungenza umsebenzi eceleni kwakhe bese uxoxa ngalokho osebenzisa.

## Uma umzali ephila ne-dementia

Eminingi yale mibono isetshenziswa kaningi kubantu abaphila ne-dementia, isifo esiphazamisa inkumbulo nokucabanga. Izinguquko ezimbalwa zisiza kakhulu: nikeza isinyathelo esisodwa ngesikhathi, mvumele akhethe phakathi kwezinketho ezimbili, sebenzisa izinto nomculo abazijwayele, bese ulindela ukuthi umsebenzi uzothatha isikhathi eside noma ushintshe ngakho phakathi. Uma indaba ihamba ngendlela engalindelekile, yijabulele bese ubuza ngomzwa: “Lokho kubonakala sengathi kwakubalulekile kuwe.”

Ikhasi le-Alzheimer's Association [imisebenzi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) liphakamisa ukugcina amakhono omuntu engqondweni, ukuqhubeka nemisebenzi ayilojayo ngaso sonke isikhathi, nokuyilungisa uma kudingeka. Ikhasi le-NHS [imisebenzi](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) lithi umsebenzi wokwabelana ungenza bobabili bajabule futhi usize bejabulele isikhathi ndawonye.

Ukuze uthole eminye imibono, bheka [imisebenzi yabantu abaphila ne-dementia](/activities/ideas/) nesikhungo se-[imisebenzi ye-dementia](/dementia-activities/).

## Izinhlobo zokuphepha

- Gcina izinto ezincane, njengezinkinobho, amapheshana, imbewu namapini, ezitsheni ezingembozwa. Zisuse lapho uyeka.
- Gcina izikelo nemimese kude nomuntu, uma ungahlalanga eceleni komuntu futhi umsebenzi udinga kona.
- Sebenzisa izinto ezingahlanzeka, ezingenakho amakhemikhali. Hlola izinkinga zokudla ngaphambi kokusebenzisa izimbali, izinongo noma izinto ezinephunga.
- Gcina izinkomishi zamanzi kude nombala, futhi ungavumeli isitsha samanzi sihlolwe njengesiphuzo.
- Uma umuntu ekhathazeka, yima, mnikeze inkomishi yetiye bese ushintsha umsebenzi. Umhlahlandlela othi [lapho umuntu ethi cha](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) unolunye ulwazi ngalokhu.

## Uma kungasebenzi

Shintsha into eyodwa: umsebenzi, isikhathi sosuku, itafula, ubude noma inani lokukhetha. Uma umuntu esaphendula ngokuthi cha, ungamcindezeli. Ukuhlala ndawonye ekamelweni neradio ivuliwe kubalwa njengesikhathi sokuba ndawonye. Zama futhi ngolunye usuku. Abantu abaningi babuyela emsebenzini abawunqabile iviki ngaphambili.

## Nawe ngokwakho

Wena uyingxenye yomsebenzi. Isikhathi esifushane nonke elijabulisayo siyangcono kunesikhathi eside esikhathazayo ninokukhathala nonke. Gcina inhloso incane: iculo elilodwa, isithombe esisodwa, umugqa owodwa wamathawula agoqiwe. Kuyalungela ukuphetha lapho.
