Amapheshana nemidlalo yetafula zifanela amaqembu ahlukahlukene. Ipheshana etafuleni livumela abantu ukuthi bahlale ndawonye bengadingi ukukhuluma, futhi isithombe sinika wonke umuntu into ayingeza ngayo. Umjikelezo wokuxhumanisa noma umdlalo wamakhadi olula usebenza kumdala, kwizingane esebaselula nakumntwana omncane abahlala ekamelweni elifanayo, uma imithetho ivumelana nabo. Leli khasi lihlanganisa amapheshana, imidlalo yamagama, imidlalo yokuxhumanisa nemidlalo yetafula, nokuthi yenziwa kanjani ijabulise hhayi ikukhathaze.

## Kungani le midlalo isebenza etafuleni elabelwanwa

Inika abantu into yokwenza ngezandla nangamehlo, nendawo elula yokuqala. Futhi kulula ukuyimisa nokuyiqedela kamuva. Ikhasi le-NHS [imisebenzi](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) lithi imidlalo namapheshana ku-inthanethi kuyindlela yokuhlala ubambe iqhaza. I-Alzheimer's Association [ikhasi lemisebenzi](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) iphakamisa ukugxila ekujabuleni hhayi ekufezeni.

Amapheshana nemidlalo yokujabulisa nokuba nabanye. Akuyona ukwelashwa, futhi injongo ihora elinakile ndawonye.

## Ukukhetha ipheshana kanjani?

Hamba ngalokho umuntu akujabulelayo. Bheka lezi zinto ezine.

- **Inani lezingcezu.** Qala ngecishe uhhafu wenani elasetshenziswa ngesikhathi esedlule, bese ukhuphuka uma lilula kakhulu.
- **Usayizi wezingcezu.** Izingcezu ezinkulu zilula ukuzibona nokuzithatha. Izingcezu ezinkulu nezinamandla zihlala zisendaweni yazo.
- **Isithombe.** Khetha izihloko zabantu abadala njengamagadi, amakhishi, izikebhe, izilwane zasepulazini noma isithakazelo somuntu. Gwema izinhlelo zezithombe nezindawo ezinengqondo eningi. Izindawo ezinemibala ehlukile, njengesilimo esibomvu esigcekeni esiluhlaza, ziyalula kunesibhakabhaka esimhlophe.
- **Ukuqedwa.** Ukuqedwa okungabonakali kuncishisa ukukhanya okuvela kumalambu nasemafasiteleni.

Isithombe esithathwe ekhaya esiphrintiwe singaba ipheshana elihle kakhulu. Amanye amashaphu okuprinta anikeza lesi sevisi.

## Ungalilungisa kanjani ipheshana ndawonye?

1. **Khetha indawo enhle** enokukhanya okuvela ohlangothini nesihlalo esinezandla.
2. **Vula isivalo sebhokisi** ukuze nobabili nibone isithombe.
3. **Phendula izingcezu zibhekise phezulu** ngaphambi kokuqala.
4. **Thola imiphetho ndawonye**, noma wenze umngcele wena bese ushiya maphakathi kubo.
5. **Hlela izingcezu ngombala** zibe amathreyi ambalwa ajulile.
6. **Nikeza isingcezu esisodwa ngesikhathi** uma isitaki sizwakala sinkulu kakhulu: “Ngicabanga ukuthi lesi siyingxenye yesibaya.”
7. **Xoxa ngesithombe,** hhayi ngenqubekela phambili: “Lokho kufana nesikebhe ayesinaso ubaba.”
8. **Yishiye ibukeke.** Buya ngezinsuku ezimbalwa. Akukho ukuphuthuma.

## Imidlalo yamagama nokuthola amagama

Imidlalo yokuthola amagama nezindlela ezilula zokuqagela ngezinhlamvu ezinkulu zifanele abantu abaningi abathanda amagama. Ibhalele umugqa noma ukhamise amagama ngombala okhanyayo, bese nifunda amagama ndawonye ngezwi eliphakeme. Khetha uhlu lwamagama mayelana nesihloko umuntu asaziyo: izithelo, izimbali, izimoto, imikhumbi, izidolobha. Gwema izikhathi. Igama elingatholwa lingalindela kamuva, noma ningaphawula kulo ndawonye.

Umdlalo wokuthola amagama [wamahhala](/games/word-search/) kulesi sayithi awunasikhathi namaphuzu.

## Imidlalo yokuxhumanisa nemidlalo yenkumbulo

Imidlalo yenkumbulo yakudala, lapho amakhadi aphendulwa abheke phansi futhi kudingeka ukukhumbula lapho kukhona khona ngayinye yepheya, ingaba nzima. Icela into entsha ukuthi iphathwe futhi ikhunjulwe, okuvame ukuba yinto yokuqala ezwakala inzima. Ukuxhumanisa kusengaba yinjabulo uma ushintsha indlela isebenza ngayo.

- **Shiya izithombe zibhekise phezulu.** Ukuxhumanisa izithombe ozibonayo kuyumdlalo wangempela.
- **Qala ngamapheya amabili noma amathathu.** Engeza amanye kuphela uma efuna okwengeziwe.
- **Khetha izithombe ezinkulu, ezijwayelekile, zabantu abadala,** njengendebe yetiye, isikebhe sokuhamba ngomoya noma imbali yelanga.
- **Dlala njengeqembu.** “Ake sithole izinja” kusebenza kangcono kunokuthi “Yithi ngesakho.”
- **Susa amaphuzu nesikhathi.** Akudingeki muntu anqobe.

Zama [amapheya ezithombe](/games/picture-pairs/) nezithombe ezibonakalayo, noma [thola okufanayo](/games/find-the-same/) ukuze ukhethe okulula kakhulu, isithombe esisodwa kwezimbili noma ezintathu. [Hlela ndawonye](/games/sort-together/) icela ukuthi ubeke isithombe ngasinye lapho sifanele khona, njengekhishi noma igadi, okusebenzisa ulwazi umuntu asenalo.

## Imidlalo yetafula yamaqembu ahlukahlukene

Imidlalo eminingi elula yetafula isebenza kahle ezinzini ezihlukene. Zama umdlalo wamakhadi olula wokuxhumanisa noma ufanane nokuwuthinta ngaphandle kokugcina amaphuzu, noma ibingo yezithombe lapho wonke umuntu ememeza khona lokho akubonayo. [Ibingo yezithombe](/games/picture-bingo/) isebenzisa izithombe ezinkulu futhi ingabelwanwa yitafula lonke. Idominoes ingadlalwa ngababili noma ngeqembu lamabili, ngomthetho wokuthi niyasizana.

Kuyo yonke imidlalo yetafula, vumelana ngalezi zinto ekuqaleni:

1. Akekho ogcina amaphuzu uma kungavumelani bonke ukuthi kube njalo.
2. Abadlali bangacela usizo, nomsizi angakhomba noma adlulise ikhadi.
3. Noma ubani angabuka futhi ahambe etafuleni noma nini.
4. Umdlalo uphela lapho abantu beyeka ukujabulela, hhayi lapho kunomuntu owinile.

## Izinguqulo ezilula

- Sebenzisa izingcezu ezimbalwa, amakhadi ambalwa noma amapheya ambalwa.
- Sebenzisa ithreyi elinemiphetho ephakanyisiwe ukuze izingcezu zingashuquzeli.
- Hlela izingcezu ngombala noma ngesimo ngaphambi kwesikhathi.
- Nika umuntu ngamunye ikhadi elilodwa ngesikhathi.
- Dlala uhlezi, nebhodi ibe sendaweni elinganayo.
- Khetha izingcezu ezinokukhanya okuphambene ukuze abantu abanokubona okubuthakathaka, nombhalo omkhulu emidlalweni yamagama.

## Amanothi okuphepha

- Gcina izingcezu ezincane, njengezingcezu zephesheni, amathokheni omdlalo nodayisi, kude nalowo ongaphansi kwaleminyaka emithathu nakunoma ubani ongafaka izinto emlonyeni.
- Gcina izingcezu ethreyini noma ebhokisini onganwebeka. Bala izingcezu ekupheleni kwesikhathi.
- Sebenzisa imidlalo engenazo izingxenye ezincane zemagnethi noma amabhethri ezinkinobho.
- Uma umdlali ekhathazeka, yima, shintsha umdlalo bese unikeza isiphuzo.

## Uma kungasebenzi

Shintsha inani lezingcezu, isithombe noma imithetho, noma nje hlala ndawonye uhlele izingcezu. Uma umdlalo uzwakala njengomsebenzi, uwubeke eceleni. Shiya ipheshana elingaqedile etafuleni noma ethreyini ubuyele kulo kamuva. Abantu abaningi bajabulela ukubuyela kupheshana aqale ngaliviki elidlule.

## Nomuntu ophila ne-dementia

Kumuntu ojwayele ukujabulela amapheshana, isithombe ajwayele nezingcezu ezimbalwa ezinkulu zivame ukubuyisa injabulo. Gcina isithombe sibonakala, hlala eduze kwakhe esikhundleni sokuhlala ngaphambi kwakhe, bese unikeza isingcezu esisodwa ngesikhathi. Bheka izimpawu zokuthi umsebenzi unzima kakhulu, njengokuphendukela eceleni noma ukusunduza izingcezu. Nikeza ukwenza esilandelayo ndawonye, noma shintshela kokulula. Khetha isikhathi lapho bephumule kungekho ukulamba noma ukuphuthuma.
