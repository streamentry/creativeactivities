Iseshini yezandla ngeqembu ingahlanganisa abantu etafuleni elilodwa, noma ilabhulali, ikhaya lokunakekela, ikilabhu, igumbi lesikole noma igumbi lasendlini emhlanganweni womndeni. Iqembu alidingi umholi ongugoti noma imishini ekhethekile. Lidinga uhlelo olujwayelekile, olufushane nolulula ukujoyina kunoma ubani, nomholi okwenza kucace ukuthi yonke inhlanganyelo iyabaluleka. Leli khasi lichaza ukuthi uhlela kanjani futhi uqhuba uhlobo lweseshini enjalo, nokuthi uguqula kanjani into eyodwa yezandla ukuze isebenze kubantu abanamakhono ahlukene.

Lezi seshini zilungele amalabhulali aqhuba itafula lezandla ngenyanga, abasebenzi bemisebenzi ekhaya lokunakekela, abasebenzi abazinikele kwiklabhu yomphakathi, othisha beklasi exubile, nemindeni efuna ukwenza okuthile ndawonye emhlanganweni. Zilungele abantu abajabulela ukuba nabanye nokwenza izinto, nabaholi ababasekelayo. Iziphakamiso zichaza okwenza iqembu lisebenze kahle. Azisho ukugweba noma ubani weqembu.

## Yini eyenza izezandla zeqembu zisebenze?

Inhlanganyelo yezandla yeqembu ijwayelekile, imfushane, ilula ukujoyina esigabeni noma yisiphi, futhi ayinangcindezi. Ngokusebenza lokho kusho:

- **Okwejwayelekile kunokusha.** Khetha into abantu besejwayele kakade, noma into esebenzisa izinto abazaziyo, njengephepha, upende noma isithombe sento yesikhathi esedlule.
- **Okufushane kunokude.** Hlela ngesikhathi sokunaka kweqembu. Yakha indawo yokumisa yemvelo ngaphakathi.
- **Ukwenza kunokubuka.** Nikeza umuntu ngamunye okuthile okokubamba, okokudatsha, okokuthinta, okokukhetha noma okokusho.
- **Amazinga amaningi, umsebenzi owodwa.** Hlela izindlela ezilula nezinzima zokubamba iqhaza, ukuze kungabikho omunye oshiyiwe.
- **Akukho abawinayo nakukho amaphuzu.** Izindlela zokuncintisana zishiya abanye bezizwa beshilo. Amaprojekthi abelwanayo awagwemi lokhu.

I-[Alzheimer's Association ethi Izincomo Zokunakekelwa Kwe-Dementia](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/1a0020aa-0bfb-47ac-a545-1dd8a3ee1444/alzheimers-dementia-care-practice-recommendations.pdf) iwumthombo osizayo kubaholi abasebenza ezindaweni zokunakekela.

## Iqembu kufanele libe likhulu kangakanani?

Okuncane kuvame ukuba lula. Iqembu labantu abane kuya kwabayisishiyagalombili nomholi oyedwa nomsizi kutafula ngalinye liyisisekelo esihle. Umuntu othola umhlangano omkhulu ekhathaza kakhulu angabamba iqhaza kalula eqenjini elincane.

Ukuhlola okusizayo: ingabe wonke umuntu angabona ubuso bomholi, azwe kahle, futhi umsizi angafinyelela kuye ngemizuzwana embalwa? Uma kungenjalo, hlukanisa iqembu, engeza abasizi, noma uhambele egumbini elincane. Emcimbini omkhulu, hlala abasizi phakathi kwabantu hhayi emadongeni.

## Kufanele uhlele kanjani igumbi?

- **Itafula noma umjikelo.** Abantu bahlala besondelene ngokwanele ukwabelana ngezinto, nomholi esigcawini esisodwa noma ngaphakathi emjikelweni.
- **Umholi useduze, ezingeni lamehlo.** Hlala uma kungenzeka, kunokuma.
- **Ukuzwa nokubona kuqala.** Hlala noma ubani onokungezwa kahle eduze nomholi, ohlangothini lwakhe olungcono. Qinisekisa ukuthi izibuko nezinsizakalo zokuzwa ziyagqokwa.
- **Ukukhanya okuvela ohlangothini noma phambili.** Gwema ukuhlala umuntu nomhlane wakhe ngasefasiteleni elikhanyayo.
- **Vala umsindo owenza ukuphazamiseka.** Vala i-TV noma umsakazo, futhi gcina umsindo ongemuva uphansi.
- **Ingxoxo eyodwa ngesikhathi.** Ukukhuluma ngesikhathi esifanayo kunzima ukukulandela kubantu abaningi, ngakho umholi angacela umuntu oyedwa ukuthi akhulume ngesikhathi.
- **Indlela ephumayo esobala nendawo ethulile eseceleni.** Noma ubani ofuna ukuhamba kufanele akwazi ukwenza lokho kalula. Gcina indawo ethulile eduze, nomsizi ongahamba naye.

## Izinto zezandla ezisebenza eqenjini

Lezi zinhlelo zinomphumela abelwane ngawo, ngakho ngamunye wengeza ingcezu. Amakhadi angezansi anezinyathelo ezigcwele nezinguqulo ezilula:

- [Penda isithombe esabelwanayo](/activity/paint-a-shared-picture/). Isithombe esikhulu esisodwa ephepheni elibhanqwe etafuleni, nabo bonke bengeza ngokuthatha iturn. Sifanele amaqembu amancane namaqembu ahlanganisa izizukulwane.
- [Yenza ugarlands weketango lephepha](/activity/make-a-paper-chain-garland/). Umuntu ngamunye wenza izixhumanisi, bese iqembu lonke ligxumela ushintshe ekamelweni. Kulula ukuyenza kubantu abaningi ngesikhathi esisodwa.
- [Imosaic yephepha eladatshulwe](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/). Wonke umuntu udatshula iphepha elinemibala, bese ulinamathisela ekhadini elabelwanayo. Akukho izikelo.
- [Yenza umsundu womndeni wezandla](/activity/make-a-family-tree-of-hands/). Umuntu ngamunye ulandelela isandla ngopensela ezungeze isiqu esidwetshiwe. Akukho upende esikhumeni, ngakho lusebenza kuzo zonke izinkathi.
- [Cindezela amaqabunga nezimbali](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/). Iqembu lingaqoqa, lihlele futhi licindezele amaqabunga ndawonye, bese lenza amakhadi.
- [Yenza incwadi yengoma yomuntu siqu](/activity/make-a-personal-songbook/). Umuntu ngamunye ubiza izingoma azithandayo nezindaba ezingemuva kwazo. Isebenza kahle ngengxoxo nangokwenza.
- [Thinta ukuhamba komculo ndawonye](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/). Wonke umuntu uthinta ngokuhambisana nengoma ezinzile, ngokuthatha iturn yokuhola.

## Ungayilungisa kanjani into eyodwa yezandla ukuze isebenze kubantu abanamakhono ahlukene?

Hlela okungenani izindlela ezintathu zokujoyina yonke imisebenzi. Ithebhula elilandelayo libonisa ukuthi isithombe sepende esabelwanayo singasebenza ezingeni elingathathu kanjani:

| Izinga | Umuntu wenza ini | Umsizi wenza ini |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uzimele kakhulu | Ukhetha umbala bese upenda ingxenye | Ugcina amapende efinyeleleka futhi akhulume ngesithombe |
| Usizo oluthile | Uthinta isipontshi ngombala umsizi awukhethile | Uqondisa isipontshi nokubeka ishidi |
| Usizo oluningi | Ukhetha phakathi kwemibala emibili, noma ubheke aphawule | Ubamba ibhulashi noma isipontshi bese upenda ingxenye ndawonye |

Indlela efanayo isebenza ketangeni lephepha (yenza ixhumanisi eliphelele, namathisela ixhumanisi elilodwa, noma ubambe ketango njengoba likhula), kumosaic (dathula ingcezu, beka ingcezu, noma khetha umbala), nakwincwadi yengoma (bhala isihloko, shoyo isihloko, noma khomba elilodwa).

## Abaholi kufanele bakhulume kanjani neqembu?

- Zethule njalo, futhi sebenzisa igama ngalinye umuntu alithandayo.
- Khuluma kancane, nikeza isinyathelo esisodwa ngesikhathi, futhi khombisa ngaphambi kokuchaza.
- Memezela ngemibuzo evulekile efana nelathi "Ngitshele ngalesi sithombe," noma ngokukhetha okulula. Gwema imibuzo yokuhlola efana nelathi "Uyakhumbula yini?"
- Ungabalungisi abantu. Bonga yonke inhlanganyelo ngendlela efanayo.
- Gcina amazwi okuvula nokuvala afanayo njalo, ukuze iseshini izwakale ijwayelekile.

Isiqondiso sethu [samaqembu ezobuciko](/activities/group-art-activities/) sinezinye izinto eziningi mayelana nokuqhuba iseshini yobuciko neqembu elixubile.

## Ungaphela kanjani kahle?

Ukuyeka kuyisiphumelo esihle. Qaphela izimpawu zokuthi umuntu uyakhathala, uyaxaxa noma ukhathazekile. Vumela umsizi anikeze uhambo noma isihlalo esithulile kwenye indawo, futhi vumela abantu bahambe ngaphandle kokuphawula. Umuntu olele phansi ngesikhathi seseshini angase abe nje ekhululekile.

Vala yonke iqembu ngendlela efanayo, ngengoma ejwayelekile noma ngokubonga okulula, ukuze ukuphela kuzwakale kuthulile futhi kulindeleke. Ngemva kwalokho, qopha okusebenze nokuthi ubani ajabulele yini. Lezo zinqophe zenza iseshini elandelayo ibe ngcono. Gcina umsebenzi owuqedile nomuntu owenzile, bese ucela imvume ngaphambi kokuwuboniswa.

## Ukukwenza ekhaya lokunakekela noma endaweni yokunakekela inkumbulo

Ekhaya lokunakekela noma endaweni yokunakekela inkumbulo, imigomo efanayo iyasebenza, ngezinyathelo ezengeziwe ezimbalwa. Khetha izinto zezandla ezincike ezimpilweni nasezintweni ezijwayelekile, njengoba i-[ikhasi lemisebenzi ye-NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) liphawula ukuthi imisebenzi ivame ukudinga ukwenziwa ibe lula futhi ingagxila ekuthintweni, ekubukeni, ekuzweni nasekunukeni ezigabeni zokugcina. Isikhathi somuntu ngamunye phakathi noma ngemva kweqembu kungase kumlungele kangcono kuneqembu ngokwalo. Buza imindeni ukuthi ubani wajabulela ini, bese usebenzisa lezo zinto njengesiqalo.
