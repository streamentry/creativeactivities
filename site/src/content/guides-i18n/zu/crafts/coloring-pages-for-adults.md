Ukugcwalisa ngemibala yisenzo esilula sokudala okungatholakala kumuntu omdala. Akudingi ukufunda, inkumbulo noma impendulo eyodwa efanele. Izandla ziyanyakaza, imibala iyavela, kube khona isithombe okungakhulunywa ngaso. Abantu abaningi abathi abakwazi ukudweba bayagcwalisa ngemibala, ngoba imigqa seyivele ikhona, nokukhetha kungokwabo. Leli khasi libhekene nendlela yokukhetha imiklamo, ukuthi yini okufanele ilungiselelwe, ukugcwalisa ngemibala ndawonye, nokusebenzisa umdlalo wokugcwalisa ngemibala kule sayithi.

Ukugcwalisa ngemibala kufanele abantu abadala abathanda ukwenza izinto etafuleni, abantu abane amandla amancane kunangaphambili, nanoma ubani ofuna isenzo esifushane nesithulile sokwabelana. Kungenziwa nokwenziwa wedwa, egumbini lokulinda noma embhedeni. Lezi yizincomo ezisekelwe kokudingwa yisenzo. Azichazi ukuthi umuntu ngamunye angakwenza noma angakwenzi yini.

## Yini oyidingayo?

- **Izinsiza ezinkulu, ezilula ukuzibamba.** Amakhilayoni amakhulu, amamakhala amakhulu nopensela emibala onomzimba omkhulu kulula kunamapeni amancane.
- **Ipaleti elincane.** Imibala emithathu noma emine iyanele. Imibala eminingi ingenza ukukhetha kubonakale kukhulu kunokuba kunjalo.
- **Iphepha noma ikhadibhodi eliqinile.** Iphepha elincane liyadabuka futhi liyashelela. Ncamathelisa amakhona, noma sebenzisa iklibhodi.
- **Itafula elicacile.** Beka ikhasi elilodwa ngesikhathi.
- **Into yokuxuba ngayo.** Indwangu ethambile, i-cotton bud noma i-sponge inikeza umphumela othambile.

## Ungayikhetha kanjani imiklamo?

Abantu abadala bavame ukungakuthandi amaphepha abonakala enganeni kakhulu. Funa amaphepha anezihloko zangempela nezindawo ezinkulu nezilula:

- **Imvelo.** Ingadi enezimbali ezinkulu, ichibi nesikebhe, izinyoni esihlahleni.
- **Izinto ezijwayelekile.** Ibhodwe letiye, ibhayisikili, isitsha sezithelo, igeja.
- **Amaphethini amakhulu.** Izimo ezinkulu zejiyometri noma i-mandala enezingxenye ezimbalwa ezinkulu.
- **Izindawo ezilula.** Intaba, insimu noma ukubuka ngefasitela.

Gwema amaphepha anezindawo eziningi ezincane nezigcwele. Zinzima ukuzigcwalisa, futhi umuntu angayeka phambi kokuthi ajabulele isithombe. Iphepha elinezimo ezimbalwa ezinkulu ngokuvamile lingcono kunelinemininingwane eminingi. Khetha umklamo ofanele ukuthanda komuntu. Umuntu othanda ukulima angathanda izimbali, nomuntu othanda izikebhe angathanda isithombe sesikebhe esisechwebeni.

[Ikhasi lezithombe elingaphrintwa](/pictures/) linemidwebo emihle yezinto zansuku zonke, njengebhodwe letiye, inja, irozi noma ibhasi elibomvu. Phrinta ezimbalwa ngesikhathi kuphepha eliqinile, ukuze kube nokukhetha ngaphandle kwesitaki.

## Ukugcwalisa ngemibala ethebhulethi

[Umdlalo wokugcwalisa ngemibala](/games/coloring/) kule sayithi usebenza ngokuthepha umbala, bese uthepha ingxenye yesithombe. Awudingi upensela noma ukulawula izandla okunembile, ngakho ungafanela umuntu ongasabambi kahle. Hlala eduze komuntu ukuze nabelane ngesikrini. Mvumele akhethe imibala, futhi athephe kangangoba efisa. Vala izaziso kudivayisi ukuze isithombe sihlale sisesikrinini.

Ithebhulethi ifanele ntambama ekhuthele, embhedeni noma esihlalweni eduze newindi. Futhi ayikhiqizi ukungcola. Abanye abantu bakhetha iphepha, nokho lokho kuhle. Sebenzisa okuthandwa umuntu.

## Ungagcwalisa kanjani ndawonye?

1. **Qala ukushaya kokuqala ndawonye.** Bambisa ikhilayoni nomuntu, noma beka isandla sakho phezu kwesakhe imigqa embalwa, bese uyasikhulula.
2. **Xoxa ngemibala nangendawo.** "Lo mbala oluhlaza ulumekile. Wakhe wawubona kuphi amanzi anemibala enjalo?" Lokhu kukhuthaza ingxoxo ngaphandle kokuba yisivivinyo.
3. **Dumisa izinqumo, hhayi ukucwebezela.** "Ukhethe imibala efudumele ngempela" izwakala ingcono kunokuthi "Uhlale emigqeni."
4. **Mvumele bahole.** Uma befuna ukugcwalisa ikhasi lonke ngemigqa engahleliwe, vumela. Kusahlala kungukugcwalisa ngemibala.
5. **Yeka lapho beyeka.** Beka amakhilayoni phansi, bese unikeza ukuphuza itiye noma uhambo olufushane.

Ukugcwalisa ngemibala eceleni komunye kungazwakala njengokwabelana, kunokuqapha. Sebenzisani ikhasi lenu ngokwehlukana, eceleni komunye.

## Iziguquko ezilula ongazama?

- **Uhlezi.** Ukugcwalisa ngemibala kwenziwa uhlezi, ngakho beka itafula ngokuphakama okufanele umuntu nesihlalo sakhe.
- **Kulula ukubamba.** Sebenzisa amakhilayoni amakhulu noma amamakhala, noma faka ithephu yefoam ozungeza ngayo upensela omncane ukuze ube mkhulu. Iklibhodi ingabanjwa emaqanjeni.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Khetha imigqa emikhulu enokuqhathanisa okuphezulu, futhi usebenzise umbala omkhulu owodwa ngesikhathi. Ilambu elihle phezu kwetafula liyasiza.
- **Izinketho ezimbalwa.** Nikeza imibala emibili, noma ikhasi elilodwa imibala seyikhethiwe. Vumela umuntu akhethe umbala owodwa ensimini enkulu eyodwa.
- **Isikhathi esifushane.** Cela umumo owodwa, noma ikhona elilodwa lekhasi. Ukuphetha ngemizuzu emibili kuyisikhathi esihle.
- **Ngaphandle kwamazwi.** Phakamisa amakhasi amabili, noma imibala emibili, bese ulinda ukufinyelela noma ukubheka. Ukukhomba nokuqhuba ikhanda kuyizimpendulo.

## Izincomo zokuphepha

- Sebenzisa amakhilayoni, amamakhala nopensela emibala abangenayo ubuthi. Hlola ukuthi ilebula ithi akunabuthi. [Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety)ichaza ukuthi uphawu lwe-AP lusho ukuthini ezinsizeni zobuciko.
- Gcina izinsimbi zokubukisa nezimbobo ezincane kude nomuntu ongazifaka emlonyeni.
- Kumuntu ofaka izinto emlonyeni, sebenzisa izingcezu ezinkulu ezingenabuthi, futhi uzihlole njalo.
- Ithebhulethi kufanele igcinwe kude namanzi, nokudla nesiphuzo.

## Uma kungasebenzi

- **Ikhasi linabantu abaningi kakhulu.** Ligeleze ngomklamo olula onezindawo ezinkulu.
- **Ukucindezeleka kuyabonakala.** Beka ikhilayoni phansi, bese unikeza ukugcwalisa indawo elandelayo ndawonye. Noma yeka, ubuyele ngolunye usuku.
- **Umuntu usunquma ikhasi.** Lokho yimpendulo ecacile. Nikeza omunye umsebenzi, njengengxoxo noma uhambo.
- **Ufuna ukugcwalisa ngemibala, kodwa hhayi ngalesi sisetshenziswa.** Zama ummaki omkhulu, ikhilayoni elinkulu noma umunwe ocwiliswe kupeyinti yamanzi.

## Ukukwenza nomuntu ophila ne-dementia

Ukugcwalisa ngemibala kuvame ukunikezwa abantu abadala, kuhlanganise nabantu abaphila ne-dementia, ngoba akudingi inkumbulo noma impendulo eyodwa efanele. Ezigabeni zokuqala, abantu abaningi bayajabulela amaphepha ayinkimbinkimbi futhi bakhetha imibala yabo. Esigabeni esiphakathi, izithombe ezinkulu nokukhetha okumbalwa kuyasiza. Ezigabeni zokugcina, isimo esisodwa esikhulu, ishidi elinemibala ethambile, noma ukubamba nje ikhilayoni ngenkathi ucula kancane kungase kwanele. Abanye abantu bayajabulela ukubheka isithombe esiqediwe kunokusidala.

Imisebenzi isebenza kangcono lapho ihambisana nalokho umuntu asakwazi ukukwenza, futhi lapho ilungiswa njengoba amakhono eshintsha. Ikhasi le-NHS [elingemisebenzi](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) liphawula ukuthi imisebenzi yesigaba sokugcina ivame ukugxila ezinzweni. Ukuze uthole ezinye izindlela, bheka [umhlahlandlela wemisebenzi yesigaba sokugcina](/activities/late-stage/).

Uma ufuna umdlalo ozolandela iseshini yokugcwalisa ngemibala, zama [ibingo yezithombe](/games/picture-bingo/), esebenzisa izithombe ezicacile esikhundleni sezinombolo.
