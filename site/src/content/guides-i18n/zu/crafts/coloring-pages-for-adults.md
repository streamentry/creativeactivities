Ukudweba ngemibala yisisebenzi esilula sokunikeza umuntu omdala. Akudingi ukufunda, inkumbulo noma impendulo eyodwa elungile. Izandla ziyanyakaza, imibala iyaphuma, futhi kukhona isithombe okungakhulunywa ngaso. Abantu abadala abaningi abathi abakwazi ukudweba bayadweba nje, ngoba imigqa seyivele ikhona, futhi ukukhetha kungokwabo. Leli khasi lichaza ukuthi ukhetha kanjani imiklamo, yini okufanele uyibeke phansi, ukuthi nidweba kanjani ndawonye, nokuthi usebenzisa kanjani umdlalo wokudweba kule sayithi.

Ukudweba kulungele abantu abadala abajabulela ukwenza izinto etafuleni, abantu abanamandla amancane kunangaphambili, nanoma ubani ofuna umsebenzi omfushane nothulile wokwabelana. Ungenziwa nawedwa, endaweni yokulinda noma embhedeni. Lokhu kuyiziphakamiso ezisekelwe kokudingwayo yisisebenzi, hhayi incazelo yokuthi yini umuntu ngamunye angayenza noma angayenzi.

## Udinga ini?

- **Amathuluzi amakhulu nalula ukuwabamba.** Ama-crayon amakhulu, ama-marker ajiyile nopensela abanemibala abanobubanzi balula kunamapeni amancane.
- **Iphalethi elincane.** Imibala emithathu noma emine yanele. Imibala eminingi ingenza ukukhetha kubonakale kukhulu kunokuba kunjalo.
- **Iphepha noma ikhadibhodi eliqinile.** Iphepha elincane liyadatsheka futhi liyashelela. Bopha amakona ngethepi, noma sebenzisa ibhodi lokubhala.
- **Itafula elicacile.** Beka ikhasi elilodwa ngesikhathi.
- **Okuthile okokuxuba ngako.** Indwangu ethambile, i-cotton bud noma isiphonjana kunikeza umphumela othambile.

## Ukhetha kanjani umklamo?

Abantu abadala bavame ukungathandi amakhasi abonakala enganeliseki kakhulu. Funa amakhasi anezihloko zangempela nezindawo ezinkulu nezilula:

- **Imvelo.** Igadi elinezimbali ezinkulu, ichibi nesikebhe, izinyoni esihlahleni.
- **Izinto ezijwayelekile.** Ibhodwe letiye, ibhayisikili, ibhasikidi lezithelo, isigqoko.
- **Imiklamo emikhulu.** Izimo ze-geometry ezinemibala ezinkulu noma i-mandala enezingxenye ezimbalwa ezinkulu.
- **Izindawo ezilula.** Intaba, insimu noma ukubuka ngefasitela.

Gwema amakhasi anezindawo eziningi ezincane nezinamagatsha. Zinzima ukuzigcwalisa, futhi umuntu angahlala aphume ngaphambi kokuthi ajabulele isithombe. Ikhasi elinezimo ezimbalwa ezinkulu imvamisa lingcono kunelinemininingwane. Khetha umklamo ofanele ukuthanda komuntu. Umuntu owayethanda ukulima angathanda izimbali, nomuntu owayethanda izikebhe angathanda isithombe sedokhi.

[Ikhasi lezithombe ezingaphrintwa](/pictures/) linemidwebo emihle yezinto zansuku zonke, njengebhodwe letiye, inja, uphondo noma ibhasi elibomvu. Phrinta kwezimbalwa ngesikhathi esisodwa ephepheni eliqinile, ukuze kube nokukhetha ngaphandle kwesitaki esikhulu.

## Ukudweba ethebhulethi

[Umdlalo wokudweba](/games/coloring/) kule sayithi usebenza ngokuthepha umbala, bese uthepha ingxenye yesithombe. Awudingi upensela noma ulawulo oluncane lwezandla, ngakho ungafanela umuntu amandla esandla sakhe ebuthakathaka. Hlala eduze komuntu ukuze nihlanganyele isikrini. Mvumele akhethe imibala, futhi mvumele athephe izikhathi eziningi njengoba efisa. Vala izaziso kudivayisi ukuze isithombe sihlale sibonakala esikrinini.

Ithebhulethi ifanele ntambama ethulile, umbhede noma isihlalo eduze nefasitela. Futhi ayinamanyosi. Abanye abantu bakhetha iphepha, futhi lokho kulungile. Sebenzisa okujabulisa umuntu.

## Nidweba kanjani ndawonye?

1. **Qala umugqa wokuqala ndawonye.** Bamba i-crayon nomuntu, noma beka isandla sakho phezu kwesakhe imigqa embalwa, bese uyakhipha.
2. **Khuluma ngombala nangendawo.** "Lo mbala oluhle kakhulu. Wawuke wawubona kuphi amanzi anombala onjalo?" uvusa ingxoxo ngaphandle kombuzo wokuhlola.
3. **Ncoma izinketho, hhayi ukucophelela.** "Ukhethe imibala efudumele kakhulu" kuzwakala kungcono kunokuthi "Uhlale emigqeni."
4. **Vumela aholele.** Uma efuna ukuthinta ikhasi lonke ngokuhlahla, mvumele. Usadweba.
5. **Yeka lapho eyeka.** Beka ama-crayon phansi, bese ucela ikomishi letiye noma uhambo olufushane.

Ukudweba eceleni kwomunye nomunye kungazwakala njengokwabelana hhayi njengokuqapha. Sebenzela ikhasi lakho eduze kwelakhe.

## Yiziphi izinguqulo ezilula ongazama?

- **Ehlezi phansi.** Ukudweba kungumsebenzi wokuhlala, ngakho beka itafula libe nobude obufanele umuntu nesihlalo sakhe.
- **Kulula ukubamba.** Sebenzisa ama-crayon noma ama-marker amakhulu, noma ufake upensela omncane ethepeni lefoam ukuze ube mkhulu. Ikhadibhodi lingaphathwa ezinyaweni.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Khetha imigqa emikhulu enokuqhathanisa okukhulu, bese usebenzisa umbala omkhulu owodwa ngesikhathi. Isibani esihle phezu kwetafula siyasiza.
- **Izinketho ezimbalwa.** Nikeza imibala emibili, noma ikhasi elinemibala esele ikhethiwe. Vumela umuntu akhethe umbala owodwa wesigaba esikhulu esisodwa.
- **Okufushane.** Cela isimo esisodwa, noma ikhona elilodwa lekhasi. Ukuphela emizuzu emibili kuyisikhathi esihle.
- **Ngaphandle kwamazwi.** Phakamisa amakhasi amabili, noma imibala emibili, bese ubheka ukufinyelela noma ukubheka. Ukukhomba nokunyakazisa ikhanda kuyizimpendulo.

## Izincomo zokuphepha

- Sebenzisa ama-crayon, ama-marker nopensela abanemibala okungenayo ubuthi. Hlola ukuthi ilebula ithi akunabuthi. I-[Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety)ichaza ukuthi uphawu lwe-AP lusho ukuthini ezintweni zobuciko.
- Gcina izikelo nezivalo ezincane kude nomuntu ongazifaka emlonyeni.
- Kumuntu othanda ukumunya izinto, sebenzisa izingcezu ezinkulu nezingenaubuthi, futhi uzihlole kaningi.
- Ithebhulethi kufanele igcinwe kude namanzi nokudla nesiphuzo.

## Uma kungasebenzi

- **Ikhasi linzima kakhulu.** Lishintshe ngomklamo olula onezindawo ezinkulu.
- **Ukuthukuthela kubonakala.** Beka ukhilayoni phansi, bese unikeza ukwenza indawo elandelayo ndawonye. Noma yeka, ubuyele ngolunye usuku.
- **Umuntu usunduza ikhasi.** Lokho yimpendulo ecacile. Nikeza omunye umsebenzi, njengengxoxo noma uhambo.
- **Ufuna ukudweba, kodwa hhayi ngalelo thuluzi.** Zama i-marker ejiyile, i-crayon enkulu noma umunwe ocwiliswe ku-watercolor.

## Ukwenza ngomuntu aphila ne-dementia

Ukudweba ngemibala kuvame ukunikezwa abantu abadala, kuhlanganise nabantu abaphila ne-dementia, ngoba akudingi inkumbulo nempendulo eyodwa elungile. Ezigabeni zokuqala, abantu abaningi bajabulela amakhasi anemininingwane futhi bakhethe imibala yabo. Esigabeni esiphakathi, izithombe ezinkulu nokukhetha okuncane kuyasiza. Ezigabeni zokugcina, isimo esisodwa esikhulu, ishidi lombala othambile, noma nje ukubamba i-crayon ngenkathi uhumusha ingoma kungaba kwanele. Abanye abantu bajabulela ukubheka isithombe esiqediwe kunokusenza.

Imisebenzi isebenza kangcono lapho ihambisana nalokho umuntu angakwenza futhi lapho ilungiswa njengoba amandla eshintsha. I-[ikhasi lemisebenzi ye-NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) lichaza ukuthi imisebenzi yezigaba zokugcina ivame ukugxila ezimpilweni zomzwelo. Ukuze uthole imibono eminingi, bona [umhlahlandlela wemisebenzi yesigaba sokugcina](/activities/late-stage/).

Uma ufuna umdlalo ophephile ukuzeulandele iseshini yokudweba, zama [i-picture bingo](/games/picture-bingo/), esebenzisa izithombe ezicacile esikhundleni samanani.

I-Creative Activities ayiyona ithherapi. Ukudweba kuyumsebenzi wokujabulela ndawonye, hhayi ukwelashwa.
