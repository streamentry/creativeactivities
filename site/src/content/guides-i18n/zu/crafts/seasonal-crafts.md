Isikhathi sonyaka sinika isizathu sokwenza into. Amaqabunga ayaguquka kube nomfanekiso okufanele wenziwe. Izinsuku ziyafinyeleka futhi ingarland ikhanyisa igumbi. Amasonto okuqala afudumele alungele isitsha sembewu esihlezi endaweni yefasitela. Izinto zezandla zezikhathi zonyaka zilula ukuzihlela, futhi izinto ezidingekayo zivame ukuba mahhala. Leli khasi linemibono yezigaba zonyaka ngayinye, namaholide emasikweni amaningi, kanye nendlela yokugcina ukwenza kuthule futhi kuphephile.

Le mibono ifanele imindeni, abangane, izindawo zokunakekela namaqembu omphakathi. Ifanele omuntu othanda izikhathi zonyaka, omuntu onomcimbi weholidi ayefuna ukuwugubha, nomuntu ofuna nje isizathu sokuhlala ndawonye nokwenza okuthile. Ukukhetha umsebenzi kuncike kokujabulisayo komuntu, hhayi ukwahlulela ukuthi yini angayenza.

## Ukhetha kanjani izinto zezandla zezikhathi zonyaka?

Qala ngalokho okuseduze kwakho. Uhambo lokuqoqa amaqabunga, noma ukubheka okuqhakazayo, ngokuvamile kunikeza iprojekthi enhle kakhulu. Bese ucabanga ngomuntu:

- **Uyayithanda yini indawo yangaphandle?** Khetha into esebenzisa imvelo, njengesithombe samaqabunga noma ingilazi yembewu.
- **Uyawathanda yini amaphepha namakhadi?** Ikhadi eligotshiwe womuntu omkhathalelayo, noma ingarland yephepha, kungafanela.
- **Uyathanda izinyoni noma izilwane?** Isitsha senyoni esivela esihlahleni (pine cone) kungenziwa siphachikwe endaweni ayikwazi ukuyibona ngefasitela.
- **Ngabe uthanda into ethulile?** Ibhodi lokuthinta noma i-shaker evaliwe kunikeza umphumela othulile wokwenza ngezandla.

## Izinto zezandla zezikhathi zonyaka unyaka wonke

### Entwasahlobo

- **Tshala imbewu esitsheni.** Imbewu ekhulu nelula esitsheni somquba, ithelwa kancane. [Ikhadi lesitsha sembewu](/activity/plant-seeds-in-a-pot/) linenguqulo yokuhlezi nenguqulo elula.
- **Penda ilitshe legadi.** Ilitshe elibushelelezi elipentwe ngamachashaza noma amagagasi aphindaphindekayo, lokhu kwendlela noma ibhokisi lefasitela lezimbali. Bheka [ikhadi lelitshe legadi](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/).

### Ehlobo

- **Yenza isikhwama selavenda.** Gcwalisa bese ubopha isikhwama esincane sendwangu ngelavenda eliwomile. Asidingi ukushelela, futhi kukhona nezinguqulo zasebhedeni nezingenaphunga. Bheka [ikhadi lesikhwama selavenda](/activity/make-a-lavender-sachet/).
- **Phrinta ngamaqabunga.** Penda ingemuva leqabunga bese ulicindezela ephepheni ukuze kubonakale imithambo. Lokhu kungenziwa phandle noma etafuleni. Bheka [ikhadi lokuphrinta ngamaqabunga](/activity/print-with-leaves/).

### Ekwindla

- **Cindezela amaqabunga nezimbali.** Zicindezele embhukwini elinzima isonto elilodwa, bese uzinamathisela ekhadini. Bheka [ikhadi lokucindezela](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/).
- **Yenza ikholaji yezinkumbulo.** Ikholaji yezikhathi zonyaka ezenziwe ngezithombe zamamagazini nezithombe zomndeni, etafuleni elabelwanayo. Bheka [ikhadi lekholaji yezinkumbulo](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/).

### Ebusika

- **Yenza ingarland yeketango lephepha.** Xhumanisa imichilo yephepha enombala, ngayinye ngesikhathi. Ingaphachikwa egumbini, futhi kulula ukuyabelana. Bheka [ikhadi leketango lephepha](/activity/make-a-paper-chain-garland/).
- **Yenza isitsha senyoni esivela esihlahleni (pine cone).** Cindezela amafutha nembewu yezinyoni esihlahleni, bese uyiphachika lapho ungakwazi ukubheka khona izinyoni. [Ikhadi lesitsha senyoni](/activity/make-a-pine-cone-bird-feeder/) linenketho engenawo amantongomane.
- **Yenza ikhadi lokubingelela.** Ikhadi eligotshiwe lesikhathi sebusika, elithunyelwa komuntu omkhathalelayo. Bheka [ikhadi lokubingelela](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/).

## Amaholide emasikweni amaningi

Abantu abaningi bagubha imicimbi ngalesi sikhathi sonyaka, kanti abanye abagubhi. Ezinye izinkathi zisebenzisa izikhwama zephepha, amabhanela ephepha asikiwe, izikhwama ezibomvu noma amaphethini adwetshwe. Ezinye azisebenzisi lutho kulokhu. Le sayithi ayithathi ukuthi kunoholide oyedwa, inkolo eyodwa noma uhlobo olulodwa lomndeni.

Lapho uhlela into yeholidi:

1. **Buza kuqala.** Buza ukuthi umuntu ufuna ukugubha ini, nokuthi yiziphi izinsuku ezibalulekile kuye. Mvumele akhethe umcimbi, noma akhethe ukungawukhethi.
2. **Sebenzisa izinto, hhayi izimpawu.** Iphepha, amaqabunga, indwangu nembewu kulungele cishe noma iyiphi isikhathi sonyaka. Gwema izimpawu zenkolo nezesiko ngaphandle uma wazi incazelo yazo futhi umuntu ezozamukela.
3. **Buza umuntu osuke esiswini leso.** Uma ufuna ukwenza into ehlotshaniswa neholidi elithile, buza umuntu owaziyo kahle ukuthi igugwa kanjani nokuthi yini efanele. Ukuhumusha kodwa akwanele.
4. **Gcina ukukhetha kuvulekile.** Nikeza izinketho ezintathu noma ezimbili, bese wamukela ukuthi cha.

## Yiziphi izinguqulo ezilula ongazama?

- **Ehlezi phansi.** Imisebenzi eminingi yezikhathi zonyaka isebenza etafuleni. Beka izinto endaweni efinyeleleka.
- **Kulula ukubamba.** Sebenzisa iphepha elijiyile namathuluzi abanzi. Goba ikhadi kusengaphambili, noma usike imichilo yeketango kusengaphambili.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Khetha iphepha elikhanyayo nokuqhathanisa okunamandla, nesithombe esikhulu esisodwa ekhadini ngalinye.
- **Izinketho ezimbalwa.** Nikeza imibala emibili, noma iqabunga elilodwa nesitsha esisodwa. Sebenzisa isampula eqediwe ukuze kukopishwe.
- **Okufushane.** Namathisela ixhumanisi lokuqala noma iqabunga lokuqala kusengaphambili, ukuze umuntu engeze ingxenye yokugcina kuphela.
- **Akukhulumi.** Bonisa izinto ezimbili ezedlule ezenziwe, bese ubheka ukufinyelela noma ukubheka.

## Izincomo zokuphepha zezinto zezandla zezikhathi zonyaka

- **Izibani.** Sebenzisa izibani ze-LED noma ze-battery ezikhwameni lephepha namaketango. Ungasebenzisi amakhandlela eduze kwephepha, indwangu noma izitshalo ezomile.
- **Izihlobiso.** Ungasebenzisi amaswidi, noma izithelo nokudla okwenziwe ngokwemvelo njengezihlobiso. Abanye abantu abaphila ne-dementia bangazicabanga ukuthi ziyiqiniso ukudla. I-[umhlahlandlela weholidi we-Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) uphawula futhi ukuthi izibani ezikhanyayo ezivuleka nezicima zingadida.
- **Izingxenye ezincane.** Gcina izinkinobho, izinhlamba, izihlobiso ezincane nembewu engagcini kude nabantwana abangaphansi kweminyaka emithathu. I-[ikhasi lokuvimbela ukubhoboza ye-HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/choking-prevention.aspx) libala izingxenye ezincane phakathi kwezimbangela ezivamile zokubhoboza.
- **Izitshalo.** Ungamunyi noma ubambe izitshalo noma amajikijolo ongawazi. Geza izandla emva kokubamba inhlabathi noma umjiki.
- **Ngaphandle.** Hlola izinkupha, ilanga nokuluma zinambuzane emva kwesikhathi esiphandle.

## Uma kungasebenzi

- **Usuku lunomsebenzi omningi.** Hambisa umsebenzi ugumbi elithulile, ugcine ufushane. Iqabunga elilodwa noma ikhadi elilodwa liyiseshini ephelele.
- **Ukuthukuthela kubonakala.** Yeka, bese unikeza ukuphumula noma uhambo. Umsebenzi wezikhathi zonyaka ungaqedwa ngolunye usuku.
- **Umuntu akafuni ukwenza lutho.** Hlalani ndawonye nibheke isikhathi sonyaka. Khuluma ngalokho okubonayo ngaphandle. Lokho kubalulekile.

## Ukwenza kuthule ngosuku

Amaholide angaletha umsindo, izixuku nokuguquka komkhuba ojwayelekile. Hlela ngokuhambisana nomkhuba ojwayelekile womuntu, futhi gcina usuku lube lincane uma ungakwazi. Nikeza imisebenzi elula, njengokugoqa amasondo noma ukugeza imifino, bese ulungisa igumbi elithulile uma usuku sekuba nzima kakhulu. Amakholi evidiyo angavumela umndeni okude ukuthi ubambe iqhaza ngaphandle kokujoyina isixuku.

## Ukwenza nomuntu omdala noma nomuntu aphila ne-dementia

Izinto zezandla zezikhathi zonyaka zingaba yindlela enomusa yokugubha isikhathi sonyaka nomuntu omdala, kuhlanganise nomuntu aphila ne-dementia. Khetha into ejwayelekile, njengekhadi noma isithombe samaqabunga, bese uyigcina esinyathelweni esisodwa. Mvumele akhethe imibala noma acindezele iqabunga. Izithombe zakudala zezikhathi zonyaka ezedlule zingaqala ingxoxo, futhi [umhlahlandlela wokukhumbula](/activities/reminiscence/) uchaza ukuthi ungakhuluma kanjani ngesikhathi esedlule ngaphandle kokubuza imibuzo. Ukuhlabelela ingoma yezikhathi zonyaka, okuncoma i-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays), kuyindlela enye yokubamba iqhaza.

Uma umuntu esanda kutholwa, noma esaqhubeka ezijwayeza indawo entsha yokunakekelwa, [umhlahlandlela wethu ngemva kokutholwa yi-dementia](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) ungasiza.
