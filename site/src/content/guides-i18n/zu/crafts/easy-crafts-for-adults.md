Abantu abaningi bathi abakwazi ukudweba, ukupenda noma ukwenza izinto. Ngokuvamile bakusho ukuthi abakakwenzi kusukela esikoleni, noma ukuthi bakhathazekile ukuthi umphumela uzobonakala ungumntwana noma ungahle. Leli khasi lelabantu abafuna ukwenza into nomuntu omunye futhi abanalo ukuqeqeshwa kobuciko okuyonikeza. Imisebenzi engezansi ilula, isebenzisa izinto zansuku zonke, futhi ayinalo umphumela ongalungile.

Lezi zinto zezandla zisebenza kumkhulu, kumngane, kumnakekeli noma kumsebenzi womphakathi. Zilungele nomuntu onamandla amancane kunangaphambili, omuntu othanda ukuhlala phansi, noma omuntu ofuna nje ihora elithulile nezandla zakhe zisebenza. Lezi ziphakamiso zisekelwe ezidingweni zomsebenzi, hhayi ukwahlulela ukuthi ubani angakwenza yini.

## Udinga ini?

Iningi lalezi zinto zezandlaludinga izinto zasendlini kuphela nokuthile okuthengwa esitolo:

- Itafula elinendwangu eyimbozo engaphezulu noma ishidi elidala
- Iphepha elinzima noma ikhadibhodi elincane, njengebhokisi lokusanhlamvu, ipuleti lephepha noma ishidi eligoqiwe lephepha lomshini wokuprinta
- Igilu eliqinile (glue stick), elilula ukulisebenzisa kunoketshezi
- Ipende lemibala yamanzi noma imibala esebenza ngokudla, ibhulashi elikhulu elithambile namakhomishi amabili amancane amanzi
- Izithombe ezivela emagazini ezidala, emakhadini okubingelela noma emaphaketheni embewu
- Izimbali ezomile noma ezicindezelwe, amaqabunga noma izinduku ezimbalwa ezivela egadini

[Umhlahlandlela wethu wezinto zobuciko](/activities/art-supplies-for-older-adults/) uveza izinto eziyishumi ezibiza kancane uma uqala ungenalutho.

## Yiziphi izinto ezilula kakhulu ukuqala ngazo?

Lezi zinhlelo ngazinye zinesinyathelo sokuqala esisodwa esicacile. Khetha esisodwa, bese uhlala kuso lapho seshini.

### Ikholaji yombala

Khetha umbala owodwa bese udatshula izithombe zalowo mbala emagazini. Ukudatshula kulula kunokusika, futhi akunawo amathuluzi abukhali. Namathisela izingcezu ekhadini ndawonye. [Ikhadi lekholaji yombala](/activity/make-a-color-collage/) linezinyathelo ezigcwele.

### Imibala yamanzi

Mata iphepha elinzima ngamanzi ahlanzekile, bese umuntu ephonsa umbala phezu kwalo ngebhulashi elikhulu. Imibala iyasabalala futhi ihlangane. Akukho okungahle kube yiphutha. [Ikhadi lemibala yamanzi](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/) ligubha iphepha, upende nokuhlanza.

### Ikhadi eligoqiwe

Goqa ishidi lekhadi libe lezingxenye ezimbili. Namathisela isithombe esisodwa, bese nibhala umlayezo omfushane ndawonye womuntu omthandayo. Buza ukuthi ikhadi ngelani ngaphambi kokuqala, ngoba isizathu sangempela sokwenza into siyenza ijabulise kakhulu. [Ikhadi lokubingelela](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) linezinyathelo.

### Izimbali embizeni

Sika izinduku ezimbalwa ubude obufanele, bese uzibeka embizeni eyisisindo, eyodwa ngesikhathi. Mema phansi ubheke ukuhlelwa ndawonye. [Ikhadi lokuhlela izimbali](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) linezinguqulo ezilula zesinduku esisodwa noma indebe encane.

### Amaqabunga nezimbali ezicindezelwe

Qoqa amaqabunga noma izimbali ezincane, uzicindezele phakathi kwamakhasi ebhuku elinzima isonto elilodwa, bese uzinamathisela ekhadini eligoqiwe. [Ikhadi lokucindezela](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/) lichaza izinyathelo.

### Ibhodi lokuthinta

Namathisela izicucu zendwangu, uchungechunge nephepha ekhadini eliqinile. Ukuthinta ibhodi eliqediwe kuyingxenye yomsebenzi, ngakho lusebenza kubantu abathanda ukubamba izinto. Bheka [ikhadi lebhodi lokuthinta](/activity/make-a-texture-board/).

### Ingilazi yobumba

Yakha isitsha esincane ngobumba obuyoma emoyeni ngokucindezela ngamathambo ezithupha. Lidinga amathuluzi, futhi liyoma ebusuku. Bheka [ikhadi lesitsha sokucindezela](/activity/shape-a-pinch-pot/).

## Ungayiqhuba kanjani iseshini?

1. **Lungisa kuqala.** Beka izinto ezimbalwa, indwangu nendwangu enamanzi yezandla. Sika futhi udatshule noma yini oyidingayo ngaphambilini.
2. **Khombisa isinyathelo sokuqala.** Cwilisa ibhulashi, dathula ingcezu yokuqala noma cindezela ikhasi lokuqala, bese umemela umuntu enze okufanayo.
3. **Sebenzela eceleni.** Gcina ingcezu yakho eduze, ukuze umsebenzi uzwakale njengowabelwanayo hhayi njengowokuqapha.
4. **Khuluma ngalokho okubonayo.** Phawula ngombala, ukuthungwa nesimo. Gwema imibuzo yokuhlola efana nelathi "Lokho kuyini?" nokuncoma okufana nelathi "Wenze kahle", okungazwakala njengokungumntwana.
5. **Yeka ngaphambi kokuthi amandla ehle.** Imizuzu eyishumi kuya kweyishumi nanhlanu ivame ukwanela. Iprojekthi ingaqedwa ngolunye usuku, noma ishiywe njengoba iyunjalo.

I-Alzheimer's Association [ichaza](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) ukuthi amaprojekthi obuciko angadala umuzwa wokuphumelela nenjongo, futhi anikeze ithuba lokuziveza. I-[ikhasi lemisebenzi ye-NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) lichaza ukuthi imisebenzi ivame ukusebenza kangcono lapho ilula futhi isebenzisa imizwa, njengokuthinta nokubona.

## Yiziphi izinguqulo ezilula ongazama?

Yonke imisebenzi engenhla ingenziwa ibe lula. Khetha ushintsho olufanele umuntu ngalolo suku.

- **Ehlezi phansi.** Konke lokhu kusebenza etafuleni. Hlalani eceleni komunye nomunye, ungemi phezu komuntu.
- **Kulula ukubamba.** Sebenzisa amabhulashi amakhulu, ama-crayon amakhulu noma igilu eliqinile elinomzimba obanzi. Bopha isibambo esincane nge-foam tape. Bopha iphepha ngethepi ukuze lingashelelwa.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Sebenzisa iphepha elikhanyayo nomugqa omnyama esithombeni, umbala omkhulu owodwa ngesikhathi, nesibani phezu kwetafula. Yenza isampula eqediwe ibonakale.
- **Izinketho ezimbalwa.** Nikeza imibala emibili, noma izithombe ezine ezidatshulwe kusengaphambili. Vumela umuntu akhethe phakathi kwazo, hhayi phakathi kwetafula eligcwele izinto.
- **Okufushane.** Namathisela ingcezu yokuqala kusengaphambili, ukuze umuntu anamathisele eyokugcina kuphela. Ikhadi elinesithombe esisodwa lingaqediwa ngemizuzu emihlanu.

## Izincomo zokuphepha

- Sebenzisa izinto ezingenabuthi, bese uhlola ukuthi imibala negilu balebuliwe ukuze zisetshenziswe ngalokho. I-[Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety) ichaza uphawu lwe-AP, olumaka izinto zobuciko eziqinisekisiwe ukuthi azinalo ubuthi ngamanani anele. Hlola ilebula, hhayi umbala wephakheji kuphela.
- Gcina izikelo zingabukhali noma sebenzisa ukudatshula esikhundleni salokho. Ukusika nganoma yiluphi uhlobo lwesikelo esibukhali wenze wena ngaphambilini.
- Qaphela noma ubani efaka umbala, ubumba, izimbali noma izingcezu ezincane emlonyeni. Gcina izingcezu ezingagcini ethreyini.
- Hlola ukuthi amagilavu alutha lwe-latex yini, nokuthi ukhona yini ukungezwani kwesikhumba nogilu, umbala noma umjiki wesitshalo.
- Ezinye izitshalo nezimbewu azivikelekile ukuthintwa noma ukunambitheka. Sebenzisa kuphela izitshalo ozazi igama lazo.

## Uma kungasebenzi

- **Umuntu ubeka izinto eceleni.** Yeka, bese ubeka iprojekthi endaweni ephephile. Mnike ikomishi letiye noma uhambo. Ukuzama futhi ngolunye usuku kuyisinqumo esijwayelekile.
- **Ukuthukuthela kubonakala.** Thatha isinyathelo esinzima ngokwakho bese umemela ukuthi enze elilandelayo. Iprojekthi encane, noma eneyingxenye elungiselelwe kusengaphambili, ingasiza.
- **Bathi abakwazi ukukwenza.** Mqinisekise ukuthi umphumela awubalulekile. Qala umugqa wokuqala bese ushiya indawo yokuthi aqhubeke. Uma besaqhubeka ukwenqaba, yiyeka.
- **Awuqinisekile ukuthi yini okufanele ulandele.** Gcina impahla eyodwa nesinyathelo esisodwa. Okulula kuhle.

## Ukwenza nomuntu aphila ne-dementia

Izinto zezandla ezinjena zingafanela umuntu omdala, kuhlanganise nomuntu aphila ne-dementia. Olunye ushintsho oluwusizo kakhulu ukubheka umsebenzi njengomsebenzi wangempela, njengokwenza ikhadi lomngane. Gcina izinyathelo zifushane kakhulu, bese uvumela umuntu enze ingxenye angasaba, njengokukhetha imibala noma ukucindezela ishamuza phansi. Ezigabeni zokugcina, ukugeza ngemibala yamanzi noma ukucindezela ubumba kungase kwanele ukuze abe yingxenye. Ukukubheka wenza ingcezu yakho, nokukhuluma ngemibala, nakho kubalulekile. Ukuze uthole okwengeziwe kulokhu, bona [umhlahlandlela wethu wemisebenzi yobuciko yeqembu](/activities/group-art-activities/).

Uma umuntu engasafuni ukubamba iqhaza, hlonipha lokho. Umhlahlandlela wethu wokuthi [lapho bethi cha](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) uchaza ukuthi ungabuyela emuva kanjani ngaphandle kocindezelo.

I-Creative Activities ayiyona ithherapi. Ubuciko bokwelapha yimisebenzi ehlukile, enekhono elingokwezokwelashwa elinikezwa umuntu oqeqeshiwe. Lezi zinto zezandla zokujabulela nokuchitha isikhathi ndawonye.
