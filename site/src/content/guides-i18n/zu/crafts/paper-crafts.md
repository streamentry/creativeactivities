Iphepha lingenye yezinto ezilula kakhulu ukwenza ngazo. Liyagoba, liyadatshuleka, liyanamathiseleka futhi liyagqoka, futhi lidla imali encane kakhulu. Alidingi nezikelo ezibukhali, okwenza kube isinqumo esihle lapho ufuna umsebenzi wezandla ophephile eqenjini elixubile nolula ukuwuhlanza ngemva kwawo. Leli khasi lichaza imisebenzi yephepha esinayo kule sayithi, indlela yokuyilungiselela, nendlela yokuyilungisa.

Amaprojekthi alapha afanele abantu abadala abafuna into elula yokwenza, imindeni enezingane ezikhulile, namaqembu alabhulali noma ekilabhini. Afanele nomuntu ofisa ukuhlala phansi asebenze ngezandla zakhe, noma othanda ukungasebenzisi izikelo. Lezi ziphakamiso zalo msebenzi, hhayi ukwahlulela noma ubani.

## Udinga ini?

- Iphepha elinzima noma ikhadibhodi. Amabhokisi okusanhlamvu, amakhadi okubingelela namapuleti ephepha konke kuyasebenza. Iphepha elincane lomshini wokuprinta lilungele ukugoba kodwa liyadatsheka kalula.
- Igilu eliqinile, noma i-double-sided tape, zemisebenzi edinga ukubamba ngaphandle kokulinda ukoma.
- Iphepha lethishu noma amakhasi amagazini adala okudatshulwa.
- Ithreyi enemiphetho ephakanyisiwe, ukuze izingcezu ezincane zihlale endaweni eyodwa.
- Ithepu yokufihla ukuze ibambe iphepha lihlale etafuleni.
- Izikelo zokuphepha ezingabukhali, uma usebenzisa izikelo nhlobo. Ummese wokusika (craft knife) ungowabantu abadala kuphela, futhi awudingeki kunoma yiliphi iprojekthi lapha.

## Yiziphi izimisebenzi zephepha ezilula ukuqala ngazo?

### Goba isikebhe sephepha

Goba iphepha elilodwa libe isikebhe esilula, bese usifaka esitsheni samanzi ukuze siphaphame. Ukugoba kusebenzisa izandla kuphela, futhi umphumela uyaphaphama, into abantu abaningi bayithola inelisekayo. [Ikhadi lesikebhe sephepha](/activity/fold-a-paper-boat/) linikeza izinyathelo, linenguqulo elula nenguqulo yeqembu.

### Yenza ketango lephepha

Sika noma udatshule imichilo yephepha elinombala, uyengeze ngayinye kwelokugcina, bese unamathisela noma uthepha iziphetho. Ketango lephepha likhula ngexhumanisi elilodwa ngesikhathi, futhi lingaphachikwa egumbini, emcimbini noma ngesizini. [Ikhadi leketango lephepha](/activity/make-a-paper-chain-garland/) lichaza ukuthi uyilungisa kanjani eqenjini.

### Luka imichilo yephepha

Luka imichilo enombala phezu nangaphansi kwesisekelo sekhadi esesinqunyiwe kusengaphambili. Ukuhamba kokuluka kuyathula futhi kuphindaphindeka, futhi into eqediwe iyakhanya. [Ikhadi lokuluka](/activity/weave-paper-strips/) limboza isisekelo nemichilo.

### Imosaic yephepha eladatshulwe

Dathula iphepha elinombala libe izingcezu, bese unamathisela ekhadini ukuze wenze isithombe esilula. Akukho ukudweba nezikelo. [Ikhadi lemosaic yephepha eladatshulwe](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/) linikeza izinyathelo zomuntu oyedwa noma iqembu elincane.

### Yenza ikhadi lokubingelela

Goba ikhadi libe lezingxenye ezimbili, unamathisele isithombe esisodwa phambili, bese ubhala umlayezo omfushane ngaphakathi. Lesi yisinye sezimisebenzi zephepha ezisiza kakhulu, ngoba ikhadi linenhloso yangempela. Bheka [ikhadi lokubingelela](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/).

### Ikholaji yombala

Khetha umbala owodwa bese udatshula izithombe zalowo mbala emagazini. Namathisela ekhadini endleleni yanoma iyiphi. [Ikhadi lekholaji yombala](/activity/make-a-color-collage/) linezinyathelo zokulungiselela nenguqulo yokubona kancane.

## Ungayilungisa kanjani iseshini yephepha?

1. **Goba umgoqo wokuqala kusengaphambili.** Hudula ipeni elingabukhali noma ingemuva lesipuni emgqeni, ukuze ukugoba kokuqala kube lula.
2. **Bopha iphepha ngethepi.** Umugqa wethepi yokufihla phezu kwamakona angaphezulu ugcina ishidi lingashintshi womsebenzi wesandla esisodwa.
3. **Hlela izingcezu ngaphambi kokuqala.** Beka izingcezu ezidatshuliwe noma ezisikiwe ethreyini, zihlelwe ngombala. Lokhu kunikeza umuntu ukukhetha okucacile ngaphandle kokungcola.
4. **Khombisa isinyathelo esisodwa.** Goba ikhona lokuqala, noma dathula ingcezu yokuqala, bese uvumela umuntu alandele.
5. **Yeka ngesikhathi esihle.** Qeda ixhumanisi elilodwa leketango, ukugoba okulodwa noma ikhadi elilodwa, bese umisa.

## Yiziphi izinguqulo ezilula ongazama?

- **Ehlezi phansi.** Konke lokhu kusebenza etafuleni. Hlala eceleni, hhayi phambi komuntu.
- **Kulula ukubamba.** Sebenzisa ikhadibhodi elijiyile, elimelana nokugoba futhi lilula ukulibamba. Sebenzisa ithepu esikhundleni sokugoba, ukuze iphepha lihlale lisicaba.
- **Ngesandla esisodwa.** Bopha iphepha etafuleni, usebenzise ithreyi emi ngokwayo. Amagilu angasetshenziswa ngesandla esisodwa ngemva kokuthi isivalo sesusiwe.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Sebenzisa ukuqhathanisa okunamandla, njengekhadi elimnyama ngaphansi kwephepha elimhlophe, nesithombe esikhulu esisodwa ngekhadi ngalinye.
- **Izinketho ezimbalwa.** Nikeza imibala emibili noma izimo ezintathu ezinqunyiwe kusengaphambili. Isampula esenziwe kusengaphambili ukuze kukopishwe yenza isinyathelo esilandelayo sibe lula.
- **Okufushane.** Goba isikebhe kusengaphambili, noma namathisela amaxhumanisi amabili okuqala eketango, ukuze umuntu engeze abambalwa kuphela.

## Izincomo zokuphepha

- Gcina izingcezu ezincane zephepha, ama-confetti nezinsalela zethishu ethreyini noma esikhwameni esinesivalo. Izingcezu ezinjalo zingaba yingozi yokubhoboza ezinganeni ezingaphansi kweminyaka emithathu. I-[ikhasi lokuvimbela ukubhoboza ye-HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/choking-prevention.aspx) ibala izinto ezincane phakathi kwezimbangela ezivamile zokubhoboza ezinganeni ezincane.
- Sebenzisa izikelo zokuphepha ezingabukhali ezinganeni, bese ugcina amakhasi okusika ngabantu abadala kuphela.
- Imiphetho yephepha ingasika iminwe, ikakhulu ezandleni ezomile. Abanye abantu bakhetha ukudatshula kunokusika.
- Hlola amalebula egilu ukuze uthole ukungezwani kwesikhumba. Sebenzisa igilu eliqinile noma igilu lobuciko elingenabuthi.
- Ungavumeli muntu ukufaka iphepha noma igilu emlonyeni.

## Uma kungasebenzi

- **Ukugoba kuyashelela.** Sebenzisa iphepha elijiyile, noma faka ithepu phezu komgoqo. Umgoqo owugoqwe kusengaphambili uyasiza kakhulu.
- **Amastrip amancane kakhulu.** Sika amastrip abanzi, noma sebenzisa iphepha elijiyile. Amastrip amancane anzima ukuwabamba.
- **Umuntu ukhathele noma uyakhathala.** Yeka emva kwengcezu eyodwa. Ungabuyela kwiprojekthi ngolunye usuku.
- **Izingcezu ziqhubeka zisiwa phansi.** Sebenzela ethreyini, bese ubeka ithreyi etafuleni eliqinile.

## Ukwenza nomuntu omdala noma nengane

Eminye yalezi zinto zezandla ifanele abantu abadala nezingane ezisebenza ndawonye. Kumuntu omdala, kubandakanya nomuntu aphila ne-dementia, gcina izinyathelo zimfushane futhi mvumele enze ingxenye ayisajabulela, njengokukhetha imibala noma ukucindezela ukugoba phansi. Kwengane, gcina umuntu omdala eduze njalo, sebenzisa izingcezu ezinkulu, bese ugcina izinto ezincane kude nezingane ezincane. Izimpahla zephepha ziwukujabulela ukwenza. Izingane zingajabulela umsebenzi, futhi umphumela ungaba ikhadi noma isikebhe azigcinayo.
