Ubuciko obusetshenziswa kabusha buqala ngezinto ezisendlini eziningi: amabhokisi okudla kwasekuseni, amabhokisi amaqanda, imibhobho yekhadibhodi, izitsha ze-yogathi, izimbiza, amathini nezinsalela zendwangu. Azibizi lutho, zilula ukuzithola, futhi ukwenza into ewusizo ngazo kungaba nenjabulo ngendlela okungenzeki ngayo ngokuthenga isethi. Leli khasi lichaza ukuthi yiziphi iphrojekthi ezisebenza kahle, indlela yokulungisa izinto ngokuphepha, nokuguqula ngayinye.

Lezi phrojekthi zifanele umndeni onamabhokisi ambalwa atholakalayo, ikilasi, umndeni onezingane ezikhulile, noma indawo yokunakekela lapho izinto ezifanayo zifika njalo ngesonto. Zifanele futhi umuntu othanda ukuhlanganisa izinto, noma othanda ukusebenza ngezinto ezinendaba yazo ngaphambili. Izincomo lapha zichaza ukuthi yini into ngayinye edinga. Akuwona ukwahlulela muntu.

## Lungiselela izinto zakho kuqala

Ukulungiselela kuyingxenye enkulu yokuphepha, futhi kuhle ukukwenza ngaphambi kokuthi umuntu ahlale phansi.

1. **Hlanza futhi womise isitsha ngasinye.** Sebenzisa amanzi ashisayo anesepha, hlanza kahle, bese uvumela ukuthi somise ngokuphelele. Isitsha esimanzi singakhulisa isikhunta ngaphakathi kwephrojekthi evaliwe.
2. **Hlola ukuthi yini ebisigcine.** Ungasebenzisi izitsha ebezigcine imikhiqizo yokuhlanza, amakhemikhali, imithi, ukudla kwezilwane noma nanoma yini ongeke uyihlanze ngokuphelele.
3. **Susa imiphetho ebukhali.** Hlola amathini nezivalo. Yigudluze ngefayili, uyembeze ngethephu, noma ushiye leyo ngxenye.
4. **Sika ngaphambili.** Sika ikhadibhodi, usike amawindi nezikhala ezincane ngokwakho ngemese yobuciko. Gcina lo mese nawe, ungafinyeleleki, futhi uwubeke ngaphansi ngemva kokusebenzisa.
5. **Hlela izingxenye ezincane esitsheni.** Izinkinobho, izivalo zamabhodlela namapheyili kufanele kugcinwe esitsheni esinemiphetho ephakanyisiwe. Akufanele zibe etafuleni ngokukhululekile.

## Yiziphi izinto zobuciko ezilula ukuqala ngazo?

### Izimbali zamaqanda

Sika izindebe ebhokisini lamaqanda, ushiye umphetho omncane. Zipende, uvumele zome, bese usika amacembe ambalwa uwagcobe ekhebulini elicwebezelayo noma enduku. Izimbali ezimbalwa embizeni zenza ukuhlobisa kwetafula okukhanyayo. Izindebe ezisikiwe ngaphambili zilula ukubamba kunamabhokisi aphelele.

### Uhlaka lwesithombe ngebhokisi lokudla kwasekuseni

Sika unxande ohlakeni olusemaphakathi lwebhokisi lokudla kwasekuseni, unamathisele isithombe noma ikhadi ngemuva kwalo, bese uphinda ucezu lwekhadi ukwenza isisekelo. [Ikhadi lokuhlobisa ibhokisi lesikhumbuzo](/activity/decorate-a-keepsake-box/) lisebenzisa indlela efanayo yokumboza ibhokisi ngephepha noma ngendwangu.

### Isitsha sokutshala se-yogathi

Hlanza isitsha, wenze izimbobo zokukhipha amanzi ngesikhewu noma ngesipikili (kubantu abadala kuphela), bese uhlobisa ngaphandle ngopende noma ngephepha, bese wengeza umquba nesitshalo. [Ikhadi lesitsha sokutshala](/activity/decorate-a-plant-pot/) licacisa izinyathelo nenguqulo elula ukuyibamba.

### Isishaka esivaliwe

Gcwalisa ibhodlela lepulasitiki elincane nelihlanzekile ngerayisi elomile noma ngamalentili, vala isivalo ngethephu eqinile noma ngogovo, bese uyahlobisa. Umsizi kufanele avale isivalo. [Ikhadi lesishaka esivaliwe](/activity/make-a-sealed-shaker/) linezinyathelo nenguqulo yezikhathi ezithulile.

### Umfanekiso wamanikinobho

Hlukanisa izinkinobho ngombala noma ngosayizi, bese uzinamathisela ekhadini ukwenza isithombe esilula. Izinkinobho ziyingozi yokubhoxa kwezingane ezineminyaka engaphansi kwemithathu, ngakho le phrojekthi ifanele abantu abadala nezingane ezikhulile kuphela. [Ikhadi lomfanekiso wamanikinobho](/activity/make-a-button-picture/) linezinyathelo ezigcwele.

### Ibhodi lokuthinta lendwangu

Namathisela izingcezu zendwangu, ibhande nephepha ekhadini eliqinile. Alidingi ukusikwa futhi lisebenzisa kahle izinsalela. Bheka [ikhadi lebhodi lokuthinta](/activity/make-a-texture-board/).

## Yiziphi izinguqulo ezilula ongazama?

- **Ehleli.** Konke lokhu kusebenza etafuleni. Beka izinto eduze, ukuze umuntu angadingi ukuma.
- **Kulula ukubamba.** Sebenzisa izitsha ezibanzi nezilula, futhi gcoba izinti ezincane noma izivalo ngethephu ukuze zibe zinkulu. Khetha isivalo esilula ukusijikela.
- **Isandla esisodwa.** Namathisela isitsha etafuleni, noma usibeke esitsheni esinemiphetho ephakanyisiwe ukuze singanyakazi.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Khetha izitsha ezinombala ocacile wokuqhathanisa, futhi upende ngaphandle ngombala oqinile. Gcina umsebenzi endaweni ecacile, ekhanyayo.
- **Izingxenye ezimbalwa.** Khetha amaphrojekthi anengxenye eyodwa noma ezimbili. Isitsha esisodwa esihlotshisiwe noma uhlaka lwesithombe esisodwa kuyubuciko obuphelele.
- **Kufushane.** Pendela izingcezu kusengaphambili, uvumele zome, ukuze umuntu agcobe noma ahlobise kuphela.

## Amanothi wokuphepha

- Ungasebenzisi ingilazi nhlobo, futhi gwema amathini anemiphetho eqinile noma esikiwe.
- Gcina imimese yobuciko kubantu abadala kuphela, futhi uyisebenzise endaweni ezinzile yokusika kude nezandla zomuntu.
- Vala noma yini eyogcina izinto ezincane. Sebenzisa ithephu noma ugovo oqinile esivalweni, futhi hlola ukuthi asingavuleki.
- Gcina izinkinobho, izivalo zamabhodlela, amapheyili nezinhlamvu zamabhontshisi ezomile kude nabaneminyaka engaphansi kwemithathu. Ikhasi le-[HealthyChildren.org choking prevention page](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/choking-prevention.aspx) libala izinto ezifana namabhola amancane, izivalo zamapeni namabhethri anjengezinkinobho phakathi kwezinto ezibangela ukubhoxa.
- Sebenzisa upende nogovo okubhalwe ukuthi awunamakhemikhali anobuthi. [Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety) icacisa ukuthi i-AP seal iyisitifiketi sezinto zobuciko, nokuthi abantu abadala abangase bangafundi amalebula kufanele basebenzise izinto ezingenabuthi.
- Qaphela ukungezwa kugovo, kumadayi nakuzinto zezitshalo.

## Uma kungasebenzi

- **Isitsha siqhubeka sigingqika noma sishelela.** Sibeke esitsheni esinemiphetho ephakanyisiwe, noma unamathisele isisekelo esibanzi phansi.
- **Isivalo asiphenduki.** Gcoba isivalo ngethephu noma ngebhande lerabha ukuze sibambeke, noma khetha esinye isitsha.
- **Iphrojekthi ibonakala inkulu kakhulu.** Khetha isitsha esincane, noma yenza ingxenye eyodwa bese uyeka.
- **Kukhona iphunga noma umswakama.** Hlanza futhi womise isitsha futhi ngaphambi kokuqhubeka. Ungasebenzisi isitsha esinephunga lalokho ebesigcine.

## Ukwenza kanye nomuntu omdala noma ingane

Ubuciko obusetshenziswa kabusha bungabiyelwa phakathi kwezizukulwane. Umuntu omdala, kubandakanya nomuntu ophila nesifo sokulahlekelwa yinkumbulo, angajabula ngokuhlukanisa izinkinobho, ukukhetha imibala yopende noma ukugoqa isitsha ngentambo. Mvumele enze ingxenye asayijabulela, futhi wena wenze ukusika. Ngengane, gcina umuntu omdala eseduze, sebenzisa kuphela izingxenye ezephephile ngeminyaka yayo, futhi gcina izingcezu ezincane kude nezingane ezincane.

Abanye abantu bathola ukuthi isitsha esijwayelekile, njengembiza esuka ekhishini, yisona sinto evamise ukwamukelwa kakhulu. Cela imvume kuqala, bese uyigcina iseduze.
