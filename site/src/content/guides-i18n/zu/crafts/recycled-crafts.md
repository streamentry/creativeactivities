Izinto zezandla ezisetshenziswe kabusha ziqala ngezinto ezitholakala ezindlini eziningi: amabhokisi okusanhlamvu, amakhishi amaqanda, amathubhu kakhadibhodi, izitsha ze-yoghurt, izimbiza, amathini nezingcezu zendwangu. Azidli lutho, zilula ukuzithola, futhi ukwenza into ezosiza ngazo kungajabulisa ngendlela okungayenzi ngayo ikiti esithengiwe. Leli khasi lichaza ukuthi yimaphi amaprojekthi asebenza kahle, indlela yokulungiselela izinto ngokuphepha, nokuthi kungenziwa kanjani ngayinye ibe lula.

Lawa maprojekthi afanele indlu enamabhokisi ambalwa esandleni, igumbi lesikole, umndeni onezingane ezikhulile, noma indawo yokunakekela lapho izinto ezifanayo zifika zivela njalo ngesonto. Afanele nomuntu othanda ukuhlanganisa izinto, noma othanda ukusebenza ngezinto ezinendaba yazo ngaphambilini. Lezi ziphakamiso zichaza ukuthi umsebenzi ngamunye udinga ini. Azisho ukwahlulela muntu.

## Lungisa izinto kuqala

Ukulungiselela yingxenye enkulu yomsebenzi wokuphepha, futhi kuhle ukukwenza ngaphambi kokuthi umuntu ahlale phansi.

1. **Hlanza bese womisa yonke isitsha.** Sebenzisa amanzi afudumele anesipho, uzikhuhlele kahle, bese uvumela ukuthi kome ngokuphelele. Isitsha esimanzi singakhulisa isikhunta ngaphakathi kwephrojekthi evaliwe.
2. **Hlola ukuthi sake saba nantoni.** Ungasebenzisi izitsha ezake zabamba imikhiqizo yokuhlanza, amakhemikhali, imithi, ukudla kwezilwane noma noma yini ongeke uyihlanze ngokuphelele.
3. **Susa imiphetho ebukhali.** Hlola amathini nezivalo. Ngcwiba umphetho, uwembese ngethepi, noma yeka leyo ngcezu ingasetshenziswa.
4. **Sika ngaphambi kwesikhathi.** Sika ikhadibhodi, sika amawindi futhi wenze izintshi ngokwakho ngesikelo sokwenza izinto. Gcina ikhelu lakho eduze kwakho, lingafinyeleleki, bese usilalisa ngemva kokusisebenzisa.
5. **Hlela izingxenye ezincane ethreyini.** Izinkinobho, izivalo zamabhodlela namahlamvu kufanele zihlale ethreyini enemiphetho ephakanyisiwe. Akufanele zibe etafuleni zingagcini.

## Yiziphi izinto ezisetshenziswe kabusha ezilula ukuqala ngazo?

### Izimbali zekhishi lamaqanda

Sika izindebe ezivela ekhishini lamaqanda, uzishiye zinomphetho omncane. Zipende, uzivumele zome, bese usika amaphethelo ambalwa unamathisele endodeni yepayipi noma esikhundleni sayo. Izimbali ezimbalwa embizeni yiyona ndlela ekhanyayo yokuhlobisa itafula. Izindebe ezisikiwe kusengaphambili zilula ukuzibamba kunamakhishi amaphelele.

### Uhlaka lwezithombe ngebhokisi lokusanhlamvu

Sika unxande ngaphakathi kwebhokisi lokusanhlamvu, unamathisele isithombe noma ikhadi ngemva kwalo, bese ugoba ibhande lekhadi ukuze wenze isiseko. [Ikhadi lebhokisi lesikhumbuzo](/activity/decorate-a-keepsake-box/) lisebenzisa indlela efanayo yokumboza ibhokisi ngephepha noma indwangu.

### Isitsha sezitshalo esivela esitsheni se-yoghurt

Hlanza isitsha, yenza izimbobo zokukhipha amanzi ngesikelo somgodi noma ngesipikili (abadala kuphela), hlobisa ngaphandle ngepenti noma iphepha, bese ufaka umquba nesitshalo. [Ikhadi lesitsha sesitshalo](/activity/decorate-a-plant-pot/) lichaza izinyathelo nenguqulo elula yokubamba.

### I-shaker evaliwe

Gcwalisa ibhodlela elincane, elihlanzekile lepulasitiki ngerayisi elomile noma amalentili, uvale isivalo ngethepi eqinile noma ugilu, bese uyihlobisa. Umsizi kufanele avale isivalo. Ikhadi [le-shaker evaliwe](/activity/make-a-sealed-shaker/) linezinyathelo nenguqulo yezikhathi ezithulile.

### Isithombe ngezinkinobho

Hlukanisa izinkinobho ngombala noma ngobukhulu bese uzinamathisela ekhadini ukuze wenze isithombe esilula. Izinkinobho ziyingozi yokubhoboza ezinganeni ezingaphansi kweminyaka emithathu, ngakho lolu hlelo lwabantu abadala nezingane ezikhulile kuphela. Ikhadi [lesithombe ngezinkinobho](/activity/make-a-button-picture/) linezinyathelo ezigcwele.

### Ibhodi lokuthinta lendwangu

Namathisela izicucu zendwangu, uchungechunge nephepha ekhadini eliqinile. Alidingi ukusika futhi lisebenzisa kahle izinsalela. Bheka [ikhadi lebhodi lokuthinta](/activity/make-a-texture-board/).

## Yiziphi izinguqulo ezilula ongazama?

- **Ehlezi phansi.** Konke lokhu kusebenza etafuleni. Beka izinto endaweni efinyeleleka, ukuze umuntu angamisi.
- **Kulula ukubamba.** Sebenzisa izitsha ezibanzi nezilula, bese ufaka ithepu emibhobo emincane ukuze ibe mikhulu. Khetha isivalo esigoqeka kalula.
- **Ngesandla esisodwa.** Bopha isitsha etafuleni, noma ubeke ethreyini enemiphetho ephakanyisiwe ukuze lihlale linyanyeke.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Khetha izitsha ezinokuqhathanisa okunamandla kombala, bese upenda ngaphandle ngombala omkhulu. Gcina umsebenzi endaweni elula nekhanyayo.
- **Izingxenye ezimbalwa.** Khetha amaprojekthi anezingxenye ezimbili noma ezintathu. Isitsha esisodwa esihlotshisiwe noma uhlaka olulodwa lwesithombe kuyisiciko esiphelele.
- **Okufushane.** Pendela izingxenye kusengaphambili bese uzivumela zome, ukuze umuntu agcwalise noma ahlobise kuphela.

## Izincomo zokuphepha

- Ungasebenzisi noma yini eyengilazi, futhi gwema amathini anemiphetho eyiqhwa noma esikiwe.
- Amakhelu okwenza izinto ngabantu abadala kuphela, bese uwasebenzisa ebhodini lokusika elinzile kude nezandla zomuntu.
- Vala noma yini ebamba izinto ezincane. Sebenzisa ithepu eqinile noma igilu esivalweni, bese uhlola ukuthi asikwazi ukuvuleka.
- Gcina izinkinobho, izivalo zamabhodlela, izinhlamba namabhontshisi omile kude nabantwana abangaphansi kweminyaka emithathu. I-[ikhasi lokuvimbela ukubhoboza ye-HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/choking-prevention.aspx) libala amabhola amancane, izivalo zamapeni nama-battery ezinkinobho phakathi kwezinto ezibangela ukubhoboza.
- Sebenzisa upende nogilu okubhalwe kuwo ukuthi akunabuthi. I-[Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety) ichaza ukuthi uphawu lwe-AP wumqinisekiso wezinto zobuciko, nokuthi abantu abangase bangafundi amalebula kufanele basebenzise izinto ezingenabuthi.
- Qaphela ukuthi kukhona ukungezwani ngegilu, imibala nezitshalo.

## Uma kungasebenzi

- **Isitsha siqhubeka sigingqika noma sihamba.** Beka ethreyini, noma unamathisele isisekelo esibanzi ezansi.
- **Isivalo asiphenduki.** Bopha isivalo ngethepi noma ibhande likarabha ukuze kube nokubamba, noma khetha esinye isitsha.
- **Iprojekthi ibonakala inkulu kakhulu.** Khetha isitsha esincane, noma yenza ingxenye eyodwa bese uyeka.
- **Kukhona iphunga noma umswakama.** Hlanza bese womisa isitsha futhi ngaphambi kokuqhubeka. Ungasebenzisi isitsha esinephunga lalokho ebesibambe.

## Ukwenza nomuntu omdala noma nengane

Izinto zezandla ezisetshenziswe kabusha zingabelwana phakathi kwezizukulwane. Umuntu omdala, kubandakanya nomuntu aphila ne-dementia, angajabulela ukuhlukanisa izinkinobho, ukukhetha imibala yepenti noma ukugoqa isitsha ngentambo. Mvumele enze ingxenye ayisajabulela, bese wena wenza ukusika. Kwengane, gcina umuntu omdala eduze, sebenzisa kuphela izingxenye ezephephile ngeminyaka yayo, bese ugcina izingcezu ezincane kude nezingane ezincane.

Abanye abantu bathola ukuthi isitsha esijwayelekile, njengembiza yasekhishini, yisona sinto ejabulisa kakhulu. Buza kuqala, bese uyigcina eduze.
