Ukubamba, amandla nokulawula okucophelelayo kungashintsha ngezizathu eziningi. I-arthritis, ukugula okusanda kuba khona, ukulimala, isikhathi eside embhedeni kanye noshintsho olujwayelekile lokuguga ngayinye ingenza ipeni, isikelo noma iphepha elincane libe nzima ukulibamba. Umsebenzi wezandla usengajabulisa, futhi isilungiselelo esifanele vele sibalulekile kunephrojekthi ngokwayo. Leli khasi lichaza izilungiselelo ezisebenza kwezinye izinto zezandla eziningi, namakhadi asesayithini akhethwe ngoba adinga ukunyakaza okuncane okucophelelayo.

Lezi ziphakamiso zabantu abafuna ukwenza okuthile kodwa bathole imisebenzi yezandla ethile inzima. Azisho ukuxilongwa, futhi azisho iseluleko sezokwelapha. Uma ukubamba ithuluzi kubangela ubuhlungu, yeka. Udokotela, ingcweti yokwelashwa ngomzimba noma ingcweti yokusebenza nezandla ingakunika iseluleko mayelana nempilo yezandla nezinsizakalo.

## Yisiphi isilungiselelo esisiza kakhulu?

Umehluko omkhulu uvame ukuvela ekuhlelweni kwezinto, hhayi emsebenzini ngokwawo.

- **Bopha iphepha.** Sebenzisa ithepu yokumboza emakhoneni ephepha, ukuze lihlale liyisicaba ngaphandle kwesandla sesibili.
- **Sebenzisa indwangu ebambayo noma indwangu enamanzi** ngaphansi komsebenzi, ukuze ibhodi noma ithreyi lingashelelwa.
- **Sebenzisa ithreyi elinamagilo aphakanyisiwe.** Izingcezu zihlala ethreyini, futhi umuntu angazithatha endaweni eyodwa.
- **Gcina konke kufinyeleleka.** Beka amathuluzi phambi komuntu, ohlangothini lwesandla sakhe esinamandla, ukuze angadingi ukufinyelela kude.
- **Sika futhi ulungiselele ngaphambili.** Sika, goqa futhi ubeke izingcezu phambi kokuthi umuntu ahlale phansi, ukuze umsebenzi ube ekwenzeni hhayi ekulungiseleleni.

## Ngingenza kanjani amathuluzi abe lula ukuwabamba?

- **Khulisa isibambo.** Faka ithepu yefoam ephenyeni elincane, ibhulashi noma upensela, noma faka ushintshe wefoam phezu kwalo. Isibambo esikhulu sidinga ukucindezelwa okuncane.
- **Sebenzisa amathuluzi abanzi.** Ama-crayon amakhulu, ama-marker ajiyile nama-bhulashi amakhulu anesibambo esifushane nesibanzi alula ukuwabamba kunezincane.
- **Zama ukubamba ngepalama.** Abanye abantu bathola ukuthi ithuluzi elibanjwe ngesandla sonke lilula kunelibanjwe phakathi kweminwe. Lihlole ngaphambi kokuqala.
- **Khetha ithuluzi elihlukile.** I-glue stick ingaba lula kunebhodlela le-glue. Isipontshi noma i-roller ingaba lula kunebhulashi. Isitembu singaba lula kunokudweba.
- **Sebenzisa ithepu esikhundleni sokubopha.** Ithepu noma iklipu lingabamba iribhoni, ikhadi noma ucezu ndawonye ngesandla esisodwa.

I-[Arthritis Foundation](https://www.arthritis.org/partnership/ease-of-use) inohlelo lwe-Ease of Use olugunyaza imikhiqizo yansuku zonke njengelula ukuyisebenzisa, kuhlanganise nezingxenye zokuvikela amalunga nemikhiqizo elula ukuvula. Uphawu lwayo luyisiqalo esiwusizo lapho uthenga, kodwa alukwenzi indawo yokuzama umkhiqizo nomuntu kuqala.

## Yimaphi amaprojekthi adinga ukulawula okucophelelayo okuncane?

Khetha amaprojekthi anokunyakaza okukhulu, ukubamba ngepalama noma isenzo esisodwa esicacile. Lawa makhadi asesayithini afanele abantu abanokubamba okulinganiselwe, futhi ngamunye unenguqulo elula:

- [Imosaic yephepha eladatshulwe](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/). Ukudatsha kwenziwa ngeminwe, akukho izikelo. Ikhadi linezinguqulo ezilula nezinguqulo zasebhedeni.
- [Hlela izimbali ndawonye](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/). Izinduku ezinqunyiwe zibekwa embizeni ebanzi nezinzile. Ikhadi linenguqulo elula nenguqulo yasebhedeni.
- [Thinta umculo ndawonye](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/). Ukuthinta ngesandla sonke etafuleni noma ethreyini, akukho thuluzi elidingekayo.
- [Yenza ibhodi lokuthinta](/activity/make-a-texture-board/). Izicucu zendwangu nephepha zinamathiselwa ekhadini, nesinyathelo sokuqala esicacile.
- [Yenza ikhadi lokubingelela](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/). Ikhadi lingagotshwa ngaphambili, nesithombe esisodwa sesinamathiselwe, ngakho umsebenzi kuphela umlayezo.
- [Yenza i-shaker evaliwe](/activity/make-a-sealed-shaker/). Umsizi uvala isivalo, ngakho umuntu ugcwalisa futhi ahlobise kuphela.
- [Yenza isikhwama selavenda](/activity/make-a-lavender-sachet/). Asidingi ukushelela, futhi ikhadi linenguqulo elula nenguqulo yasebhedeni.

Ezinye izinkathi zinezingcezu ezincane, njengezinkinobho noma izinhlamba. Ikhadi [lokuhlukanisa izinkinobho ngombala](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/) lisebenzisa izinkinobho ezinkulu ethreyini, futhi lilungele abantu abadala nezingane ezikhulile kuphela, ngoba izinkinobho ziyingozi yokuthi umuntu angaziphazamela ingcindezi ezinganeni ezingaphansi kweminyaka emithathu.

## Yiziphi izinguqulo ezilula ongazama?

- **Ehlezi phansi noma embhedeni.** Ezinye izinkathi zalawa makhadi zisebenza zihlezi noma embhedeni. Beka ithreyi endaweni eqinile, ngobude obungadingi ukufinyelela.
- **Kulula ukubamba.** Sebenzisa amathuluzi akhulisiwe nezitsha ezibanzi nezilula. Khetha izivalo ezigoqeka kalula noma ezivuleka ngokushesha.
- **Ngesandla esisodwa.** Sebenzisa ithepu, i-clamp noma indwangu ebambayo, nethreyi elinamagilo aphakanyisiwe. Ibhulashi elifakwe kusengaphambili noma i-glue stick esakhishwe kusengaphambili kuyasiza.
- **Izinketho ezimbalwa.** Nikeza imibala emibili noma izingcezu ezimbili, bese umuntu ekhetha kulezo.
- **Okufushane.** Cela isingcezu esisodwa, uhlamvu olulodwa noma ikhadi elilodwa. Iseshini emfushane ephelela kahle iseshini enhle.
- **Ukungcola okuncane.** Sebenzela ethreyini enendwangu ngaphansi. Khetha imisebenzi eyomile njengokudatsha noma ukuhlela.

## Izincomo zokuphepha

- Gcina izingcezu ezincane, njengezinkinobho, izinhlamba nembewu, ethreyini. Abantwana abangaphansi kweminyaka emithathu bangabhoboza ngazo.
- Sebenzisa izikelo ezingabukhali noma izingcezu ezisikwe kusengaphambili. Amakhasi okusika abantu abadala kuphela.
- Khetha izinsimbi nama-glue anemikhiqizo engenabuthi, afakwe ilebula. I-[Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety) ichaza uphawu lwe-AP ezintweni zobuciko.
- Hlola ukuthi isisiza sokubamba, njengefoam tape, asishelelwa, asibambi iminwe noma asikhululeki.
- Yeka uma umuntu enobuhlungu, ukuzwa kwesikhathi esithile okuphelile noma ukuvuvuka emva komsebenzi, bese ukhuluma nodokotela noma nengcweti yokwelashwa ngomzimba mayelana nakho.

## Uma kungasebenzi

- **Ukubamba ithuluzi kuyalima.** Yeka, bese uzama ithuluzi elibanzi noma elilula. Noma khetha omunye umsebenzi.
- **Iphepha liqhubeka lihamba.** Engeza ithepu eningi, noma ulibambe ikhona itafula.
- **Izingcezu ziqhubeka ziwa.** Sebenzisa ithreyi enemiphetho ephakeme, noma inani elincane lezingcezu.
- **Umuntu udiniwe.** Yeka emva kwengcezu eyodwa. Iphrojekthi ingaqedwa ngolunye usuku, noma ishiywe njengoba iyunjalo.
- **Akukho okusebenza.** Khetha iphrojekthi enokunyakaza okuncane okucophelelayo, njengokuthinta umculo noma ukubheka iwindi ndawonye.

## Ukwenza nomuntu omdala noma nomuntu aphila ne-dementia

Ezinye zezilungiselelo ezingenhla zifanele abantu abadala, kuhlanganise nabantu abaphila ne-dementia, abangase babe nokubamba okulinganiselwe. Hlukanisa umsebenzi wezandla ube izinyathelo ezincane, bese uvumela umuntu enze izingxenye angazilawula, njengokukhetha imibala noma ukucindezela ingcezu phansi. Wena wenza umsebenzi ocophelelayo, bese umuntu enquma ukuthi uzobamba iqhaza kangakanani kulowo msebenzi.

I-Creative Activities ayiyona ithherapi, futhi lezi zilungiselelo azisho ukwelashwa. Zenza umsebenzi wezandla ube lula ukuzama, futhi umuntu angayeka noma nini.
