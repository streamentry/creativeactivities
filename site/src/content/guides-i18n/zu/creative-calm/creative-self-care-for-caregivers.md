Ukunakekela umuntu kunzima, futhi ukuzinakekela wena kuyingxenye yomsebenzi. Leli khasi linikeza izikhefu ezimfushane zobuciko, futhi likhombisa usizo lwangempela uma udinga ngaphezu kwekhefu. Lezi yimisebenzi yobuciko, hhayi ukwelashwa. Uma wena noma umuntu onakekelayo unenkinga yezempilo, khuluma nodokotela wakho noma nochwepheshe ogunyaziwe.

## Ikhefu lobuciko lemizuzu emihlanu linjani?

Izikhefu ezincane ziyabaluleka. Azilungisi ukukhathala, kodwa zingakusiza udlule ntambama enzima.

- **Cindezela igqabi.** Beka igqabi elilodwa phakathi kwamakhasi encwadi enzima, bese ubuyela kulo emuva kwesikhathi. Ikhadi [cindezela amagqabi nezimbali](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/) linezinyathelo.
- **Dweba okubonayo.** Dweba into eyodwa ngewindi, noma etafuleni, ngepensela. Akudingeki ukuthi noma ubani ayibone.
- **Dweba iphethini.** Phinda isimo esilula esisodwa ngaphezu kwesiqeshana sephepha. Ikhadi [dweba amaphethini aphindwayo](/activity/draw-repeating-patterns/) linikeza isinyathelo esicacile njalo.
- **Dlala iculo oliyithandayo.** Vala amehlo, noma ushaye ngokuthula ngokuhambisana nalo. Ikhadi [yenza uhlu lomculo ndawonye](/activity/make-a-playlist-together/) lingakusiza ukwakha elinye.
- **Bhala ikhadi elifushane.** Imigca embalwa emngane, nomdwebo olula. Ikhadi [yenza ikhadi lokubingelela](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) yindlela esheshayo yokukwenza.

## Kuthiwani ngekhefu elide?

Beka eceleni ihora kanye noma kabili ngesonto zokwenza into engenayo inhloso. Penda itshe, hlela izimbali embizeni, noma yakha upoti omncane wobumba. Amakhadi [penda itshe lasesiyingini](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/), [hlela izimbali ndawonye](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) kanye [nokwakha upoti ngokucindezela](/activity/shape-a-pinch-pot/) konke kungasetshenziswa lapha. Ungenza into wedwa, noma nomuntu omnakekelayo uma ekujabulela.

Uma ungakwazi ukushiya uhlangothi lomuntu omnakekelayo, ikhefu lobuciko lingabelwana. Ikhadi [hlela izinkinobho ngombala](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/) noma iphethini elula esihlokweni sephepha lingenziwa ngokuhlanganyela, eceleni komunye nomunye.

## Izikhefu ezilula

- **Ukulala phansi.** Imizuzu emihlanu unamehlo avaliwe nculo oliyithandayo ngemuva.
- **Izinketho ezimbalwa.** Khetha ipensela elilodwa lombala nephepha elilodwa. Lokho kwanele.
- **Amandla amancane.** Hlala eduze newindi ubale izinyoni, noma ubambe itshe elibushelelezi.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Sebenzisa ama-marker anemibala eqinile namaphepha amakhulu, bese uchaza ngezwi eliphakeme okwenzayo.

## Ungathola kuphi ukuphumula kwabanakekeli?

Ukuphumula kwabanakekeli ukunakekelwa okufushane komuntu oyimunakekelayo, ukuze wena uphumule. I-[Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/care-options/respite-care) ichaza izinhlobo ezintathu ezivamile:

| Uhlobo | Yini |
| --- | --- |
| Usizo ekhaya | Umngane, isihlobo, umsebenzi wokuzithandela noma umsizi okhokhelwayo weza ekhaya |
| Isikhungo sabantu abadala sosuku | Imisebenzi ehlelekile ngeqembu, njalo kuhlanganisa ukuthuthwa nokudla |
| Ubusuku noma ukuhlala isikhashana | Umphakathi wokunakekela isikhathi eside ulondoloza umuntu ubusuku noma ngaphezulu |

Ukuphumula okuvela emndenini, kubangane noma kubavolontiya kungase kungabizi lutho. Izinsiza ezikhokhelwayo zivame ukukhokha ngehora, ngosuku noma ngesikhathi sokuhlala. Buza i-Helpline ngezimali nezinketho endaweni yakho, ngaphambi kokuthi uzidinge. Zama umhlinzeki ngesikhathi esithulile, ukuze umazi uma kudingeka ikhefu elingalindelwe.

## Ungathola kuphi usizo?

- **I-Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline: 1-800-272-3900.** Lo mugqa wase-US ungowamahhala, uyimfihlo futhi usebenza zonke izinsuku nawo wonke amahora. Usizo lutholakala ngezilimi eziningi ngabasebenzi abakhuluma izilimi ezimbili noma ngesevisi yomhumushi. Ungaxoxa ngokucindezeleka komnakekeli, izinketho zokuphumula nezinhlelo zasendaweni. Abasebenzi abanikezi iseluleko sezokwelapha.
- **Eldercare Locator: 1-800-677-1116 noma eldercare.acl.gov.** Insiza yomphakathi e-US exhumanisa nezinsiza zasendaweni zabantu abadala nemindeni yabo.
- **Amaqembu osekelo we-Alzheimer's Association.** Amaqembu abanakekeli, ngokubonana ngqo nokuxhumana ku-inthanethi, aholwa abasizi abaqeqeshiwe. Buza i-Helpline ukuthi yiliphi eliseduze nawe.
- **Carers UK.** Ulwazi neseluleko kubanakekeli e-UK, kuhlanganise nosizo lwezinto ezisebenzayo zokunakekela.
- **Samaritans: 116 123 (UK nase-Ireland).** Mahhala, uvulekile amahora angu-24 ngosuku, izinsuku ezingu-365 ngonyaka, kunoma ubani odinga ukukhuluma.
- **Find A Helpline.** Inikeza imigqa yosizo yamahhala emazweni amaningi, uma ungazi ukuthi yiliphi isevisi okufanele uyishaye.

## Ungabacela kanjani umndeni nabangane usizo?

Yiba nencazelo. Abantu abaningi bafuna ukusiza kodwa abazi ukuthini okufanele banikeze.

- **Gcina uhlu** lwemisebenzi abanye abangayithatha, bese uvumela abantu bakhethe eyodwa.
- **Qala kancane.** Ntambama eyodwa, noma ucingo olulodwa ngesonto, kuyisicelo esihle sokuqala.
- **Fanisa umsebenzi nomuntu.** Umzala opheka angaletha isidlo sakusihlwa ngoLwesine. Umakhelwane onolwazi ngamafomu angasiza ngemibhalo.
- **Thumela umyalezo noma i-imeyili** uma ukucela ngobuso nobuso kunzima.
- **Lindela ukuthi kwesinye isikhathi kuzoba yi-cha.** Zama ukungakuthathi kuwena, bese ucela omunye umuntu.

## Kufanele uthole usizo oluphuthumayo nini?

Uma uzizwa ungeke usaqhubeka, uma uzizwa ungenathemba, noma uma unemicabango yokuzilimaza, finyelela manje. E-US, shayela noma uthumele umyalezo ku-988 ukuze ufinyelele i-[988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/), eyamahhala futhi itholakala amahora angu-24 ngosuku. E-UK nase-Ireland, shayela i-Samaritans ku-116 123. Kwezinye izindawo, i-[Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com/) inikeza izinsiza zamahhala ngezwe. Uma kukhona umuntu esengozini eseduze, shayela inombolo yezimo eziphuthumayo yasendaweni yakho.

Khuluma nodokotela wakho uma izimpawu zokucindezeleka zivela njalo, uma uzizwa ulusizi amasonto, noma uma ukwazi ukubekezela kunzima. Mtshele ukuthi ungumnakekeli. Angakusiza ukuhlela usizo kuwe nakumuntu omnakekelayo.

## Izinsiyo zokuphepha

- Sebenzisa izikelo nezinsizakalo zobuciko ngokucophelela, futhi zigcine kude nomuntu omnakekelayo.
- Gwema izimbali ezintsha, amagqabi nezinhlamvu zemifino ezomile uma kukhona umuntu enezinkinga zokungezwa noma zokuphefumula. Hlola izitshalo ngaphambi kokuzibamba.
- Ubumba nopende akudliwa. Geza izandla ngemva kokusebenzisa.
- Thatha izikhefu nakwesikhefu. Ikhefu lobuciko akufanele liphenduke omunye umsebenzi.

## Uma kungasebenzi

Uma ikhefu lobuciko lizwakala njengomsebenzi owengeziwe, zama okuncane noma uhlale nje. Uma ukucindezeleka okufanayo kubuya njalo, cela usizo lokunakekela ngqo, hhayi lekhefu kuphela. Iqembu lokwesekwa, ukuvakasha kokuphumula noma ingxoxo nodokotela kungenza umehluko omkhulu kunanoma yimuphi umsebenzi wobuciko. Ukuphumula akuwona umklomelo oyiwuzuzayo. Kuyingxenye yokuqhubeka.
