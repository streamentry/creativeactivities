Umculo awudingi lutho. Alukho ukuphendula okulungile futhi alukho izinyathelo okufanele zilandelwe. Ingoma ejwayelekile ingaletha intokozo, unyawo olushaya noma umugca ohlabelelwa ndawonye, ngisho nangosuku oluthulile. Lokhu kuyisenzo sobuciko, hhayi ukwelashwa. Uma wena noma umuntu onakekelayo unenkinga yezempilo, khuluma nodokotela wakho noma nochwepheshe ogunyaziwe ngaphambi kokuthembela emculweni ngaphezu kokujabula.

## Ubani lokhu okwabo?

Umculo ufanele abantu noma yiliphi iminyaka abathanda ukulalela, ukuhumusha, ukuhlabelela noma ukunyakaza ngesigingci. Ufanele umkhulu nomzukulu abelana ngengoma, umzali nendodana noma intombazane emdala ebhekisisa amarekhodi omdala, nabantu abadala abakhathele kodwa abafuna inkampani. Ufanele nomuntu osanda ukugula ofuna into elula yokwabelana ngayo. Lezi yizincomo. Umculo awuthathi indawo yokunakekela.

## Yini ofunayo?

- Isipikha noma ifoni enebhethri elihle, nencayi eseduze
- Uhlu oluncane lwezingoma ezintathu noma ezine, olunye oluthulile nolunye olujabulisayo
- Iphepha nopensela, uma ufuna ukubhala uhlu lomculo ndawonye
- Okukhethwa: udramu omncane wesandla, isisibambisi, noma izinkezo ezimbili zomthi

## Uyakha kanjani uhlu lomculo ngokuphila komuntu?

Into esiza kakhulu oyenzayo uhlu lomculo olunencazelo kulo muntu. Mbuze, noma mbuze abantu ababemazi lapho besencane.

- **Ubusha bakhe.** Yini eyayisendaweni yemisakazo, emadansini nasemafilimini lapho esencane?
- **Izikhathi ezinkulu.** Umculo emshadweni, ekudanseni kokuqala noma ohambweni lomndeni olithandayo.
- **Inkolo nesiko.** Izihlabelelo, umculo wesiphefumulo, umculo ongcwele, noma izingoma ngolimi lwakhe lokuqala.
- **Umsebenzi nokusebenzela abanye.** Amabhendi omculo, izingoma zomsebenzi, noma umculo ohlobene nendawo noma isikhathi.
- **Okukhunjulwa umndeni.** Buza abafowethu nodadewethu, izingane nabangane bakudala ukuthi bebehlala beshaya noma behlabelela yini.

Uma ungaqiniseki lapho uqala khona, zama imizuzu embalwa yezitayela ezihlukene bese ubheka impendulo. Gcina lokho okuletha intokozo noma ihlombe elishaya. Yeqa lokho okuletha ukuqubuka kobuso. Gcina izinhlu ezimbili ezimfushane: eyodwa ethulile yokuthulisa, neyodwa ejabulisayo yokuhlabelela noma ukudansa ndawonye.

## Imisebenzi okufanele uyizame ndawonye

1. **Lalelani ndawonye.** Hlalani eceleni komunye nomunye ngaphandle kwezinye izinto. Ukulalela kuyisenzo esiphelele.
2. **Hlabelela ndawonye.** Qala ukuhlabelela wena. Ukunynyeza noma ukunyakazisa amazwi kuyabalwa.
3. **Shaya ihlombe.** Shaya amadolo, shaya itafula noma usebenzise isisibambisi. Ikhadi [shaya ihlombe ndawonye](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/) linemibono eminingi.
4. **Zungeza noma dansa.** Bambisana izandla uzungeze ekhishini, noma nyakazisa izingalo ndawonye uhlezi.
5. **Xoxani ngomculo.** Mema, ungahloli. Zama ukuthi "Ngitshele ngemidanso yalowo holo" esikhundleni sokuthi "Ukhumbula le ngoma?"
6. **Yakha incwadi yezingoma yakho.** Bhala amazwi ezingoma ozithandayo ngohlamvu olukhulu, unikeze indawo yokungeza amanothi. Ikhadi [yakha incwadi yezingoma yakho](/activity/make-a-personal-songbook/) linezinyathelo.
7. **Yenza uhlu lomculo ndawonye.** Phendukisana ukukhetha izingoma. Ikhadi [yenza uhlu lomculo ndawonye](/activity/make-a-playlist-together/) linikeza ukulandelana okufanele kulandelwe.
8. **Dweba ngomculo.** Vumela imigqa nemibala ilandele into ngenkathi idlalwa. Ikhadi [dweba ngomculo](/activity/draw-to-music/) likhombisa indlela.

## Isikhathi esifushane sokwabelana

Imizuzu emihlanu kuya kwemashumi amabili yanele. Vala ithelevishini bese uvala umnyango. Dlala into eyodwa ngevolumu ethambile. Hlala lapho nizokwazi ukubheka ngamehlo, bese uvumela umuntu akhethe iculo elilandelayo uma efisa. Phela ngengoma ethulile, vala umculo, bese nihlala ndawonye isikhashana. Ukuphela ngokuthula kuyingxenye yomsebenzi.

## Ngomdala noma ngomuntu ohlala nesifo sokukhohlwa

Umculo uyinto yokuqala elula ongayabelana ngayo. Gcina okukhethwayo kulula, bese uvumela umuntu enquma. I-Alzheimer's Association ikweluleka ukuthi ukhethe umculo ojwayelekile nojabulisayo, nomthombo ongaphazanyiswa izikhangiso, okungadala ukudideka. Sebenzisa umculo ukwakha umoya ofunayo: into ethulile ukuze kube nokuthula, nengoma ethandwa kakhulu ukuze kube namandla.

Kamuva ngesikhathi sesifo sokukhohlwa, i-[NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) ithi imisebenzi ngokuvamile idinga ukugxila ezimweni, nokuthi ukudlala umculo kungaba ukukhetha okuhle. Hlala eduze, dlala izingoma ezithandwa kakhulu ngokuthambile kakhulu, bese ubamba isandla noma unyakaze ndawonye. Ukuze uthole imibono engeziwe elingana nezigaba zokugcina, bheka [imisebenzi yezigaba zokugcina](/activities/late-stage/).

## Izinguqulo ezilula

- **Ahlezi noma ulele embhedeni.** Sebenzisa isipikha esiseduze, nengoma eyodwa ngesikhathi.
- **Izigqoko zezindlebe noma isipikha.** Abanye bathanda izigqoko zezindlebe, ezivimba umsindo. Abanye bazithola ziyadideka. Zama isipikha kuqala ukuze nikwazi ukulalela ndawonye.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Phrinta amagama ezingoma ngohlobo olukhulu bese ulifunda ngelizwi eliphakeme.
- **Izinsiza zokuzwa.** Qinisekisa ukuthi zifakiwe futhi zisebenza ngaphambi kokuqala, bese ugcina ivolumu ilungele.
- **Izinketho ezimbalwa.** Nikeza iculo elilodwa, noma uhlu olufushane lwezingoma ezintathu.

## Izinsiyo zokuphepha

- Gcina ivolumu ilungile. Uma umculo uphezulu kangangokuthi kunzima ukukhuluma, wehlisele phansi.
- Ezinye izingoma zivusa ukulila, ukulahlekelwa noma izinkumbulo ezibuhlungu. Uma kwenzeka lokho, duduza umuntu, shintsha iculo, bese unganyanzeli ukuthi achaze.
- Ungakopisheli amazwi ezingoma emakhadini noma emaqoqweni ahlanganyelwe uma ungenaso isimo sokwenza kanjalo. Ukulalela nokuhlabelela ndawonye kulungile.
- Uma usebenzisa isisibambisi noma udramu, khetha esingenazo izingxenye ezincane ezingalunywa.

## Uma kungasebenzi

Uma umuntu evela ekhathazekile, edinwa noma engaphilile, misa umculo bese uhlola izimbangela ezilula: ubuhlungu, ukoma, indlala, isikhwama somchamo esigcwele, ukukhathala noma igumbi elinomsindo. Zama into ethulile, noma nihlale ndawonye ngokuthula. Imizuzu emihlanu yomculo yisikhathi esihle, nokuwucisha nakho kunjalo.

## Ucwaningo luthini?

Ubufakazi bunomkhawulo. Ukubuyekezwa kwe-[Cochrane](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003477_does-music-based-therapy-help-people-dementia) kwezikhathi ezihlelekile zokwenziwa ngomculo kwafaka izifundo ezingama-30 nabantu aba-1,720 abanesifo sokukhohlwa emazweni ayi-15, ikakhulukazi emakhaya okunakekela. Imiphumela yayo ihluka ngokuphumela, futhi ichaza ukuqiniseka kobufakazi njengokunomkhawulo ezinhlobonhlobo zazo. Lokho kubuyekezwa kuhlobene nezikhathi ezihlelwe umuntu oqeqeshiwe ezihola. Akuyona isiqondiso sokulalela ekhaya.

Sabelana ngomculo njengendlela enhle, engenabungcindezi yokuchitha isikhathi ndawonye. Akuyona ukwelashwa, futhi awuthathi indawo yokunakekelwa ngudokotela noma ngochwepheshe ogunyaziwe.
