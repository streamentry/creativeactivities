Abantu abaningi babona okufanayo. Ekuseni kuvame ukuba nokuzola, kodwa njengoba intambama iqhubeka, umuntu uba nokukhathala, ukungakhululeki noma ukucasuka kalula. Leli khasi linikeza uhlelo oluthulile lwansuku nemisebenzi ethulile yentambama ephelayo. Liyisiqondiso sokwenza ngokudalwa, hhayi ukwelashwa. Uma wena noma umuntu onakekelayo unenkinga yezempilo, khuluma nodokotela wakho noma nochwepheshe ogunyaziwe, ikakhulukazi uma ushintsho lusha.

## Uhlelo oluthulile lwansuku lunjani?

Izikhathi ziyizibonelo nje. Hlehlisa noma phambilisa lonke usuku ukuze lifane nomfutho womuntu, bese ugcina ukulandelana kuzinzile, hhayi iwashi.

| Isikhathi | Okwenzeka | Ngosuku olunzima |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ekuseni | Vuka, geza, gqoka udle ibhulakufesi ngalolo hlelo njalo ngosuku | Ihembe elisha nebhulakufesi elilula kwanele |
| Ekuseni kamuva | Umsebenzi oyinhloko: ukuhamba kancane, ukubhaka, noma umsebenzi onokuphela okucacile | Inguqulo emfushane, noma ukukubukela wenza |
| Emini | Ilunch, bese kuba yisikhashana sokuthula | Yeqa imisebenzi yasendlini |
| Kusasa ntambama | Phumula uma kudingeka esihlalweni osithandayo | Okufanayo, nesiphuzo esifudumele |
| Phakathi nentambama ephelayo | Imisebenzi ethulile nejwayelekile, isidlo esincane nesiphuzo, akukho i-caffeine | Isidlo esincane nokuba nomuntu ngokuthula |
| Ngaphambi kokuhlwa | Vala amakhethini uvule izibani zangaphakathi | Khanyisa igumbi ngaphambi kokuba izithunzi zivele |
| Kusihlwa | Ukudla okulula kancane, umculo othambile, umsindo omncane | Induduzo kuqala |
| Isikhathi sokulala | Izinyathelo ezifanayo njalo ebusuku, izibani ezibuthaka | Fingqa konke |

## Uzakha kanjani uhlelo lwakho?

Qala ngendlela umuntu ajwayele ukuphila ngayo, hhayi ngetshadi. Cabanga ngokuthanda nokungathandi kwakhe, nokuthi wayechitha kanjani usuku lwakhe ngaphambili. Umuntu ovuka kusasa njalo, owayehamba ngaphambi kokudla kwasekuseni, angafisa ukugcina leyo ndlela. Umuntu osebenza ebusuku angase angajabuli ngokuqala kusasa kakhulu.

- **Beka izinto ezidinga amandla kuqala.** Ukugeza, izinhlelo zokuphuma nemisebenzi edinga umzamo zibekwe ekuseni noma kusasa ntambama, lapho abantu abaningi besebenza kangcono.
- **Gcina ukuvuka nokulala kuzinzile.** I-Alzheimer's Association ithi izikhathi ezijwayelekile zokuvuka nokuya ulele zingasiza ngezinkinga zokulala nokungakhululeka kwasekupheleni kosuku.
- **Shiya indawo yokuphefumula.** Ungagcwalisi yonke imizuzu. Ukuphumula kuyingxenye yosuku.
- **Vumela imizuzu engalindelwe.** Isihambi, ilanga elikhanyayo noma iculo elithandwayo kungashintsha uhlelo, futhi lokho kulungile.

## Ungayivikela kanjani intambama ephelayo?

- **Ukukhanya kwemini.** Hlela isikhathi ngaphandle noma ngasewindini elikhanyayo nsuku zonke.
- **Ukunyakaza kancane.** Ukuhamba noma imizuzu embalwa yomzamo nsuku zonke, kodwa hhayi okuningi ngaphezu kohlelo olumatasa.
- **Iziphuzo.** Gwema i-caffeine notshwala kamuva emini. Nikeza isiphuzo esifudumele esingenayo i-caffeine esikhundleni salokho.
- **Ukulala kwasemini.** Nciphisa ukulala isikhathi eside nokuthambama kamuva ntambama, ikakhulukazi uma ukulala ebusuku kunzima.
- **Ukukhanya nomsindo.** Vala amakhethini uvule izibani ngaphambi kokuhlwa. Nciphisa ithelevishini, iradiyo nomsindo wasendlini.
- **Ukuhlola ukududuzeka komzimba.** Bheka ubuhlungu, indlala, ukoma, isikhwama somchamo esigcwele, ukukhathala, ukucasuka kwesikhumba, noma igumbi elishisa kakhulu noma libanda kakhulu. Noma yiliphi lalezi lingabonakala njengokungakhululeki.

## Imisebenzi ethulile yentambama ephelayo

Khetha imisebenzi ejwayelekile, engaphuthumi futhi elula ukuyeka. Gwema amagemu amasha, izinhlelo zokuphuma ezimatasa nanoma yini edinga ukugxila kakhulu. Gcina lokho kwakusasa.

1. **Goqa amathawuli ndawonye.** Amathawuli alula ukuwabamba, futhi umsebenzi unokuphela okucacile. Ikhadi [goqa amathawuli ndawonye](/activity/fold-towels-together/) linezinyathelo.
2. **Dlala uhlu lomculo oluthandwayo.** Izingoma ezijwayelekile ngevolumu ephansi. Ukunynyeza ngazo kubalwa njengokubamba iqhaza. Ikhadi [yenza uhlu lomculo ndawonye](/activity/make-a-playlist-together/) liwukuqala okuhle.
3. **Buka izithombe.** I-albhamu, noma izithombe ezimbalwa, ngaphandle kwesivivinyo. Ukubukela nje kwanele. Ikhadi [bheka i-albhamu yezithombe](/activity/look-through-a-photo-album/) likhomba indlela.
4. **Shaya ihlombe.** Shaya itafula noma usebenzise isibambisi ngokuhambisana nomculo. Bona [shaya ihlombe ndawonye](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/).
5. **Hlela ngombala.** Ukuhlela izinkinobho noma amasokisi kuwumsebenzi wezandla ozinzile. Ikhadi [hlela izinkinobho ngombala](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/) linezinyathelo.
6. **Hamba kancane.** Ezungeni lasesiyingini, endleleni noma phakathi kwendlu, ngaphambi kokuba kufiphale.
7. **Yabelana ngesiphuzo esifudumele.** Into elula, etafuleni ndawonye.
8. **Bukela izinyoni.** Ikhadi [bukela izinyoni ngewindi](/activity/watch-birds-from-a-window/) likhombisa indlela yokwenza lokhu kube isikhathi sokuhlanganyela.
9. **Dlala umdlalo wokuhlanganisa ngobumnene.** [Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) ngezithombe ezisabonakala kulula ukuqala nokuyeka.
10. **Yima uphefumule.** [Calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) ukuphumula okuhamba kancane ouqala futhi uyeke ngokwakho.

## Ngomuntu ohlala nesifo sokukhohlwa

Ntambama ephelayo, abantu abaningi abahlala nesifo sokukhohlwa baba nokungakhululeki noma bedideke kakhulu. Abanye emindenini babiza lokhu ngokuthi i-sundowning. Hlala uzolile futhi gcina izwi lakho liphansi. Sebenzisa amagama amafushane, aduduzayo, bese unikeza isidlo esincane, isiphuzo, umculo omncane noma ukuhamba kancane. Gwema ukuphikisana, ukulungisa noma ukumvimbela umuntu. Ungaqali umsebenzi omusha noma onzima kamuva emini. Gcina izinyathelo zakusihlwa ezifanayo njalo ebusuku, ukuze uhlelo ngokwalo ube induduzo.

Uma umuntu eba nodlame, gcina ibanga elisekelayo kuze kube yilapho ukuziphatha kuzola. Uma kukhona umuntu ongaphansi kwengozi, shayela inombolo yezimo eziphuthumayo yasendaweni yakho. Isiqondiso sethu [ngalapho bethi cha](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) sinezinkulumo ezengeziwe ngokuphendula ngobumnene uma umuntu enqaba.

## Izinsiyo zokuphepha

- Ungamboni umuntu noma uvimbe umnyango ukuze umvimbele ukuhamba. Hamba naye esikhundleni salokho, endaweni ephephile.
- Gcina imithi endaweni yayo ejwayelekile futhi unikeze kuphela lokho umbhali wayo ayibekile. Buza udokotela ngezikhathi ezingcono zanoma yiluphi umuthi, njengoba i-Alzheimer's Association ikweluleka.
- Ungaqali, ungamisi noma ushintshe noma yiliphi uhlelo lokulala, isengezo noma umuthi ngaphandle kweseluleko sezokwelapha.
- Gcina iziphuzo ezishisayo kude nomphetho wetafula, futhi gcina indlela yokuhamba icacile ekukhanyeni okufiphele.

## Uma kungasebenzi

Gcina ilogi elifushane isonto noma amabili. Phawula isikhathi ukungakhululeki okuqale ngaso, okwenzeke ihora ngaphambi kwalokho, ukulala kwasemini, ukudla, izivakashi, umsindo nokukhanya. Yisa lokhu kodokotela. Amaphethini ajwayele ukuvela kulogi, okunzima ukuwabona ngosuku ngosuku.

Uma kusihlwa okufanayo kunzima nsuku zonke, zama omunye umsebenzi, igumbi elihlukile, noma ukuqala isikhathi sokuthula kamuva. Uma usukhathele ngokuhlwa, bheka ikhasi [lokuzinakekela komnakekeli](/creative-calm/creative-self-care-for-caregivers/) ngezikhefu ezifushane nokwesekwa.

Ukuyeka kusenesikhathi kuyisiphetho esihle. Ukuhlala ndawonye ngaphandle kokwenza lutho nakho kuyisiphetho esihle.
