Ubuciko obuqaphelayo indlela ehamba kancane yokwenza izimpawu. Ukhetha isimo esisodwa esilula noma indikimba bese uyidweba kancane kancane, ubheka ngokucophelela phakathi komugca ngamunye. Akukho okudingeka kufane nento ethile. Okubalulekile ukunaka, isivinini namathuluzi, hhayi isithombe esiqediwe. Lokhu kuyisenzo sobuciko, hhayi ukwelashwa. Uma wena noma umuntu onakekelayo unenkinga yezempilo, khuluma nodokotela wakho noma nochwepheshe ogunyaziwe ngaphambi kokuqala okusha.

## Ubani lokhu okwabo?

Ubuciko obuqaphelayo buhambelana nabantu abadala abathanda ukwenza izinto kodwa bangaqiniseki ngokudweba. Buhambelana futhi nabantu abathanda ukuphindaphinda, amaphethini nemisebenzi ethulile engaphuthumi. Ungakwenza wedwa, noma eceleni komngane, umlingani noma umuntu onakekelayo. Kusebenza etafuleni, noma embhedeni nethreyi yemvubelo. Lezi yizincomo, hhayi isifo. Abanye abantu bazothokozela, abanye abazothokozela.

## Yini ofunayo?

- Iphepha elilula, uhhafu weshidi lephepha lomshini wokuprinta noma ipedi elincane lokudweba
- Ipeni elincane, upensela othambile nesisusi
- Amapensela ambalwa anemibala noma ama-marker amancane
- Into eyodwa encane yokudweba, njengekomishi, igqabi, igobolondo noma ukhiye
- Ibhodi lokubhala, uma etafuleni linganyakazi noma ufuna ukudweba embhedeni

Izinto zasekhaya ziyisisekelo. Sebenzisa lokho onakho.

## Uqala kanjani ukudweba ngokuhamba kancane?

1. Beka into etafuleni ekukhanyeni okuhle, kancane kuwe.
2. Khetha ingxenye eyodwa yayo, njengomphetho wekomishi noma umugca walelo gqabi elilodwa.
3. Dweba kancane kakhulu, ubheke into kakhulu kunephepha. Vumela umugca uzulazule.
4. Emva kwemigqa embalwa, yeka. Bheka umdwebo, bese ubuya ubheke into futhi.
5. Esiqeshini esihlukile sephepha, qala iphethini: umugqa wamasekhele amancane, umugqa wamagagasi, noma igridi yamachashazi. Phinda isimo esifanayo, esisemva kwesinye.
6. Engeza umbala kuphela uma ukujabulisa. Sebenzisa umbala owodwa ngesikhathi bese uvumela ugcwalise indawo eyodwa.
7. Yeka uma ufisa. Shiya ikhasi lapho lisemi bese ubuyela ngesinye isikhathi.

Ukudweba ngomugqa wangaphandle, lapho ulandela umugqa wesimo ngaphandle kokuphakamisa ipeni futhi ungabhekisisi phansi, kuyisinyathelo sokuqala esihle. Amakhadi [dweba amaphethini aphindwayo](/activity/draw-repeating-patterns/) no [dweba okusetafuleni](/activity/sketch-what-is-on-the-table/) anezinyathelo ongazishicilela.

## Yini ongayizama okulandelayo

- **Izimo eziphindaphindwayo.** Khetha isimpawu esisodwa, njengomzungezo omncane noma unxantathu omncane, bese ugcwalisa isiqeshana sephepha ngawo. Shintsha into eyodwa ngesikhathi, njengosayizi noma ibanga.
- **Amagridi ahamba kancane.** Dweba igridi yesikwele bese ugcwalisa isikwele ngasinye ngesimpawu esilula esihlukile. Akekho kudingeka agcwalise ngolandelwano.
- **Umbala ngendawo.** Beka umbala owodwa ngesimo esingaqinile, bese omunye, bese uvumela imiphetho ihlangane. Lokhu nakho kusebenza kahle ne-[penda ngemibala yamanzi](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/).
- **Ukudweba ngomculo.** Beka ingoma bese uvumela umugqa ngamunye ulandele kuyo. Ikhadi [dweba ngomculo](/activity/draw-to-music/) lichaza indlela.
- **Ukukopisha ukuthungwa.** Beka iphepha phezu kwegqabi noma ithayela elinokuthungwa bese ukhukhula ngecala le-crayon. Bheka iphethini elivela.

## Izinguqulo ezilula

- **Ahlezi noma ulele embhedeni.** Beka iphepha ebhodini lokubhala noma ethreyini yemvubelo, bese ugcina ipeni lifinyeleleka.
- **Kulula ukubamba.** Sebenzisa i-marker enkulu noma uvuneze upensela omncane nge-foam tape. Dweba izimo ezinkulu esikhundleni ezincane.
- **Ukubona kancane.** Sebenzisa i-marker emnyama ephepheni elimhlophe, into enkulu nokukhanya okuhle. Chaza isimo ngezwi uma usidweba.
- **Izinketho ezimbalwa.** Nikeza ipeni elilodwa kanye nephethini elilodwa, bese ushiya umbala.
- **Okufushane.** Dweba umugqa wezimo bese uyeka. Imizuzu emibili iyabalwa.
- **Ukuqala okusekhasini sewuvele.** Qala ngomugqa wangaphandle osuvele udwetshiwe, bese wengeza imigqa noma umbala ngaphakathi kwawo.

## Izinsiyo zokuphepha

- Gcina izisongelo zamapeni, izinsika zikapensela nezisusi ezincane kude nezingane ezincane nakunoma ubani ongafaka izinto emlonyeni.
- Sebenzisa ama-marker angageziwa nopende ongenayo ukungcola, bese ufunda ilebula.
- Uma ukubamba ithuluzi kubangela ubuhlungu noma isibambo sibuthakathaka, libeke phansi. Zama ithuluzi elikhulu, isisekelo sesihlakala noma olunye usuku.
- Sebenza etafuleni elihlanzekile, endaweni engeke yonakale ngenki noma amanzi.

## Uma kungasebenzi

Uma umuntu ekhathele, engenandaba noma edinwa, shintshela kuphethini elula noma yeka. Uma bethi abakwazi ukudweba, zama ukwenza izimpawu ngaphandle kwanoma yiyiphi indikimba: imigqa, amachashazi namalunga, ngaphandle kokulungile nokungalungile. Uma umsebenzi uvusa okubuhlungu, yeka, nikeza isiphuzo esifudumele bese wenza into ejwayelekile. Ukuyeka kuyisiphetho esihle, nekhasi lizohlala.

Umdlalo [coloring](/games/coloring/) unikeza umsebenzi othulile ohlobene nawo onemifanekiso esele elungiselwe, futhi ungaba yindawo elula yokuqala ngolunye usuku lapho ukudweba kuzwakala kunzima kakhulu.

Ukubuyekeza kwe-WHO Regional Office for Europe kwango-2019 kubheka izifundo ezingaphezu kuka-3,000 bese kuchaza izixhumanisi phakathi komsebenzi wobuciko nempilo nenhlalakahle. Kufundeke njengolwazi lwangemuva, hhayi njengobufakazi bokuthi nini umsebenzi owodwa uzosiza umuntu oyedwa.
