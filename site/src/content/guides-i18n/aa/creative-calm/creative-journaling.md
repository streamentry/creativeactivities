Kaarbuddi hal-hal ilaa warqa ginnaa, gaafaa ma'aane: midi kaa ilaa, lolo, sawarsa ama hadaa roobi. Hal-hal kaarbuddi ma'aane, ma'aane ilaa. Ma'aane ilaa qalbi-fiqqe ka'a ma'aane. Ina ama nama aminaa, ka'a dakhtar ka'a ama hel-yani ma'aane kaana.

## Kaa ma'aane?

Kaarbuddi hal-hal ma'aane, ilaa gacmaa ma'aane kaana, hal-hal dhaabbaan kaana. Ma'aane, qoraa hadaa ma'aane kaana, hal-hal gaaba gabaabbaa kaana. Kollaaj ma'aane ilaa, nama ma'aane. Kaasi ina ma'aane kaana.

## Maa ma'aane kaa?

- Warqa, gaaca ama gaaba, kaasi ma'aane
- Gaaca ilaa, makas ilaa, iyo masking tape
- Majallad, kataloog, warqa gaza, lab kaana ama sawarsa aalad
- Qalam, pencil, crayon ama marker
- Lolo kaadhan, sawarsa ama ilaa kaa ka'a

## Sadda nooc kaarbuddi fudhan

**Kaarbuddi kollaaj.** Warqa kaana maud mid, kaasi hal-hal, lolo ama waxyaabbaa dagaaga. Warqa tuma kaa, kaasi jiidh ama gar. Kaasi ina ma'aane ogoo kaana. Kaarka [make a color collage](/activity/make-a-color-collage/) setup tarah kaana.

**Kaarbuddi sawarsa.** Maalmaa mid waxyaabbaa sawarsa, pencil ama pen, bar ka'a iyo kaana. Sawarsa dhaabbaan ma'aane kaana. Kaarka [sketch what is on the table](/activity/sketch-what-is-on-the-table/) ma'aane.

**Kaarbuddi gaaba iyo sawarsa.** Gaaba gabaabbaa qoraa, kaasi sadda sagaal ma'aane maalmaa, ama sawarsa midi daaqa. Inta hal ama lamma ma'aane.

## Qalbi-fiqqe ma'aane hal-hal

Kaarbuddi ka'a, hal-hal qaano, kaasi dhaabbaan kaana.

1. Ilaa ka'a ka'a, subaxa kaa midi ama qoraa.
2. Kumma warqa ama gaza ka'a, mid mid tuma ma'aane, kaasi gacaa ilaa ka'a.
3. Sadda ka'a ilaa ka'a, daaqa, ama mid gar.
4. Sawarsa mid ilaa, mid koob, ama mid ilaa.
5. Inta tikit, leeñ ama warqa gaza ka'a, kaasi mid warqa ilaa.
6. Inta sagaal maalmaa, kaasi ilaa.
7. Inta foto ka'a, kaasi dhaabbaan ma'aane ilaa.
8. Gacaa dhaabbaan sawarsa, kaasi pattern ma'aane.

Hal-hal ma'aane, hal mid ka'a ama hal-hal ma'aane kaana.

## Qaano ma'aane

Qaano kaarbuddi ma'aane. Kaarbuddi ka'a, kaasi nama tuma. Hadaa aaladda telephone ama tablet kaana, passcode kaana. Ina ama dagaaga ma'aane, kaasi maa ka'a ma'aane. Nama ma'aane, qoraa ma'aane.

## Hal-hal laabbaan

- **Fadhaa ama sariir.** Kaarbuddi tray ama clipboard, gaaca iyo gaza kaana.
- **Gacaa ma'aane.** Marker dhaabbaan, gaaca dhaabbaan, ama warqa dhiiba.
- **Ilaa ma'aane.** Hal-hal dhaabbaan, warqa aad ka'a, marker kaana.
- **Hal-hal kaana.** Lamma warqa, hal prompt. Gaaca iyo gaza kaana.
- **Gaaba.** Sticker, midi, ama lamma ilaa mid bogga kaana.

## Ilaa nabbaa

- Gaaca ama gaaca aan-toxic kaana. Beads ama buttons ma'aane, nama tuma ilaa.
- Makas iyo warqa geeska kaana.
- Ilaa scented ama gaaca cusub, nama neefsi ama allergy kaana ma'aane.
- Foto nama kaana ma'aane, hadaa ma'aane ogoo.

## Hadaa shaqeyn ma'aane

Bogga ma'aane, dhaabbaan. Kaasi ina ama folder ka'a, ama bogga mid ka'a. Prompt qalbi-fiqqe ka'a, dhaabbaan. Kaarbuddi kaana, kaasi wax calool ma'aane ka'a, shaah ama socod. Qalbi-fiqqe ka'a, nama aminaa ama hel-line kaana.

Kaarka [finish the saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) ma'aane hal-hal. Hal-hal kaarbuddi dhaabbaan ka'a. Kaasi kaarbuddi hal-hal ma'aane, wax wanaagsan.

Hadaa ina dhibaato ama isaa dhaawac ka'a, ma dhaabbaan. Crisis route kaa bogga ka'a, ama lambarka emergency ma'aane kaana.
