Yaʼaʼ fanaʼ ilkaʼ, gaʼaʼ-ta boʼoʼ marka-ta kaʼaʼ. Shape ama hadaf yaʼaʼ-ta, boʼoʼ-ta kaʼaʼ-ta, gaʼaʼ-ta ilkaʼ gabat. Ufoʼ-ta gaʼaʼ-ta sheeʼ, fanaʼ-ta hadaf sheeʼ-ta. ## Kaa-ta fanaʼ kaʼaʼ?

Yaʼaʼ fanaʼ ilkaʼ, gaʼaʼ-ta boʼoʼ-ta, fanaʼ-ta ilkaʼ. Ilkaʼ-ta fanaʼ, pattern-ta fanaʼ. Ufoʼ-ta fanaʼ ilkaʼ, ama ilkaʼ gabat-ta. Table ama bed-ta, lap tray-ta. Ilkaʼ sheeʼ, diagnosis sheeʼ. Kanaʼ fanaʼ-ta, kanaʼ sheeʼ.

## Hadaf maʼ?

- Paper boʼoʼ, half sheet ama sketch pad
- Qalam boʼoʼ, pencil ama eraser
- Colored pencil ama marker boʼoʼ
- Hadaf yaʼaʼ, kuub, laʼaʼ, shell ama key
- Clipboard, table ufoʼ-ta ama bed-ta

## Kaa-ta slow drawing fanaʼ?

1. Hadaf table-ta, light-ta, kuufa sheeʼ.
2. Yaʼaʼ part-ta kaʼaʼ, kuub rim ama laʼaʼ outline.
3. Boʼoʼ-ta gaʼaʼ, hadaf-ta ilkaʼ paper-ta sheeʼ. Ilkaʼ wander.
4. Lafaʼ boʼoʼ, ilkaʼ, hadaf-ta ilkaʼ.
5. Sheet-ta part kaaʼ, pattern fanaʼ: circles, waves ama dots. Shape same, one after another.
6. Color ufoʼ-ta gaʼaʼ, color yaʼaʼ, area yaʼaʼ.
7. Wakhte gabat stop. Page-ta kaʼaʼ, ilkaʼ-ta another day.

## Kaa-ta fanaʼ itaʼ?

- **Repeating shapes.** Loop ama triangle, strip paper-ta fill. Size ama spacing yaʼaʼ-ta.
- **Slow grids.** Squares grid, square-ta mark kaʼaʼ. Order sheeʼ.
- **Color by area.** Color yaʼaʼ loose shape, edges meet. [paint with watercolors](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/) ufoʼ.
- **Drawing to sound.** Music fanaʼ, line music-ta follow. [draw to music](/activity/draw-to-music/) card.
- **Texture rubbing.** Paper leaf ama tile-ta kaʼaʼ, crayon side rub. Pattern ilkaʼ.

## Easier versions

- **Seated ama bed-ta.** Paper clipboard ama lap tray-ta, pen reach-ta.
- **Easier to grip.** Thick marker ama foam tape. Shape large.
- **Low vision.** Dark marker white paper-ta, hadaf large, light good. Shape-ta aloud describe.
- **Fewer choices.** Pen yaʼaʼ, pattern yaʼaʼ, color sheeʼ.
- **Shorter.** Row shapes yaʼaʼ, stop. Lamma daqiiqa bakhle.
- **Start already on page.** Outline kaʼaʼ, lines ama color inside.

## Safety notes

- Pen caps, sharpeners, erasers small children ama mouth-ta things kaʼaʼ-ta bakhle.
- Washable markers, non-toxic paint, label ilkaʼ.
- Tool pain ama grip weak, stop. Larger tool, wrist rest, ama another day.
- Clean table, ink ama water mark sheeʼ.

## If it isn't working

Bored, tired ama uninterested, simpler pattern ama stop. "Cannot draw" yaʼaʼ, mark-making subject sheeʼ: lines, dots, loops. Pain fanaʼ, stop, warm drink, familiar fanaʼ. Stop good outcome, page kaʼaʼ.
