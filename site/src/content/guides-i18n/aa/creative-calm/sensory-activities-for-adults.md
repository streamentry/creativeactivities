Dhaqamada dareenku waxay isticmaalaan dareemida taabashada, urgada, dhagaysiga iyo aragga. Dhadhanka ma ku jirto, sidaas darteed waxba ma ku jiro oo lagu cuni loo sameeyay. Waa waayo-aragnimo fudud oo ku saleysan hal dareen mar kasta, sida inaad taabato huwan jilicsan, urgeyso budeeno, ama dhagaysato roobka. Tani waa dhaqan abuurid, ma aha daawo. Hubinta yar ee nabadgelyada ayaa ka dhigta mid raaxo leh qof walba. Haddii adiga ama qofka aad daryeelayso uu leeyahay dhibaato caafimaad, sida dhibaatada liqidda, cudurka maqaarka ama xasaasiyadda, marka hore la hadal dhakhtarkaaga ama xirfadle diiwaan-gashan.

## Yaa u dhaqan kan?

Dhaqamada dareenku waxay ku habboon yihiin dadka waaweyn marxaladaha nolosha kala duwan. Waxay u fiican yihiin qof jeclaada inuu wax taabto, qof tamarta yar oo doonaya shaqo fudud, iyo qof maalinta badan kursi ama sariir ku jira. Waxaa kaloo laga wadaagi karaa jiilalka kala duwan, sida ilmo iyo qof waaweyn oo isla boorsada dharka taabanaya. Kuwani waa talo, ma aha ogaysiis caafimaad.

## Maxaad u baahan tahay?

- Dambiil ama sanduuq alaabta lagu rido, oo daboolka leh haddii aad rabto inuu nadiif yahay
- Alaabta weyn ee ammaanka leh: laba jeex oo dhar jilicsan, qashin alwood ah, dhagax jilicsan, ama geed yar oo udgoon ah
- Saliid aan urgu lahayn ama mid fudud, haddii maqaarku caafimaad qabo oo qofka aan xasaasiyad lahayn
- Qol aamusan oo TV-ga la damiyay
- Dharka kulul, koob biyo ah iyo kursi uu qofku ku fadhiisto

## Fikradaha dareenka, dareen kasta gooni

**Taabasho**

- **Masaajka gacanta.** Saliid aan urgu lahayn yar gacmahaaga ku kulayso, oo si tartiib ah ka bilow bowdada ilaa farta. Kaarka [give a hand massage](/activity/give-a-hand-massage/) wuxuu leeyahay tallaabooyinka.
- **Boorsada dharka.** Laba jeex oo weyn oo velvet, corduroy, fleece, satin iyo suuf ah, si loo taabto oo loo duubo. Kaarka [make a texture board](/activity/make-a-texture-board/) fikradaas wuxuu u beddelaa mid la kaydin karo.
- **Duubista iyo sifaynta.** Maro nadiif ah ama salfaydh la duubo, la isku dhejiyo, dib loo duubo. Kaarka [fold towels together](/activity/fold-towels-together/) waa shaqo joogto ah.
- **Dharka kulul.** Dharka kulul oo qoyan gacmaha maalinta qaboowga. Marka hore heerkulka ku tijaabi dhabarka gacmahaaga.

**Urgo**

- **Caleemaha udgoon.** Caleemo budeed, ruush ama basil ah ku kuli farahaaga intaadan ku dhowaynin sankaaga.
- **Urgaha guriga.** Qaxwo la karkarayo, rooti la gubayo, ama liin la qashayo oo u dhow.
- **Lavender qalalan.** Boorso yar oo la sooday oo xidhan. Kaarka [make a lavender sachet](/activity/make-a-lavender-sachet/) wuu tusayaa sida loo sameeyo.

**Dhagaysi**

- **Muusikada aad jeceshahay.** Heeso aad yaqaanto oo codkoodu jilicsan yahay. Eeg [music for calm and connection](/creative-calm/music-for-calm-and-connection/).
- **Codka dabeecadda.** Roob, mawjado ama codka shimbiraha oo si jilicsan loo shido.
- **Shaaqiyaha.** Kaarka [make a sealed shaker](/activity/make-a-sealed-shaker/) wuxuu sameeyaa mid ammaan ah oo lagu ciyaaro muusikada hortiisa.

**Aragga**

- **Daaqada la fiirinayo.** Koob cunto shimbiraha ah oo daaqada dibaddeeda ku yaal, ama aragga waddada. Kaarka [watch birds from a window](/activity/watch-birds-from-a-window/) fikrado ayuu leeyahay.
- **Midab iyo qaab.** Badhanka ama dhar-jeexyada midabkooda ku kala saar kaarka [sort buttons by color](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/). Badhanno weyn kaliya dooro.
- **Ubaxyo.** Kaarka [arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) waa hab jilicsan oo lagu taabto, lagu eego, oo ubaxyo cusub lagu urgeeyo.

## Sidee sanduuq dareen ah loo sameeyaa?

1. Mawduuc ku xidhan nolosha qofka dooro: xeebta, beerta, guriga cuntada, qalabka gacanta ama tolidda dharka.
2. Shan ama lix alaab weyn oo ammaan ah dooro, sida qolof badda oo weyn, qashin alwood ah, maro jilicsan, boorso lavender ah oo xidhan ama bilokki alwood ah oo jilicsan.
3. Alaab kasta hubi si aad u hubiso qaybaha yaryar, geesaha jaban, xadhkaha furan iyo wax kasta oo jabi kara.
4. Mid mid soo saar. Gacanta ku sii, sharax, oo u oggolow qofka inuu baadho.
5. Kadib sanduuqa dib u dhig, oo alaabta mar kale hubi ka hor isticmaalka xiga.

## Qof waaweyn ama qof qabta dementia ayaa la daryeelayo

Dhaqamada dareenku waxay ugu muhiimsan yihiin marxaladaha dambe ee dementia. [Alzheimer's Association](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage) waxay xusaysaa in marxaladdan dunida ay ugu horrayn la kulmaan dareenka, oo daryeelka waxaa lagu muujin karaa taabasho, dhagaysi, arag, dhadhan iyo urgo. [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) waxay soo jeedineysaa in dhaqamada marxaladda dambe ay inta badan xiiseeyaan dareenka, sida muusikada, alaabta gacanta lagu qabto, iyo masaajka gacanta.

Hore uga soo horjeeda, magaca qofka u sheeg, oo ka hor intaadan taabanin weydii ama u sheeg: "Saliid baan haysaa. Ma rabtaa in gacanta la sido?" Mid mar kasta u soo bandhig. Eeg wajiga oo jilicsan, gacmaha oo degan, ama dhoola-cadeyn. Haddii ay dhaqaajiyaan, ka xumaadaan, ama ay ku wada ahaadaan, taas macnaheedu waa jooji. Kalfadhiga ku ekaysii qiyaastii shan daqiiqo oo degan.

## Digniinaha nabadgelyada

Dhaqamada dareenku waa kuwo jilicsan, laakiin khataro ayaa muhiim ah, gaar ahaan qofka alaabta afka geli kara ama dhibaatada liqidda leh.

| Khatar | Waxa la sameeyo |
| --- | --- |
| Hunguri-gelinta | Alaab weyn oo aan la liqi karin keliya isticmaal. Badhanno, ciddin, sarcaado ama alaab yar oo sanduuqa ku jira ha dhigin. |
| Alaab u eg cunto | Saliidaha, saabuunta, shaambada iyo udgoonka isticmaalka ka dib xidh ama dhig meel ka fog, sababtoo ah waxay u eg yihiin ama u urgeysanayaan cunto. Miraha been abuurka ah ka qaad. |
| Xasaasiyadda | Ka hor bilaabista hubi xasaasiyadda ku jirta lowska, latex-ka, suufka, dhirta ama udgoonka. |
| Urgo xoogan | Urgaha xoogga leh iyo saliidaha dabiiciga ah ka fogaado, gaar ahaan qofka sambabta ama neefsashada dhibaataysan. Urgo jilicsan oo dabiici ah ayaa ammaan badan. |
| Maqaarka | Ka hor masaajka hubi maqaarka xanuunsan, jabaya ama fudud. Cadaadis jilicsan isticmaal, oo haddii ay ka qanciyaan jooji. |
| Heerkulka | Dharka kulul iyo cabitaanka marka hore gacmahaaga dhabarka ku tijaabi. |
| Qalabka geesta leh ama sun ah | Qalab geesood leh, muraayad ama alaab farshaxan oo sun ah ha isticmaalin. |

## Aromaterapiyaa ma caawisaa?

Ma aha wax la cabbiri karo. [Cochrane review of aromatherapy for dementia](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003150_aromatherapy-dementia) waxay heshay 13 daraasadood oo ay ku jiraan 708 qof, dhammaantood ku nool guryaha daryeelka. Daraasaddu way yaryihiin, natiijooyinkooduna isma waafaqin, waxayna ogaatay in aan caddayn jirin faa'iido ku saabsan natiijooyinka ay eegtay. Haddii qofku ku raaxaysto urgo jilicsan oo aad loo yaqaan, sida lavender-ka beerta ka soo baxa, taasi waa sabab wanaagsan oo lagu wadaago. Taasi ma aha daawo.

## Doorasho dareen yar

Qaar waxay ka daalaan dhaqdhaqaaq badan. Nooca dareen yar wuxuu isticmaalaa hal alaab, iftiin jilicsan, buste kulul iyo urgo la'aan. Wada fadhiista oo u oggolow iftiinka inuu darbiga ku dhex socdo, ama dhagax jilicsan oo yar qabso. Cod hoose isticmaal, oo mar kasta nasashada u bandhig. Haddii qofku dhinac u jeesto ama indhaha xidho, taasi waa kalfadhi dhamaystiran.

## Haddii aanu shaqaynin

Haddii qofku ka careysanto, jooji, alaabta dhig, oo si aamusan u fadhii. Maalin kale ama waqti kale dareen kale isku day. Qaarka waxay doorbidaan kalfadhi subaxda, qaarna waxay doorbidaan habeenka oo guriga aamusan yahay. Waqtiga hore u joogsashada waa natiijo wanaagsan.

Nasashada [calm breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) waa hab jilicsan oo lagu soo gabagabeeyo kalfadhiga, oo mid kasta neefsad tartiib ah, iyo cadaadis la'aan inuu wax dheeraad ah sameeyo.
