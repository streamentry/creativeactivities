Kuwani waa hawlo abuurid oo nafta qabsiisa oo dadka waaweyn guriga ku isku dayi karaan. Ma aha farshaxan daaweyn, ma daweeyaan, ma bogsiiyaan, ma ilaaliyaanna xaalad kasta. Waa siyaabo raaxo leh oo aad wax ku abuurto gacmahaaga, keligaa ama qof aad daryeeshaa la wada. Haddii aad rabto inaad ogaato waxa farshaxan daaweyn yahay sida xirfad, marka hore akhri [farshaxan daaweyn ama hawl abuurid](/activities/art-therapy-or-creative-activity/), ka dibna soo noqo halkan fikrado ah.

## Waa maxay hawlaha farshaxanka nafta qabsiisa ee wanaagsan ee dadka waaweyn?

Fikrad kasta hoose waxay qaadataa hal ama laba jumlad si loo sharaxo. Badankooda waxay ku xidhan yihiin kaarka hawl oo buuxa oo tallaabooyin, alaabo iyo noocyo fudud leh.

1. **Rangiyada biyaha.** Midab ku daadi warqad qoyan oo ka daa inuu fido. Natiijo qalad ah ma jirto. Eeg [rangiyada biyaha lagu rinji](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/).
2. **Mosaic warqad la jeexay.** Warqad midab leh u jeex qaybo yaryar oo ku dhaji sawir. Scissors looma baahna. Eeg [mosaic warqad la jeexay samee](/activity/tear-paper-mosaic/).
3. **Dhagax beer la rinjiyeeyey.** Dhagax jilicsan midab fudud ama qaab ku rinji, ka dibna meel dibadda ah ku dhig oo labaduba ku arki kartaan. Eeg [dhagax beer rinji](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/).
4. **Caleemo iyo ubax la dhaaftay.** Caleemo balaadhan warqad dhex ku dhig buug culus toddobaad, ka dibna kaarka ku isticmaal. Eeg [caleemo iyo ubax dhaaf](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/).
5. **Ubax habeyn.** Ubaxyo yar jarjar oo weel ku dhig mid mid. Eeg [ubax wada habeyn](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/).
6. **Xusuus collage.** Sawirro iyo sawirro joornaal ka soo qaad oo kaarka ku dhaji, oo mid kasta ka hadal intaad sii waddo. Eeg [xusuus collage samee](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/).
7. **Lawx taabasho.** Dharka yaryar, suuf iyo alaabo kale oo taabo leh kaarka adag ku dhaji si aad wax u taabato oo aad u eegto. Eeg [lawx taabasho samee](/activity/make-a-texture-board/).
8. **Sawir muusigga hoostiisa.** Muusig labadiinba jeclaataan dhig oo khudood iyo qaabab ka raac codka. Eeg [muusig hoosta sawir](/activity/draw-to-music/).
9. **Kaarar salaan.** Kaar kala laab, sawir iyo fariin gaaban ku dar qof labiinba aqoontaan. Eeg [kaar salaan samee](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/).
10. **Sawir batoon.** Batoonada midabkooda kala soo qaad oo kaarka ku dhaji. Batoonada ka fogee qofka ka cabsi qaba inuu liido. Eeg [sawir batoon samee](/activity/make-a-button-picture/).

## Sidee galab u diyaariyaa?

Wax walba ka hor inta aadan fadhiin diyaari. Warqad jeex, biyo shub, burushaha u dhig si aan qofna u sugin inuu alaab doondoono. Miiska ku dabool xaashi plastik ama mid hore loo isticmaalay.

Isku-dhinac fadhiiso oo qaybtaada keligaa shaqee. Haddii ay caawiso, koobka hore labaduba wada bilaabi. Midabka, dareenka iyo xusuusta ka hadal intaad shaqeyneysid, oo ka fogee su'aalo imtixaan sida "Maxay tahay tan?" Fikrado dheeraad ah sida marxaladda, eeg [hage farshaxan iyo sanacad](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/), habka hawlaha wada qabsadaana eeg [sida wada ciyaarto](/caregiving/how-to-play-together/).

## Sidee hawl u fudaydiyaa?

Mashaariicdan badankood saddex siyaabood ayaa lagu fudaydin karaa. Midab ama alaab yar bixi. Jarista, gooynta ama dhajinta naftaada samee. Galabta u gaabi toban ama shan iyo toban daqiiqo.

Haddii gacmaha ama indhaha ay si degdeg ah u daalaan, mashruuc fadhiyo oo weyn dooro, sida rangi biyo ah oo warqad weyn ku dhex jira. Doorashada ku dhaaf: qofku wuxuu dooran karaa mawduuca, midabka ama inuu bilaabo iyo ma bilaabo.

## Goorma ayaan joogsannaa?

Jooji marka qofku daalay, caajiyay, niyad jabay ama murugooday. Shaqada meel ammaan ah dhig, oo wax nasasho leh u soo bandhig, sida koob shaah ama socod aamusan. Shaqo dhammaan aan ahayn waa wax fiican, mar kale ayaad ku noqon kartaa maalin kale, ama aadan ku noqon.

Hakad-joojinta hore natiijo wanaagsan ayay tahay, ma aha guul-darro. Ulajeedku waa wakhti wada qaadashada oo farxad leh, daqiiqado yar oo wax wada abuurto ayaa ku filan.
