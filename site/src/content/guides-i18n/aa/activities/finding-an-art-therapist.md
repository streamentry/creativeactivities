Art therapist sirrii ah argachuuf yeroo dheeraa fudhata. Maqaan isaa bakka hunda wal fakkaatu miti, namoonni art sessions kennan hunda therapy irratti leenjifamanii miti. Fuula kun credentials US fi UK-ah akkamitti ilaaluu barbaachisu, official register akkamitti barbaaduu fi session jalqabaa dura maal gaafachuu akka qabdu ibsa. Ilaalaa jiruu yeroo mana keessan keessatti maal gochuu danda'u illee ibsa.

## Jalqabbaa eenyuu gaafachuu qabna?

Doctor ama licensed mental health professional gaafadhu. Isaan akka art therapy namicha ati kunnamtu sana toltu ta'u ta'e himuu danda'u, referral kennuus danda'u. Namichi memory loss ykn dementia qaba yoo ta'e, doctor seenaa isaa beekuun therapist experience qabu akka isiniif fuulu danda'a.

Yoo professional sa'ii keessatti argachuu hin dandeenye, namichi rakkoo ykn balaa keessa jiraate, doctor, crisis line ykn tajaajila hatattamaa quunnamaa.

## US-ah credentials akkamitti ilaaluu?

Art Therapy Credentials Board tiin tarreessu ilaali. [American Art Therapy Association](https://arttherapy.org/credentials-and-licensure/) isaan ibsa:

- **ATR** (Registered Art Therapist) barnoota art therapy sadarkaa digrii ol-aanaa fi muuxannoo hordofamaa booda isa barbaachisa.
- **ATR-BC** (Board Certified) barnoota fi muuxannoo barbaachisaa itti dabalee, sochii national exam keessa darbuudhaan argama.

Mootummaan tokko tokko art therapists licence ni kenna. Board licensing mootummaa keessan therapist-tiin bakka jiraattan keessatti hojjechuu isaa mirkaneessuu danda'a. Therapist-tii maqaa credential fi lakkoofsa isaa kennuu gaafadhu, ati mataan isaa ilaali.

## UK-ah register akkamitti ilaaluu?

UK-ah, "art therapist" fi "art psychotherapist" maqaa eegaman dha. Hojjettoonni Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC)-ah registered ta'uu qabu. [GOV.UK fuula art therapist registration irratti](https://www.gov.uk/art-therapist-registration) barbaachisummaa kana ibsa.

Therapist ilaaluuf:

1. Maqaa guutuu isaa fi lakkoofsa registration isaa gaafadhu.
2. [HCPC register](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/check-the-register/) barbaadi, kun online-tti bilisa fayyadamuu danda'u.
3. Maqaan fi maqaan art therapist isa therapist-tiin si himte wal fakkaatu mirkaneessi.

Yoo therapist-tii register irratti argachuu hin dandeenye, session hin booki. Ilaalchisee ibsa akka kennu gaafadhu.

## Session jalqabaa dura maal gaafachuu qabna?

Gaaffii gabaabaa tokko filannoowwan wal bira qabuuf si gargaara:

- **Muuxannoo.** Older adults wajjin hojjettee beektaa? Namoota dementia qaban wajjin? Meeqa?
- **Iddoo.** Sessions eessa taa'u: kiliinikii, mana kunuunsa, mana keessan, ykn online?
- **Cost.** Session tokko meeqa godha? Insurance ykn coverage biraa ni fudhattaa? Sliding scale jiraa?
- **Akkaataa.** Session baay'ee maal akka fakkaatu? Hamma turti? Namoonni baay'ee sessions meeqa qabu?
- **Maatii.** Miseensonni maatii hirmaachuu danda'u? Milkaa'ina akkamitti ibsatu?
- **Dhaabbachuu.** Namichi itti fufuu yoo hin barbaadne, ykn sessions rakkoo yoo fide maal ta'a?

Therapist gaarii gaaffilee kana ifatti deebisa. Yoo saaxilamaa ykn cunqaa'aa kan itti dhagayamu yoo ta'e, sun odeeffannoo barbaachisaa ti.

## Ilaalaa jiruu yeroo mana keessan keessatti maal gochuu dandeenya?

Referral eeggachuun homaa hin hojjettu jechuu miti. Creative activities mana keessan keessatti torbee keessan keessa yeroo gammachuu ta'uu danda'u, therapy jedhamuu hin qaban. Garaagarummaa profession fi sochii keenya irratti, [art therapy fi creative activities](/art-therapy-and-creative-activities/) ilaali. Jalqabbaa salphaa kanneen armaan gadii:

- [Draw to music](/activity/draw-to-music/), sirba dhagayuun sararoonni fi halluuwwan akka hordofan godhi.
- [Make a greeting card](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) namicha lamaanuu beektan tokkoof.
- [Arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/) bitaa tarree irratti waliin bokkaa keessatti.

Yeroo garaagaraaf filatamu ilaalchisee, [arts and crafts guide](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/) keenya ilaali. Garaagarummaa art therapy fi creative activity ibsa salphaa ta'een dubbisuuf, [art therapy or a creative activity](/activities/art-therapy-or-creative-activity/) dubbisi. Namicha kunuunsuun humna ni gaba, kanaaf boqonnaa kee ittiin qabdu yeroo ni qopheessi. Ilaalchisee [caregiver self-care guide](/creative-calm/creative-self-care-for-caregivers/) ilaali.
