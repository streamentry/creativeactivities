Creative arts therapies waa qoys xirfado caafimaadka maskaxda ah. Mid kastaa wuxuu isticmaalaa nooc farshaxan oo gaar ah xiriir daawo oo ku dhisan: art, musiqa, daansa iyo dhaqdhaqaaq, drama iyo poetry. Mid kastaa tababar gaar ah buu leeyahay, therapist kastaana shahaado u gaar ah xirfaddiisa buu haysta. Bogaggan wuxuu sharraxayaa noocyada, sida expressive arts therapy u isku daraa, sida ay uga duwan yihiin waxqabadyada abuurista ee maalinlaha ah, iyo waxa cilmi-baadhistii dementia muujisay.

## Maxay yihiin shan creative arts therapies?

[National Endowment for the Arts](https://www.arts.gov/initiatives/creative-forces/clinical-program/type-of-creative-arts-therapies) wuxuu liistada creative arts therapies. [National Coalition of Creative Arts Therapies Associations](https://nccata.org/) ayaa isku keena ururrada xirfadeed.

### Art therapy

Art therapy wuxuu isticmaalaa abuurista farshaxan, sida rinjiyeynta, sawirka, collage ama farshaxanka dhuxusha, xiriir daawo dhexdiis. Therapist-ku wuxuu la shaqeeyaa qofka iyo farshaxankiisa si uu u gaaro ujeedooyinka qorshaha ku jira. Art therapists waxay shahaadooyinkooda ka helaan ururradooda xirfadeed. Eeg [sida loo helo art therapist](/activities/finding-an-art-therapist/) si aad u hubiso mid.

### Music therapy

Music therapy wuxuu isticmaalaa musiqa, sida dhegeysiga, heesta, ciyaarta aaladaha iyo qoraalka heesaha, si loo gaaro ujeedooyin daawo. [American Music Therapy Association](https://www.musictherapy.org/about/musictherapy/) ayaa sharraxda xirfadda. Mareykanka, music therapists waxay helaan shahaadada MT-BC.

### Dance and movement therapy

Dance and movement therapy wuxuu isticmaalaa dhaqdhaqaaqa jirka iyo daansada si loo baadho dareenka iyo ujeedooyinka. Therapist tababaran ayaa hogaamiya kulanka, wuxuuna eegaa sida qofku u dhaqdhaqaaqo, u jawaabo oo ula xiriiro dadka kale.

### Drama therapy

Drama therapy wuxuu isticmaalaa role play, sheeko sheegid iyo farsamooyinka riwaayadda qorshe daawo dhexdiis. Waxaa hogaamiya drama therapist tababaran.

### Poetry therapy

Poetry therapy wuxuu isticmaalaa akhrinta iyo qorista gabayada, iyo noocyada kale ee qoraalka, xiriir daawo dhexdiis. Waxaa hogaamiya poetry therapist tababaran.

## Waa maxay expressive arts therapy?

Expressive arts therapy wuxuu isku daraa in ka badan hal nooc farshaxan galab keliya. Therapist-ku wuxuu u gudbi karaa sawir ka dib gabay gaaban, ka dibna layli dhaqdhaqaaq. Waa hab daawo, ma aha kooxo waxqabad fudud. Sida creative arts therapies kale, waxaa ku shaqeeya xirfadle tababaran.

## Sidee ayay uga duwan tahay sing-along ama galab farshaxan?

Sing-along, koox rinjiyeyn ama fasal daansa waa waxqabad abuuris. Waxaa loogu talagalay raaxo, saaxiibtinimo iyo waxyaabo la sameeyo wadajir. Ma jiro ujeedo daawo, qiimayn, ama qorshe daaweyn.

Farqiga waa tababarka iyo ujeedada. Creative arts therapist wuxuu tababaran yahay inuu la shaqeeyo baahiyada caafimaadka maskaxda, wuxuuna qorsha u dhigaa ujeedooyin. Hogaamiye kooxeed oo heeso duugoo ah u ururiya kooxda guriga daryeelka waa wax wanaagsan oo bulsho, laakiin ma aha daawo. Midkoodna ma liito. Waa kuwo kala duwan oo keliya.

[Hagaha musiqa](/creative-calm/music-for-calm-and-connection/) iyo [hagaha farshaxanka iyo garaacidda](/crafts/easy-crafts-for-adults/) ayaa sharraxaya waxqabadyada abuurista guriga iyo goobaha daryeelka. Waxqabadyo sida [rinjiyeynta muusikada ku salaysan](/activity/draw-to-music/) ama [buug heesaha shaqsiga](/activity/make-a-personal-songbook/) waa fikrado abuur oo caadi ah, ma aha daawo.

## Waa maxay waxa cilmi-baadhistii dementia muujisay?

Cilmi-baadhistu waa mid xaddidan. [Cochrane review](https://www.cochrane.org/CD011073/DEMENTIA_art-therapy-people-dementia) oo 2018 ah wuxuu eegay art therapy dadka ku nool dementia. Wuxuu helay laba cilmi-baadhis oo yaryar, oo wadarta dadka ahaa 88. Dhaqaaqayaashu waxay soo gabagabeeyeen in caddaynta aan ku filnayn in la yiraahdo art therapy waa shaqeysaa mise maya. Caddaynta waxay ku qiimeeyeen tayo aad u hooseeya.

Taasi waa qoraal ku saabsan cilmi-baadhis, ma aha sabab lagu joojinayo farshaxan raaxo leh. Farshaxan iyo musiqa wadajir ayaad weli ku raaxaysan kartaa iyaga oo aan sugeyn natiijo daawo. [Bogga Alzheimer's Association ee musiqa iyo farshaxanka](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/art-music) waa bilow aad wax uga akhrin karto. Fikrado u habboon heerarka kala duwan, eeg [hagaha xusuusta](/activities/reminiscence/), oo si fiican ula socda waxqabad sawir ama musiqa.
