Ciyaar wayya dementia qabbanaytiyya ummadoo ta'ii, ayta ta'ay qooli ta'a. Ummadoo lamma ta'ay dhaboota ilaalan, ta'ay ta'ay ta'a, ta'ay ta'ay. Ciyaar ta'ay ummadoo kaka.

Qaadhan guuddi ciyaar dhaamaan, ta'aynaan, ciyaaroota \"[games](/games/)\" ta'ta iyo kaarta, faasa'a, ciyaar sirra ta'a gabaa.

## Ciyaar ummadoo dementia qabbanaytiyya ta'ay ayga hayyasa?

Ayga ta'a, jaaraa hoorima kasmina. Kasmina [Alzheimer's Association guidance on activities](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities): kayaa ummadoo ayga, ta'ay; kasmina ummadoo ta'ay, dhaboota ayga aakta.

Ayga ta'a:

- **Gaa'a ta'ay, dhaqqaaq kaan.** Gaa'a ta'ay, ilaalaa tuffa ta'a.
- **Ciyaar tuffa ta'ta.** Ciyaar lamma ta'ay ummadoo.
- **Kaa ta'iyya dhaqqaaqa.** Ummadoo dhaboota kaa ta'a, ta'ay gaarii sirra ta'a.
- **Ta'ay gabaa, kasmina ciyaar.** Ta'ay gabaa ta'ay qaadhi.

## Ciyaar ta'ayta ifo ta'ay ka'a?

Ayga ceeysi, kasmina ta'a. [National Institute on Aging (NIA) guidance on adapting activities](https://www.nia.nih.gov/health/alzheimers-caregiving/adapting-activities-people-alzheimers-disease) ta'a ummadoo wakti ta'ay ceeysiisa. Lamma ta'a: ta'ay gabaa. Nagaa ta'a: [calm late-afternoon activities](/creative-calm/calm-afternoon-activities/).

1. **Wakti ummadoo ta'ay fili.** Kasmina ta'ay.
2. **Bakka nagaa ta'ay.** TV ta'ta radio ta'ay. [Family Caregiver Alliance](https://www.caregiver.org/resource/caregivers-guide-understanding-dementia-behaviors/) ta'ay.
3. **Ummadoo sahal ta'ay.** Ta'ay, ifo, kursi sahal, muuka iyo dhaboo.
4. **Ifo gabaa.** Ifo ta'ta ayga ilaali.
5. **Ciyaar ta'ay kasmina.** Ta'ay sabbuuro ta'ay.
6. **Ta'ay ayga ilaali.** Ayga ta'ay ta'a ta'ay.

## Ummadoo ta'ay ayga ta'a kaan?

Ta'ay kasmina ta'a. Kaa'a sabbuuro:

- \"Ummadoo ta'ay ilaali?\"
- \"Gabaa ta'ay, kaa ta'ay.\"
- \"Lamma ta'ay: haakhi ta'ay, kasmina?\"
- \"Ta'ay kasmina ta'ay.\"

Alzheimer's Association ta'a kasmina ta'ay ayga ta'a. Ummadoo kasmina, ta'ay kasmina.

Ummadoo \"ayga\" ta'a, gabaa ta'ay. Ta'ay [what to do when they say no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) kasmina.

## Ciyaar daqiiqa akum, dhaqqaaq akum?

Ummadoo dhaqqaaq ta'ay. Ciyaar ta'ay; daqiiqa kaan.

- **Gabaa ta'ay.** NIA ta'a ummadoo gabaa.
- **Ta'ay ta'ay.** \"Ta'ay ilaali\". Ilaalaa kasmina.
- **Ayga ta'ay.** Ummadoo ta'a ta'ay.
- **Ummadoo ilaali.** NIA ta'a ilaali ummadoo.

## Jawaabta dogaysi ta'a?

Kasmina. Dogaysi ummadoo sirra ayga, ummadoo ta'ay. Alzheimer's Association ta'a: ta'ay kasmina.

| Ta'ay | Kaa |
| --- | --- |
| \"Kasmina, ta'ay.\" | \"Ayta kaanaa ta'imee.\" (NIA ta'a) |
| \"Kasmina, ilaali.\" | Ta'ay, dhaboo ta'ay: \"Lamma dhaboo.\" |
| \"Ummadoo akaadhaa?\" | \"Ummadoo ilaali.\" |
| \"Kasmina, ta'ay.\" | \"Sahal ta'ay. Ta'ay ta'ay.\" |
| \"Ta'aa, ta'ay.\" | \"Ta'ay ilaali?\" |

Ummadoo ta'ay, ta'ay ayga. Dhaboo kaa, ta'ay ayga. Sabbuuro ta'ay, ta'ay.

## Ciyaar sahal, ta'ay akum?

Ciyaar ummadoo ta'a wakti ta'ay. Ummadoo sahal ta'ay, NIA ta'a ummadoo ta'ay.

| Ciyaar | Sahal ta'ay | Lamma ta'ay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Picture Pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) | Start with 4 tiles and leave pictures visible | More tiles, or hide pictures if that feels comfortable |
| [Find the Same](/games/find-the-same/) | 2 choices | Up to 4 choices |
| [Sort Together](/games/sort-together/) | You hold the picture, they point to the basket | They tap both picture and basket on their own |
| [Picture Puzzle](/games/picture-puzzle/) | 4 pieces, talk about the whole picture first | 9 pieces |
| [Finish the Saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) | Show the ending, then say it together | Let them say the ending before tapping |
| [Word Search](/games/word-search/) | Print it, look for one word together, point to its first letter | Let them choose the next word to find |
| [Coloring](/games/coloring/) | You pick two colors, they choose between them | They choose all the colors |
| [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) | Just look at the picture together | Use the open prompts on the card |
| [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) | A few well-loved family photos | Add more photos |
| [Picture Bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) | A helper sits with each player | Players mark their own cards |
| [Calm Breathing](/games/calm-breathing/) | Just sit and watch it together | Breathe along slowly |

Ciyaar hoorima: dhaboo dhaboota, \"ani kasmina\" ta'ay, ta'ay ayga, ummadoo ta'ay. Ciyaar sahal: ta'ay, ta'ay kasmina, ummadoo ta'ay. Ta'ay ta'ay, kasmina, ta'ay.

## Ciyaar ta'ay sabbuuro akum?

Sabbuuro ta'ay ta'ay ummadoo. Sabbuuro dhaboo ta'a.

- **Ta'ay ta'ay.** \"Ta'ay ilaali?\" \"Ta'ay kasmina?\"
- **Kaa ta'ay.** \"Ta'a ta'ay.\" Ta'ay ummadoo kaa.
- **Ta'ay ta'ay.** Dhaboo ta'ay, ta'ay ta'ay.
- **Ta'ay ta'ay.** Ta'ay ta'ay ta'ay.
- **Ta'ay ta'ay.** [Finish the Saying](/games/finish-the-saying/) ta'ay, [Conversation Cards](/games/conversation-cards/) ta'ay. [Photo Pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) ta'ay.

Ta'ay [reminiscence activities](/activities/reminiscence/). Ta'ay [first steps after a diagnosis](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/).

## Yaad ciyaar ta'ay?

Ummadoo gabaa ta'ay, ta'ay. Alzheimer's Association ta'a: ta'ay.

Ilaali:

- ta'ay ta'ay;
- ta'ay, ta'ay;
- ta'ay \"ta'ay ta'ay\";
- ta'ay ta'ay;
- ta'ay ta'ay.

Ta'ay:

- **Sahal ta'ay.**
- **Ummadoo ta'ay.** \"Kaa ta'ay?\"
- **Ta'ay ta'ay.**
- **Ta'ay kasmina.** \"Ta'ay kaa ta'ay.\"

Ta'ay, ta'ay, ta'ay ta'ay ta'ay ta'ay.
