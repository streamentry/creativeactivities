Dementia kabbalha ka ajale, hayne kabbalha, daa'ay, tabbaxa deeqa ta'iyo, aagisaa mooraa ta'iyo gaaradhaa. Nagaa kaa'ay kabbalha dhaggaa'a bahaa; kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa, ta'isa ta'iyo mooraa ta'iyo. Kan ta'isa kaa'ay tilaamsitaa, aagisaa kan ta'isa dhaqaa'a. Kaa'ay amaraa ta'iyo, aagisaa ta'isa ta'iyo kabbalha ta'iyo.

[Alzheimer's Association guide to life after diagnosis](https://www.alz.org/alzheimers-dementia/diagnosis/life-after-diagnosis) follow-up tayse, deeqa tabbaxa, aagisaa planning ta'iyo ta'isa. [Dementia Australia's guidance on coping with a diagnosis](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/coping-your-diagnosis) kaa'ay ta'isa amaraanaa dhaqaa'a, deeqa ta'iyo kabbalha ta'iyo. Hayne bahaa kaa'ay, personal medical, legal ko financial plan ta'aa.

## Inta tilaamsitaa jalqabbitaa?

Mooraa kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa, kan ta'iyo ammi-ayyan deeqa. Kan ta'iyo follow-up tayse, kan ta'iyo informasiyo amaraa, ko kan ta'isa dhaggaa'a amaraa. Kaa'ay ta'isa, mugi ta'iyo, ko aagisaa deeqa ta'iyo.

Follow-up kan ta'iyo tayse, kaa'ay ta'isa dhaggaa'a. Kabbalha ta'iyo aagisaa, kaa'ay ta'iyo, kan ta'iyo follow-up, ko kan ta'iyo ta'isa amaraanaa. Kaa'ay ta'iyo kabbalha ta'iyo yaasaa tayse, kan ta'isa dhaggaa'a kabbalha.

Kaa'ay dementia bahaa jalqabbi ta'iyo. Kan ta'iyo informasiyo amaraa tayse, aagisaa dhaggaa'a. Kaa'ay ta'isa cure ko deeqa ta'iyo, tabbaxa ta'iyo kan ta'isa.

## Deeqa ta'iyo inta tabbaxaa?

Kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa, deeqa ta'iyo kan ta'isa dhaggaa'a. Amaraa ta'iyo, ka'isa ta'iyo, ko kan ta'isa kaa'ay. Kaa'ay ta'iyo ta'isa dhaggaa'a, ko kan ta'iyo tabbaxa aagisaa.

Deeqa kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa, ta'isa ta'iyo ta'iyo. Aabaa kan ta'isa ta'iyo deeqa, ko kan ta'iyo mooraa. Kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa ta'iyo. Kaa'ay ta'isa ta'iyo dhaggaa'a.

Our [caregiver self-care guide](/creative-calm/creative-self-care-for-caregivers/) kaa'ay ta'isa deeqa ta'iyo ta'isa. Kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa, kan ta'iyo tabbaxa ta'iyo, kaa'ay ta'isa dhaggaa'a ta'iyo.

## Inta kaa'ay ta'iyo aagisaa ta'iyo?

Dementia kabbalha bahaa, kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa, ko aagisaa tayse ta'iyo ta'isa. Kaa'ay ta'isa dhaggaa'a. Aagisaa dhaggaa'a, kan ta'isa amaraanaa, ko kan ta'iyo ta'isa.

Kaa'ay ta'isa dhaqaa'a, kan ta'iyo aagisaa. Kaa'ay ta'isa ta'iyo, kaa'ay ta'iyo dhaggaa'a. Kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa, ko kan ta'iyo tayse.

[Dementia Australia planning guide](https://www.dementia.org.au/living-dementia/what-next-after-your-diagnosis/planning-ahead) kaa'ay ta'isa dhaggaa'a, aagisaa tayse ta'iyo. Legal dokumentaa ko kaa'ay ta'isa, location kan ta'iyo. Kaa'ay ta'isa amaraanaa dhaqaa'a. Bill ko scam kaa'ay ta'isa bank ko adviser.

### Inta ta'iyo ta'isa planning ta'iyo?

Kaa'ay ta'isa, kaa'ay ta'iyo tayse. [Alzheimer's Association recommends several small conversations, plain language and listening for the person's wishes, beliefs and values](https://www.alz.org/professionals/health-systems-medical-professionals/management/advanced-care-planning). [Alzheimer's Society notes that people differ in how ready they feel to think ahead](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/get-support/living-with-dementia/planning-ahead). Kaa'ay ta'isa pause, ta'iyo kan ta'isa.

Kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa ta'iyo, kan ta'isa:

- "Inta ta'isa aagisaa kaa'ay ta'iyo?"
- "Kaa'ay ta'isa planning ta'iyo kan ta'isa?"
- "Inta ta'isa comfortable ko respected ta'iyo?"
- "Inta ta'isa care team ta'iyo kan ta'isa?"
- "Kaa'ay ta'isa aagisaa tayse, kan ta'isa dhaggaa'a?"

Dhaggaa'a, decision bahaa. [National Institute on Aging describes meaningful conversations about values and preferences as central to advance care planning](https://order.nia.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-03/caring-for-a-Person-with-alzheimers-disease.pdf). Kaa'ay ta'isa, date, ko kan ta'iyo. Kaa'ay ta'isa permission. Notes kaa'ay ta'iyo, legal dokumentaa ko medical order ta'aa.

Diagnosis ko conversation kaa'ay ta'isa ta'iyo assumption bahaa. Kaa'ay ta'isa ta'iyo, pause ta'iyo, ko tayse ta'iyo.

## Inta kan ta'isa diagnosis dhaggaa'aa?

Kaa'ay ta'isa, kaa'ay ta'isa ta'iyo deeqa. Kan ta'isa, kan ta'iyo ta'isa. Kaa'ay ta'isa ta'iyo, ko kaa'ay ta'isa dhaggaa'a.

Kaa'ay ta'isa ta'iyo aagisaa, kan ta'isa. Kaa'ay ta'isa informasiyo, kan ta'iyo ta'isa. Doctor, social worker ko adviser kaa'ay ta'isa.

## Daily life kaa'ay ta'iyo?

Kaa'ay ta'isa routine ta'iyo, kan ta'iyo ta'isa. Diagnosis kaa'ay ta'isa hobby ko outing bahaa. Kaa'ay ta'isa harder, kan ta'isa change ta'iyo.

Kaa'ay ta'isa space ta'iyo, planning bahaa. Check in ta'iyo, care meeting bahaa. Planning break ta'iyo, aagisaa ta'iyo.

## Professional inta ta'iyo?

Kaa'ay ta'isa clinician ta'iyo, diagnosis, health, medicine ko symptom. Legal ko financial kaa'ay ta'isa qualified professional. Website kaa'ay ta'isa treatment ko service ta'iyo.

Sudden confusion ko change kaa'ay urgent medical attention. [NHS guidance on sudden confusion](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/confusion/) medical help immediately. Local emergency ko urgent-care system ta'iyo; sudden change usual ta'aa assume ta'iyo bahaa.

Planning kaa'ay ta'iyo, ta'isa trusted person ko service ta'iyo. Kaa'ay ta'isa pace ta'iyo. Diagnosis important ta'aa, ko person interest, relationship, daily life define ta'iyo bahaa.
