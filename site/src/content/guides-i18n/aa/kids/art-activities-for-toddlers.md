Ilmaha art ila ka'a. Ilmi marka ka'a, ha'a dhiga, stickers irraa ayta, ila hayye ta'a. Kulli kaa'a ila hanbaya ta'a, ummuta gaarah ila mahab. Wayyo ta'a ila ka'a, ila gaarah ila dulaaya ta'a. Hanbaya ila ummuta 1 to 3 gaarah ilmaha ta'a, ila gaarah ummuta. Hazaya ila ka'a: kireyon wayyo, ha'a ila dhiga, stickers ila ayta.

Hazaya ila dulaaya, ilmaha ila hayye. Ilmi kulli ta'a, ila hanbaya kireyon ila ayta, ha'a ila dhiga ta'a ila hayye.

## Hazaya ila hanbaya

Hazaya ila ilmaha ila hayye, ila dhiga ila ka'a. Ilmaha 1 to 3 gaarah, ummuta gaarah ila mahab. Ilmi kulli ila hayye ta'a, ila ta'a ila gaarah ila dulaaya. Hazaya kulli ila hayye ta'a, ila gaarah ila tiyoo.

## Nagaa hanbaya

Ila dulaaya kulli ila dhiga.

- **Ummuta gaarah ila mahab.** Ilmi marka ka'a. Ila hanbaya ummuta gaarah, ha'a ila ilmo ila irraa ayta.
- **Wayyo ila kaa'a.** Ilmi ni'a ila mahab. Nemours KidsHealth ila hanbaya, balloon, sente, art supplies, ila gaarah ila kaa'a. Tubuk ila hanbaya ila ta'a, ilmaha 3 gaarah ila wayyo.
- **Ila dhiga kaa'a ila hanbaya.** Ilmaha 3 gaarah ila bead, button, bean, ila qabay ila hanbaya.
- **Balloon hin ila.** Ilmaha 3 gaarah balloon hin ila, latex ila foil. Balloon ila irraa ilmo ila wayyo.
- **Non-toxic, washable ila hanbaya.** Label ila ilaaq. Art and Creative Materials Institute ila seal ila hanbaya, Approved Product ila Cautionary Labeling. Label kulli ila dulaaya ila ilaaq.
- **Allergy ila ilaaq.** Ummuta ila food ila hanbaya, ila gaarah ila ilaaq. Food dye ila hin hanbaya, washable paint ila hanbaya.
- **Ni'a afar ila hin ila.** Kireyon, sticker backing, waraqa ila hayye ta'a ila irraa ayta.

## Kireyon wayyo waraqa guutu

Kireyon jumbo ila hanbaya, ilmi ila ka'a. Kireyon qabay ila hanbaya, ila gaarah.

**Hanbaya:** kireyon qabay non-toxic washable; waraqa guutu ila wallpaper; masking tape; tray ila wayyo; wet wipes.

**Hazaya:**

1. Waraqa table ila floor ila dhiga, ila hin dhaqa.
2. Ummuta ilmaha ila ta'a. Dhiga dheeraa ila ka'a, "Gadi, ila ka'a" ila dubbata.
3. Ilmaha kireyon tokko ila hanbaya, ila dulaaya.
4. Ilmi ila copy ta'a, ila dulaaya. Kireyon afar ila ni'a, ila gaarah ila hanbaya.
5. Dhiga ila mahab: "Kun diimaa." Mark ila ka'a, "Kun dheeraa."
6. Ilmi hayye, ila hayye. Harka wipe ila, waraqa ila tiyoo.

**Ila gaarah:** tray raised sides ila hanbaya. Kireyon ilmaha harka ila, harka ila hanbaya.

**Calm:** color tokko ila hanbaya. Floor ila cushion ila waraqa, ilmi ila ilaaq.

## Ha'a ila dulaaya

Ha'a ila dulaaya ila wayyo. Marka ka'a, ila dhiga ila hin ayta.

**Hanbaya:** bokkoo ha'a; brush ila sponge; wall ila fence ila paving; towel.

**Hazaya:**

1. Paving ila wall ila outdoor ila hanbaya, ila indoor card ila tray.
2. Bokkoo ha'a ila dhiga, ummuta ila ta'a.
3. Brush ila sponge ila dhiga, ilmaha ila ka'a. Ilmi ila gaarah ila ka'a.
4. Marka ila ayta, "Ha'a ila diimaa?" ila gaarah ila dubbata.
5. Brush ila dhiga, ilmi ila ta'a ila hanbaya.
6. Ilmi hayye ta'a, ha'a ila dhiga, brush ila wayyo.

**Ila gaarah:** sponge ila hanbaya, harka ila wayyo.

**Calm:** ilmi cup ila ha'a ila dhiga, tray ila ka'a.

## Stickers dulaaya

Stickers ila hanbaya, pictures ila ka'a, ila ayta ila dhiga. Ilmaha stickers ila hanbaya ila ta'a.

**Hanbaya:** stickers yaa'a non-toxic; waraqa; tray.

**Hazaya:**

1. Sticker tokko ila hanbaya, ilmaha ila ka'a.
2. Waraqa ila dhiga, ilmi ila ka'a.
3. Shape ila dubbata: "Round," "Star."
4. Stickers ila dhiga, ilmi ila dhiga.
5. Dulaaya ila mahab, sticker ila dhiga, ila hanbaya.

**Ila gaarah:** sticker ila hayye, waraqa ila dhiga.

**Calm:** stickers qabay ila hanbaya. Dulaaya dheeraa ila gaarah ila hin ta'a.

Stickers sheet ilmaha hin hanbaya. Stickers small parts ta'a, backing ila wayyo.

## Ilmaha ila hanbaya

Gaarah ila dulaaya ila ka'a. Ila waraqa ila paint ila, ilmi ila copy ta'a. Ummuta ila ta'a ila, ilmi ila hayye. Ideas ila [together ideas](/together/) ila ka'a.

Ila mess ila wayyo ta'a. Page ila ka'a ila hanbaya.

## Marka hin ta'a

- **Ilmi ni'a afar ila mouth ila dhiga.** Hayye, ila ka'a ila wayyo. Gaarah ila tiyoo.
- **Ilmi kireyon ila brush ila dhiga.** Kulli ta'a. Tokko tokko ila dhiga.
- **Ilmi ila cry, ila hayye.** Ila dulaaya ila hayye ta'a.
- **Ilmi dhaqa qabay.** Hayye ta'a, ila gaarah ila dulaaya.

Label ila ilaaq. Ilmaha health ila clinician ila dubbata.
