Pimaana tiyo gaaddal, gaalib, isbitaal ama hospiisa dhaqan, dhaqan kaaysa. Maay waqtaa kaa, maay gaaddal, dhaqan gaaddal ka ka. Pimaanka gaaddaa sanaa, kaa wassaa yaa kaayta. Kaa dhaqan kaa, maa ka aada maa, kaa ka tiyo hamma ka. Isbitaalka, hospiisa ka gaadda gaaqa baanda gaaddal.

## Dhaqanka gaaddal

Waqti dhaddal, dhaqanka ka tayyar.

- **Waqti gaaddaa.** Pimaana kaa waqtu yaa, kaa beyah ka maayyan gaaddal. Pimaana kaa ka gaaddaa wayyah ka ka.
- **Jadwalka baraa.** Family Caregiver Alliance dhaqan gaaddal, waqtu kaa dhaqan ka ka, dhaqanka gaddin ka ka.
- **Bakkaaliyoota ka ka.** Gaaddaa maa' gaaddal, kaa waqtu gaaddal ka, dhaqan, dhibaato, geeya gaddal.
- **Bakkaaliyoo gaadda.** Maay ka gaaddal ka, kaa ka yaa ka dhaqan. Gaaddal, ilqilmi, guuruu yaa.
- **Yaa gaaddaa.** Dhaqan giy gaddal gaaqa, dhaqan qaadqan. Kaa ka gaaddal, kaa dhayta.

## Maay gaaddaa?

Gaaddaa hamma, ka gaadda gaaqa tiya.

- **Baanda sawra** gaaddal, sawra fiyaysa. Gaaddaa sawra ka.
- **Playlist** kaa dhaqan, gaaddaa, mini fiyaysa.
- **Gaaddal dhaqan,** gaddal, shaal, ama galgal, gaadda gaaqa.
- **Kaa gaaddaa** pimaana kaa ka gaaddal, kaa ka tiyo.
- **Majalla ama baanda** dhaqan, gaaddal, kaa ka gaaddal, gaaddaa gaadda.
- **Gaaddaa ama lillii,** gaaddal ka ka, bakkaaliyoota gaaddal.
- **Tablet** gaaddaa sawra ama dhaqan. [Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) gaaddal, [photo pairs](/games/photo-pairs/) sawra gaaddal.

## Maay gaaddaa dhaqan?

Gaaddal dhaqan, dhaqan ka tiyo.

1. **Baanda sawra.** Maay dhaqan: "Kaa gaaddal lawwaa." Pimaana gaaddaa ka. "Kaa ti?" gaaddaa ka.
2. **Playlist, gaaddal.** Gaaddal dhaqan, dhaqan ka tiyo.
3. **Gaaddal dhaqan.** Gaaddaa gaddal gaaqa.
4. **Gaaddaa maa' ka,** pimaana gaaddaa. Gaaddal, ka gaaddaa.
5. **Gaaddaa gaadda,** pimaana gaaddaa. Ka gaaddaa.
6. **Dhaqan gaaddaa,** kaa, domino, ama sawra gaaddal.
7. **Gaaddaa sawra,** gaaqa, ama gaaddal filim.
8. **Gaaddal qoori,** gaaddal, baanda gaaddal.
9. **Ka gaaddal.** Gaaddaa yaa gaaddal, kaa waa' gaaddal.

Gaaddaa dhaqan ka, [reminiscence](/activities/reminiscence/) ama [activity ideas](/activities/ideas/) ka.

## Isbitaalka

Isbitaalka dhaqan dhaddaa. Isbitaalka bakkaaliyoo ka gaaddaa.

- **Maay gaaddaa.** Gaaddaa, dhaqan, kaa ka. Gaaddal ka, gaaddaa ka.
- **Bakkaaliyoota gaaddal.** Pimaana kaa, gaaddal dhaqan, kaa gaaddal.
- **Pimaana dhaqan.** Gaaddaa ka, gaaddal, gaaddaa ka.
- **Ka gaaddal.** Dhaqan ka, gaaddaa gaaddal, pimaanka gaaddal.
- **Waqti gaaddaa.** Gaaddaa ka, waqti gaaddaa.

## Ka dhaqan waqti

Hospiisa, ama dhaqan waqti kaa, dhaqan gaaddal. NHS [end of life care](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/end-of-life-care/) gaaddal.

- Gaaddaa maay ka, gaaddaa.
- Gaaddaa gaaddal, kaa ka.
- Gaaddaa gaaddal, waqti gaaddaa.
- Gaaddaa ka, gaaddaa gaadda.

## Dhaqan, hamma

- Gaaddaa dhaqan, gaaddaa ka.
- Gaaddaa ka, ka gaaddaa.
- Gaaddaa, gaaddaa dhaqan.

## Ka gaaddaa

Gaaddaa, kaa gaaddaa, gaaddal. "Gaaddal kaa." Bakkaaliyoota gaadda.

## Kaa gaaddaa

Gaaddaa, kaa dhaqan. Gaaddaa maa' kaa, gaaddaa gaadda. Gaaddaa gaddal ka, gaaddaa dhaqan.
