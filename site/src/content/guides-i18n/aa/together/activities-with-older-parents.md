Ayyo kaddiyan dhaqan uummu ayyo ilmo, ayyo abbo kaa ayyo qarraa, ka ayyo dhaabbah gaarra fayyaa. Dhaqannoh kaa ayyo gaarri ka, ayyo tahhaa. Dhaqankaa fayyaa iyo jirtu, dhaqan uumuu ka. Ayyo dhaqanno waa, ayyo kaddiyan ayyo ka ayyo, ayyo gaarra ka.

## Maqaaltuu?

Dhaqannoh gaarra ayyo gurracha guyyaan: qalam iyo warqa, radiyo ama talafoon ayyo mirkaanu, kuuba ceelli, kaarta, ama sheeko ayyo. Kaarta kaa dhaqan kaa maqaalta kaa. Dhaqan kaa ayyo qaraamuyyi, hin ilmo dhaqan gaarra.

## Dhaqannoh guyyaa fayyaa gaarra

Guyyaan fayyaa gaarra ayyo, dhaqannoh tamman iyo sadda daqiiqa gaarra. Dhaqannoh gaarra kaa ayyo fayyaa.

1. **Mirkaanu wada.** Sadda ama lamma ayyo kaddiyan dhaqan ka, ayyo ilmo ayyo filla. [Draw to music](/activity/draw-to-music/) qalam kaa ayyo.
2. **Albam sawira ilaali.** "Kanaa waa sheeko," ayyo ka, "Kun hin ma?" hin ka. [Photo album card](/activity/look-through-a-photo-album/) dhaqannoh kaa ayyo.
3. **Ciro gaaya kaa.** Kaleessa gaarra. Dhaqan kaa dhaqan ka. Eeg [wind a ball of yarn](/activity/wind-a-ball-of-yarn/).
4. **Shimbiro daaqqa ilaali.** Ifo gaarra ayyo ka. Eeg [watch birds from a window](/activity/watch-birds-from-a-window/).
5. **Dhaqan tap wada.** Dhaqan ka, gaaria ka. [Tap a rhythm together](/activity/tap-a-rhythm-together/) tallaaba.

Ilmo ayyo gacca ka, [give a hand massage](/activity/give-a-hand-massage/) dhaqankaa ayyo. Kaleessa qaraamuyyi, gacca kaa ayyo kaa hiifi.

## Dhaqannoh guyyaa fayyaa gaarra

Guyyaan fayyaa gaarra, dhaqan ayyo ka dhaqan.

- **Ubbaa wada qabbaa.** Gaaddih gaarra, dhaqan ka. Eeg [arrange flowers together](/activity/arrange-flowers-together/).
- **Watercolors kaa.** Warqa bal'aa ka. Eeg [paint with watercolors](/activity/paint-with-watercolors/).
- **Gaaddih wada dhaabbi.** Eeg [fold towels together](/activity/fold-towels-together/).
- **Sawira gaarra collage.** Eeg [make a memory collage](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/).
- **Batan midhaan kaa.** Eeg [sort buttons by color](/activity/sort-buttons-by-color/).
- **Playlist wada.** Eeg [make a playlist together](/activity/make-a-playlist-together/).

## Gacca, ilaalaa, dhaggaa iyo dhaqan

**Gacca.** Marker bal'aa, crayon bal'aa, ama burush gaarra. Warqa kaa ayyo.

**Ilaalaa.** Dhibaan ayyo bar, warqa ka gaarra. Ifo ayyo gaarra ka.

**Dhaggaa.** Ayyo kaa jiraa, hadal ayyo dhaqan ka, radiyo kaa hiifi.

**Dhaqan.** Dhaqan kaa gaarra kursi ama sariir. Biyo iyo ayyo ayyo ka.

**Hadal.** Sawira ama gacca ayyo ka. Dhaqan ka, dhaqan ka hadal.

## Abbo ama ayyo dhibaan jirrah

Dhaqannoh kaa ayyo dhibaan jirrah ka. Gaaddih: tokko tokko ka dhaqan, lamma ka ayyo, sheeko ayyo ka. Sheeko ka gaarra, "Kun ayyo ka" ka.

Alzheimer's Association [activities page](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) dhaqannoh kaa ayyo. NHS [activities page](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) dhaqan wada ayyo.

Dhaqanno ayyo ka, eeg [activities for people with dementia](/activities/ideas/) iyo [dementia activities hub](/dementia-activities/).

## Fayyaa dhaqannoh

- Batan, qaraanjo iyo hanqaa dhaabbi, gaarra ayyo. Dhaqan ka kaa qaraamuyyi.
- Mirkaanu iyo gaaddih ayyo gaarra.
- Dhaqan, gaaysa ayyo kaa, dhibaan ayyo wada.
- Dhaqan ka kaa, ayyo dhaqan ka biyo kaa.
- Yoo ayyo dhibaan, dhaqan ka, shaay ayyo, dhaqan kaa. [Guide on when someone says no](/caregiving/when-they-say-no/) kaa ayyo.

## Yoo dhaqan hin ayyo

Tokko kaa bedal: dhaqan, guyyaa, meeza, daqiiqa ama filla. Yoo ayyo hin ka, hin qaraamuyyi. Guyyaan dhaqan kaadiya. Ayyo gaarra ayyo.

## Ati ayyo

Ati dhaqan keenya. Dhaqan gaabbaa, ayyo fayyaa iyo tani. Hadal tamman, sawira tokko, gaaddih lamma. Dhaqan kaa sadda ayyo.
