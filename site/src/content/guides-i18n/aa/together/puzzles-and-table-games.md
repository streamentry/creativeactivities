Hindhiyo iyo taaqa gasa umuri kalkaliyo baay'aa ammi lakka. Jigsaw ammi dagaaso, ummuta hadal hin kaaye hinnay yaa gasa, pictures ammi dagaaso kaa siyata. Lakkaan gasa ama taaqa gasa siyaaki saddeen ummuta, abbaa, ilmaa iyo ilmaa gaddi, yoo rules ammi gasa ta'a. Ammi bogaa hindhiyo, taaqa gasa iyo lakkaan gasa lakka, ammi gasa kaliyo ta'a gasa bayyana.

## Maaliif hindhiyo taaqa ammi baay'aa?

Ummuta hiido iyo ayuuki dagaaso, ammi hin tuqa gasa. Daa'iyo ta'a yaa yaa, ammi gasa kaliyo hin ta'a. [Activities page](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dementia/living-with-dementia/activities/) NHS ammi gasa kaa siyata, online gasa iyo hindhiyo ummuta gasa yaa. [Activities page](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/activities) Alzheimer's Association ammi dagaaso, ayaanaa gasa ammi siyata, lakkaa hin ta'in.

Hindhiyo iyo gasa ayuuki iyo ummuta yaa. Ammi bogaa, ammi wa'a hin ta'a.

## Jigsaw kaasi yaa ammi lakkaa?

Ummuta dagaaso kaa siyaaki. Afar kaa fakha:

- **Pieces lakkaa.** Hindhiyo lakkaa ammi siyaaki, yoo ammi caafo ta'a, kaliyo.
- **Piece size.** Pieces ammi bal'a, ummuta dagaa gasa. Pieces mullu ta'a, bayyana.
- **Picture.** Garden, kitchen, doonii, horiiyo, ama hobby ummuta dagaaso. Cartoon iyo jireenta hin dagaaso. Kalo ammi dagaa, red barn ammi green field, plain sky ammi kaliyo.
- **Finish.** Matte finish ammi ifa gasa hin ta'in.

Photo ummuta ayuuki jigsaw ammi ceekkeeh ta'a. Print shop ammi tajaa ta'a.

## Jigsaw ammi ceekkeeh maaliif kaa?

1. **Bakka gasa pick.** Ifa gaddiyo iyo kursi harfo.
2. **Box lid prop.** Pictures ummuta dagaa.
3. **Pieces face up.** Gasa kaliyo.
4. **Edges ammi dagaa,** ama border kaa, middle ummuta dagaaso.
5. **Pieces color ammi lakkaa** trays shallow.
6. **Piece tuqa,** yoo pile caafo ta'a: "Kaa barn ammi part ta'a."
7. **Picture ammi hadal,** progress hin: "Kaa boat abbaayu ammi siyata."
8. **Ammi kaliyo.** Days ammi dagaa. Gasa hin ta'in.

## Hindhiyo ayuukiyo iyo word search

Word search iyo crossword bal'a kaasi ummuta hindhiyo siyaaki. Words bright color ammi circle, ummuta hadal. Topic ummuta dagaa: fruit, flowers, cars, ships, towns. Timer hin kaa. Word hin dagaa, kaliyo ama ummuta dagaa.

[Word search](/games/word-search/) ammi site timer hin, score hin.

## Lakkaan gasa iyo memory-style gasa

Memory gasa ammi cards face down, pair dagaa, hard ta'a. Pictures face up kaa, lakkaa gasa ta'a.

- **Pictures face up kaa.** Pictures dagaa gasa.
- **Pairs saddeen iyo sadeen kaa.** Caafo kaa.
- **Pictures bal'a, ummuta dagaa:** teacup, sailboat, sunflower.
- **Team ammi gasa.** "Dogs ammi dagaa" yoo "your turn" hin.
- **Score iyo clock hin.** Kulli win hin.

[Picture pairs](/games/picture-pairs/) pictures visible, ama [find the same](/games/find-the-same/). [Sort together](/games/sort-together/) pictures ammi kitchen ama garden ammi dagaa.

## Taaqa gasa umuri kalkalo

Taaqa gasa umuri kalkalo baay'aa. Card game matching hin score, ama [picture bingo](/games/picture-bingo/) pictures bal'a. Dominoes pairs ammi gasa.

Kulli taaqa gasa rules ammi dagaa:

1. Score hin, yoo kulli ummuta kaa.
2. Gargaar kaa, helper point ama card tuqa.
3. Kulli ummuta bira ama taaqa gasa daa'iyo.
4. Gasa ammi kaliyo ummuta gasa hin, win hin.

## Gasa caafo

- Pieces, cards ama pairs caafo.
- Tray edges ammi kaa, pieces hin dhaqqa.
- Pieces color ama shape ammi pre-sort.
- Card ummuta tuqa.
- Seated ammi gasa, board gasa.
- Low vision ammi high-contrast pieces, word games ammi large print.

## Safety notes

- Pieces caafo, game tokens iyo dice, saddeen umuri ammi hin kaa, ummuta afaa objects ammi kaa.
- Pieces tray ama box ammi kaa. Session ammi dhumma pieces lakka.
- Small magnetic parts ama button batteries hin.
- Ummuta caala yoo ta'a, gasa kaliyo, gasa bedda kaa, drink kaa.

## Yoo hin dagaaso

Pieces, pictures ama rules bedda. Ayuuki gasa ammi kaliyo, pieces sort kaa. Gasa dhagg yoo ta'a, kaliyo. Half-finished puzzle tray ammi kaa, hin siwis. Ummuta jigsaw ammi dagaa, gasa baay'aa.

## Someone living with dementia

Puzzle gasa ummuta dagaa yoo ta'a, picture familiar iyo pieces bal'a ammi pleasure bedda. Picture visible kaa, ummuta gasa kaa, piece piece tuqa. Caala ammi dagaaso: turning away ama pieces pushing. Next piece wa'a gasa, ama simpler gasa kaa. Ummuta rested ammi, hungry hin, rushed hin.
