Waqtiyuh kalinta maqlisah sababa. Gaalah waraaqa ka'in. Maalih ifan dhaqqan, laambad gaala ka'in. Ayyu waqtiyuh kaadah, ayyu tuuraah ka'in. Kalinta waqtiyuh sadhalah ka'in, qaadanah ayyu tarah ka'in. Fadhaa waqtiyu ayyu, aadaa dheebah ayyu, iyo kalinta kaadah mirgiyuh ayyu, kulli ummah baashiyah gaalah.

Kulli kalinta maqlisah, ayyu kawlaasi ayyu, maatih iyo kaadah. Ayyu waqtiyuh kaadah, kalmaah kaabah ayyu, kalinta kaadah ka'in. Kalinta waqtiyuh nam ayyu mirgiyuh, kulli nam kalinta ayyu baashiyah ka'in.

## Kalinta waqtiyuh maaysah mihqal?

Kaadah ayyu ka'in. Gaala waraaqa ayyu, ayyu tuura ayyu gaala kaadah. Ayaantuh nam mihqal:

- **Gaalah kaadah ayyu?** Gaala waraaqa, tuura ayyu, kalinta gaala kaadah.
- **Waraaqa iyo kaarta kaadah ayyu?** Kaarta dhaqqan, paper garland kaadah.
- **Kaaban iyo bahal kaadah ayyu?** Pine cone bird feeder kaadah, daqqa ifan.
- **Qaadan kaadah ayyu?** Texture board ama sealed shaker kaadah.

## Waqtiyu kulli waqtiyah kalinta caasihi

### Gaala

- **Tuura gaala ayyu.** Tuura gaalah ayyu, kompost kaadah. [Seed pot card](/activity/plant-seeds-in-a-pot/) kaadah.
- **Dhagaa ayyu gaala.** Dhagaa gaala kaadah, gaala ayyu iyo daqqa ayyu. [Garden stone card](/activity/paint-a-garden-stone/) kaadah.

### Qaadan

- **Lavender sachet kaadah.** Bu'ah lavender kaadah, fabric pouch. [Lavender sachet card](/activity/make-a-lavender-sachet/) kaadah.
- **Waraaqa gaala kaadah.** Gaala waraaqa dhaqqan, paper kaadah. [Leaf print card](/activity/print-with-leaves/) kaadah.

### Kaaban

- **Gaala iyo tuura ayyu.** Heavy book kaadah, hal usbuu', kaarta kaadah. [Pressing card](/activity/press-leaves-and-flowers/) kaadah.
- **Memory collage kaadah.** Waraaqa iyo sawirka kaadah. [Memory collage card](/activity/make-a-memory-collage/) kaadah.

### Tuura

- **Paper chain garland kaadah.** Waraaqa daqqa kaadah. [Paper chain card](/activity/make-a-paper-chain-garland/) kaadah.
- **Pine cone bird feeder kaadah.** Pine cone iyo bahal kaadah. [Bird feeder card](/activity/make-a-pine-cone-bird-feeder/) kaadah, nut-free kaadah.
- **Greeting card kaadah.** Kaarta waqtiyuh kaadah. [Greeting card card](/activity/make-a-greeting-card/) kaadah.

## Aadaa badan kaadah waqtiyuh

Nam badan waqtiyuh kaadah, nam kaabah ma. Aadaa ayyu laambad waraaqa, lugah, kaaba ayyu kaadah. Kalinta kaadah ayyu ma ahiyah. Kalinta boodah, aadaa ayyu, ummah ayyu ma ahiyah.

Kalinta waqtiyuh kaadah:

1. **Ayaantuh ka'in.** Ayyu waqtiyuh ka'in, kaadah ayyu. Nam waqtiyuh ka'in, ama ma ka'in.
2. **Materiaal kaadah, symbol ma.** Waraaqa, gaala, lugah, tuura kaadah. Symbol diinta ama aadaa ma kaadah, ma'in kaadah.
3. **Aadaa dhaqan nam ayyu ka'in.** Aadaa ayyu kaadah, nam ayyu ka'in. Tarjamah kaabah ma.
4. **Kalinta ayyu kaadah.** Laba ama sadeh kaadah, nam ayyu "maya" ka'in.

## Kalinta gaabah maaysah?

- **Fadhiyyuh.** Kalinta kaadah ayyu mirgiyuh, miiz kaadah.
- **Qabbiyah ayyu.** Waraaqa dhaqqan, kaadah dhoobah. Kaarta kaadah dhaqan.
- **Ifan gaabah.** Ifan iyo waraaqa kaadah, sawir daqqa kaarta kaadah.
- **Kalinta kaabah.** Laba ifan kaadah, ama gaala iyo tuura kaadah. Sawir ayyu kaadah.
- **Gaabah.** Hal daqqa kaadah ayyu. Hal gaala kaadah ayyu.
- **Af ma.** Laba kaadah gaalah, kaaban kaadah iyo kalinta kaadah.

## Amaanah kalinta waqtiyuh

- **Ifan.** LED ama battery ifan laambad kaadah. Shamaa waraaqa, lugah, gaala dhaqqan ma ahiyah.
- **Kaadah.** Candy ama lugah dhaqqan ma kaadah. Nam kaabah ifan kaadah ayyu. Alzheimer's Association [holidays guidance](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) kaadah, ifan ayyu ayyu.
- **Qaybah daqqa.** Button, beads, ornaments kaadah, kaabah ummah dhaqqan. [HealthyChildren.org choking prevention page](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/choking-prevention.aspx) qaybah daqqa kaadah.
- **Gaala.** Gaala iyo bahal ma dhadhaa ma. Kaaba wadaa iyo harraa kaadah.
- **Bahal.** Kaadah baashiyah ma, kaaban dhaqan.

## Kalinta ma hoofa

- **Waqti ummah.** Kalinta gaala ka'in, ummah kaadah. Waraaqa ayyu, kaarta ayyu, gaabah.
- **Qaadan kaadah.** Fadhiyyuh kaadah, kalinta kaabah. Kalinta boodah kaadah.
- **Ummah kaadah.** Kalinta kaadah, dhaqan kaadah.

## Kalinta waqtiyu gaabah

Waqtiyuh kalinta ummah ayyu, kalinta kaadah aadaa ayyu. Kulli nam kaabah ayyu, kalinta kaadah ka'in. Waqtiyuh ayyu, nam ayyu waqti kaadah.

## Kalinta nam kaabah ummah, nam dheebah

Kalinta waqtiyuh ummah kaadah, nam dheebah kaadah. Kalinta kaadah ayyu, waqtiyuh kaadah. Kaarta ayyu, waraaqa kaadah, kalinta kaadah. [Reminiscence guide](/activities/reminiscence/) kaadah, waqtiyuh kaadah ayyu. Alzheimer's Association [suggests](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/holidays) kaadah, kalinta kaadah.

Nam dhaqan kaabah, ama kaaba ka'in, [after a dementia diagnosis](/caregiving/after-dementia-diagnosis/) kaadah.
