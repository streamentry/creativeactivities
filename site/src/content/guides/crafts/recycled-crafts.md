Recycled crafts start with things most homes already have: cereal boxes, egg cartons, cardboard tubes, yoghurt pots, jars, tins and fabric offcuts. They cost nothing, they are easy to find, and making something useful from them can feel satisfying in a way that buying a kit does not. This page covers which projects work well, how to prepare materials safely, and how to adapt each one.

These projects suit a household with a few boxes to hand, a classroom, a family with older children, or a care setting where the same materials come round week after week. They also suit someone who enjoys putting things together, or who likes working with materials that already have a story. The suggestions here describe what each activity asks for. They are not a judgment about anyone.

## Prepare your materials first

Preparation is most of the safety work, and it is best done before the person sits down.

1. **Wash and dry every container.** Use hot, soapy water, rinse well and let it dry fully. A damp container can grow mould inside a sealed project.
2. **Check what it held.** Do not use containers that held cleaning products, chemicals, medicine, pet food or anything you cannot wash out fully.
3. **Remove sharp edges.** Check tins and lids. File the edge, cover it with tape, or leave the piece out.
4. **Cut ahead of time.** Trim cardboard, cut windows and make slits yourself with a craft knife. Keep the knife with you, out of reach, and put it away after use.
5. **Sort small parts into a tray.** Buttons, bottle caps and beads go in a tray with raised edges. They should not be on the table loose.

## Which recycled crafts are easy to begin?

### Egg-carton flowers

Cut the cups from an egg carton, leaving a little edge. Paint them, let them dry, then cut a few petals and glue them onto a pipe cleaner or a stick. A few flowers in a jar make a bright table decoration. Ready-cut cups are easier to hold than whole cartons.

### Cereal-box picture frame

Cut a rectangle from the middle of a cereal box, glue a picture or a card behind it, and fold a strip of card to make a stand. The [decorate a keepsake box card](/activity/decorate-a-keepsake-box/) uses a similar approach to cover a box with paper or fabric.

### Yoghurt-pot planter

Wash the pot, make drainage holes with a skewer or a nail (adult only), decorate the outside with paint or paper, then add compost and a plant. The [plant pot card](/activity/decorate-a-plant-pot/) covers the steps and a version that is easier to hold.

### Sealed shaker

Fill a small, clean plastic bottle with dried rice or lentils, seal the lid with strong tape or glue, and decorate it. A helper should seal the lid. The [sealed shaker card](/activity/make-a-sealed-shaker/) has the steps and a version for quieter moments.

### Button picture

Sort buttons by color or size and glue them onto card to make a simple picture. Buttons are a choking hazard for children under three, so this project is for adults and older children only. The [button picture card](/activity/make-a-button-picture/) has the full steps.

### Fabric texture board

Glue scraps of fabric, ribbon and paper onto stiff card. It needs no cutting and makes good use of offcuts. See the [texture board card](/activity/make-a-texture-board/).

## What easier versions can you try?

- **Seated.** All of these work at a table. Set the materials within reach, so the person does not need to stand.
- **Easier to grip.** Use wide, lightweight containers, and wrap thin handles or lids with tape to make them thicker. Choose a lid that twists easily.
- **One-handed.** Tape the container to the table, or place it in a tray with raised edges so it stays still.
- **Low vision.** Pick containers with strong color contrast, and paint the outside with a bold color. Keep the work on a plain, light surface.
- **Fewer parts.** Choose projects with one or two pieces. A single decorated pot or one picture frame is a complete craft.
- **Shorter.** Pre-paint the pieces and let them dry, so the person only glues or decorates.

## Safety notes

- Never use glass, and avoid tins with rough or cut edges.
- Keep craft knives with adults only, and use them on a stable cutting board away from the person's hands.
- Seal anything that holds small items. Use strong tape or glue on the lid, and check that it cannot open.
- Keep buttons, bottle caps, beads and dried beans away from under-threes. The [HealthyChildren.org choking prevention page](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/choking-prevention.aspx) lists small balls, pen caps and button batteries among the items that cause choking.
- Use paints and glues labelled non-toxic. The [Art & Creative Materials Institute](https://www.acmiart.org/classroom-art-materials-safety) explains that the AP seal is a certification for art materials, and that adults who may not read labels should use non-toxic materials.
- Watch for allergies to glues, dyes and plant material.

## If it isn't working

- **The container keeps rolling or sliding.** Put it in a tray, or tape a wide base to the bottom.
- **The lid will not turn.** Wrap the lid with tape or a rubber band for grip, or choose a different container.
- **The project feels too big.** Choose a smaller container, or make one part and stop.
- **Smell or damp.** Wash and dry the container again before you continue. Do not use a container that smells of what it held.

## Doing it with an older adult or a child

Recycled crafts can be shared across generations. An older adult, including someone living with dementia, may enjoy sorting buttons, choosing paint colors or wrapping a pot with string. Let them do the part they still enjoy, and do the cutting yourself. For a child, keep an adult close, use only the parts that are safe for their age, and keep small pieces out of reach of younger children.

Some people find that a familiar container, such as a jar from the kitchen, is the most welcome material of all. Ask first, and keep it close to hand.

Creative Activities is not therapy. These crafts are a way to make something useful and enjoy the process together.
